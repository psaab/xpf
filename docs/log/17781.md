# #17781 — Keep a yielded arm-debt owner secondary

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-28
  - **Action**: A preempt-enabled primary with positive dataplane-arm debt
    yielded to a verified healthier peer, but the raw-priority arm could
    immediately promote it again while it remained secondary at weight 1.
    The local readiness flag is independent of this debt. `electRG` now
    reuses the verified arm-debt peer ordering for the yield and holds the
    yielded node secondary while that peer remains primary. Clearing the
    debt restores normal preempt ordering.
  - **File(s)**: `pkg/cluster/election.go`,
    `pkg/cluster/preempt_weight_threshold_16739_test.go`,
    `docs/log/17781.md`
