# #13638 — gate transit checks before session-hit side effects

- **Base**: `761931ff6` (origin/master at branch start)
- **Branch**: `fix/13638-campaign`

## Why

Session-hit packets could enter TCP close handling, TTL-expiry reply generation,
and session accounting before the common transit guards ran. That allowed
packets that should fail selector, source-class, or L2-group checks to trigger
side effects even though later forwarding would be denied.

## What

- Apply selector-fence, source-class, and L2-group transit checks immediately
  after destination-class denial on the session-hit path.
- Drop and recycle rejected frames before injected-session bookkeeping,
  promotion, deferred owner-close handling, and TTL-expiry reply generation.
- Add session-hit regressions covering L2-group RST, TTL 1, martian source, and
  bindless selector-fence rejection, plus a positive L2-group control.

## Validation

Targeted: cargo test --bin xpf-userspace-dp -- tests_session_hit_guards_13638 tests_martian_source_10689 tests_ipsec_selector_fence_10683 -> 23 passed. RED evidence: with prod hunk stashed, all 4 new tests failed (session_hit 1 vs 0).

## Risk

The checks follow existing flow-cache-hit guard semantics and run before
session-hit side effects. Rejected frames are recycled through the existing
owner, and allowed session-hit traffic is unchanged.
