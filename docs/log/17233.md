    mirroring `classifyOwnedBackend`. #3735's never-delete-at-the-wrong-endpoint
    rule and the #3734/#5285 restart/pending behaviour are untouched.

    their distinct fingerprint namespaces — Surface A `backendFingerprint`

    `dhcpBackendFingerprint` (fpb1) includes them (withdraw-routing signal).
    Only the both-non-empty mismatch RULE is unified. A TSIG-only or bind-only
    in-place Surface A edit still settles until the floor — accepted and
    documented in `pkg/ddns/README.md`.
