- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-17
  - **Action**: Fixed #14720 (D18-03) cutover identity confusion deleting the committed rollback target on verify/start failure. For a fresh cut, `j.PreviousVersion` records the *live* version (e.g. 2.0.0), so when the live version's committed predecessor (1.0.0) was re-staged under a new generation, `cleanupFailedVerifyCopy`'s guard `ver == j.PreviousVersion || ver == cur` never fired for 1.0.0: verify failure `RemoveAll`'d `versions/1.0.0` plus `.<ver>.dbsnap` (identical-bytes, changed-bytes, and same-generation-reuse arms), and `copyStaged`'s predecessor-preservation was demoted on byte mismatch before the replace. `defaultRollbackTarget("2.0.0")` then reported no restorable target. Fix: `Run` captures a `predecessorStampBackup` (committed stamp bytes + `.<ver>.dbsnap` copy under `.predbak-*`) via `backupCommittedPredecessor` before COPY whenever the target is the live version's committed predecessor; `cleanupFailedVerifyCopy` (now variadic) skips deletion for a backed-up predecessor, restores its stamp/snapshot, and keeps its `.dbsnap` instead of sweeping it as an orphan; pre-STOP refuse paths restore the backup. `copyStaged` marks the backup `demotedByReplacement` when it demotes changed/unverifiable bytes, so Case-C demotion is never resurrected: a just-failed or changed-byte target stays non-committed after auto-rollback (#12177 controls unchanged). Regression `cutover_predecessor_verify_14720_test.go`: cuts 1.0.0→2.0.0, re-stages 1.0.0 under a new generation, fails VERIFY for identical and changed bytes plus a same-generation-reuse arm — asserting dir, binaries, stamp (identical arm), and `.1.0.0.dbsnap` survive and `defaultRollbackTarget("2.0.0")=="1.0.0"` — plus START-failure controls asserting the dir/snapshot survive while the stamp stays demoted. RED on `origin/master` (version dir destroyed in all verify arms), GREEN after the fix; full `pkg/upgrade` suite green; RED-on-revert re-verified by stashing the production change.
