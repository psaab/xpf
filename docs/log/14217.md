# #14217 — IPv6 AH protocol identity in IPsec-inner zone policy

## Why

Issue #14217 reported that the merged #14211 squash evaluated an IPv6
inner packet whose extension chain sights AH (51) before TCP as TCP with
its inner ports, so a `tcp dst-port 443 permit` term could admit traffic
every other flowless/policy path classifies as protocol 51. The claimed
AH regression was absent from that squash.

## What

- The fail-closed production fix landed concurrently as PR #14215
  (merge `43ebebd38` on `origin/master`): `d14_zone_gate` now derives
  `effective_protocol` via `flowless_effective_protocol(&owned, meta)`
  before port parsing, suppresses ports unless the effective protocol
  is TCP/UDP, and evaluates policy with protocol 51 and
  `l4_present=false` for AH-sighted chains. This branch is based on that
  master and does not duplicate or revert that production change.
- NOT changed: `packet_rel_l4_offset_and_protocol_for_ipsec` / shim
  derivation — the correction belongs at the policy-evaluation site,
  mirroring PBR, NoRoute and embedded-ICMP, which already use
  `flowless_effective_protocol`. Rewriting the descriptor protocol
  would disturb offsets/payload derivation for other consumers.
- Complete the Expected regression matrix from #14217: factor the
  AH packet/input construction into shared test helpers, keep the
  landed `d14_ipv6_ah_sighted_tcp_443_does_not_match_tcp_term_14090`
  cell, and add
  `d14_ipv6_ah_sighted_tcp_443_matches_only_ah_term_14217`
  (the identical AH→TCP/443 packet admitted only by a protocol-51
  `ah` term) plus
  `d14_ipv6_ah_sighted_udp_443_does_not_match_udp_term_14217`
  (AH→UDP/443 denied against a UDP/443 permit). The v6 fixture is
  generalized to take the policy protocol/port without changing the
  plain IPv6 TCP/UDP cells' packets or policies.

  effective protocol were TCP (TCP has no `ah` term) and the deny

  test-only-green.

## Risk

Test-only change on top of the landed #14215 fix; no production
behavior delta beyond what #14215 already shipped. Residual: D14 is
the only IPsec-inner policy site; other ingress paths already used
`flowless_effective_protocol` and are untouched.
