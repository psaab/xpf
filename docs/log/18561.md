## Crash detection after #18570: mechanism, baseline, and the check cadence (2026-10-31)

- **Action**: mechanism analysis from 20 sysrq crashes on tcp (the #18597
  A/B), posted as issuecomment 6546215241.
  - A crashed VM sends nothing: zero packets from fw0 between its last
    heartbeat and fw1's `marking peer lost`, in 20/20 trials. The sync
    socket stays half-open and `IsConnected()` stays true.
  - The recency timestamp is genuine: it is the last frame fw0 sent before
    dying.
  - Detection fits `max(T, W - g)` plus one 200 ms tick across all 20
    trials, with a residual of 0.004-0.195 s. Here T is the threshold, W the
    2 s window, and g the last sync frame's lead over the last heartbeat.

    under iperf3 -P4 that was 3 of 20 trials.
