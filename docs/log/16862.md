# #16862 — Junos-host jump must not precede mandatory ICMP/ND admits

- **Issue**: R3-B06-02. In `xpf_hostinbound` the per-zone
  `iifname <zone> jump junos_host_<n>_<zone>` came before the ICMPv6 error
  and ND (133-136) accepts. A restricted permit's #11065 terminal
  `meta nfproto ipv6 drop` (including the v6 terminal a v4-only permit
  adds) or an explicit `application any` deny therefore dropped NS/NA/RS/RA
  on that zone, breaking IPv6 neighbour resolution in both directions
  under the canonical management hardening (`permit <mgmt-net> junos-ssh`).
- **Change**:
  - `pkg/daemon/daemon_nft.go` (text oracle): `emitHostInboundICMPAccepts`
    now runs before the fine junos-host jumps in the programs branch;
    stale "coarse-then-fine / denied source's ND must drop" commentary
    replaced. The no-programs shape already had this order.
  - `pkg/nftables/netlink_hostinbound.go` (production): same move in
    `buildHostInboundNetlink`; T1 host_inbound parity subtests pass.
  - Docs: `docs/host-inbound-service-matrix.md` records the ordering.
  - Echo/other ICMP stay subject to authored denies; `ndp except` views
    emit no ND admit and still drop ND. Multicast-group jumps/guards are
    untouched (ND dests are not catalog groups). Screens now meter ND
    after admission instead of pre-drop (accepted residual).
