# #16508: record_owed dedups owed bindings on (key, mac) instead of key

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
- **Action**: `KernelNeighborProgramLimiter::record_owed` in
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/neighbor_program_limiter.rs` deduped on
  `(key, mac)` via `bucket_owes`, contrary to its documented "update
  that key's existing owed entry": one key advertising 4 distinct MACs
  inside one interval occupied every owed way of its bucket and evicted
  a victim key's owed kernel program, which then lagged until its next
  changed advert. Now `record_owed` first finds `desired_key ==
  Some(key)` and overwrites its MAC and timestamp in place, so at most
  one owed way per key exists; the free-way and oldest-eviction fallbacks
  are unchanged, and no programmed binding/timestamp is touched, so the
  #5288 rate cap is preserved. Also fixed the dead `clear_owed` in
  `should_program`: the `!owes_other` branch implied `mac` equals the
  programmed MAC, so its inner different-MAC check could never run. A
  newer advert for the programmed binding now calls
  `clear_owed_superseded`, clearing owed entries for the key whose MAC
  differs from the programmed one; the proven-present early return for
  a binding the owning way does not hold is preserved as an explicit
  branch. Strengthened `owed_binding_superseded_by_newer_mac_15247`
  with owed-occupancy assertions and added
  `one_key_flapping_macs_does_not_evict_victim_owed_16508` (4-MAC flap
  vs single-advert positive control through the production
  `should_program`, asserting victim survival and `<=1` owed way per
  key) and `programmed_mac_advert_supersedes_older_owed_change_16508`.
  Clarified the owed semantics in `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/README.md`.
- **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/neighbor_program_limiter.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/README.md`, `docs/log/16508.md`
- **Validation**: `cargo test --bins neighbor_program` 16 passed
  (14 pre-existing + 2 new, with the strengthened supersede test).
  RED-verified: the new/strengthened tests against pre-fix production
  logic fail with `<=1 owed way per key: left 4, right 1`, `newer MAC
  must supersede: left 2, right 1`, and `stale owed MAC must be
  superseded: left 1, right 0`; all 13 other limiter tests stay green
  on the pre-fix logic. `rustfmt --check` clean on the touched file.
