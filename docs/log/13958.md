# Issue #13958: Preserve WireGuard cookie challenges under rotating-source floods

## Why

The per-source cookie-reply bucket starts each newly observed source with a full burst. A valid-MAC1 attacker rotating spoofed source IPs can consume the global 40-reply window budget and suppress a legitimate peer's first cookie challenge. The 2,048-entry fail-closed source-table limit also rejects new peers while the rotating flood keeps it full.

## What

Remove the tunnel-global cookie-reply budget while retaining the under-load gate and per-source token buckets. Keep the source table bounded, but evict the least-recently-refilled bucket on overflow so a full table does not deny new sources. Add coverage for 3,000 rotating sources and a subsequent legitimate challenge, plus bounded-table eviction coverage. Preserve single-source throttling and the bad-MAC1 no-reflection path.

## Risk

Cookie reply volume is bounded per source rather than globally; a rotating-source flood may generate many replies. The source bucket map remains capped at 2,048 entries, and overflow replaces the least-recently-refilled bucket to admit new peers.
