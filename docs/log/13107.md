# Issue #13107 — reject host-inbound `except` without a positive `all` (D1-02)

- **Issue:** `hostInboundFilterExcept` applied every recognized exclusion to
  the stanza's positive tokens without validating the grammar or ensuring a
  positive `all` base. Thus `system-services telnet except`,
  `system-services all except`, `system-services any-service except`, and
  `protocols all except` silently compiled to empty admission lists. The
  non-nil empty stanza is enforced as deny-all, with no commit error or
  warning; zone or interface management and routing traffic can be blocked.
- **Source:** Deep-review D1-02, High severity, EXECUTED/MATERIAL; three-way
  adversarial research validated the finding. Source report:
  `/var/tmp/deep-review-reports/gemini-4-argon-high-review-zone-enforcement-001.md`
  §3.
- **What:** Preserve whether each stanza authored `all`, including through
  same-key merges, clones, and physical/unit runtime unions. Strict validation
  rejects exclusions of `all`/`any-service` and any exclusion dimension with
  no positive `all` base. Tolerant ingestion retains the existing #1960
  compile-and-warn behavior. Regressions cover zone/interface and service/
  protocol cases, valid `all except <token>`, cross-scope exclusion after
  interface union, and a physical exclusion that leaves one unit without a
  positive base.

## Scope and impact

- Invalid host-inbound exclusion forms now fail strict commit. Tolerant loads
  remain bootable and emit a diagnostic rather than silently accepting an
  unbased exclusion. A valid named exclusion against `all`, including one
  whose positive base is contributed by another physical/unit override,
  remains supported. No runtime wire-format or dataplane change.
