  resume evidence from T_hi on. Same verdict at both ends is kept; any
  disagreement is VOID.

    0.07 s; without the margin one fell 17 ms outside); the 5 VOIDs judge
    PASS at both ends; the 4 agreed fallbacks stay PASS; the bounded verdict
    equals the precise verdict on all 11 precise runs.
