# #9716: an un-incarnated BulkEnd from a dead peer process completes the live bulk

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11T10:40Z
- **Action**: worktree `/var/tmp/wt-9716-bulkend-conn-fence` on origin/master 199f0a0cf (includes
  #9715), branch `fix/9716-bulkend-connection-fence`.
- **Action**: research, at 199f0a0cf.
  - The BulkEnd arm (`sync_conn_read.go`) refuses a marker only when BOTH boot incarnations are known
    and differ (#9174 V013). When either side is un-incarnated (a peer on an older build, or a bulk
    primed without one), completion is matched on the epoch alone.
  - Every peer process restarts its epoch at 1, so a BulkEnd buffered on a dead process's
    still-established socket carries a colliding epoch.
  - `notePeerBootIncarnation` reports a switch for zero -> X as well as for X -> Y. So an incarnated
    BulkStart on the other fabric, arriving while a legacy bulk is in progress at the same epoch, is
    ACCEPTED (not a #8966 stale refusal). The defect is then exactly the legacy BulkEnd that follows
    on the old connection.
  - `BulkSync` pins one connection (`getActiveConn`) for a whole bulk, so a legitimate BulkEnd never
    arrives on a connection other than its BulkStart's.
- **Action**: design and scope.
  - Record the connection that carried the accepted BulkStart (`bulkRecvConn`, under `bulkMu`, beside
    `bulkRecvIncarnation`).
  - In the BulkEnd arm, AFTER the incarnation check (so #9174's dead-incarnation counter keeps its
    meaning), refuse and count a BulkEnd on any other connection.
  - Count every completion the incarnation check could not judge (`BulkEndsEpochOnlyMatched`).
  - Clear the recorded connection with the epoch and incarnation on a full disconnect.
  - No wire change. A legacy peer's bulk, started and ended on one connection, still completes.
  - Out of scope: the between-bulks un-incarnated BulkStart. With no incarnation on the wire, a
    rebooted peer restarting its counter and a stale frame are the same bytes, and #9174 pins accepting
    it (`TestUnincarnatedStaleBulkStartBetweenBulksStillAccepted9174`). The acceptance asks for the
    BulkEnd fence and the counter.
- **Action**: change.
  - `pkg/cluster/sync.go`: `bulkRecvConn`; stats `BulkEndsForeignConnDropped` and
    `BulkEndsEpochOnlyMatched`, with their snapshot fields.
  - `pkg/cluster/sync_conn_read.go`: record the connection on the accepted BulkStart; the connection
    fence and the epoch-only count in the BulkEnd arm.
  - `pkg/cluster/sync_conn.go`: clear `bulkRecvConn` on full disconnect.
  - `pkg/cluster/status.go`: render both counters when nonzero.
  - `docs/sync-protocol.md`: a subsection for the fence, and the two status lines. The existing
    dead-incarnation line, which the list lacked, is added too.
  - Cells in `pkg/cluster/sync_bulkend_conn_fence_9716_test.go`:
    - the issue's acceptance: a legacy bulk on fabric 0, an incarnated BulkStart on fabric 1, then
      a legacy BulkEnd on fabric 0 does NOT complete the bulk; fabric 1's install lands, and its
      own BulkEnd completes it;
    - a legacy single-connection bulk still completes and counts as epoch-only;
    - an incarnated bulk is not counted as epoch-only.
