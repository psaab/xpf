# Issue #12819 — reject fused enforcement below policy exemptions

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-13
- **Change**: `security policies` previously treated `traceoptions`, `policy-stats`, and `pre-id-default-policy` as benign by keyword alone. Schema validation skipped their entire subtrees, while policy compilation skipped them without recording quarantine. A merged/fused enforcement subtree under one of those names could therefore disappear on strict and tolerant compilation. The exemption now accepts only their known non-enforcement AST shapes; any other shape is rejected strictly and recorded as an enforcement-bearing unknown for tolerant quarantine. Packed traceoptions values are constrained to their leaf grammar (`file` size/files options, implemented flags, one-value packet-filter leaves), so rulebase tokens cannot hide in a leaf tail. Per-entry status is retained separately from the warning-name list, so a benign sibling cannot hide a fused sibling. Existing supported non-enforcement forms remain accepted.
