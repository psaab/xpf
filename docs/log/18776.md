# #18776: keep gauge maxima independent of monotonic counters

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-02
  - **Action**: Added a gauge-specific sampled maximum reducer and routed the
    report-only `Pending TX local` and `Outstanding TX` rows through it. The
    existing monotonic reducer remains in use for counters and interface
    counters.
  - **Why**: `Outstanding TX` is a published transient in-flight gauge and can
    decrease normally. `Pending TX local` describes queue depth, but its
    status field currently has no publisher. The reducer is routed for both
    report-only rows, but this change does not claim that an emitted
    `Pending TX local` value can fall. Counter rewinds remain evidence of
    helper restarts and still VOID their rows.
  - **Cells**: Synthetic assurance fixtures use distinct sequences for the
    two metrics: `Pending TX local` 14→6 (phase-pre 20; sample maximum 14) and
    `Outstanding TX` 23→4 (phase-pre 30; sample maximum 23). They also cover
    all-zero samples with a higher baseline and a counter rewind (still VOID).
  - **Source trace**: `worker/loop_body/debug_report.rs` reads
    `pending_tx_local.len()` as a queue-depth gauge. However,
    `BindingLiveState.debug_pending_tx_local` is initialized to zero and
    snapshot-read without a `.store()` writer in the current
    `userspace-dp/src` tree. This publication gap is separate and unchanged.
  - **Validation**: Before the fix, a window with `Outstanding TX` samples
    `14, 6` returned status 2 with `counter rewound from 14 to 6 at
    sample-02`. `make test-ha-assurance-lib` passes: the production mapping is
    pinned, the distinct synthetic gauge sequences report 14 and 23 despite
    higher baselines, all-zero samples report 0, and the counter rewind still
    VOIDs. `bash -n` on all three changed shell files and `git diff --check`
    pass.

    `test/incus/test-failover.sh`, `test/incus/ha-assurance-selftest.sh`,
    `docs/log/18776.md`
