- **Action**: Proved the TEST stale before changing it.
  - The first-bad merge 8fdb112b3f (#17021) deliberately moved the
    new-session and missing-neighbor-seed SNAT decision into ONE composer,
    `session_glue::compose_outbound_source_nat` (forward_commit.rs). The

  `record_source_nat_failure`. A pool failure there leaves no source-NAT
  exception: a counting gap, not a fail-open. It is now pinned (drop on
  failure) and documented in the plan doc. Not filed separately; for
  team-lead to decide.
