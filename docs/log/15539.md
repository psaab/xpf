## What was unfenced

The #14969 fix checked `checkHostAuthSeam14969` only at the seven
owner entries and once before `exec.CommandContext` spawned. An owner
that passed its entry while still current, then was superseded
mid-owner (a newer closeout or a normal apply tail advancing
`hostAuthGeneration14969`), remained the last writer of:

- `daemon_hostauth_apply.go` — the authorized_keys durable write
  (was :498), the sudoers grant write/removal (was :778/:736 via
  `writeSudoersGrant`/`reconcileSudoers`), the sshd drop-in
  write/remove/revert (was :1193/:1119/:1208 via the `sshdWriteFile`/
  `sshdRemoveFile` seam vars), root's authorized_keys write/removal
  (was :1553/:1580), plus every provenance claim
  (`claimOwnership`) feeding them;
- `daemon_nft.go` — `InstallLo0` (was :241), `InstallHostInbound`
  (was :909), `InstallLo0ColdBootFence` (was :438), the gap/cold-boot
  fences and all `DeleteTable` teardowns behind
  `applyLo0Filter`/`applyHostInboundFilterWithOverlay`;
- `login_password.go` — `reconcileAbsentLoginUsers` (was :611) had NO
  entry fence at all, and its direct revocations
  (`removeManagedAuthorizedKeys`' `os.Remove`, `forgetProvenance`,
  the per-resource `removeProvenanceMarker` calls) were unfenced;
- `exec_timeout.go:42-47` — check-then-exec TOCTOU: a command spawned
  while current (chpasswd/useradd/usermod/systemctl) ran to completion
  and landed after the newer authority began.

The #14969 docs also claimed seams that did not exist
(`nftInstallerGuard14969`, sshd drop-in write/remove seam guards), and
`docs/log/15219.md` was missing.

## Change

- **Guarded seam helpers** (`hostauth_fence_14969.go`):
  `guardedWriteFileDurable14969`, `guardedWriteFileAtomic14969`,
  `guardedRemove14969`, `guardedMkdirAllDurable14969`,
  `claimOwnershipFenced14969`, `forgetProvenanceFenced14969`,
  `removeProvenanceMarkerFenced14969` — each re-checks the calling
  goroutine's generation immediately before its syscall and returns
  `errHostAuthStaleOwner14969` without performing it once superseded.
  Every direct write/remove/claim in `daemon_hostauth_apply.go` and
  the direct revocations in `login_password.go` go through them; the
  sshd seam-var defaults now ARE the guarded functions
  (`sshdWriteFile = guardedWriteFileAtomic14969`,
  `sshdRemoveFile = guardedRemove14969`).
- **NFT installer guard** (`daemon_nft.go`): `guardedNftInstaller14969`
  wraps `nftInstaller`; every mutating method re-checks immediately
  before the netlink transaction (read-only probes pass through). All
  lo0/host-inbound install/delete/fence call sites in `daemon_nft.go`
  go through `hostAuthNftInstaller14969()`. This lands the
  `nftInstallerGuard14969` the #14969 docs named.
- **Exec seam** (`exec_timeout.go`): `runWithHostAuthExec15539`
  re-validates BEFORE the spawn (a superseded owner never spawns),
  kills the in-flight child via a generation watcher when a newer
  authority advances mid-exec, and re-validates after return —
  a killed/stale exec reports `errHostAuthStaleOwner14969`, never
  success. Uncaptured goroutines run the plain pre-#15539 path.
- **`reconcileAbsentLoginUsers`** is fenced at entry; its key-removal
  and marker revocations are fenced at their seams (above).
- **Comment corrections** (`daemon_apply_hostauth.go`): the closeout
  budget comment now states the 15s(+5s WaitDelay)=20s figure is the
  per-command ceiling, not the 30s total budget; the abandonment
  comment no longer claims acceptability "only on the daemon-stop
  path" — since #14356 abandonment also happens on live
  post-promotion aborts, which is why the fence exists.
- **Docs**: README + `docs/log/14969.md` claims corrected to the seams
  that actually exist; `docs/log/15219.md` created (pointer to
  #14969/#15539 for A32-05).

No commit gating was added: the fence remains check-at-the-seam,
never a wait — the #12169 contract (a new commit returns while an old
revocation is blocked; the applySem settlement cannot deadlock) is
preserved. Uncaptured goroutines (normal tail, direct reconciler
tests, every non-host-auth exec/installer user) are never refused, so
neighbouring behaviour is unchanged.

## Risk

Bounded to the cited seams. A superseded owner abstains at its next
guarded seam and its remaining work is reconciled by the newer
authority's full tail (fail-closed direction, as #14969). The exec
watcher adds one 1ms ticker goroutine per captured-generation exec
only; uncaptured exec is untouched. Goroutine-id parse failure still
disarms the fence for that goroutine (pre-#14969 behaviour), never a
new corruption. Root-keys control in a non-root sandbox cannot land
the `WithOwner(0,0)` chown — the test asserts non-refusal there and
full landing where the sandbox permits.
