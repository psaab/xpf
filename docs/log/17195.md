- **Pre-existing unrelated `--tests` error — RESOLVED by merge**: the
  fold merges current `origin/master`, which carries the typed
  `UserspaceSnapshot` construction for `shared_umem_tests.rs` (#18234,
  fixing the #17134/#17149 `json!` mismatch). On the fold head
  `cargo check --tests` is rc 0 with no errors (see Validation), so

  pre-fold history below remains intelligible. Historical note: the
  error was `afxdp/shared_umem_tests.rs:247` constructing
  `ConfigSnapshot { userspace: json!({...}) }` against a typed field;
  it failed on stripped and unstripped trees alike and was untouched
  by this change.
- Items dead in both builds but retained under `#[expect]` (35 plain + 5 inner)
  are **not** deleted: the issue's fix direction (3) allows conversion to
  `#[cfg(test)]` **or deletion** for honest test-only items, and (4) mandates
  `#[expect]` for every remaining allow. Deleting staged-but-unwired surfaces
  (e.g. the WireGuard engine surface, `cold_path_hist` — the latter's allow was
  in fact stale and is deleted) is a separate per-item call for their owners;
  the expectation now makes that call unavoidable when a caller appears or
  when an owner next touches the item. `[INFERENCE]` a future cleanup could
  delete several of these outright.
- `event_stream/drain.rs stalled` keeps a plain `#[expect]` rather than a
  `#[cfg(test)]` gate because production constructors assign it; gating the
  field would require gating three constructor sites for no behavioural gain.
