# #13744 — refresh cached fragment neighbor resolution

## Why

A first fragment arriving with a cold neighbor is admitted to a missing-neighbor
session and publishes a fragment association carrying `MissingNeighbor`. If the
neighbor resolves before a later fragment arrives, the association hit reuses
the cached cold resolution instead of the live neighbor entry. The sibling is
therefore parked again, even though current FIB lookup resolves it as a forward
candidate; this also prevents the fragment-overlap retry regression from
exercising its intended path.

## What

- On a live fragment-association hit, refresh a cached direct
  `MissingNeighbor` resolution from the current neighbor table before applying
  the cached decision. Promote to `ForwardCandidate` only when that neighbor is
  present, preserving the committed route, translation, source MAC, and VLAN.
- Leave unresolved, tunnel-marked, and non-missing-neighbor association hits
  unchanged.
- Keep the #10659 two-order parked-fragment overlap regression and its
  assertions; it fails on the base and now reaches overlap admission in both
  orderings.

## Risk

Only a cached direct missing-neighbor hit with a currently present neighbor is
promoted; a missing lookup retains the existing park/retry path. Tunnel
resolutions continue through their existing outer-neighbor behavior.
