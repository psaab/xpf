## Mechanism (takeover-instant probe, 2026-10-31)

- **Finding**: the duplicate is created on the OLD owner during its demotion,
  not on the new owner's landing worker.
  1. handleClusterEvent's demotion edge scheduled
     `scheduleConnectedRGRepublish("cluster-state")` BEFORE
     `SetRGActive(false)`. When its routes-only snapshot won the manager lock,
     the helper held a FIB without the RG's connected rows while its HA map
     still said the RG was active. In master move 13 on node0:
     - 28.341 `apply_snapshot`;
     - 28.371 SESSION_CLOSE of the live 50 Hz ping6 (age 100 s, reason "idle
       Timeout", which is close class 0);
     - 28.390 "RG state updated rg=1 active=false";
     - 28.439 VIPs removed.

     #16215 StaleSourceNat path. Per-move status deltas attribute it:
     `snat_revoked_sessions_total` +12 (m44, node1) and +16 (m49, node0), each
     followed by a hit on the next move. On the #18598 arm the same window
     revoked every LAN->WAN flow at once: 8 v4 iperf3, 2 v6 iperf3 and 3 pings
     in 25 ms.
  3. The next packet, now with the egress RG on the peer, minted a #7770
     FabricPuntSeed on the forward landing worker (L(5/0); the siblings' rows
     were gone or demoted).
  4. When the RG came back, every forward packet hit the seed: NAT-free, a
     fabric redirect, never idle. Replies stopped. Outages ran 18 s, and
     455-515 s in trap-m2.

## Fix

- **File(s)**: pkg/daemon/daemon_ha.go,
  pkg/dataplane/userspace/manager_ha_preactivate.go,
  userspace-dp/src/afxdp/session_glue/resolve.rs,
  userspace-dp/src/session/entry.rs, userspace-dp/src/session/install.rs,
  userspace-dp/src/afxdp/session_glue/commands/delete_synced.rs, the cells,
  pkg/daemon/README.md, pkg/dataplane/README.md,
  userspace-dp/src/session/README.md, docs/ha-failover-status.md.
- **(a) Go, demotion ordering by construction.**
  - The demotion edge now schedules the FRR, connected-RG and ip-monitoring
    reconciles after the `SetRGActive(false)` write.
  - `routeRGPrimaryLocked` reports an RG as locally primary while the current
    helper last ACKNOWLEDGED it active (`helperActiveRGsLocked`). The connected
    rows therefore leave the FIB only after the helper is told, whichever
    publisher builds the snapshot.
  - The FRR and ip-monitoring schedules could reorder the same way only when
    `SetRGActive` was delayed past their 250 ms debounce. They now start after
    the write.
  - `injectBlackholeRoutes` is a no-op on the userspace dataplane, so it
    cannot move the helper FIB.
- **(c) Rust, a punt seed is not authority.**
  - `retire_fabric_punt_seed_if_owner_active` deletes a worker's forward seed
    before the session lookup once its egress RG is forwarding-active here.
  - `SessionOrigin::yields_to_peer` lets a peer import or delete replace a
    seed on the worker guards.
  - Authored rows and MissingNeighborSeed keep the #9048/#9714 refusal.
- **Not in this PR**: (b), the helper first-hit revalidation (#18637).
