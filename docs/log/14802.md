# Issue #14802 — origin/master does not compile: policy_revalidation persistent_inbound_lookup arity

- **Issue:** psaab/xpf#14802 master build break. #14793 (#14322) extended
  `PortAllocator::persistent_inbound_lookup` with `from_zone: &str,
  ingress_zone: &str` and updated only the admission call site in
  `poll_descriptor/mod.rs`; the revalidation mirror in
  `policy_revalidation.rs:461` still passed 6 args (E0061), blocking every
  Rust test build on master.
- **Second site found by the compiler:** `nat/tests_det_flip_14625.rs:44`
  called `allocate_translation` with 9 args after the same change added the
  trailing `egress_scope: u32` parameter (E0061, 10 expected).

## Fix

- `afxdp/poll_descriptor/policy_revalidation.rs`: thread real zones rather
  than empty strings. `persistent_nat_destination` gains an `ingress_zone:
  &str` parameter fed from `scope.zone_name` — the `prerouting_ingress_scope`
  result already computed in `destination_nat_translation_still_valid` from
  the arrival metadata, exactly as admission resolves it — and passes
  `&rule.from_zone` plus that ingress zone to
  `persistent_inbound_lookup`, mirroring `poll_descriptor/mod.rs:3558`.
  Empty strings would make the allocator treat the lookup as UNSCOPED (any
  grant bucket admits, `permits()` in `nat/allocator.rs`) and re-open the
  #14322 cross-zone grant leak on the revalidation path; empty arises only
  when a zone is genuinely unknown, the allocator's documented unscoped case.
- `nat/tests_det_flip_14625.rs`: pass the unscoped sentinel `0` for
  `egress_scope`. The helper is a non-persistent PAT claim (`persistent_nat:
  false`); the scope only buckets persistent grants, and every other
  non-persistent test caller passes `0` (e.g. `tests_idle_lease_sync_8121`
  mint helpers).

## Regression

New cell `persistent_nat_cross_zone_grant_does_not_validate_revalidation_14802`
in `policy_revalidation_tests.rs`: two rules share one pool/allocator
(`trust->dmz` any-remote-host, `trust->wan` target-host-port); the binding is
minted via dmz, then revalidation of an arbitrary-remote wan-arriving session
must fail while the same tuple arriving on dmz validates. RED with empty
strings threaded (wan lookup admits the dmz bucket), GREEN with the real
(trust, wan) pair. Existing `persistent_nat_inbound_survives_revalidation_14315`
(lan->wan, ingress wan) continues to validate.

## Verification

- Base (origin/master 13905b0650): `cargo check --tests` fails with exactly
  2 E0061 errors — `persistent_inbound_lookup` 6 vs 8 args at
  `policy_revalidation.rs:461` and `allocate_translation` 9 vs 10 args at
  `tests_det_flip_14625.rs:44`.
- After the fix: `cargo check --tests` in `userspace-dp` passes.
- `cargo test --bin xpf-userspace-dp -- persistent_nat det_flip_14625`:
  5 passed, 0 failed (new 14802 cell, 14315 persistent cell, both det_flip
  cells, persistent-lease control-verb cell).
- RED/GREEN for the new cell: temporarily threading `"", ""` instead of the
  real zones makes it fail at the wan assertion (dmz grant admits); restoring
  `&rule.from_zone, ingress_zone` makes it pass.

## Risk

Revalidation-only: no admission, wire, or allocator change. A session whose
persistent grant was minted under a different egress zone than its current
arrival zone now revokes on revalidation instead of surviving — that is the
#14322 scoping admission already enforces, applied to the mirror path.
