# #15164 — aborted peer failover re-promotes the requester (B22-01)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-19
- **Action**: After `RequestPeerFailover` aborts locally (commit, commit-ready,
  or commit-send failure), the requester reported an error and demoted, but the
  owner kept advertising `StateSecondaryHold` under its #5079 lease and
  `electRG`'s "Peer transfer out" arm promoted the requester again on the next
  heartbeat; under preempt the owner reclaimed the RG at lease expiry (an
  unrequested second move). Fix, both halves reqID-bound: the abort now sends
  an additive abort frame (`syncMsgFailoverAbort`/`syncMsgFailoverBatchAbort`,
  old peers ignore it, lease stays as fallback) and the owner restores promptly
  via `AbortRemoteTransferOut`; the requester records the aborted reqID
  (`abortedPeerTransferReqID`) and `electRG` refuses "Peer transfer out" while
  that hold is still advertised. Marker expires when the peer stops advertising
  secondary-hold, on commit, reset, peer loss, or RG removal. Wired through
  `SessionSync.SendFailoverAbort(Batch)` / `OnRemoteFailoverAbort(Batch)` and
  the daemon HA comms builders, with hook/wiring bound tests updated.
- **Validation**: New `failover_abort_15164_test.go` (single + batch abort,
  preempt/non-preempt, stale-abort control, legitimate-transfer control, wire
  send + receive dispatch) fails to build on master (abort API absent) and is
  GREEN post-fix; the captured pre-fix mechanism (owner hold + heartbeat
  re-promotion) is the asserted RED behaviour. Neighbour suites (lease #5079,
  hold-restore #10004/#10244, sync failover, daemon wiring) re-run with the fix.
- **Risk**: Additive wire frames only; an abort-send failure is logged and
  never masks the original failover error, and the #5079 lease still restores
  a crashed/lost requester's owner. No behaviour change outside the aborted
  transfer path.
