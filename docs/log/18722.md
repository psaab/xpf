# #18722: idle established TCP sessions are never reaped

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-01
- **Action**: Found by the Rust known-failure triage (seat V). On master
  5927babf07, `seed_lifecycle_expires_on_app_window_not_default_10267` is
  red: its default-timeout control, a completed and answered TCP pair, is
  not reaped past its window.
- **Root cause**: `opening_companion_keeps_alive` (#16223 round 2, merged in
  a936a6f802 / PR #16217) kept any idle-crossed row on
  `promoted_and_served()` and re-stamped it to `now_ns`. The proof is
  cumulative, and `node_served_evidence` counts the expiring row itself, so
  an answered established flow re-stamped itself on every GC pass.
- **Action**: The fix scopes the arm to opening rows and requires the
  node-wide served packet total (`NodeServedEvidence::served_packets`) to
  advance past a per-row baseline (`SessionEntry::node_served_seen`).
  Rejected alternatives:
  - excluding the row's own pair (a shared mirror is indistinguishable from a
    sibling's serving row);
  - a time stamp on `SyncedSessionEntry` (269 literal sites);
  - the opening gate alone (an opening replica can still pin itself through
    its own ct-slice-promoted shared row).
- **File(s)**:
  - `userspace-dp/src/session/{ctx.rs,mod.rs,install.rs,expire.rs,README.md}`
  - `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/shared_ops/lookup.rs`
  - `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/mod.rs`
  - new cells `userspace-dp/src/session/idle_served_keepalive_18722_tests.rs`
    and `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/tests_idle_served_keepalive_18722.rs`
- **Live control (udp, deployed xpf 0.0.29565+g84d00b08dd8d, contains
  a936a6f802)**:
  - Setup: established-timeout 60 s set for the run (restored on exit), 20
    TCP flows to a silent listener, then a tc egress drop on the client
    before killing it, so no FIN or RST leaves.
  - All 20 sessions stayed on BOTH nodes for 241 s (four windows).
  - A second run 5 minutes later found those 20 still present beside its own
    20 (40 per node), unchanged at its end.
  - node1 held the ESTABLISHED rows (Timeout 60), node0 the OPENING replicas
    (Timeout 20). Those are the two shapes the cells pin.
- **Cells** (release, `session:: 18722 10267 18520 18550 16223`): 684
  passed, 5 failed, all in master's known-failing list (16042 x3, which is
  #18528; `icmp_port_handling_unchanged_with_dnat_ports`;
  `session_decision_typed_snat_layout_11064`). Green:
  - `seed_lifecycle_expires_on_app_window_not_default_10267`, red on master;
  - all six #18722 cells;
  - 33 `16223` cells and 48 `18520`/`18550` cells.
- **Contract**:
  - An idle completed row reaps one window after its last packet.
  - An idle opening replica is kept at the window-1 check (it still sees the
    last advance) and reaped at the window-2 check. This is the zero install
    baseline; #18739 seeds the baseline at install to make it one window.
- **Edit log**:

## Review round 1b: the summed evidence was not monotonic

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-01 ~11:30
- **Observed** (release run b2, `!=` on the summed total): with frozen
  evidence, the established and opening replica cells gave [kept, kept,
  reaped] instead of [kept, reaped]; the drop cell gave one extra keep.
- **Mechanism** (the coordinator's hypothesis). A temporary trace cell,
  removed before commit, ran on the per-contributor fold in b3. It showed
  the fixed sequence: at +21 s both halves were kept with baselines (80,80)
  and local `Some(80)`; at +42 s both were reaped. The mechanism it replaced:
  - The forward half is checked first and reaped at window 2.
  - The reverse half's local contributor was that forward row's counters.
  - They vanish, the SUM drops, and `!=` reads the drop as a change, giving
    a third window.
  - The same sum compared with `>` false-reaps a live flow whose source is
    replaced.
- **Fold**: the evidence is per contributor. `local_served` / `shared_served`
  are each `Some` only while their source row exists. There are two
  baselines (`node_served_seen_local` / `_shared`). A PRESENT contributor
  that moved either way is a change; an absent one is neither.
- **Second artefact** (run b3, per-contributor):
  - The established replica still gave [kept, kept, reaped]. At window 1 the
    node arm re-stamped the forward half to `now_ns`; `companion_keeps_alive`
    then kept the reverse half from that MANUFACTURED freshness, without
    updating its baselines. At window 2 the reverse half re-read the same
    evidence as a change against zero.
  - Folded: a node-level keep applies to the PAIR
    (`align_node_kept_companion`). The companion is re-stamped with the same
    contributor baselines, unless it is closing, non-TCP or a completed row
    this node serves.
  - Also folded: `local_served` counts a node-served stamp even with no
    forward row (the #16223 B1 split replica). Run b3 had red-celled
    `syn_ack_first_replica_completes_from_node_evidence_16223` without it.
- **Cells**:
  - `a_removed_companion_is_not_activity_18722` (case 2: kept once, then the
    forward half is removed; the reverse reaps at its window-2 check, +42 s);
  - `a_live_flows_replaced_source_is_activity_not_silence_18722` (case 1:
    the forward row is deleted and the shared row republished lower while
    live, so the replica survives).
- **Size**: `SessionEntry` is 616 B (600 B on master, plus 16 B). The
  expiry-scan hot fields keep their cache lines (`last_seen_ns`,
  `expires_after_ns`, `wheel_tick`, `held_wheel_tick` on line 4); the
  layout print is in the run log.

## Review round 1d: cadence grace, incarnation identity, and exact tuple scope

- **Verified at PR head**: a live peer replica with a 5 s configured window can
  cross its expiry checks before the shared ct-slice restamps its counters
  (10–13 s); equal-count shared-source replacement was not observable; and the
  SNAT reverse scan could accept another flow sharing the server endpoint.
- **Fix**: present shared evidence gets a bounded grace until 13 s after its
  last observed change, while frozen evidence remains bounded and idle replicas
  still reap. Stable shared `(session_id, generation)` changes count as

## Review round 1e: scale grace to the actual refresh slab

- **Verified**: 13 s is the default-cap/2x-import cadence, not a hard ceiling.
  The conntrack refresh walk is bounded by `slot_high_watermark`, and HA
  imports have no per-table cap; a larger live extent stretches its cycle.
- **Change**: housekeeping now computes the cadence from the exact refresh-walk
  extent, `max(10 s, ceil(slot_high_watermark / 2048) * 100 ms)`, with the
  13 s default bound as a floor. That value travels through `NodeServedEvidence`
  and governs short-window grace. `SessionTable::ct_refresh_slab_len()` shares
  the same high-watermark bound as `iter_with_idle_budgeted`.
- **Cells**: a 5 s replica survives a modeled 30 s over-cap cycle until its
  next count restamp; the formula cell pins 300,000 slots to a 14.7 s bound.
- **RED evidence**: with the 13 s expiry bound hard-coded, the over-cap cell
  reaped at +24 s before the +30 s restamp. With the dynamic formula replaced
  by the 10 s target, the slab-bound cell returned 10 s instead of 14.7 s.

## Review round 2: an RSS-moved sibling must preserve the mirror

- **Verified**: after RSS moves service from worker A to B, B's shared
  same-session counter advances while A's completed local rows remain quiet.
  A's completed-local opening gate discarded B's evidence; because GC fences
  teardown only for `WorkerLocalImport`, A then deleted B's live conntrack
  mirror and descriptor.
- **Fix**: a completed local row may use node-level evidence only when the
  shared count advances beyond its baseline and the shared `(session_id,
  generation=0)` exactly matches that row's canonical forward session
  identity. For a reverse row, the identity is resolved from its forward
  companion. Missing, stale, or unrelated evidence cannot protect the row.
- **Cell**: `rss_moved_completed_local_row_preserves_sibling_mirror_18722`
  models workers A and B, shared session IDs, B's real restamp operation, and
  A's expiry plus GC teardown; it asserts A's pair, B's row, mirror, and
  descriptor all survive.
