# 13925 — Enforce host-inbound policy on non-unicast destinations

- **Issue**: #13925 (deep-review finding H04-01)
- **Root cause**: The XDP shim's early-pass path sends IPv4 limited broadcast and IPv6 multicast to the kernel. The host-inbound input chain scopes ordinary service admission to local unicast destination addresses, so 255.255.255.255 and non-catalog ff02::/16 traffic could fall through to `policy accept` and reach wildcard UDP listeners without the ingress zone admitting the service.
- **Fix**: Extend host-inbound text and netlink rules to scope limited broadcast and link-scope multicast service tuples to the ingress zone's effective service/protocol set, with a default drop for closed, unzoned, and ambiguous ingress. Existing global exceptions remain ahead of this gate (raw ESP/AH, ICMP errors/PMTU, and ND); link-scope MLD retains its prior base-chain policy fall-through. Keep `any-service` semantics and preserve the #11571 routing-multicast catalog's earlier exact group/protocol checks. Extend the cold-boot host-inbound fence to drop these destinations until generation-specific policy is installed, preserving ND and MLD handling. DHCPv6 UDP `ff02::1:2` is now governed by the `dhcpv6` token; the advisory explains its default-deny, while DHCPv4 raw AF_PACKET delivery remains outside netfilter.
