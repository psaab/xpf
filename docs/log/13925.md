# 13925 — Enforce host-inbound policy on non-unicast destinations

- **Issue**: #13925 (deep-review finding H04-01)
- **Root cause**: The XDP shim's early-pass path sends IPv4 limited broadcast and IPv6 multicast to the kernel. The host-inbound input chain scopes zone admission to local unicast destination addresses, so 255.255.255.255 and non-catalog ff02::/16 traffic matched no per-zone rule and fell through to `policy accept`. An on-link peer could therefore reach main-context wildcard UDP sockets and multicast echo handling without the ingress zone admitting the service.
- **Fix**: Extend host-inbound text and netlink rules to scope limited broadcast and link-scope multicast to the ingress zone's effective service/protocol set, with a default drop for closed, unzoned, and ambiguous ingress. Keep `any-service` semantics; preserve the #11571 routing-multicast catalog's earlier exact group/protocol checks; retain global ND and MLD handling. Extend the cold-boot host-inbound fence to drop these destinations until generation-specific policy is installed. DHCPv6 UDP `ff02::1:2` is now governed by the `dhcpv6` token; the advisory explains its default-deny, while DHCPv4 raw AF_PACKET delivery remains outside netfilter.
