## Live check, pre-registered (run later; it needs a udp slot after #19115 gates)

**Setup:** a lab build of this branch plus the #19115 build-tagged export
fault (the `lab/19115-fault-v2` seam, never on master), deployed on the udp
cluster with the frozen G5 cell, as #19115's lab was. All times are from
fw0's own journal.

**Arming a forced resync.** `forceResync` is armed by a delete-journal
overflow (`armDeleteResync`, `sync_conn_write.go`). The harness must:
- stop fw1's xpfd;
- churn short UDP flows on fw0 until its delete journal overflows (the
  "delete journal overflow" log line);
- arm a PERSISTENT export fault on fw0;
- restart fw1.

The harness is self-checked first on the B build: it must show the overflow
line and at least one forced bulk attempt that fails on the fault. **If the
overflow cannot be produced deterministically, this cell reads UNRUN, not
PASS.**

**Predictions** (arm B = this branch plus the fault):
- **F1:** while the fault persists, the gaps between consecutive
  authoritative bulk attempts (`bulk sync starting`, forced or owed) follow
  the schedule 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 30 s. Each gap is the step plus one
  export, within −50/+500 ms (#19115's T1 criterion). No two attempts start
  closer than the current step.
- **F2:** the forced obligation survives. Once the fault is disarmed, a
  forced bulk completes (`bulk sync complete`) within the then-current
  step plus one sweep tick.
- **L4:** at the 10k standing-UDP load, xpfd CPU over a 60 s persistent-fault
  window is at most +2 % of one core over the fault-free baseline.

**Control arm** (the #19115 head plus the same fault):
- **C1:** forced attempts recur at the sweep tick, not on the schedule.
  This is the defect, observed. If C1 does not show it, F1 cannot
  discriminate, and the cell is VOID.

**Restore:** as #19115's R: the deploy and the CoS re-apply succeed, and F1
(`make test-connectivity`) passes.

**Confounds, written down before the run:**
