# #14968 — Tolerant duplicate-policy fold cross-product permit widening, excluded-set deny disarming, and scheduler narrowing (D30-02)

- **Base**: `b8f1ff7ac9` (origin/master at branch start)
- **Branch**: `fix/14968-campaign-pub`

## Why

The tolerant (#8752) duplicate-policy fold merges repeated `policy <name>`
statements into the first occurrence. #9571 already refuses a fold that
turns a restrictive statement (explicit deny/reject, or an actionless
statement defaulted to deny) into a merged PERMIT. It compared only the
statements' terminal actions, so three survivable re-gatings compiled to one
unpoisoned policy on `CompileConfigLenient` /
`CompileConfigForNodeLenient` (measured RED on this branch's base):

- **Cross-product permit widening**: two PERMIT statements differing in two
  or more match dimensions fold into the dimension-wise cross product
  (source-union × destination-union × application-union), permitting
  source/destination/application combinations no statement permitted.
- **Excluded-set deny disarming**: a `source-address-excluded` /
  `destination-address-excluded` flag and list authored on one statement is
  OR-ed/unioned onto a surviving deny; the merged deny matches the
  complement of the union and no longer covers traffic its own statement
  denied (e.g. an unconditional deny folded with an excluded permit becomes
  a deny that spares the excluded set).
- **Scheduler narrowing**: a `scheduler-name` authored on one statement is
  inherited last-wins by a surviving unconditional deny, which then applies
  only inside that other statement's window (and symmetrically a deny can
  pick up a second conflicting scheduler).

## What

- `pkg/config/dup_instance_merge_8752.go`: `mergeInstancesUnder` now
  captures every source statement's compiled policy (`compilePolicy`, the
  same reader #9571 uses) before folding, and after the fold classifies the
  merged policy with `foldChangesPolicySemantics14968`:
  - merged PERMIT: poison when two explicit-permit sources differ
    non-trivially in ≥2 match dimensions (neither value set containing the
    other, `any` counting as universal — a containment in a dimension
    cannot create a new combination);
  - merged DENY/REJECT: poison when any restrictive source's excluded
    flags/lists or scheduler set differ from the merged policy's, or the
    source named no scheduler and the merged policy gained one.
  The existing #9571 action leg is preserved via the widened list (a
  `semantic` flag on `foldWidenedPolicy9571` selects the #14968 warning
  text in both compiler entry points in `pkg/config/compiler.go`).
  Poisoning is the existing fail-closed mechanism: `LenientContentDropped`,
  the snapshot builder's `__unsupported__` sentinel, helper refusal
  (previous-good retained; fresh-boot default-deny).
- NOT changed: the fold itself, strict-commit behaviour (#3473/#14449
  gates), scheduler definitions, and folds whose merged semantics are the
  union of the statements (single-dimension permit differences, identical
  excluded sets, one shared scheduler) — those stay merged and unpoisoned.
- Docs: `pkg/config/README.md` (#14968 paragraph beside #9571),
  `pkg/config/types_security.go` (`LenientContentDropped` cause list).

## Risk

Tolerant loads carrying one of these three duplicate shapes now refuse the
policy snapshot instead of enforcing re-gated semantics — the intended
fail-closed direction (previous-good retained; fresh-boot default-deny).
Legitimate duplicate folds are pinned unpoisoned by the control rows.
Group-inherited (`apply-groups`) duplicates are still classified on the
pre-expansion tree, as with #9571; the expanded-tree direct-conflict scan
(#11063) is unchanged.
