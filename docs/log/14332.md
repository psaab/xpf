# #14332 — Lifeline VRF-master blanket in hostInboundIngressClaims

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-16
- **Action**: Read the issue (narrowed MATERIAL core, both research lanes)
  and traced `hostInboundIngressClaims` in
  `pkg/dataplane/userspace/zones_host_inbound.go`. The helper rewrote each
  lifeline netdev to its VRF master in `effectiveLifelines`, then dropped
  every non-lifeline claim whose rewritten target equalled that master —
  so one lifeline slave in a VRF erased all co-VRF claims from both
  consumers: `hostInboundViewIngressNetdevsWithMasters` (flat
  `IngressNetdevs`, the legacy flood-screen scope) and
  `hostInboundViewIngressDenyNetdevs` (#10431 ambiguous fail-closed
  `IngressDenyNetdevs`). Renderer check: the per-slave
  `IngressVRFScopes`/`LifelineVRFScopes` (meta sdifname) paths are computed
  independently and stay correct, host-inbound service rules strip the
  master from direct scope when scopes exist
  (`hostInboundDirectIngressNetdevs`), and the ambiguous drop is flat
  `iifname <master>` with no per-slave fallback — so the deny half was a
  genuine fail-open and the flood half lost its master scope.
