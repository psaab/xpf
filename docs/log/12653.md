# #12653 — explicit short PREF64 lifetime encoded as zero

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-13
- **Action**: Round effective positive PREF64 lifetimes up to the RFC 8781 8-second wire quantum before building the `ndp.PREF64` option. This prevents a configured 1–3s lifetime from being marshaled as zero, which tells hosts to remove the NAT64 prefix. Existing 0=inherit semantics and #12536 over-maximum explicit-lifetime dropping remain unchanged.
- **Base**: `origin/master` `8a23991a9` (issue #12653 is open and reports the defect on master).
- **Files**: `pkg/ra/sender.go`, `pkg/ra/sender_pref64_tiny_12653_test.go`, `pkg/ra/README.md`, `docs/log/12653.md`.
- **Regression**: `TestBuildRA_12653_ExplicitTinyPREF64LifetimeNotZeroOnWire` marshals and parses the RA, then asserts decoded wire lifetimes for 1–9s, 15–17s, 64s, 1800s, and near/max boundaries. The control PIO and prefix are also checked by the shared round-trip helper. Positive values are rounded up, exact multiples stay unchanged, and 65529 remains dropped.

## RED → GREEN proof

- **RED**: 1s, 2s, and 3s all decoded as 0s; 9s and 17s rounded down to 8s and 16s; 65521s decoded as 65520s. Assertions decode the actual marshaled advertisement, not the in-memory option.
- **GREEN**: `go test ./pkg/ra -count=1` passed after the send-sink rounding change. The matrix additionally checks 0 (inherit/default = 1800s), exact 8s multiples, and over-maximum drop.

## Validation

- RED: `go test ./pkg/ra -run '^TestBuildRA_12653_ExplicitTinyPREF64LifetimeNotZeroOnWire$' -count=1` failed before the fix on decoded 1s, 2s, 3s, 9s, 17s, 1800s (initial expectation corrected: 1800 is an exact multiple of 8), and 65521s cases.
- GREEN: `go test ./pkg/ra -count=1` passed after the fix, including the new 0/default-inheritance row and all neighboring RA tests.

## Risk

- Positive effective PREF64 lifetimes not divisible by 8 now advertise up to 7 seconds longer than the configured value rather than being shortened by nearest-unit rounding. Zero continues to mean inherited lifetime per the configuration contract; values above 65528 continue to be dropped for explicit settings.
