# Issue #10433: fence ACK timeout linearization

## Disposition

Fixed the scheduler race in `SessionSync.SendFenceAwait`. Fence ACK completion,
disconnect release, and the timeout-arm waiter drain now linearize under
`fenceAckMu`. When the deadline is ready together with an ACK already buffered
for the waiter, the ACK is consumed as success; only an empty waiter is removed
and counted as a timeout.

The ACK cell is deterministic rather than scheduler-luck: it preloads the
buffered waiter, starts a real `time.Timer`, waits for its `timer.C` to fire
while the ACK remains buffered, then consumes only that deadline to force the
timeout-arm continuation. The locked nonblocking drain must consume the
already-delivered ACK. The genuine-timeout and disconnect controls exercise the
other timeout-arm outcomes.
