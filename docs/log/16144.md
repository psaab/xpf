# #16144 - config: extract interface-name parser per-caller with raw suffix (universal parser unsound)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
- **Issue**: W2.12 part E (deep-review, Low/maintenance). `pkg/config/types.go` had divergent ad-hoc first-dot parsing at `ResolveReth`/`ResolveFab`/`ResolveKernelIfName`/`DHCPLeaseKey`, with the same shape at `pkg/flowexport/manager.go:parseIfaceRef`. Validators (Codex) ruled a universal `(name, unit int, hasUnit bool)` parser UNSOUND: suffix-copying callers carry arbitrary raw suffixes, so only the narrowed raw-suffix form `(name string, suffix string, hasSuffix bool)` survives, adopted per-caller.
- **Change**:
  - `pkg/config/types.go`: added `SplitIfaceRef(ref) (name, suffix string, hasSuffix bool)` — a first-dot `strings.Cut` returning the suffix RAW (no validation, canonicalization, or integer parse). Its doc comment carries the per-caller suffix-semantics table required by the issue's decisive acceptance check.
  - Adopted (raw copy): `ResolveReth` and `ResolveFab` now split via `SplitIfaceRef` and copy the suffix verbatim onto the resolved name, preserving non-decimal/multi-dot suffixes (`reth0.50.60` → member + `.50.60`).
  - Adopted (discard): `DHCPLeaseKey` splits via `SplitIfaceRef` and discards the suffix; unit/VLAN identity still comes from the separately supplied `unitNum`.
  - NOT adopted (declared-name-wins): `resolveKernelIfNameWith` already applies exact-declared-name precedence (#8994) then `SplitInterfaceUnitRef` longest-declared-prefix (#9821). Downgrading it to the raw first-dot split would reintroduce the #9821 misroute of authored dotted names (`gr-0/0/0.0`); left byte-identical.
  - NOT adopted (different grammar): `pkg/flowexport.parseIfaceRef` is cfg-free, splits on the FINAL dot, and strictly parses a decimal unit (rejecting `1abc2`, `-1`, signs/spaces). Folding it into the raw parser would lose that strictness or force an int parse onto raw-copy callers; left unchanged with the table recording why.
  - Newtypes deferred per the issue; the plain triple is the agreed equivalent. Marshal output is untouched (pure refactor of three call sites onto one helper).
- **Validation**:
  - New pins in `pkg/config/iface_ref_parser_16144_test.go`: `TestSplitIfaceRefRawSuffix16144` (raw/multi-dot/non-decimal/padding/trailing-dot table — RED under any integer-unit parser), `TestResolveRawSuffixAdoption16144` (raw-copy at ResolveReth/ResolveFab + discard at DHCPLeaseKey), `TestDeclaredDottedNameUnchanged16144` (declared `gr-0/0/0.0` resolves to its own device; undeclared control takes the tunnel arm).
  - Existing suites green: `pkg/config` resolver tests, `TestSplitInterfaceUnitRef*`, `pkg/flowexport` `TestParseIfaceRef`/`TestBuildSamplingZones*`, and `TestResolverAgreement8994` (declared-name agreement pin) in `pkg/dataplane/userspace`.
- **Risk**: Behavior-preserving by construction — `strings.Cut` is exactly the prior `SplitN(ref, ".", 2)` semantics at all three adopted sites. No config, wire, or marshal surface changes.
