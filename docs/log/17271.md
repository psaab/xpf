## Fix

`bpf_map/conntrack_io.rs` is now the conntrack I/O boundary. It owns `ConntrackTarget` and the sole family-aware selector, typed `conntrack_lookup`, `conntrack_update(flags)`, and `conntrack_delete` primitives, plus exact-byte snapshot/restore I/O that preserves key/value padding. Its `#[cfg(test)]` backend is selected only by the fd chosen for the key's family.

The test-row map, bare-key normalization, restore-failure guard, AppID lookup/write counters, and refresh counter live in `conntrack_io.rs`. The fake projects only fields present in `ConntrackPublishRecord` and synchronizes AppID, origin, and policy zones on successful test updates. `mod.rs` keeps the publish-call/override instrumentation and #10590 restamp-attempt hook, but has no inline row selection or per-session conntrack libbpf operations; `publish_conntrack.rs` is limited to value builders and predicates.

Publish, snapshot, restore, session-id lookup, delete, AppID and policy restamps, refresh, and origin updates route through the I/O boundary. The test-fd publish returns the established fake `Written` result without entering builders or libbpf (including the NAT64 v6-key/IPv4-rewrite case), and commits the row only after publish overrides resolve. The #6923 v6 publishability refusal remains before value construction. Existing no-map/cross-family outcomes are retained, and `with_conntrack_rmw` remains around refresh outside the backend.

The v4/v6 wrong-slot matrix exercises publish, snapshot, session-id lookup, delete, AppID/policy/origin restamps, refresh, session delete, and restore. Wrong-slot policy restamp is a successful no-op with an unchanged row; selected-slot policy restamp updates the fake row's zones. The selected-family cell also covers both transposition directions. `shared_session_refs_16185_tests.rs` cell 2 uses the single sentinel in its v4 slot.

### After-migration census

`CONNTRACK_TEST_MAP_FD` is defined only in `conntrack_io.rs`, which also owns the sentinel predicate; `CONNTRACK_TEST_MAP_FD_V6` is gone. `mod.rs` and `publish_conntrack.rs` contain neither inline test-row selection nor direct per-session conntrack libbpf calls. The “any conntrack map present” check uses the I/O module's shared fd-availability predicate. Whole-map clear and unrelated steering-map I/O remain unchanged.
