# #14941 — Reverse-direction revalidation writes forward egress zone into reverse ingress

## Defect
Reverse metadata is direction-relative (`shared_ops/reverse_synth.rs:90-91`:
reverse `ingress_zone = forward egress_zone`, `egress_zone = forward
ingress_zone`). The stale-hit resolver derives `new_egress_zone` from the
matched packet's direction — including reverse hits (`session_glue/resolve.rs`,
the `DeferredForwardingRevalidation` construction) — but the persist sites
wrote it as the forward flow's egress zone unconditionally:

- `session/mod.rs` `revalidate_forwarding_resolution_with_egress_zone` writes
  forward `egress_zone = new_zone` and reverse `ingress_zone = new_zone` for
  either hit direction.
- `poll_descriptor/mod.rs` (descriptor persist) and
  `session_glue/resolve.rs` (Owner-verdict persist in
  `resolve_flow_session_decision`) both forwarded the raw
  `revalidation.new_egress_zone` and mirrored it into the local snapshot the
  same way.

For a reverse (WAN->LAN) hit, the fresh lookup resolves the reply's egress
toward the client — LAN. Writing LAN as the pair's forward egress flipped
`reverse.ingress_zone` WAN->LAN. The next ACK arrived from WAN, so
`session_hit_authority` (`poll_descriptor/session_hit_authority.rs`) judged it
Foreign (arrival WAN != recorded ingress LAN), skipped the deferred Owner
refresh, could not re-seed the flow cache, and was policy-dropped under the
ordinary one-way permit. Forward data still passed; sessions idle-timed out
at ~28s into every TCP stream (#14300).

## Neighbor-generation lead (assessed, not the defect)
`poll_descriptor/flow_cache_hit.rs:160-196` evicts a cached descriptor on a
neighbor-MAC shard-epoch mismatch (`neighbor_mac_stale`,
`reclassify_hit_as_miss`) and falls through to the slow path. That is a
plausible *trigger* for the ~28s transition (a neighbor refresh evicts the
ACK flow's cache slot and forces the slow-path revalidation that carried the
bug), but the invalidation itself is correct: same-MAC refreshes and
absent-key removals do not bump the shard epoch, and serving a stale MAC
would be worse. No change there; the orientation fix removes the harm the
trigger exposed.

## Fix (direction-relative mapping at both persist sites)
- `shared_ops/lookup.rs`: new `forward_egress_zone_for_hit(hit_is_reverse,
  resolved_zone, reverse_ingress_zone) -> ForwardEgressZone`. Forward hits
  pass the resolved zone through; reverse hits map to `Preserve` — a reverse
  re-resolve names the forward *ingress* leg, never the forward egress, so
  the table is called with `None` and the recorded pair is preserved. The
  #9604 forward-companion re-derivations in `policy_revalidation.rs` judge a
  forward route by construction and are deliberately left unmapped, as is
  `session/mod.rs`'s pair write itself (its contract is now documented as
  forward-egress-only).
- `session_glue/resolve.rs` and `poll_descriptor/mod.rs`: both
  `DeferredForwardingRevalidation` persist sites map through the helper
  before calling `revalidate_forwarding_resolution_with_egress_zone` and
  before mirroring into the local `resolved.metadata` snapshot.
- `session/mod.rs`: the pair-write contract is documented as
  forward-egress-only (no behavior change there; the #9604 arms that
  legitimately supply a forward egress keep working).
## Regression
- `forward_egress_zone_mapping_is_direction_relative_14941` — the mapping
  truth table (forward pass-through, forward-terminal preserve, reverse
  preserve even when the reverse lookup resolved LAN).
- `reverse_hit_revalidation_preserves_pair_zones_14941` — a stale reverse
  hit driven through `resolve_flow_session_decision` (Owner verdict) leaves
  forward egress = WAN, reverse ingress = WAN, reverse egress = LAN stored;
  the following WAN arrival is `HitAuthority::Owner` (not Foreign) and the
  deferred Owner refresh (`apply_deferred_owner_hit`, the ACK-refresh
  replay) still applies, so the ACK stream keeps refreshing past the 30s
  idle cliff.
- `forward_hit_revalidation_still_rebinds_pair_14941` — control: a stale
  forward hit rerouted to dmz still rebinds forward egress + reverse ingress
  together (#14318 not regressed).
