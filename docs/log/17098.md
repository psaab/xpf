# #17098 — R4-Q46-05: dead lenient parser twins pinned by tests

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-27
  - **Action**: Deleted the dead pre-fix parser twins from
    `pkg/upgrade/cluster_cli.go` and repointed their tests at the live
    strict helpers.

    #14991 added `parsePeerSessionSyncWireStrict` and moved the verdict
    onto it, but kept the lenient `parsePeerSessionSyncWire` — which
    returns `(0, true)` on a malformed present value, exactly the
    permit-on-garbage shape #14991 removed — under the shorter name,
    pinned only by the #7990 parser table. `parseHAProtocolCompatible`
    (a one-line wrapper over `haProtocolVerdictFromStatus`) and
    `grpcCluster.configuredRGs` (unused; its caller calls
    `configuredRGsFromStatus` directly) were likewise dead, and the
    combined godoc for the HA/wire parsers sat above the lenient twin
    so the wrapper had none.

    Fix, in `pkg/upgrade`:
    - Deleted `parsePeerSessionSyncWire`,
      `parseHAProtocolCompatible` and `grpcCluster.configuredRGs`;
      no shim or alias left behind.
    - Moved the HA half of the orphaned godoc onto
      `haProtocolVerdictFromStatus` and the wire half (extended with
      the malformed-present outcome) onto
      `parsePeerSessionSyncWireStrict`.
    - Repointed `TestParsePeerSessionSyncWire7990` at
      `parsePeerSessionSyncWireStrict`: the table now covers present,
      unknown, absent, local-line-only and malformed (`banana`,
      `70000`) rows, the malformed rows expecting an error.
      `TestAbsentStatusLineFailsRatherThanPermits7990`'s fixture check
      drives the strict parser too.
    - Repointed `TestParseHAProtocolCompatible` and
      `TestParsePeerHAProtocolMalformedFailsClosed14991` at
      `haProtocolVerdictFromStatus`; updated the stale parser name in
      `rolling.go`'s interface comment.
