# #16630: coordinator drain overflow arm re-arms LossResync on bulk residue

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-25
- **Issue**: #8593 residual (R3-A27-02). The push-side arm was made
  bulk-aware by #8593 (`push_session_delta_bulk_export` does not arm on
  cap drop), but the drain-side arm was not: `Coordinator::drain_session_deltas`
  armed `set_delta_loss` on every binding with ANY undrained residue when
  the fair drain's budget ran out, and `drain_session_deltas_fair_tracked`
  computed `overflow` from `has_pending_session_deltas()` (any entry).
  A LossResync exporting >256 owned sessions into the RPC-fallback buffer
  therefore re-armed itself on the next Go tick (256 drained per tick,
  5 s connected / 100 ms fallback): worker re-ran LossResync, refilled
  the buffer, looped for as long as owned sessions exceeded the per-tick
  budget, regardless of traffic — whole-table candidate walk per worker
  per tick plus N peer installs on the sync link.
- **Fix**: queue entries now carry their origin
  (`pending_session_deltas: VecDeque<(SessionDeltaInfo, bool)>`, bool =
  bulk-export, set by the producer-routed push, mirroring the #8593
  delta-carried marker). New
  `BindingLiveState::has_pending_incremental_session_deltas()` reports
  only incremental residue. Both drain-side sites key on it:
  `drain_session_deltas_fair_tracked`'s overflow bit and the coordinator's
  per-binding arm in `drain_session_deltas_attributed`. Bulk residue is
  still drained and delivered to the RPC consumer exactly as before; it
  simply cannot trigger the resync that produced it. Incremental residue
  arms exactly as #5290 intended. NOT changed: the #16584
  write-failure attribution arms (drained-batch-driven, not
  residue-driven) and the push-side cap arm.
