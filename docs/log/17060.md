## Fix

- `clearFabricSlots` (`daemon_ha_fabric.go`): iterates the parameterized
  slots and zeroes iface, overlay, peer IP/MAC, populated, probe/log rate
  limits, and queued-probe state. It drops each distinct peer's helper
  identity constraint (`RegisterFabricPeerMACConstraint(peer, nil)`).
  Both state clearing and constraint removal run under `fabricMu`, the
  same lock used for non-nil identity publication.
- `beginClusterCommsEpoch` creates a generation-owned `clusterCommsEpoch`
  with separate constructor and populate-loop WaitGroups. Constructor and
  populate-loop registration validate the current generation and Add under
  `clusterCommsMu`.
- `stopClusterComms` detaches its captured epoch under `clusterCommsMu`,
  cancels it, and joins only its constructor and populate loops outside the
  lock. Its final channel/session/slot clear checks the captured stop
  generation under the lock, so a successor epoch is neither joined nor
  cleared by the old stop.
- The populate join means an in-flight `refreshFabricFwdSlot` cannot latch
  `populated` after the clear. Each loop also gates its initial slot and peer
  identity publication under `clusterCommsMu` against its captured generation
  before taking `fabricMu`; a stale/cancelled old loop cannot clear or overwrite
  its successor's slot state. `monitorFabricState` remains untracked: it only
  pokes channels and never writes slot state. `refreshFabricFwdSlot` skips
  `SyncFabricState` when its epoch context is cancelled after the latch.
- Peer-change constraint removal, identity publication, and clear-time
  removal are serialized under `fabricMu`, preventing stale re-registration.
- `RefreshFabricFwd` now queues the probe in slot state and calls the
  non-blocking `triggerFabricRefresh()` poke. The per-slot 2s rate-limited
  probe is executed by that epoch-owned populate loop on wake, with the
  epoch ctx, before its refresh — preserving probe-then-refresh order,
  probe target (`overlayOrParent`), rate limit, and coalescing, while
  nothing runs on `context.Background()` or outside an epoch. With comms
  stopped the channels are nil and the poke is a no-op.
  The three `go d.RefreshFabricFwd()` sites call it directly (it never
  blocks, so fire-and-forget goroutines are now pure waste).

Failover behaviour preserved: populate loops, the 30s safety-net
ticker, `monitorFabricState`/`triggerFabricRefresh`, readiness gating,
retain-on-neighbour-miss, identity recording, fab1 handling, and the
#7072 `activeClusterTransport` non-clear are all untouched.

## Risk

- Fail-closed direction: a fabric event arriving while comms are
  mid-restart now no-ops instead of refreshing stale state; the new
  epoch's populate loop does its immediate fast-retry pass, so no
  refresh is lost beyond the restart window that already cancelled
  the loops.
- A probe queued just before a stop is dropped with the slot (flags
  cleared); the next epoch re-probes on its own schedule.
- Fold risk: `stopClusterComms` now also waits for populate loops to
  observe cancellation. A loop parked in a blocking resolver/netlink
  call delays teardown until that call returns (same class as the
  existing constructor join); the loops' fast-retry path checks
  `ctx.Err()` before every probe, so the wait is bounded by one
  in-flight refresh.

## Epoch join review finding (2026-10-28)

The hostile review observation was valid. The daemon-scoped populate
WaitGroup allowed a stop to join successor loops or clear their state.
The fold replaces it with constructor and fabric-populate WaitGroups owned
by a `clusterCommsEpoch`: `beginClusterCommsEpoch` creates a fresh group,
work registration is generation-checked under `clusterCommsMu`, and stop
captures/detaches only its epoch before waiting. After the join, stop clears
published channels and fabric slots only if its generation is still current.
Peer-change identity constraint removal now remains under `fabricMu` with
the corresponding slot identity clear.
