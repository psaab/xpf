# Issue #12686 — prevent rollback re-authorization over-deletion

## Why

PR #12681 retained stable rule provenance so nested commit-confirmed rollback
could re-authorize candidates against a restored root policy. Its recheck still
rejected valid `from-zone any` / `to-zone any` rules by comparing the literal
`any` key to a concrete session zone. It also skipped the recheck when the last
confirmed publish was rejected, and a changed default policy could classify a
previously retained row as an orphan/default candidate in the rollback's fresh
capture. Those paths deleted sessions the restored policy permits.

## What

Rollback re-authorization now treats `any` as a wildcard for the corresponding
zone axis. For rollback plans it rechecks the pre-rollback retained candidate
source against the restored config, then removes permitted retained identities
from the merged fresh capture so an orphan/sentinel classification cannot
reintroduce them as deletes. Fresh candidates not present in retained debt are
left under the ordinary policy invalidation behavior, preserving log-only
`policy-rematch` semantics.

The rollback source and intent persist through a same-target retry after a
rejected publish. Same-target retries incorporate new capture rows; a different
rollback target sources the latest capture rather than stale earlier rollback
debt. Unknown, denied, malformed, or ambiguous candidates remain fail-closed.

Regression cells verify each stored rule key against `policymatch.Match` with
its actual application tuple. The P4 under-deletion control has a retained
candidate stamped with `trust->any` provenance but concrete `other->untrust`
zones; rollback must still delete it. Candidate removal also keeps a fresh
tuple that reuses a permitted row's RT_FLOW session ID.

## Validation

- RED on detached pre-fix base (`HEAD^`): the focused P4/P9/P7 selector failed with over-deletion in any-zone session 1, rejected-publish session 1, wildcard to-any/both-any sessions 1/2, default-policy session 1, and the rejected-rollback retry session 1.
- GREEN: `go test ./pkg/daemon -run 'TestPolicyInvalidationRollbackAnyZone12686' -count=1` passed with the concrete-axis mismatch control.
- `go test ./pkg/daemon -count=1 -timeout 30m` passed; `go vet ./pkg/daemon` and `git diff --check` also passed after adding the control.

## Risk

Only pre-rollback obligations are re-authorized; fresh candidates keep the
existing delete/rematch policy. Provenance that cannot prove a restored permit
remains debt rather than being discharged.
