  - **Action**: CORRECTION. The round-2 and round-3 entries above say the claim
    that fd pressure could accumulate toward the 30 s bound was "refuted by
    measurement". That was WRONG. The probe measured a listener WITH a pending
    connection (EMFILE, EMFILE, success, EAGAIN) and the result was generalised
    to every listener. Codex round 3 pointed out the kernel reserves the result
    descriptor before inspecting the backlog; re-measured, an EMPTY listener at
    RLIMIT_NOFILE=256 returns EMFILE, EMFILE, then EAGAIN once one descriptor
    frees -- no successful accept, so no episode reset. An idle listener under
    fluctuating pressure could therefore reach the bound and restart the only
    forwarding path. Codex round 3 also showed errno cannot classify "restart
    helps" (an LSM hook can return EINVAL/EOPNOTSUPP; system-wide ENFILE/ENOMEM
    is not fixed by a restart).
