# #12528 — reject fused firewall `then` tails

- **Issue**: #12528 — `is-fragment` and `flexible-match-range` swallow fused `then` actions.
- **Branch**: `fix/12528-campaign-pub`
- **Worktree**: `/var/worktrees/campaign-pub/wt-12528`

## Why

`is-fragment` is a bare flag: `compileFilterFrom` sets `IsFragment` and reads
nothing else from the node. `flexible-match-range` is read only through its
named `range` instances and their six fields. A missing `;` or a misplaced
brace parks the term's action under one of them, and the term compiles with an
empty action: a fall-through, so an intended discard or reject accepts while
`show configuration` still prints it. The #8437 fused-sibling gate does not
see it, because `then` is a sibling of `from`, not of the leaf.

The fused tail lands in different AST positions by ingress: extra `Keys` on
the leaf (braced `from { is-fragment then discard; }`), a child statement
beneath it (flat `set`, interactive `set`, the `load merge` replay), a range
body, a range field's value, or a packed run `packedBody` declines to expand.

## What

`validateFirewallFusedThenStrict12528`
(`pkg/config/compiler_firewall_fused_then_12528.go`) runs in the compiler
pre-walk after the #8480 gate. It walks the readers `compileFirewall` uses:
`namedInstances` for filters and terms, `firewallTermFromNodes` plus
`packedBody` under the from-schema for the real `from` leaves. It reports:

- any token or statement after a real `is-fragment` leaf;
- an unquoted `then` at a statement position inside a real
  `flexible-match-range`: beside its ranges, in a range body, under or after a
  range field's value, or in an unexpanded packed run. A range named `then` is
  the range's argument and never matches;
- the packed term spelling `term T from is-fragment then ...;` (and its
  `flexible-match-range` and trailing `{ then ...; }` forms), which compiles
  with its action but whose set form nests `then` under the leaf.

Strict compilation refuses the first offending term; tolerant compilation
(`Store.Load`, `Store.SyncApply`) warns about every offending term and keeps
the historical compilation (#1960). Nameless terms are left to the #10294
gate through `firewallNamelessTermNodes`, the predicate `compileFirewall`
already used, now shared. `firewallPackedTermFromStatements` reports the token
that ends each packed `from` statement; `firewallPackedTermFromNodes` is
unchanged for its callers.

The rationale and the scope limits are recorded in `docs/config-schema.md`
under "A fused `then` under `is-fragment` or `flexible-match-range` (#12528)".

## Risk

- Packed term spellings `term T from is-fragment then discard;`,
  `term T from is-fragment { then discard; }` and
  `term T from flexible-match-range range R ... then discard;` are refused at
  commit. Master compiled them with their action through `load override`; their
  set form (`display set`, `load merge`) lost it. A persisted config with one
  still boots and warns.
- Flat `set ... from is-fragment protocol udp` (one command for two matches) is
  refused: SetPath nests the second match under the flag, where master silently
  dropped it. The hierarchical `from is-fragment protocol udp;` commits, but its
  `load merge` and `display set` round trip lands in the nested shape and is
  refused.
- Tolerant boot and peer sync keep a fused term's fall-through until the
  operator fixes it, with one warning per term (#1960).
- Other unread statements under `flexible-match-range`
  (`range R { byte-offset 9; discard; }`) are not gated.
- The compact `term T { from is-fragment then discard; }` stays with #8437 and
  the `from then` gate, as on master.
