# Issue #16269 — aged RG1 failover: fabric-ingress cache hit consumes demoted DiscardRoute

## Finding

On clean master `2c8575b70e` (FZDiag8 cell2, hash-proven), aged-30s P4
with RG1 fw0→fw1 dies at failover+1s permanently (130 zero intervals)
while sessions stay established (Timeout 1800) on both nodes. fw0
correctly fabric-redirects retransmits (`FabricRedirect`, `TX_INLINE`),
but on fw1 the fabric-ingress hit serves a cached `DiscardRoute`
(`nat=(None,None)`, `stamp_owner_rg=0`, egress 0) as a terminal drop
(`flow_cache_hit.rs` deny arm). fw1 TX/SNAT/ForwardCandidates freeze;
zero WAN egress.

The stored session rows on fw1 remain `ClusterTerminalHAInactive`
(the pre-demotion shape); the demotion to plain `DiscardRoute` happens
in the cached flow decision (`enforce_ha_resolution_snapshot`,
`forwarding/ha.rs`), whose peer-owned test via the stamped
`source_ifindex` misses for this row shape. No takeover stage
re-judged fabric-ingress cache entries, and the cache-hit deny arm
short-circuits the established synced sessions.

## Fix (option b, origin-gated)

`cached_flow_decision_invalid_reason_inner` now evicts a
fabric-ingress cached `DiscardRoute` when its cluster-terminal origin
is CERTAIN and its would-be egress RG is locally forwarding-active
(`discard_route_cluster_terminal_now_local`): provenance stamp
(`source_ifindex > 0`) present AND `owner_rg_for_flow(source_ifindex)`
maps to an RG that `is_forwarding_active` now. The packet falls
through to the slow path, re-resolves against the live FIB under the
new owner, and forwards as the WAN `ForwardCandidate`. Evictions
attribute to the new `PostHitFabricIngressDiscardNowLocal` arm
(exported through Go metrics/status as
`post_hit_fabric_ingress_discard_now_local` /
`fabric_ingress_discard_now_local`).

Fail-closed controls (unit-proven):
- No provenance stamp (`source_ifindex == 0` — operator discard or
  unattributable row): stays a terminal drop. Never a rescue guess.
- Provenance present but RG still peer-owned/inactive: stays a
  terminal drop; fabric ingress never re-redirects.
- `PolicyDenied` entries are untouched (policy origin, not route
  origin); the #13635 live-session supersession above the deny arm is
  unchanged.

Option (c) (keep the row `ClusterTerminalHAInactive` for this shape)
was not taken: the demotion site cannot distinguish "attribution
missed transiently" from "genuinely locally owned" without the
takeover-time HA state, which is exactly what the hit-path check
consults. Option (a) (scan/invalidate at activation) was not needed:
RG-epoch/lease fences do not cover deny entries stamped
`owner_rg_id == 0`, but the per-hit check is precise, allocation-free,
and covers entries seeded after activation as well.
