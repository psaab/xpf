- **Action**: Chose the EXEMPTION contract over routing the stream through the
  funnel, and the reason is availability rather than tidiness.

  A full-RIB stream is deliberately allowed a 10-minute progress budget, against
  the funnel's 15 s. Making it hold one of four shared slots would let a single
  slow reader occupy a quarter of the budget for EVERY FRR status surface on the
  box for ten minutes — trading a documentation inaccuracy for a real
  regression. A shared limiter would also imply a cross-surface bound that does
  not exist, which is `pkg/api`'s own stated reason for keeping
  `ribStreamLimiter` REST-local: the gRPC and CLI `show route protocol bgp`
  paths call the BUFFERED `GetBGPRoutes` and cannot be pinned by a slow reader.

  That rationale was already written down at the call site. Reading it first is
  what stopped me from "fixing" the inconsistency in the direction that breaks
  something.
