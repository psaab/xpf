# #18626 — target-services down misses orphan listeners and can self-match

## Fix

- **Action**: Replaced both `pkill -f` sweeps in
  `test/incus/target-services.sh down` with per-port socket-owner discovery via
  `ss -tlnpH "sport = :$port"`. The sweep signals listener PIDs only when
  they differ from the unit `MainPID` and are outside that unit's cgroup; it
  runs before stopping systemd services so their ownership remains observable.
  This catches default-port `iperf3 -s -D` orphans and does not put a match
  pattern in the remote shell's command line.
- **Hermetic coverage**: Extended `target-services-selftest.sh` to execute the
  actual remote cleanup script against fake socket rows and `/proc` cgroup
  records. It proves default-port iperf3 and echo orphans are signaled, unit
  MainPIDs/cgroup children are spared, and an active unit with no reported
  ControlGroup is handled fail-closed. The echo sweep and unit stop complete;
  the fake `pkill` models the prior self-termination.
- **RED/GREEN**: Before the fix,
  `bash test/incus/target-services-selftest.sh` failed its #18626 cells: the
  iperf3 and echo orphans were not found, the echo sweep did not complete, and
  `pkill -f` remained in the subject. After the fix, the complete selftest
  passes, including all existing target-services checks and the new
  missing-ControlGroup case.
- **Cgroup-boundary RED/GREEN**: Before the fail-closed guard, the new
  fixture failed because PIDs 146115 and 2003 were signaled while the active
  iperf3 unit's ControlGroup was unavailable. After the guard, the full
  selftest passes and the separately classifiable echo orphan is still
  signaled.

- **Files**: `test/incus/target-services.sh`,
  `test/incus/target-services-selftest.sh`, `docs/log/18626.md`.
