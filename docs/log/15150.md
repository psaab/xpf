- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-19
  - **Action**: Re-anchored at `origin/master 314f4587ff` (worktree `/var/worktrees/campaign-pub/wt-15150`, branch `fix/15150-campaign`). Confirmed mechanism: `IpsecInnerVerdictQueue::try_post` (`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/ipsec_inner_queue.rs:634-645` at base) returned `Err` on `try_lock` failure and incremented `IPSEC_INNER_VERDICT_QUEUE_FULL_TOTAL` (E24); `post_worker_verdict` (`:1889-1900`) converted any such failure into `Deny { stage: "d11_verdict_queue_full", reason: VERDICT_UNCERTAIN }` on the bounded fallback queue; `requeue_worker_verdict` did the same. The E24 "verdict queue full" counter lied (the queue could be empty).
