# #15068 - IPsec capture queue cap consumed by old plus staged generation (A35-03)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-18
- **Action**: The capture queue allocator in
  `pkg/daemon/ipsec_queue_lifecycle.go` was capped at 128 handles
  (32 tunnels x 4 provenance classes), but the guarded commit keeps the
  old generation live while the staged generation allocates, and old
  handles free only one supervisor tick after the old runtime closes.
  Staging a 17th tunnel (or rezoning any of 17-32 tunnels, which rotates
  the generation) therefore needed up to 256 live handles, exhausted the
  allocator, and drove the commit into a QUEUE_OPEN `QuarantineAll` that
  dropped every tunnel. The allocator is now sized for two complete
  generations (`ipsecQueueAllocatorCapacity = 256`), and
  `stageIpsecCapture` in `pkg/daemon/ipsec_capture_wiring_9506.go`
  bounds a single staged generation to the supported 32 tunnels
  (128 handles, counted via the new
  `generationQueueHandleCount`/`generationHandleCount` helpers): the
  129th handle of one generation still fails closed as QUEUE_OPEN.
  The A35-01 prerequisite (valid-tunnel candidates must not survive
  `finalize` into a non-quarantined divert spec) landed separately as
  #15066/#15401; this change was rebased over it and relies on it so a
  valid generation stages instead of self-quarantining.
- **Validation**: New `ipsec_capture_queue_capacity_15068_test.go`
  stages real generations through `stageIpsecCapture` with hermetic
  link/queue/pipeline seams: `TestIpsecCaptureAdd31To32StagesWithoutQuarantine15068`
  (31 live + 32 staged = 252 handles) and
  `TestIpsecCaptureRezone32StagesWithoutQuarantine15068` (32 live + 32
  staged = 256 handles, zone-only rotation) fail on `origin/master`
  (`fb980ad435`) with a quarantined staged generation and pass after the
  fix; `TestIpsecCaptureBeyond32TunnelsStillQuarantines15068` is the
  fail-closed control (33 tunnels still QUEUE_OPEN, from empty and over
  a live 32). `TestIpsecQueueAllocatorRefusesBeyond128Handles9506` was
  renamed to `...BeyondTwoGenerationHandles9506` for the 256 cap.
  After the rebase onto `origin/master` `6fd1efe432` (which contains
  #15401), the targeted 15068 tests plus the adjacent
  `TestIpsecCapture*`/`TestIpsecQueue*` suites were rerun green; `go
  vet ./pkg/daemon` clean.
- **Risk**: Queue numbers are unchanged (1000.. within the existing
  uint16 space; two generations use at most 256 distinct numbers).
  A config with more than 32 route-based VPNs in one generation still
  quarantines fail-closed, now by the explicit per-generation bound
  rather than incidental allocator exhaustion. No behaviour change for
  generations of 16 or fewer tunnels.
