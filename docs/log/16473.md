- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
  - **Action**: Moved the standalone VM's (`test/incus/xpf-test.conf`,
    pushed to `xpf-fw` by `test/incus/setup.sh`) NAT pools off the two
    collided addresses on the shared `172.16.50.0/24` segment:
    `nat64-pool` `172.16.50.11` → `172.16.50.14` (`.11` is the udp
    cluster's WAN RETH in `docs/ha-cluster-udp.conf`) and
    `nat64-snat-pool` + its proxy-ARP `172.16.50.6` → `172.16.50.15`
    (`.6` is the legacy local cluster's WAN RETH in
    `docs/ha-cluster.conf`). Both pools are now proxy-ARPed on
    `ge-0/0/3.50` — the NAT64 pool previously had no ARP answerer at
    all (the round-2 A92-03 blackhole), and proxy-ARPing the old `.11`
    would have fought the udp RETH, so the A92-03 fix direction lands
    on the new address. The hermetic census (below) also exposed a
    third, previously unreported leg of the same class:

    fw0 view) put `nat64-snat-pool` + proxy-ARP on `172.16.50.7`, the
    loss legacy cluster's WAN RETH; moved to legacy-local-only
    `172.16.50.12` and proxy-ARPed its `nat64-pool` (`.9`) alongside.

    legacy local cluster (`.6` RETH, `.9`/`.12` pools) in the §3.3
    collision registry in `docs/incus-tcp-udp-plan.md`, which
    previously listed only loss/tcp/udp, and documented the accepted
    `172.16.80.7` leg there (loss legacy RETH + loss userspace's own
    SNAT pool — same loss stack, no second claimant). Added the
    hermetic census `TestSharedSegmentAddressPlan16473` +
    `TestStandalonePoolsProxyARPed16473` in
    `pkg/configstore/shared_segment_address_plan_16473_test.go`: it

    `CheckText`, groups claims by stack (interface/VRRP, proxy-ARP,

    COLLISION other than the documented `172.16.80.7` leg.
