# #14090 — IPsec-inner zone policy port matching

## Why

D14 passed `0, 0, l4_present=false` to the zone-policy evaluator for every decrypted IPsec packet. Port-bearing application terms therefore failed closed even when the inner packet carried a parseable TCP/UDP header and a matching port.

## What

- Reuse the existing frame parser to read declared-packet-bounded TCP/UDP ports from the built logical-ingress frame and pass the tuple and L4-presence signal to policy evaluation.
- Keep non-first fragments flowless: their nominal L4 offset contains payload, not transport ports. First fragments remain L4-present when the shared parser can read the header; protocols without ports or truncated headers remain flowless.
- Add IPv4 and IPv6 TCP/UDP matching and nonmatching port regressions, plus IPv4 first/non-first fragment cases.

## Risk

Unreadable or absent ports remain flowless, preserving the policy evaluator's fail-closed behavior for port-bearing terms. The shared parser bounds port reads by the IP-declared packet length and handles the existing IPv6 extension-header and fragment conventions.
