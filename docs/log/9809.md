## Defect (three findings, one scope)

1. **SYN-CMP-01.** `validateVRFOverlap` learned a steering term's space only from literal prefixes `netip.ParsePrefix` accepts. The same overlap written as a prefix-list, a literal plus a prefix-list, a bare host, `any` or a term with no `from` committed clean, with no warning and no tolerant admission. The literal controls were refused.
2. **SYN-CMP-02.** The detector and `RibGroupConnectedPrefixes` read a bare member (`interface ge-0/0/1`) as unit 0. The userspace FIB binds every configured unit (#9132). An overlap on unit ≥ 1 was invisible, and an interface-routes rib-group leaked none of those units' prefixes. With unit 0 unaddressed, the warning blamed a DHCP-only member.
3. **SYN-C-CMP-07.** The texts still said the session identity has no routing-instance discriminator, and that colliding traffic is "never adjudicated by any policy". Neither has held since #7160 and #9519.

## Blast-radius check and the pairing rule

The acceptance asked for a no-`from` term to be refused as match-all. Measured first on the shape the lab loads:
- `test-fbf-steering.sh` applies the FBF overlay (ISP-B steered by `from dscp af31`) on `docs/ha-cluster-userspace.conf`, whose `sfmix` virtual-router has member `gr-0/0/0.0` at `10.255.192.42/30`.
- On master, strict accepts the shipped DSCP-only steer. It refuses the same term written with `source-address 0.0.0.0/0`.

So pairing match-all with everything would have refused the shipped overlay, on a member-versus-steered pair #7160 already separates by routing domain. The rule implemented instead, also posted on the issue:
- **Prefix-lists and bare hosts** become ordinary steered prefixes and pair with everything, like the literal CIDRs they stand for.
- **Match-all spellings** (`any`, no address match, an empty `except`) add match-all steered space. It pairs only with steered space from **another filter**, which is the residual: one 5-tuple steered into two instances from default-instance ingress, both in domain 0.
  - **Why another filter, measured.** The first cut paired match-all with any steered space and reddened `TestFirewallFilter`. Its multi-WAN filter `inet-source-dscp` steers `172.16.80.198/32` into `Atherton-Fiber` with an address term, then DSCP-marked traffic into `Comcast-GigabitPro` with a DSCP-only term. Within one filter the first matching term wins, so a given 5-tuple always takes the same term, and the pair is not a collision. The issue's own fixture uses two filters on two interfaces, and that pair is still refused.
- **A literal `0.0.0.0/0` / `::/0`** is unchanged and still pairs with everything. Relaxing it is the maintainer question the issue already flags: member overlap plus an unrelated PBR term, row 1 of `compiler_validate_vrf_overlap_pbr_7924_test.go`.
