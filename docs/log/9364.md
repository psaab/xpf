## Why this landed anyway
The plumbing is correct, mutation-scored, and is what a #9546 remedy plugs into;
the belt independently prevents a #9482 recurrence on this capability. What makes
it honest rather than a landed partial fix is that the inertness is stated at the
plumbing site, pinned by a cell, filed as its own issue, and #9364 stays OPEN. The
#9364 cells prove a domain SUPPLIED reaches the wire — deliberately not the claim
that the GC supplies one.
