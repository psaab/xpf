# #17751 — CommandExport echoes replay after a streamed close

Issue: psaab/xpf#17751. Branch: `fix/17751-campaign`. Base: master
`3c49ffd7a4b9ea3f3c351d5826a3ba4f209d441d`.

## Defect and ratified design

A paged `CommandExport` returns its open through the control export buffer and
also emits a ring echo. The event-stream leg intentionally suppresses that
provenance (#9630), leaving `streamed_seq == 0`; before this fix, the flush
still copied the echo into the per-binding RPC-fallback FIFO. Provenance is not
on the wire, and Go's connected discard predicate retains sequence-zero
deltas. If the session then closes through the stream, the later fallback drain
can replay the stale open; `QueueSessionV4` gives it a fresh generation and
resurrects the closed session.

The ratified Option C is a Rust-only producer change: do not mirror
`CommandExport` provenance into the fallback FIFO because its paged control
response is already authoritative. Keep the ring echo, recent-deltas push,
existing event-stream suppression, and every other flush leg unchanged. Do not
change Go behavior, wire fields, or fingerprints. Other non-CommandExport
deltas retain their existing enqueue-failure fallback and loss-of-sync arming
semantics.

## Change and coverage

`flush_session_deltas` now excludes `ExportProvenance::CommandExport(_)` from
both RPC-fallback push paths. The `streamed_seq` stamp and recent-deltas push
remain in place, as do RT_FLOW/ring behavior and the existing stream-suppression
branch. The paged export echo fixtures now emit explicit CommandExport
provenance, rather than the unrelated LossResync provenance, and assert that
these echoes do not enter fallback. The regression composes control export,
CommandExport ring echo, a later streamed close, and fallback FIFO drain; it
also checks the close's stream sequence, recent-deltas entries, and suppression
without loss-of-sync arming. Existing incremental/full-queue and bulk-drop
controls preserve the previous non-CommandExport fallback and arming rules.
