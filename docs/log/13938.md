# Issue #13938 — defer session-hit mutation until zone authority is known

## Why

A TCP session lookup ran before the #9519 authority verdict. Its existing
close deferral protected only FIN/RST: a foreign SYN-ACK could promote an
opening entry, propagate promotion to its companion, refresh the idle deadline,
and queue an HA Update before the packet was dropped. A following owner ACK
could then complete a handshake the server never answered, moving the pair to
the 300-second established timeout. Foreign ACKs could also refresh
`last_seen_ns` despite being dropped.

## What

Local session-hit resolution now returns a read-only snapshot while deferring
all packet-state mutation until the poll path establishes that the arrival is
an Owner. The Owner replays the ordinary lookup; a foreign arrival can still
use its session snapshot for policy and forwarding verdicts but never refreshes,
promotes, closes, propagates, emits an HA update, or refreshes the expiry wheel.
Peer-synced materialization and promotion remain on their existing
Owner-authorized paths.

Regression coverage exercises the real poll path: a dropped foreign SYN-ACK
leaves both opening halves unchanged, emits no HA Update, and cannot let the
following owner ACK complete the handshake; an Owner SYN-ACK still promotes
both halves to HandshakePending. A separate established-session case pins the
foreign ACK `last_seen_ns` invariant.

## Risk

Local Owner hits perform the deferred lookup after authority is established;
foreign packets that are independently permitted still retain their existing
packet-local forwarding decision but no longer extend or otherwise alter the
session they hit. No live-cluster dataplane smoke was run.
