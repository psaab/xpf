## Smoke pass criteria (pre-registered)


These go into `docs/log/19140.md` as the branch's FIRST log entry, committed before any run. They include the loadavg amendment of 2026-11-16: the VOID rule keys on the firewall nodes, not the orchestrator. The smoke is `make test-member-shim-loss`, on loss, in its own lock cell when the coordinator schedules it. Each arm uses the head deb and a master control deb.

**Main arm.** Node0 primary for RG1; iperf3 `-P8` and a 10 ms ping train through RG1; `bpftool link detach` of ge-0-0-2's XDP link on node0 only.

| # | Criterion | Head | Master control |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | The node0 ERROR "RETH data member has no XDP shim" appears within 3 s of injection | yes | n/a (master has #19110's line only if #19110 has landed) |
| P2 | Member-shim debt is installed on RG1 only (not RG0 or RG2), 10 s ± 2 s after injection | yes | no debt (the defect) |
| P3 | RG1 moves to node1 within 3 s of the debt (by 15 s after injection at most) | yes | **no move within 60 s** (the defect reproduced) |
| P4 | Ping via RG1 recovers on node1; total loss at most 16 s from injection | yes | loss continues for the window |
| P5 | No RG1 flap back to node0 for 60 s after the move while node0's member stays detached | yes | n/a |
| P6 | RG2's ping and throughput are unaffected (no loss over 1 s) throughout | yes | yes |
| P7 | Recovery: a `commit` on node0 re-attaches the shim; debt clears 10 s ± 2 s after the attach; with preempt, the planned move back has at most the usual planned-move loss | yes | n/a |

**Variants** (each its own run):
- `--both`: detach on both nodes. RG1 does not move and does not flap for 60 s; debt on both nodes. The RG1 path is broken; that is expected and recorded.
- `--lone`: node1's xpfd stopped. RG1 stays on node0, with the ERROR, the metric = 1 and the debt; no ownerless window (`show chassis cluster status` has a primary throughout).
- `--standby`: detach on node1 (the standby). No move. node1 has debt and is not takeover-ready. node0 stays primary with no debt.

**VOID conditions** (re-run, do not score):
- `bpftool` missing on a node;
- the detach does not take (IFLA_XDP still shows the shim);
- a lock-cell interruption;
- clock skew above 1 s between nodes, measured before pairing journals;
- the 1-minute loadavg of either FIREWALL NODE (fw0 or fw1, read on the node) above 8 at injection.

  The ORCHESTRATOR workstation's loadavg is recorded alongside as context only and never VOIDs a run. It routinely sits at 8-10 and does not run the dataplane.

**Confound written down before the run:**
- the takeover readiness hold time and the #12164 echo-lease refresh can add up to about 2 s to P3;
- a heartbeat at 200 ms × 5 adds nothing here (no peer loss).
