# #16499 — WireGuard pad capped at inner budget

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
- **Action**: Capped the WG §5.4.6 pad at the inner budget
  (`outer_mtu − overhead`) on both encap sites, matching Linux
  `calculate_skb_padding` and wireguard-go `calculatePaddingSize`.
  Added `padded_len_capped` (wg/mod.rs SSOT) and
  `WgEngine::try_encap_capped`; TUN-origin (`wg_control/dispatch.rs`)
  and transit (`frame/wg.rs`) now thread the per-peer/per-egress inner
  budget. Size models (`wg_control/mtu.rs`, `frame/wg.rs`) and the
  advertised inner MTU (`wg::mss::wg_inner_mtu` = `outer − overhead`,
  no worst-pad subtraction) are the capped inverse. Go
  `wgTunMTUForEndpoint` default drops the worst-pad subtraction and
  warns on an operator `mtu` above the default-underlay budget.
  Previously a standard-formula operator `mtu` (1420 v6/1500, 1432
  v4/1492) dropped every full-size TUN-origin packet at
  `encap_mtu_drops` with no PTB.
