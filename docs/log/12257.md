# Issue #12257 — preserve fabric session ownership and unique SNAT replies

## Why

An unstamped fabric reply with one established routing-instance SNAT match was rejected before session authority could use that match, while a reverse session hit could lose its forward-origin owner attribution during stale route revalidation. Both paths broke established-session behavior under fabric ingress.

## What

The fabric-zone gate now carries a locally and shared-unique established forward-NAT match into session resolution. The resolver rechecks that exact candidate and installs the reverse companion in the forward session's routing domain. Reverse stale-route revalidation refreshes the route without replacing the companion's forward-owner RG attribution. The reply-admission fixture now seeds the existing flow's receiver-local policy and interface-SNAT provenance, isolating this test from independent fail-closed stale-policy/NAT gates while still asserting the packet has no fabric-zone stamp.
