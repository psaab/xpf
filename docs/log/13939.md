# Issue #13939

## Change

A peer-synced TCP entry promoted by an owner hit used the FIN/RST packet flags during `update_session` before the poll path applied its deferred owner close. The later close saw no new state progress and skipped companion propagation, leaving the other half on its established timeout.

`promote_synced_with_origin` now treats a closing packet as zero TCP flags in
the promotion update. This defers FIN/RST state changes until the owner-only
close step and prevents a combined FIN|ACK from falsely completing a pending
handshake after FIN is stripped. The owner-only deferred-close step remains
responsible for recording the observed close and propagating it to the
companion, preserving the authority gate and normal close-window handling.
Regressions cover FIN/RST propagation and a pending peer-synced FIN|ACK.

## Risk

The promotion wrapper now treats closing packets as zero flags. This keeps ACK
bits from completing a pending handshake before the owner caller's deferred
close step propagates FIN/RST. The production promotion call is owner-verdict
gated; foreign arrivals do not invoke promotion. Deferred close retains the
existing propagation, expiry, and HA update behavior.
