# Issue #13939

## Change

A peer-synced TCP entry promoted by an owner hit used the FIN/RST packet flags during `update_session` before the poll path applied its deferred owner close. The later close saw no new state progress and skipped companion propagation, leaving the other half on its established timeout.

`promote_synced_with_origin` now withholds FIN/RST from the promotion update. The owner-only deferred-close step remains responsible for recording the observed close and propagating it to the companion, preserving the authority gate and normal close-window handling. Added FIN and RST regression cases that create a peer-synced pair, promote the reverse half with the packet flags, then apply the deferred close and verify both halves' close state and timeout.

## Risk

The promotion wrapper now deliberately leaves close bits for its owner caller's deferred-close step. The production promotion call is owner-verdict gated; foreign arrivals do not invoke promotion. FIN/RST on the deferred close retains existing propagation, expiry, and HA update behavior.
