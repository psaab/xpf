# #15288 — two zones fill a fragment-overlap shard with incomplete entries and ShardFull-drop all other zones

- **Base**: `71a24075c6` (origin/master at branch start)
- **Branch**: `fix/15288-campaign`

## Why

`OverlapTracker` caps a shard at 64 entries (`OVERLAP_CAP_PER_SHARD`)
and a `(routing_domain, ingress_zone)` pair at 32
(`OVERLAP_CAP_PER_ZONE_PER_SHARD`, #14321). At the per-zone bound only
that zone's COMPLETED anchors may be reclaimed, and at the shard cap
#15103 reclaims only the globally oldest COMPLETED anchor of any zone
in the domain. Two zones that each fill their 32-entry reserve with
incomplete entries (first fragments forwarded, tails not yet arrived —
the normal shape of fragmented traffic) therefore leave a third zone
no reclaimable anchor at all: its first fragment drops `ShardFull`
fail-closed for the anchors' 30s TTL, defeating the #14321 per-zone
reserve for exactly the traffic it exists to protect. Adjudicated
MATERIAL (gemini D4-01): the bounded probe is two zones at 32
incomplete entries each in one shard, third zone refused.

## What

- `userspace-dp/src/fragment_overlap/mod.rs`, shard-cap branch only:
  when no completed anchor of any zone in the candidate's routing
  domain is reclaimable, and the candidate's own `(domain, zone)` is
  strictly below its 32-entry reserve (guaranteed by the ordering —
  the own-zone check above either reclaimed, refused, or left it
  below 32), reclaim the globally oldest settled-INCOMPLETE anchor of
  a DIFFERENT zone in the same routing domain before refusing.
  Settled-incomplete is `pending == 0 && !failed && !complete`
  (new `overlap_entry_incomplete`): every recorded fragment has
  settled, none failed, ranges do not cover a terminal end — the
  settled counterpart of a completed anchor. Still-pending anchors
  (`pending > 0`, live admission tokens queued for TX settlement) and
  failed (poisoned) anchors are never reclaimed on this path.
  Per-zone fairness is preserved: admission can only displace another
  zone's anchor, never grow the candidate's zone past 32, because a
  candidate already at 32 has already exited through the own-zone
  branch. The 64-entry shard bound is unchanged.
- New observability counter
  `FRAG_OVERLAP_CROSS_ZONE_INCOMPLETE_RECLAIMED` (global atomic
  alongside its #15103 sibling; like that sibling it is read as a
  delta in tests and not yet wired to the binding/status metrics —
  the refusal itself stays counted by `FRAG_OVERLAP_SHARD_FULL_DROPPED`
  and the binding-local shard-full counter, both unchanged).
- Module docs (fairness + failure-direction bullets), the counter
  doc, the shard-cap branch comment, and the `commit_admission` doc
  updated at the mechanism; the stale "incomplete anchors are never
  reclaimed" claims now name this sole exception. The #15103
  regression's Control 2 (incomplete fill refuses a third zone) is
  updated to the surviving invariant: a candidate from a DIFFERENT
  routing domain must still fail closed, because the fallback is
  scoped to the candidate's own domain. Same-domain third-zone
  admission over incomplete anchors is now covered by the new
  #15288 regression.
- NOT changed: sender quota (8/shard), per-zone reserve (32), shard
  cap (64), TTLs, range geometry, shard hashing, own-zone completed
  reclaim, cross-zone completed reclaim ordering (completed anchors
  are always preferred — the incomplete fallback runs only when no
  completed anchor exists), `FragAssoc` (no completed/settled-anchor
  reclaim class there; the D4-01 mechanism is OverlapTracker-only),
  and all callers (they already drop on `ShardFull` and forward on
  admission).

Security trade, explicit: an evicted settled-incomplete anchor loses
overlap protection for the rest of its reassembly window, and a later
fragment of that datagram plants a fresh entry (checked against the
surviving ranges only). That is the identical trade #14321 already
accepts at the per-zone cap for completed anchors and #15103 extends
across zones; the alternative (third-zone blackhole behind two zones'
incomplete fills) is the worse failure. Failed anchors keep their
poison protection unconditionally.

## Risk

Residual: under sustained multi-zone incomplete churn in one shard,
the oldest incomplete anchors rotate before their 30s TTL; the
`FRAG_OVERLAP_CROSS_ZONE_INCOMPLETE_RECLAIMED` rate makes this
visible. When one zone's incomplete datagrams are repeatedly the
oldest, that zone bears the evictions — bounded by the same
oldest-first rule the completed paths already use. No behaviour
change when a shard is below 64, when a completed anchor is
reclaimable, or when the candidate's zone is at its own reserve.
