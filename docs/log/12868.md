## Why

The ICMP owner and foreign session-hit verdicts evaluated policy through the wrapper that obtains a fresh monotonic timestamp. The poll descriptor already captures the packet's `now_ns`; scheduled policies must be evaluated against that same instant.

## What

The three policy evaluations used by owner forward, owner reverse, and foreign ICMP hit verdicts now call `evaluate_policy_result_without_counting_at` with the descriptor's captured `now_ns`. Both production callers pass the captured value through. Evaluation remains side-effect-free for policy hit counters.
