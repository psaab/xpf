# #15096 — per-binding pending-neighbor cap exceeds the RX frame pool (A66-02)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-18
- **Issue**: `MAX_PENDING_NEIGH` (4096) bound distinct unresolved next-hops
  per binding without regard to the binding's RX frame budget. At the
  default ring-entries 1024 the whole RX fill pool is 2048 frames
  (fill = 2*ring for every driver: total = reserved TX + 2*ring, so
  non-virtio totals 512 + 2048 and virtio 1024 + 2048), so a permitted
  sweep of a large connected prefix (an IPv6 /64 is the usual case)
  parking one RX frame per distinct unresolved hop could pin twice
  the pool, drain the fill ring, and stall all RX on the affected
  queues.
- **Base**: `origin/master` `f524416374` (worktree created from it;
  `origin/master` advanced to `fde73e9b28` during the lane — no
  conflicting pending-neigh change in between).
- **Files**: `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/bind.rs` (`rx_fill_frames_for_driver`
  + contract test), `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/mod.rs`
  (`pending_neigh_cap_for_rx_fill`), `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/worker/mod.rs`
  (`BindingWorker::pending_neigh_cap`, wired in `create` + both test
  constructors), `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/neighbor_dispatch.rs`
  (admission takes the binding cap; regression cells),
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/poll_descriptor/mod.rs` (sole production call
  site passes `binding.pending_neigh_cap`),
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/neighbor_resolver_tests.rs` (call-site
  signature), `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/binding_state/mod.rs` +
  `coordinator/status.rs` (counter docs), `docs/log/15096.md`.

## Mechanism (verified at base)

- `mod.rs:580` `const MAX_PENDING_NEIGH: usize = 4096` was the only
  bound. `neighbor_dispatch.rs::pending_neigh_admission(already_pending,
  len)` compared `len < MAX_PENDING_NEIGH`, ignoring the frame pool.
- `bind.rs`: total frames = `reserved_tx + ring_entries*2`
  (`binding_frame_count_for_driver`); reserved TX = ring/2 (ring for
  virtio), clamped [256, 8192]. `BindingWorker::create` puts ALL frames
  past reserved TX into the initial fill.
- `poll_descriptor` Buffer arm: `pending_neigh.insert(pending_key, …)`
  then `recycle_now = false` — the parked packet's UMEM frame leaves
  circulation until resolution or the 2 s timeout. One frame per
  distinct hop means 4096 parked frames at ring 1024 against a
  2048-frame fill pool.

## Fix

- `pending_neigh_cap_for_rx_fill(rx_fill) = min(MAX_PENDING_NEIGH,
  max(rx_fill / 4, 1))` — at most one quarter of the binding's own RX
  fill pool may be parked; three quarters always remain for RX/TX
  circulation. Degenerate pools still admit one representative.
- `rx_fill_frames_for_driver` derives fill as total − reserved from the
  existing bind arithmetic (never restated), so a reserved-TX policy
  change moves the cap with it.
- `BindingWorker::pending_neigh_cap` is computed once at bind time
  (production `create` via the driver/ring the binding actually bound
  with; test constructors from their synthetic pools) and the poll
  loop's single admission call passes it. Refusals past the derived
  cap take the existing `CapacityDrop` arm: counted in
  `pending_neigh_capacity_drops` (#2375), frame recycled, slow-path
  reinject suppressed — fail-closed, no silent park.
- NOT changed: probe schedules, the 2 s/800 ms pending timeout, the
  negative-neighbor cache, per-source/per-prefix sub-caps (the issue
  lists them as optional; the per-binding quarter-pool cap alone
  restores the fill-ring invariant), and the `MAX_PENDING_NEIGH`
  ceiling for large rings (a ring ≥ 8192 binding keeps the historical
  4096 behaviour).

  pre-fix helper is `pending_neigh_admission(already_pending, len)`

  pass, and `BindingWorker` has no `pending_neigh_cap` field, so the
  committed cells fail on the base by construction and the two-arg

    non-virtio/virtio/mlx5, asserts `cap == rx_fill/4 < 4096`,
    `cap <= rx_frames/4`, Buffer at `cap-1`, CapacityDrop at `cap`,
    DuplicateDrop for a duplicate at `cap`; plus the large-ring
    ceiling and degenerate-pool floors.
  - `binding_worker_parks_at_most_quarter_of_its_pool_15096` — a real
    `BindingWorker::new_for_mirror_test` (256-frame pool) carries

  - `rx_fill_frames_is_total_minus_reserved_tx_15096` (bind.rs) — the

- Lab leg (deferred to main): a /64 SYN sweep at ring=1024 should leave

## Risk

- Bindings on small rings now refuse new distinct-hop parks earlier
  (512 at ring 1024, since fill = 2048): under a genuine multi-hop
  outage more cold connects take the counted capacity-drop + retransmit
  path instead of parking. That is the intended trade — the alternative
  was total RX stall on the queue — and the refusal is observable via
  `xpf_userspace_pending_neigh_capacity_drops_total`.
- Test-constructor pools model all frames as fill (no reserved-TX
  carve-out), so fixture caps (e.g. 64 on the 256-frame mirror pool)
  differ from a same-ring production binding's; no fixture asserted
  the old 4096 headroom, so nothing silently shrinks below a test's
  seeding needs (mirror fixtures seed at most 4096 keys only in the
  sweep-budget cell, which inserts directly, bypassing admission).
