# #16818 — aborted node-targeted failover leaves the requester primary (R3-F08-01)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-25
- **Action**: After `commitRequestedPeerFailover` promoted the requester, a
  post-ACK abort (local commit-ready settle failure or commit-send failure)
  reported the failover as failed but left the requester primary whenever it
  outranked the owner, or whenever the owner's secondary-hold heartbeat had
  been seen before the local commit. `abortRequestedPeerFailover` only re-ran
  the election when it restored a peer snapshot: restoring the pre-request
  PRIMARY snapshot made the demotion a dual-active decision a
  higher-priority requester wins, and restoring the hold snapshot ran no
  election at all; a delivered abort frame could also restore the owner,
  whose next non-hold heartbeat expired the #15164/#16592 aborted marker
  before any election applied it. Both abort paths (single-RG and batch) now
  run the election unconditionally after recording the aborted reqID and
  restoring any pre-request local manual-failover hold, so the existing
  aborted-marker arm in `electRG` demotes the promoted requester ("Peer
  transfer aborted") regardless of the restored snapshot and before the
  abort frame can restore the owner. No wire, marker-lifetime, or
  owner-side change: a lost abort frame still leaves the owner to restore
  at #5079 lease expiry (documented ownerless window), and the "Peer
  transfer out" guard is unchanged.
- **Validation**: New `failover_abort_16818_test.go` drives two real
  `cluster.Manager`s through `RequestPeerFailover`/`RequestPeerFailoverBatch`
  with the daemon's owner wiring (`ManualFailover` +
  `ArmRemoteTransferOutLease`, `AbortRemoteTransferOut`): 16 single-RG rows
  (requester-outranks vs shipped control ordering × commit-ready/commit-send
  abort × abort frame delivered/lost × hold heartbeat before/after commit)
  plus 2 batch rows. Pre-fix the 8 requester-outranks rows and both batch
  rows failed with the requester still primary immediately after the abort
  (RED); post-fix all rows report `reqPrimary=false` after the abort and
  after heartbeat exchanges, with `ownPrimary=true` on delivered-frame rows.
  Shipped `TestAbortedPeerFailoverDoesNotRepromoteRequester15164`, the batch
  twin, stale-abort, #16592 ack-gate, #6527 commit-rollback, and #15211
  fence-abort suites stay green.
- **Risk**: The abort now always runs one election pass under `m.mu` (the
  restored-override path already did); the aborted-marker arm only demotes
  a primary RG or holds a non-primary one, so ACK-failure aborts that never
  promoted are unchanged. No behaviour change outside the aborted
  transfer path; no live-lab smoke (in-process managers, no VIP/rg_active
  actuation).
