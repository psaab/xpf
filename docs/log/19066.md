## Risk

`XPF_REQUIRE_NETNS=1` puts every existing consumer into forcing mode under
testguard: pkg/daemon, pkg/routing, pkg/fabricsegment, pkg/networkd and
pkg/nftables. Under testguard, a missing forcing prerequisite (a tool, a
kernel feature) is now a FAIL instead of a skip. That is what the forcing mode
exists for. The full-package name diff above measures the change. Outside
testguard nothing changes: `NetnsUnavailable` skips exactly as `t.Skipf` did.
