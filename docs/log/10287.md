# 10287 — Reopen TCP tuples on valid SYN reuse

## Boundary

A TCP session entry keeps `closing`, `reset`, and FIN-direction state after a
FIN or RST. A new connection that reuses the same 5-tuple during the old
close window therefore hit the old entry and inherited the 30 s FIN or 2 s
RST reap window instead of starting a fresh 20 s OPENING state and promoting
to the 300 s ESTABLISHED window.

## Fix

- `SessionTable::evict_closing_tcp_pair_for_syn` recognizes only a valid bare
  SYN (SYN set, ACK/FIN/RST clear) and removes the exact local forward and
  reverse halves after the poll path has established Owner authority and the
  new-flow policy has permitted the SYN (#10886).
- The authority probe is read-only. A foreign bare SYN remains on the existing
  session-hit path and cannot retire local halves or shared forward, reverse-
  NAT, wire-alias, owner-RG, or reverse-prewarm copies before its verdict.
- After an owner permit, the normal session-miss path installs a fresh pair,
  preserving new session IDs, OPENING timeout selection, handshake promotion,
  and all normal FIN/RST/TIME-WAIT transitions.
- SYN|FIN and SYN|RST are rejected as reopeners and continue through the old
  close path, preserving TCP close protections.

## RED then GREEN evidence

- Before the implementation, `cargo test
  syn_on_closing_tuple_evicts_both_halves_and_misses_10287 -- --nocapture`
  failed at the new assertion because a SYN on the closing tuple returned a
  hit instead of a miss (`0 passed; 1 failed`).
- After the implementation, the issue-focused set passed: `cargo test 10287
  -- --nocapture` — 9 passed, 0 failed.
- The reuse lifecycle cells cover both FIN and RST windows, assert fresh
  20-second OPENING state, complete SYN/SYN-ACK/ACK promotion to 300 seconds,
  and verify server-first traffic after an opening-window idle gap.
- Controls cover ACK and SYN-ACK close semantics, SYN retransmission on an open
  tuple, shared-map preservation before authority, foreign SYN preservation,
  policy-denied owner SYN preservation, and owner replacement after permit.
