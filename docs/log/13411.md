## Disposition

With mixed direct and VRF ingress, the trusted-reinject fallback was emitted
with a nonempty exclusion list that contained direct interfaces and
`xpf-usp0`, but not the covered VRF master. Consequently the owner fallback
could match replies arriving through `iifname vrf-blue` and overrule the VRF
slave's ingress-zone permission. The fallback now excludes each covered VRF
master; the separate VRF-scoped fallback still handles uncovered slaves via
`sdifname`, preserving their owner-based stale-reply protection.
