- **Action**: Identified the two holders of 172.16.80.200 (read-only on loss).

    loss/tcp/udp hosts and the orchestrator.

    (REACHABLE).
  - A passive VLAN 80 ARP capture on tcp fw1 (01:35-01:39) saw no frame with
    sender `.200` from either holder. The hazard is latent; no switch has
    been observed.
