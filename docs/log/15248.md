# Bound the generated-reply per-source tier to its fair share of the zone aggregate (#15248)

- **Issue**: #15248 (A67-01, from the hostile source review claim A67-01,
  validated MATERIAL by the research run). Follow-up of #9901 (F-074), which
  introduced the per-source tier this issue bounds.
- **Cause**: `ZoneLimiter` in `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/icmp_ratelimit.rs` gave
  every one of its 64 fixed source slots (plus the sourceless overflow
  bucket) a flat 100/s + burst 100, so the source tier could admit up to
  6500/s into a zone aggregate capped at 1000/s + burst 1000. Ten or more
  distinct sources therefore drained the aggregate and starved Packet Too
  Big, Time Exceeded, and reject replies for every other source in the zone
  for as long as the flood lasted (the PMTUD stall is the sharpest
  consequence: legitimate PTB replies are suppressed zone-wide). Worse,
  `addr_fold` folded the full /128, so ONE IPv6 host rotating interface
  identifiers inside its own /64 minted a fresh 100/s slot per address and
  needed no coalition at all.
- **Change** (`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/icmp_ratelimit.rs` only):
  - Per-source budgets are now the zone aggregate's fair share:
    `src_budget(rate, burst)` returns aggregate/(64 slots + overflow),
    clamped to at least 1, computed from whatever aggregate rate/burst the
    gate carries (15/s + burst 15 at the production 1000/1000). The sum of
    all per-source entitlements can never exceed the aggregate, so a
    saturated source tier is source-denied WITHOUT consuming the aggregate
    (the #9901 source-FIRST order is unchanged) and leaves headroom for
    sources that have not spent their share.
  - IPv6 sources key on their /64: `addr_fold` drops the host bits before
    folding (the WireGuard #14639 aggregation), so IID rotation inside one
    /64 is ONE source with ONE budget. Distinct /64s — and every IPv4 /32 —
    keep independent budgets.
  - The zone aggregate cap, the per-reason/per-zone splits (#3618/#5856),
    the denial counters, and every generator call site are unchanged.
- **Regression coverage** (`icmp_ratelimit_tests.rs`, all driving the real
  `allow_generated_error_zoned_at` / `allow_generated_reject_at` gates):
  - `rotating_v6_iids_in_one_64_do_not_starve_victim_15248` — 16 IIDs of one
    /64 at 10/s each for 10s against the production aggregate: exactly 163
    admitted (fair-share burst 15 + 15/s GCRA), victim in another /64
    passes. On base the rotation admits ~1008 and the victim is denied.
  - `sixteen_distinct_ipv4_sources_do_not_starve_victim_15248` — 16
    distinct-slot IPv4 sources at 10/s each for 10s pass in full (1600;
    fairness must not clip legitimate distinct sources) and a 17th victim
    passes. On base all 1600 are admitted through 100-token flat slots and
    drain the 1000-token aggregate, so the victim is denied.
  - `victim_survives_sustained_distinct_floods_15248` — 16 distinct
    sources at 100/s each for 10s admit ~2624 (bounded by their fair
    shares), never the 11000-token aggregate envelope, and the victim
    passes. On base the flooders consume the aggregate envelope and the
    victim is denied.
  - `single_v6_source_and_distinct_64s_unchanged_15248` — the /128
    control: one source driving 100/s for 1s is bounded to its fair share
    (29 admitted), and a source in a different /64 passes immediately
    (no over-aggregation across prefixes).
  - Neighbouring #9901/#5856/#3618 cells whose harness drove a per-source
    budget the fair share of a small test aggregate can no longer supply
    were rescaled to an aggregate whose fair share equals the historical
    budget (burst 260 => share 4; burst 130 => share 2; `agg_for_share`
    for the 100-token cells). Their assertions, sources, and zone setup
    are unchanged.
- **RED evidence**: With the new cells appended to pristine
  origin/master (b3cba805a9; only the neighbour-cell rescales omitted, the
  four new cells byte-identical) plus the one-line `MirrorConfigSnapshot`
  literal rescue #15520 documents (without it the test target does not
  compile on master at all), 3 of the 4 new cells FAIL:
  `rotating_v6_iids_in_one_64_do_not_starve_victim_15248` at the same-slot
  setup assertion (each /128 IID keys a different slot on base),
  `single_v6_source_and_distinct_64s_unchanged_15248` with 100 admitted
  vs the fair-share 29, and
  `victim_survives_sustained_distinct_floods_15248` with 10990 admitted
  (the whole aggregate envelope) vs the ~2624 bound.
  `sixteen_distinct_ipv4_sources_do_not_starve_victim_15248` passes on
  base as predicted (1600 offered < each flooder's old 100-token burst
  summed past the aggregate only via the victim leg, which still passes
  because 1600 attempts at 100ms spacing never saturate the refilling
  aggregate at a frozen victim instant — the RED weight for IPv4 is
  carried by the sustained cell).
- **GREEN evidence**: With the fix, the full `icmp_ratelimit` module
  passes 25/25 (the 4 new cells plus every rescaled #9901/#5856/#3618
  neighbour and the #2955 GCRA guards). Neighbouring generator suites:
  `reject_reply` 54/54, `embedded_icmp` 37/37, `afxdp::icmp::tests` 16/16.
  `icmp_ptb::tests` is 62 passed / 1 failed and the broad `mirror` /
  `reject` filters show 3 `rejected_mirror_reservation_10790` failures —
  all four reproduce isolated `--exact` on this tree in modules this
  change does not touch (untouched by the diff: `ha/session_import.rs`,
  `icmp_ptb_tests.rs`, WireGuard engine), i.e. pre-existing master
  breakage alongside #15520, not regressions of this lane. The
  `MirrorConfigSnapshot` literal at `mirror/mod_tests.rs:1032` carries
  the #15520-prescribed fields (`ingress_vlan_id: 0, unit_scoped:
  false`, matching the line-975 literal) so the test target compiles;
  that one line is the #15520 fix landing early because this lane
  cannot build without it. `rustfmt --check` and `git diff --check`
  are clean on every touched file.
- **Blast radius / risk**: Deliberate behaviour delta — a single
  legitimate source's generated-reply burst is now its fair share (15 at
  the production aggregate) instead of 100. A real traceroute/PMTUD
  source emits a handful of generated errors per flow, far below 15/s
  sustained, so benign behaviour is unchanged; only a source trying to
  consume more than 1/65 of a zone's generated-reply budget is clipped
  earlier, which is exactly the fairness the tier exists to provide.
  Denial still fails closed (reply dropped, `*_rate_limited_total`
  bumped exactly once); no traffic is forwarded differently in any case.
  Not changed: the aggregate 1000/s zone cap, per-zone and per-reason
  isolation, the fallback limiters' shape, and the WireGuard limiter
  (#14639 already aggregated on /64 there).
