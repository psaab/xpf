# 15021 — eventengine: defer budget/cooldown-denied edges instead of discarding them

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-18
- **Action**: A global-budget denial (or the 30 s cooldown) permanently
  discarded the only `ping_test_failed` edge of a real outage: RPM emits that
  event only on a status change, `runAction` dropped the action on
  `globalBudgetAvailable()==false` (only `dropped_total{reason=global_budget}`),
  and `evaluateEvent` cooldown-suppressed with no counter. Now a pre-commit
  budget denial parks the action in `pending.budget` (one per policy) until
  the oldest slot expires and replays it (revalidated by the existing
  stale/reservation gates; a second denial drops as the residual
  `global_budget`); cooldown suppressions park per (policy,event) direction
  in `pending.cooldown`, are counted, and replay after the cooldown — a
  different-direction edge is never suppressed by a parked one. New metrics:
  `xpf_event_actions_deferred_total`, `xpf_event_cooldown_suppressed_total`,
  `xpf_event_pending_deferred`. The #10871 storm cap (4 commits / rolling
  minute) is preserved. RED on master: the only-edge tests timed out with no
  commit after expiry; GREEN after the fix.
