- **Action**: Exported `upgrade.DefaultRejoinDeadline` as 180s (the daemon's approximately 150s bulk-prime attempt schedule plus 30s grace), replaced the 60s rolling default, and threaded `--rejoin-deadline` through forward and rollback rolling upgrades.
