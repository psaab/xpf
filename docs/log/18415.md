# #18415 — Configure the HA rolling rejoin deadline

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-29
- **Action**: Exported `upgrade.DefaultRejoinDeadline` as 180s (the daemon's approximately 150s bulk-prime attempt schedule plus 30s grace), replaced the 60s rolling default, and threaded `--rejoin-deadline` through forward and rollback rolling upgrades. Rejoin confirmation now honors the rolling poll interval and checks the deadline before each poll. Named regressions cover the default, explicit short overrides, a bulk prime completing after a scaled 60s but before scaled 180s, and a stuck prime timing out at its configured short bound.
