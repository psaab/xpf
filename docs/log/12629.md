# 12629 — Quarantine misnested security-policy DENYs

## Why

Tolerant `SyncApply` compiled only the correctly named `policy` children at the `global`, keyed from-zone/to-zone, and nested from-zone/to-zone contexts. A misspelled policy/context keyword could therefore hide an authored `deny` without producing the #12217 warning or its synthetic `LenientContentDropped` carrier. Under a permit default, the incomplete policy snapshot could be installed.

## What

- Detect policy-shaped children skipped by the global and zone-pair compiler walks, without treating apply statements or ordinary non-policy children as dropped enforcement content.
- Record skipped children in #12217's existing unknown-policy diagnostic, warning, and synthetic-carrier path. The strict schema still rejects the misspelled context.
- Add `SyncApply` regressions for global `polcy`, keyed from-zone `polcy`, and keyed-context `to-zon`. Each checks the warning and dropped-policy locator and retains a strict-schema rejection control. A correctly named policy-context control verifies valid rules and Junos apply macros are not quarantined.

## Validation

- RED-before: the global `polcy`, keyed from-zone `polcy`, and keyed-context `to-zon` rows failed because the dropped deny had no `LenientContentDropped` locator. The nested-container `to-zon` row also pins its existing malformed-zone carrier path.
- `go test ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore -run 'TestInactive_UpgradeEquivalence|TestSyncApplyQuarantinesMisnestedSecurityPolicyDeny12629|TestSyncApplyKeepsCorrectSecurityPolicyContexts12629' -count=1` — PASS.
- `go build ./...` and `go vet ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore` — PASS.
- Full `go test ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore -count=1`: `pkg/configstore` passed in that run; `pkg/config` reported failures in `TestInventoryDropShapeMatchesTheLiveCensus8662`, `TestMultiLeafSelfRepeatIsRefused9027`, `TestLegitimatelyNamedObjectIsAccepted9029`, `TestElidedQuotedValueKeepsItsQuote8921`, `TestSystemConfigWebManagementEnhanced`, `TestSystemConfigSetSyntax`, `TestPackedRoutingInstanceSurvives8787`, and `TestSlotEscapeCoverage`, then hit the 10-minute timeout in `TestSchemaSpellingDifferentialGate`. The run also exposed an interaction with the legacy `inactive:` AST compatibility case; the detector now excludes that inactive residue and the focused compatibility test passes.
- A later standalone `go test ./pkg/configstore -count=1` also reached the 10-minute timeout while `TestStoreLoad_AcceptsEqualFlowAboveLegacyWorkerCap` was still waiting. No base attribution was established for the full-suite failures/timeouts.

## Risk

Misnested policy-shaped content is deliberately quarantined as an incomplete rulebase on tolerant ingestion. No live-cluster validation was performed.
