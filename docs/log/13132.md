# #13132 — require inactive scheduler counter witnesses (D10-15)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-14
- **Action**: `policy_scheduler_validate.py` accepted an allowed inactive-window
  ping even when the preceding and inactive snapshots were identical. The
  scheduled-rule counter being flat does not prove policy evaluation: an
  allowed packet must increment a later rule in the same zone pair. The
  validator also returned the first matching `rule_id`, silently ignoring
  duplicate rows that make the evidence ambiguous.
- **Fix**: parse `policy_rule_counters` into a unique rule-ID map and reject
  malformed entries or duplicate IDs. When inactive traffic is allowed,
  sum packet counters for other rules sharing the scheduled rule's
  `<from-zone>-><to-zone>/` prefix and require their total to increase by at
  least `min_inactive_packets` versus the rebuild snapshot. Full-deny
  witnesses do not require a fall-through increment.
- **Validation**:
  - RED on pristine `origin/master` (`55ab81ef0`): full
    `python3 test/incus/policy_scheduler_validate_test.py` ran 17 tests and
    failed exactly the two new regressions: no exception for a stale
    fall-through counter and no exception for a duplicate `rule_id`.
  - GREEN on the fix: the same command passed, 17/17.
  - `python3 -m py_compile test/incus/policy_scheduler_validate.py
    test/incus/policy_scheduler_validate_test.py` passed.
  - Live #1378 evidence command returned `PASS` with the scheduled rule flat
    at 5 packets and the inactive witness allowed (the captured fall-through
    counter advanced 94→95).
- **Risk**: validation-tooling only; no production dataplane behavior changed.
  Allowed inactive evidence without a matching fall-through counter delta now
  fails closed; denied evidence remains valid without a fall-through hit.
