# #15949 (H34-04 arm 2) — miss-path LocalDelivery late policer metering

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-22
- **Action**: The session-miss LocalDelivery path metered its ingress
  three-color policer only at the install site — after the input-filter
  PERMIT `FilterLog` (`poll_descriptor/mod.rs`, the `emit_input_filter_log_match`
  site fed by `evaluate_non_pbr_input_filter` with
  `InputPolicerMeter::DeferToTxSelection`), after the lo0 evaluation
  (`host_inbound_gated_lo0_action` / `filter_terminal`), and after the
  junos-host policy evaluation (`junos_host_local_policy`, which charges
  the admitting rule's hit counter on a permit). In the cacheable arm
  the closing-TCP pair eviction (`evict_owner_closing_tcp_pair_for_syn`,
  for an `owner_syn_reuse` SYN) also ran before the late meter, and a
  red drop `continue`d past the persistent-inbound hold rollback. A
  policer-dropped host-bound packet therefore left a false PERMIT audit
  record, lo0/junos-host counter increments, a prematurely evicted
  closing pair with no replacement install, and a skipped hold
  rollback — while the session-HIT path folds policer drops to Discard
  before logging. Fixed by metering once, early — with the Opus fold:
  the gate classifies on the packet's FINAL decision kind, not on any
  address set. A new `meter_miss_local_input_policers` gate in
  `poll_descriptor/mod.rs` captures the flow's cached policer handles
  and meters immediately after HA finalization (the first point where
  `decision.resolution.disposition` is final), before lo0, junos-host,
  eviction, and hold anchoring, only when
  `miss_input_policer_gate_covers(disposition)` — i.e. exactly
  `ForwardingDisposition::LocalDelivery`. The first attempt instead
  gated on `effective_resolution_target ∈ local_v4/local_v6` at the
  input-filter emit site, which missed LocalDelivery targets outside
  those sets (e.g. a NAT64/NPTv6-translated host target, or the WAN
  interface address excluded from `local_v4` by the interface-mode
  SNAT egress matrix) and double-metered a transit ForwardCandidate
  routed via a DNAT VIP that IS in `local_v4`. Because the disposition
  is not yet final at the emit site, the input-filter log emission now
  defers to the post-finalization gate point (non-Accept verdicts
  still emit and recycle at the original site, bit-identical). A red
  packet re-emits its matched log (if any) with a truthful DENY
  (Discard) action — mirroring the arm-1 session-hit RED fold —
  charges the existing local/policy-deny accounting, rolls back any
  persistent-inbound hold, and recycles without installing,
  delivering, evicting, or anchoring. A conforming packet's PERMIT log
  emits at the gate point exactly once, and the cacheable arm stores
  the gate's handles on the installed session without metering twice;
  the two late per-arm meters are removed. Transit packets are
  untouched (their policer meters exactly once in TX selection).
