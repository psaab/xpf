# #15949 (H34-04 arm 2) — miss-path LocalDelivery late policer metering

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-22
- **Action**: The session-miss LocalDelivery path metered its ingress
  three-color policer only at the install site — after the input-filter
  PERMIT `FilterLog` (`poll_descriptor/mod.rs`, the `emit_input_filter_log_match`
  site fed by `evaluate_non_pbr_input_filter` with
  `InputPolicerMeter::DeferToTxSelection`), after the lo0 evaluation
  (`host_inbound_gated_lo0_action` / `filter_terminal`), and after the
  junos-host policy evaluation (`junos_host_local_policy`, which charges
  the admitting rule's hit counter on a permit). In the cacheable arm
  the closing-TCP pair eviction (`evict_owner_closing_tcp_pair_for_syn`,
  for an `owner_syn_reuse` SYN) also ran before the late meter, and a
  red drop `continue`d past the persistent-inbound hold rollback. A
  policer-dropped host-bound packet therefore left a false PERMIT audit
  record, lo0/junos-host counter increments, a prematurely evicted
  closing pair with no replacement install, and a skipped hold
  rollback — while the session-HIT path folds policer drops to Discard
  before logging. Fixed by metering once, early: a new
  `meter_miss_local_input_policers` gate in `poll_descriptor/mod.rs`
  captures the flow's cached policer handles and meters the packet
  immediately after the input-filter verdict (before the PERMIT log
  emit, lo0, junos-host, eviction, and hold anchoring) for
  `LocalDelivery` dispositions only. A red packet re-emits its matched
  log (if any) with a truthful DENY (Discard) action — mirroring the
  arm-1 session-hit RED fold — charges the existing local/policy-deny
  accounting, rolls back any persistent-inbound hold, and recycles
  without installing, delivering, evicting, or anchoring. A conforming
  packet defers its PERMIT log past the gate (emitted at the same site,
  exactly once) and the cacheable arm stores the gate's handles on the
  installed session without metering twice; the two late per-arm meters
  are removed. Transit packets are untouched (their policer meters
  exactly once in TX selection); non-LocalDelivery dispositions emit
  their log at the original site unchanged.
