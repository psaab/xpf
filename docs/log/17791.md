# #17791 — remove last-group flow teardown aliases

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-28 (`fix/17791-campaign`)
- **Action**: Deleted the `flowFamily.cancel`/`wg` last-group aliases and the unreachable empty-groups teardown fallback; live-group teardown remains the sole cancellation and join owner.
- **File(s)**: `pkg/daemon/daemon_flowexport.go`

## Why

The family-level handles aliased only the last published exporter group, so they could not represent ownership of the full live set. Their empty-groups fallback was unreachable in normal teardown, duplicating lifecycle ownership and offering incomplete cancellation/join behavior if reached.

## Change

- Removed the family-level `cancel` and `wg` fields and their last-group assignments.
- Removed the empty-groups fallback. Teardown now retires, cancels, bounded-joins, and closes the deduplicated groups captured from the live bundle.

- Grep across `pkg/daemon`: no `ff.cancel`, `ff.wg`, `last.cancel`, or `last.wg` references remain.

## Risk

No wire or exporter configuration behavior changes. Normal teardown continues to use per-group lifecycle handles and the existing bounded join.
