# Issue #14117 — NAT64-to-WireGuard dispatch selects pre-translation underlay

## Why

`wg_transit_egress_physical_egress_ifindex` (`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/frame/wg.rs`)
returned `None` for every `decision.nat.nat64` decision, so
`resolve_forward_target_ifindex` (`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/forward_request.rs`)
fell back to the pre-translation resolution: `tx_ifindex` (an unrelated
default-route NIC when present) or the logical `wgN` ifindex (no XSK binding,
`NO_EGRESS_BINDING` drop). Meanwhile `build_nat64_forwarded_frame` builds the
translated inner frame and `wg_encap_frame` selects the WireGuard peer by the
**post-NAT64** destination and resolves that peer's real outer route. With a
peer reachable only on a specific physical route and no/mismatching default
route, dispatch and encapsulated bytes disagreed on the underlay — drops, or
a frame emitted on the wrong NIC carrying another segment's L2.

## What

- `wg.rs`: remove the blanket NAT64 exclusion. A forward NAT64 packet
  (IPv6 ingress) now selects its peer from `decision.nat.rewrite_dst` — the
  translated destination the encapsulator LPMs AllowedIPs on — through the
  same `wg_peer_outer_dst` → `outer_physical_egress_resolution` →
  `outer_egress_ifindex_or_fallback` chain as DNAT. The pre-translation frame
  destination is never used as an AllowedIPs key for NAT64; a missing
  `rewrite_dst` fails closed to the logical fallback instead of substituting
  an arbitrary first peer (`wg_peer_outer_dst`'s no-key fallback).
- The reverse direction (IPv4 ingress, v4→v6) keeps the logical fallback:
  its translated destination is the original IPv6 address carried only in
  `Nat64ReverseInfo`, which this helper does not receive, so guessing a peer
  from the pre-translation IPv4 destination would be a mis-selection.
  Fragmented forward packets carry the same `rewrite_dst` on their
  association decision, so they follow their first fragment's peer.
- NOT changed: GRE NAT64 dispatch (GRE has no per-peer AllowedIPs selection;
  its outer identity comes from the endpoint row), the encap builders, the
  fallback on genuine NoRoute (logical ifindex, fail-closed), and any
  non-NAT64 path (bit-identical).
- `frame/README.md`: corrected the stale "NAT64 is excluded" sentence.

## Risk

Forward NAT64→WG flows on multi-underlay WireGuard now dispatch to the
selected peer's NIC instead of dropping/mis-dispatching — the intended
behavior change; single-underlay deployments resolve to the same NIC as
before. Reverse NAT64→WG keeps the prior logical-fallback behavior
(fail-closed drop where no binding exists) because its translated
destination is not available at this seam; recorded here as a known
residual, not fixed by guessing.
