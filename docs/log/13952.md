# #13952 — bound SYN-cookie MAC attempts before validation

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-16
- **Action**: Add a bounded per-client-key pre-MAC attempt shaper to session-miss SYN-cookie ACK validation.
- **Base**: `origin/master` `8b4d69350`
- **Files**: `userspace-dp/src/screen/mod.rs`, `userspace-dp/src/screen/syn_rate.rs`, `userspace-dp/src/screen/syncookie.rs`, `userspace-dp/src/screen/zone.rs`, `userspace-dp/src/screen/tests.rs`, this log

## Why

#13700 moved the active and standby zone-wide invalid-ACK budgets after the SYN-cookie MAC check. An off-path sender could therefore enumerate the 24-bit MAC for one chosen `(src,dst,dport)` key without the previous bound and eventually seed the validated-client cache for a spoofed source. The existing post-MAC budgets remain useful for aggregate diagnostics/work policy but cannot bound MAC attempts.

## What

- Added a fixed-size, four-row count-min sketch of token buckets to each zone with SYN cookies enabled. Each row hashes `(src_ip,dst_ip,dst_port)` using the screen's existing per-boot seeded hash; the sketch is constructed with the zone profile and reconciled with cookie enable/disable changes. It is shared by active and standby validation, so role changes do not reset a key's allowance.
- Gate plausible-epoch ACKs through the sketch before `ring.validates()` / SipHash. The production per-key rate is 16 attempts/s, allowing at most 1040 token admissions in a 64-second cookie epoch (initial burst plus refill). The active path reports `ActiveBudgetExceeded`; standby continues to fail open with `NotApplicable`. Existing post-MAC aggregate counters are unchanged.
- Added active and standby regressions: exhaust the key's attempt budget, verify its known correct-MAC ACK cannot populate the whitelist, and verify a different client key still validates. Updated the invalid-ACK flood assertion to accept either `Invalid` or the new active budget verdict while continuing to assert that no client is whitelisted.

## Risk

- The sketch allocates 4 × 1024 token-bucket cells (about 64 KiB) per cookie-enabled zone per worker. A collision in ANY row can conservatively throttle an unrelated key: all four rows must have capacity for admission, so sharing one exhausted cell is enough to refuse a key. With one sprayed hot key, a different key has about a 4/1024 chance of such a collision (higher as more cells are driven hot); this is an explicit false-positive risk to the #13700 unrelated-key isolation guarantee. Collisions cannot let a key undercount its attempts. The 16/s policy is conservative and has not been calibrated against production handshake distributions; collisions or sustained per-key legitimate ACK volume can delay legitimate validation. The bound substantially reduces a chosen key's MAC search space during a 64-second epoch, but is a rate bound rather than a cryptographic increase to the 24-bit MAC strength.
