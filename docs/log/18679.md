# Issue #18679 — UDP continuity across RG moves

## Why

RG1 Group 1 in `test-failover` judged TCP session-miss counters, but had no datagram evidence to tell a continuing session from a rebuilt NAT mapping. The RG-move soak had the same gap.

## What

- Added a long-lived, sequence-numbered UDP sender and target echo on UDP/6201. The echo reports the source IP and port observed after translation.
- Added a shared classifier for `SURVIVED` sequence advancement, `RECREATED` sequence reset or translated-identity change, `LOST` post-move silence, and `VOID` unusable evidence. Sequence gaps and reordering remain visible as metrics.
- Both test-failover and the soak checkpoint the cumulative probe log immediately before each planned CLI, so pre-move replies cannot satisfy post-move continuity. Multi-RG soak cycles classify each CLI baseline and report the worst status.
- Added the UDP continuity verdict to test-failover RG1 Group 1 and to traffic-enabled RG-move-soak moves. The soak records status in its table, log, and JSON result; unavailable evidence is fail-closed.
- Added hermetic survived, recreated, and no-post-move-reply fixtures. An identity-comparison mutation proves the recreated fixture reds when the comparison is reverted; fake-cluster tests cover both mapping recreation and post-move silence.

## Validation

- `make test-ha-assurance-lib` — PASS; includes survived/recreated/no-reply fixtures, the identity-comparison fail-on-revert mutation, and the test-failover boundary/lifecycle pins.
- `make test-rg-move-soak-lib` — PASS; 102 tests, including healthy sequence continuity, translated-identity recreation, and a no-post-move-reply failure.

## Risk

This changes only test harnesses and the managed mouse-target echo service. The probe uses IPv4 UDP/6201 and one persistent client socket. Missing probe service or unusable evidence yields VOID rather than PASS; a translated identity change or a post-move window with no replies fails the continuity verdict.
