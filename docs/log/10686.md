# 10686 — Drop IPv4-mapped and compatible IPv6 at ingress

## Contract

Wire-ingress IPv6 packets whose source or destination is an IPv4-mapped address (`::ffff:0:0/96`) or a deprecated IPv4-compatible address (`::/96`) are dropped before flow parsing, session lookup/creation, policy, NAT, BPF forwarding, or logging. The check runs after successful native tunnel decapsulation, so the authenticated inner frame is checked. It excludes the unspecified (`::`) and loopback (`::1`) addresses and diagnostic injection. Undecapsulated UDP is exempt only when its destination is both a configured WireGuard listen port and an address this router owns (configured interface or NAT/DNAT-local); transit to any other destination remains subject to the ingress drop, even if it uses that port. IPv4 packets, ordinary IPv6, and all other existing packet paths retain their behavior.

## Implementation

The shared AF_XDP poll ingress gate validates the Ethernet/L3 framing and IPv6 version nibble before reading source/destination. It increments the per-binding `v4_mapped_ipv6_dropped` batch/live counter and recycles the descriptor without creating a session or reaching policy. Its Prometheus series is `xpf_userspace_v4_mapped_ipv6_dropped_total`; binding status also reports the counter. The WG control socket binds dual-stack `[::]:port` (`coordinator/wg_control/sock.rs::bind_dual_stack_v6`), but a port-only match at XDP cannot distinguish that local socket traffic from transit. Therefore the underlay exception also requires the destination to pass `ForwardingState::owns_configured_ip` (interface addresses plus local NAT/DNAT addresses); transit to a non-local destination on the same port remains covered by the drop gate. Successful data decapsulation is classified on the inner packet.

The security basis is RFC 4291 §2.5.5.2 for IPv4-mapped addresses and RFC 4038 §4.2 for the deprecated IPv4-compatible transition form. This is an ingress frame-level rule: the policy evaluator remains family-typed. The shared corpus therefore pins the v4-only deny plus permit-any policy-core verdict reached by mapped/compatible tuples, while the poll-path tests prove those frames are dropped before that verdict can affect traffic.

## Coverage

- Predicate boundaries cover mapped/compatible source or destination, native IPv6, multicast, `::`, `::1`, non-IPv6 family, malformed/truncated frames, and valid IPv6 framing.
- Actual poll-path cells cover mapped and compatible source/destination drops, host-bound destinations, v4 source-deny controls, and diagnostic injection exclusion.
- WireGuard control coverage preserves locally addressed listen-port underlay, rejects unrelated UDP, and proves transit to a non-local destination on the listen port is dropped at the real poll ingress; decoded packets take the inner-frame ingress gate.
- Go/Rust shared policy corpus pins the family-typed policy behavior and its five v4/mapped/compatible source/destination rows.
- Metrics and status tests cover binding-counter aggregation and output.
