# #19103: an expired transfer pre-activation kept the helper forwarding

Per-issue log (see `docs/log/README.md`).

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-16 23:30Z
  - **Action**: fix, branch `fix/19103-preactivation-expiry` on #19167's
    185c9ab306 (the pre-activation state #19167 fold 1 reworked). The issue
    has no comments. Both holes were re-read at 185c9ab306 (codex-d
    re-confirmed them in its #19167 review):
    - **Path 1, degraded watchdog.** `haWatchdogSnapshot` carried no
      pre-activation deadline, and the degraded merge copied `group.Active`
      from it (`manager_ha.go`, "Active is authoritative from the latest
      m.mu-published snapshot"). A snapshot published while a
      pre-activation was live kept saying `Active=true` for as long as
      `m.mu` stayed held, and a matching-active refresh even resurrects a
      lapsed helper lease (#14943).
    - **Path 2, status poll.** The HA backstop published only on a
      NON-EMPTY effective signature (`process_status.go:804`), so an
      expiry that emptied it was never published. That gate dates from
      147ffffb48 and its rationale is not recorded.
  - **Coordinator steer, path 2:** fix the predicate rather than add a
    timer, unless the throttles leave a stale Active past what the deadline
    allows. They do:
    - `prevActiveSig` is the cluster truth, not the effective view. So
      during a pre-activation the poll re-syncs on its 5 s throttle anyway,
      and after expiry the next sync could land up to about 5 s plus one
      tick past the deadline.
    - So, without adding a timer, an expiry the helper has not seen yet is
      exempt from the 5 s throttle (`preActivationExpiryOwedLocked`).
    - **Bound once `m.mu` is free:** the deadline, plus at most one poll
      tick (1 s), plus the poll's existing 2 s skip after an
      `UpdateRGActive`.
  - **Fix:**
    - Path 1: the snapshot carries `preActiveUntil` for every group whose
      `Active` comes only from a pre-activation, and the degraded merge
      sends that group inactive once the deadline has passed.
    - Path 2: the poll's HA backstop, extracted verbatim from `statusLoop`
      into `pollHABackstopLocked`, publishes on any signature difference
      (including to empty), with the owed-expiry throttle exemption above.
  - **Acceptance 3, the helper side: kept, and pinned by the existing Rust
    cells.**
    - An inactive refresh against a stored-active RG renews the stored lease
      only while it is still valid
      (`mismatch_renews_valid_stored_active_lease_9629`).
    - It never resurrects an expired one
      (`expired_stored_active_incoming_inactive_needs_lock_9629`).
    - That is #9629 MAJOR-4: without the renewal, every demotion blocked on
      `m.mu` becomes an unowned drop, and the helper cannot tell a lapsed
      pre-activation from such a demotion.
    - The Go cell `TestDegradedRefreshCannotResurrectALapsedPreActivation19103`
      composes the fix with the #9629 Rust-mirror oracle.
  - **Residual, documented in `pkg/dataplane/README.md`.** While `m.mu`
    stays held past the deadline, degraded receipts can keep a still-valid
    helper lease alive, one lease (at most about 11 s) per receipt. The
    stale window is bounded by the `m.mu` hold plus one lease, not by the
    deadline.
