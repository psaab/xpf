# Issue #15137 — BGP neighbor `import`/`export` policy named after a keyword compiles to no policy plus the keyword's flag (A88-01)

## Why

After #14409 (c927186fe3), `bgpNeighborBody14409`
(`pkg/config/compiler_bgp_neighbor_merge_9192.go`) ran every neighbor
body through `splitFusedMultiLeaves14409`, which cut a multi-value
`export`/`import` leaf at ANY bare, unbracketed neighbor-grammar
keyword from index 1 — on the claim that "the only Keys-carrying shape
is the fused one". That claim is false for a standalone statement: a
braced `neighbor 10.0.0.1 { import passive; }`, its packed spelling
`neighbor 10.0.0.1 import passive;`, and the flat-set spelling
`set ... neighbor 10.0.0.1 import passive` all deliver exactly
`[import passive]` to the reader (the packed form via the #8662 fold).
The splitter cut at `passive`, emitted a valueless `[passive]` flag
node, and the `import`/`export` case in
`applyBGPNeighborProps9192` then wiped the inherited group list
(#5277 most-specific-level-wins) and appended nothing. The neighbor
compiled with no policy in that direction plus the keyword's flag
(`Passive`, `DefaultOriginate`, `RemovePrivateAS`, `AllowASIn`, a
`Description`, ...). With `no bgp ebgp-requires-policy` (#14584)
emitted in every `router bgp` stanza, an absent route-map means
accept/advertise all — so an eBGP peer whose import or export policy
happened to be named `passive`, `default-originate`,
`remove-private`, `loops`, `description`, `peer-as`, ... was
UNFILTERED in that direction on a clean commit, while
`show configuration` still displayed the policy name.

Measured on `origin/master` (097661712e) before the fix: braced
`import passive` compiled to `Import=[]`, `Passive=true` (the group
import wiped); `import loops` to `Import=[]` with no `allowas-in`
policy; packed and flat-set shapes identical.

## What

- `pkg/config/compiler_bgp_neighbor_merge_9192.go`: a fused-multi cut
  is now taken only where the keyword is FOLLOWED BY ITS OWN GRAMMAR.
  New `fusedCutValid15137` partitions the tail from the candidate cut
  into complete neighbor statements via the schema (`consumeNodeKeys`
  arity, the compound `family inet|inet6 [unicast]` shape, further
  export/import values absorbed as values of that leaf); a cut
  additionally requires at least one authored value before it, so a
  valueless leaf is never manufactured. A lone keyword value fails
  the partition — `loops` still needs its count, `description` its
  text, `peer-as` its number, `family` its family — and flag keywords
  (`passive`, ...) standing alone are a single policy value, not a
  statement followed by its grammar. Genuinely fused runs
  (`export EP authentication-key K`, `export passive
  authentication-key K`, chains through `peer-as`) partition cleanly
  and split exactly as before. Bracketed/quoted guards unchanged.
- NOT changed: the #14409 packed-tail expansion itself, the #9192
  (group,address) merge, the #5277 own-export/import replacement
  semantics, group inheritance, and the FRR renderer — the render leg
  below pins that the now-surviving policies render as
  `route-map <name> in|out` with no flag lines.
- New regressions:
  `pkg/config/bgp_neighbor_keyword_policy_15137_test.go` — braced,
  packed and flat-set matrices over 14 keyword-named policies ×
  import/export, each asserting `Import/Export=[name]`, the inherited
  group list replaced (not wiped to empty), and no flag field set;
  controls pin the fused `export EP authentication-key K` split, a
  fused keyword-named first policy, bracketed and quoted
  keyword-named policies, and ordinary policies.
  `pkg/frr/bgp_keyword_policy_render_15137_test.go` — end-to-end
  compile→render: `import passive; export remove-private;` renders
  both route-maps under `no bgp ebgp-requires-policy` with no
  `passive`/`remove-private-AS` flag lines.

## Validation

- RED on `origin/master` (097661712e): the braced/packed/flat-set
  cells failed with `Import = [], want [passive]` plus
  `Passive = true`, and the fused keyword-policy control failed with
  `Export = []` — matching the splitter mechanism; verified in this
  worktree before the fix.
- GREEN after the fix: `go test ./pkg/config -run
  'TestBGPNeighborKeyword|TestBGPNeighborPacked' -count=1` passes
  (0.967s on the rebased tip), including every #14409
  authentication-key fixture (packed heads, following-sibling matrix,
  strict cell, group-key controls).
- Render leg and neighbours: `go test ./pkg/frr -run
  'TestBGPKeywordNamedPolicyRendersRouteMap15137|TestBGPNeighborMergeRendersOneDeclaration9192|TestEBGPRequiresPolicy' -count=1`
  passes; `gofmt -l` on the changed Go files is empty.
- The wider `TestBGP|TestFlatRun|TestCompactNormalize|TestCompactBlock`
  run shows `TestCompactBlockEquivalenceInventory2419` and the #8690
  scope cell failing identically WITH and WITHOUT this change
  (verified by stashing the fix): `security log report` /
  `protocols lldp interface xpfarg disable` census drift and stale
  #8690 classifications at the base tip — pre-existing, base-attributed,
  untouched here.

## Risk

Behaviour changes only for neighbor `import`/`export` leaves whose
value spells a neighbor keyword AND that are followed by tokens
forming complete statements — previously those values were cut into
a flag + a (usually valueless) statement; a standalone keyword-named
policy now compiles to the policy, which is the authored meaning.
Fused packed runs keep their #14409 split, so the
authentication-key rescue is unchanged. A deliberately ambiguous
packed run such as `export loops 2` (policy `loops` + count, vs. the
`loops 2` statement) still reads as the statement — it partitioned
before and partitions now; the braced/flat-set spellings, where the
policy reading is unambiguous, are the ones repaired.
