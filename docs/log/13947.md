# 13947 — Keep injected WireGuard frames out of RX recycle during ICMP reversal

## Boundary

A WireGuard plaintext descriptor is backed by a frame borrowed from the
binding's TX free pool. The flowless NAT64 and same-family embedded-ICMP
reversal arms can consume that descriptor by queuing a prebuilt forward before
the later flowless injected-record LocalDelivery fence runs. This change keeps
injected frames on the authoritative resolution path and rejects both queued
reversal arms rather than dispatching their TX-owned address as an RX frame.

## Change

Injected records bypass the flow-cache fast path, preventing a cached transit
decision from preempting the injected LocalDelivery fence. If either flowless
ICMP-error reversal arm queues a forward for an injected descriptor, the
request is removed, its ICMP-error budget charge is refunded, and the descriptor
is recycled through the existing injected-address rescue back into
`free_tx_frames`. Native RX reversal behavior is unchanged.
