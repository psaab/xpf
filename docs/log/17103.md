- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-27
- **Action**: Already-fixed disposition (STEP-0). The defect prescribed by
  #16536 — the #914 shared_exact per-flow cap computed its BDP floor from
  the class-wide `transmit_rate_bytes` over the worker-local prospective
  flow count, while #11768 partitioned `buffer_limit` by `active_shards`,
  letting one elephant hold 75-100% of a worker's admission budget — is
  not live on current `origin/master` (`5c6d990d09`). It already landed in
  PR #17103 itself (state MERGED, merged 2026-10-25T03:24:35Z, merge

  rate by `active_shards.max(1)` before `bdp_floor_bytes`, the legacy
  `cos_queue_flow_share_limit` (`admission.rs:202-208`) delegates with
  one shard and is bit-identical, and `tx/cos_classify.rs:2349-2385`
  hoists the shard count from the root lease and threads it into the
