# Issue #12839: Keep junos-host unit ownership on the configured ingress

## Why

`junosHostZoneByInterface` projected a unit zone reference onto its physical base interface, unlike the ingress identity used by the Junos-host coverage walk. This fabricated bare-parent ownership for a VLAN zone, leading to false #4146 `Unscopable` gaps on shared trunks.

## What

Narrowed unit-to-base fan-up: removed for ordinary physical/VLAN units (the #12839 defect), retained deliberately for tunnel units and VRF parents, whose snapshot/VRF-master contracts require the physical row (review B1). Direct unit binding and bare-reference fan-down preserved. The parent remains a conservative, non-own candidate for VLAN units through the existing `addRow` path. Updated zone-map parity coverage to encode this intentional difference from `InterfaceZoneMap`, and added regressions for multi-zone tagged trunks, unzoned unit-0 ingress, and explicit bare-interface ownership.

## Risk

The Junos-host deny projection and host-inbound exemption walk share this interface map. Ordinary unit references no longer assign a physical-base row to a zone; tunnel-unit and VRF-parent references retain it by design, and explicit bare references and unit-0 rows retain their own zone resolution. Userspace parity (TestJunosHostZoneNetdevsMatchSnapshot) and VRF scope (TestJunosHostIngressScopeCoverage6619) guard suites pass with the narrowing. Existing parent-candidate and cross-zone fail-closed semantics remain in place.
