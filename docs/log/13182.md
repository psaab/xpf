- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Issue**: #13182 (deep-review D6-08)
- **Why**: GRE local-origin session pairs are created for self-originated flows on the tunnel's logical interface. The reverse half already carried the tunnel VRF's installing-table identity, but the forward decision was hardcoded to `(0, 0)`. A forward session whose installing table disappears could therefore be treated as default-table state rather than fail closed during revalidation.
- **What**: `build_local_origin_tunnel_tx_request` now computes the tunnel route table and its `(domain, check)` identity once, stamps the forward `SessionDecision`, and reuses the table and identity for reverse synthesis. Added a VRF regression asserting both halves carry the identity and that removing the table cannot yield a `ForwardCandidate`. No WG behavior or general endpoint-pinned session logic changed.
