# 14009 — preserve routing-domain reserve for completed fragment anchors

The #13937 quota fix correctly excluded completed entries from sender quotas,
but also allowed completed anchors to permanently occupy every shard slot.
One tenant could fill all 64 slots with completed anchors, denying other
tenants new fragment keys until expiry and violating #10658's domain reserve.

Completed entries remain excluded from the sender's incomplete-entry quota to
avoid #13937's conformant-rate failure. They can use spare shard capacity
beyond their domain's 32-entry reserve; when the shard is full, oldest completed
anchors from over-reserve domains are reclaimed to admit a competing domain.
Incomplete anchors are never reclaimed, and ordinary replay detection remains
unchanged while the completed anchor is retained.

The regression fills a shard with 64 completed single-fragment entries in one
routing domain, then verifies a second domain can admit 32 entries while the
attacker is reduced to its 32-entry reserve. It confirms a victim overlap is
still rejected. RED on merged #13992: victim traffic was refused with
`ShardFull`. The full fragment-overlap test module covers the #13937 throughput,
#13680 replay, and #10658 incomplete fairness behavior.

Validation: the new regression failed against the merged #13992 behavior at
`victim reserve entry 1131 is admitted`, showing the second domain could not
obtain its reserve. With pressure reclamation enabled, the targeted regression
passed (1 test), and the complete `fragment_overlap::tests` module passed
(35 tests).

Closes #14009
