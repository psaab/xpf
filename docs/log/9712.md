- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11 ~12:40 UTC
  - **Action**: Chose option 1, keep the latch, and recorded why:
    - **It keeps both properties.** A node cannot tell locally whether its latch or its wall reading is right. The latch keeps local SYN-cookie service and replay protection without new coordination. _(Superseded in round 1: the latch does not stop a replay inside the latched window. What it keeps is non-resurrection.)_
    - **Following the clock back** re-opens replay, and when the step is itself the fault it still breaks cross-node validation. _(Qualified in round 3: only when the fault leaves the nodes' current epochs more than one apart.)_
    - **Failing closed** stops SYN-cookie service for the length of the step. _(Corrected in round 2: only while the wall epoch is more than one below the latch.)_
    - **A rollback generation** needs control-plane agreement between the nodes.
    - **The cost** is the cross-node gap, which opens only under a clock fault larger than one cookie epoch. HA already assumes clocks agree well within that. _(Round 1 added a second cost, the open latched window.)_
