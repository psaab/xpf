# 13937 — keep completed fragment anchors out of fairness quotas

Issue #13680 correctly retains completed fragment ranges until the receiver's
reassembly window expires, preventing a replayed same-ident overlap from
starting a fresh tracker entry. However, retaining those entries in the
sender/domain fairness counters lets a conformant sender hit its eight-entry
per-shard quota after only a few completed datagrams per shard, silently
refusing subsequent fragmented datagrams.

Completed entries remain in the overlap tracker and continue rejecting
same-ident overlaps, but are excluded from sender and routing-domain fairness
quota counts. Incomplete entries remain subject to both quotas, and all entries
(including completed anchors) remain charged to the fixed 64-entry shard
capacity, so storage remains bounded and full shards still fail closed.

The regression sends 600 complete two-fragment datagrams over 60 seconds at
10 datagrams/second through IPv4 and IPv6 tracker keys and asserts no shard-full
refusals. It passed after the fix. With the completed-entry quota exclusion
reverted, the test failed at fragment 79 (`head fragment 79 is admitted`),
reproducing the sender-quota refusal. The complete `fragment_overlap::tests`
module passed (34 tests), including retained-anchor overlap detection, TTL
expiry, incomplete-datagram admission and fairness/capacity cases.

Closes #13937
