  - **What remains after #18598.** 7-18k generation-mismatch drops per
    planned move on the GAINING node.
    - 97-99.9% are BEHIND: the stamp is older than the worker's validation.
    - The last one lands within about 1 ms of the slowest worker's adoption,
      which is 5-26 ms after the store.
    - For 100% of them, the flow-cache entry seeded under their own view is
      still present (non-destructive peek; 0 other-gen, 0 absent).
    - The adoption delay is the binding poll phase: tens of µs per packet
      (#18714). Adopting sooner would make the drops worse: the queued
      packets would be judged under the new view sooner.
  - **v4 against v3**:
    - `MATCHED_VIEW_SLOTS` 3 → 2 and `MATCHED_VIEW_GRACE_NS` 1 s → 200 ms,
      both derived from the probes (seat W: each view pins a deep-cloned
      `ForwardingState`).
    - The held pass's flow-cache lookups are compare-only
      (`FlowCache::held_compare_only`). A key match with other generations
      is the CURRENT view's entry, so it is reported as a miss and not
      evicted (seat W: one entry per key, and a lookup with other
      generations evicts).
    - New per-binding witnesses: `generation_held_hits`,
      `generation_held_misses`, `generation_mismatch_ahead` and
      `generation_mismatch_behind`. They are carried through the status
      reply, the Go struct and the wire fixture (regenerated).
    - The README section is rewritten: the operator semantic ("a commit
      applies to packets stamped after its view store"), liveness as
      presence in THIS worker's session table, and what a held hit writes
      and why that is safe.
  - **New cells**:
    - BEHIND without a held view, dropped;
    - a held hit on a session the current HA state demoted, dropped through
      the hit stage's egress-departed arm, with no slow path and no HA
      delta;
    - a held lookup never evicts the current entry;
    - the A2 comparand (`policy_revalidation_gen`) stays current across a
      held pass;
    - counter assertions on the existing cells.
