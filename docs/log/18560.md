- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-30 15:20 PDT
- **Action**: Measured the drop. During planned RG moves on the loss
  cluster (umbrella #18559), every helper publish dropped the packets in
  flight as `config_generation_mismatch` / `fib_generation_mismatch`. That
  covers the requester's pre-activation route republish and the FRR-driven
  routes-only `apply_snapshot` plus FIB bump after each ownership edge. Go

  the old generation for the whole round trip.

    window: fw1 +10180 config + 1 FIB on a per-RG failover, +10957 on a batch
    failover, with zero HA-inactive drops.
  - That accounts for the remaining 4-13 lost pings per direction.
