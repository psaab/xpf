# #13168 — exclude quarantined zone policies before slot expansion

- **Source**: deep-review finding D4-12, validated by three-way adversarial research.
- **Files**: `pkg/dataplane/userspace/policies.go`,
  `pkg/dataplane/userspace/policy_quarantine_slots_13168_test.go`.

## Why

`walkPolicyRuleSlots` expanded every raw zone-pair set before the snapshot
builder quarantined collision-losing and poisoned zones. Discarded policies
could therefore exceed the per-set or total expansion cap and reject the entire
snapshot, while policy-ID maps included rules that were never published.

## What

Make the shared slot walker skip zone-pair sets touching zones excluded by
`ZoneQuarantineExclusionsForConfig`, retaining their raw policy-set index so
surviving policies keep the IDs used by snapshots. Preserve reserved sentinel
handling in its existing poison paths. Add regressions for per-set overflow,
total expansion overflow, phantom ID-map entries, survivor ID stability, and
fail-closed overflow on a non-quarantined zone.

lookups. Their policy-set positions remain reserved, and non-quarantined
policies retain fail-closed limits and existing IDs.
