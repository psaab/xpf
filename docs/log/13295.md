# #13295 — expose event-stream resync and disconnect telemetry

## Why

`EventStream` increments `telemetryGapResyncs` when a telemetry sequence gap
forces a full resync, but `Status()` discarded it. Pending-callback queue
overflow and rejected peer credentials also closed/refused event-stream
connections without cumulative counters. Operators therefore could not see
resync storms, callback backpressure disconnects, or untrusted local peer
attempts in status or Prometheus.

## What

Added the missing resync and disconnect counters to `EventStreamStatus` and
copied the atomics in `EventStream.Status()`. Count both bounded callback-queue
overflow branches and privileged-peer verification rejection. Prometheus
exports actual resyncs, rate-limited resync suppression, callback-overflow
disconnects, and peer-credential rejections as separate counter families,
plus gauges for connection, pause, ACK batch depth, sequence, and acked
sequence.

## Validation

- Regression RED on pristine `origin/master` (`f59c739af`):
  `go test ./pkg/dataplane/userspace -run
  '^TestEventStreamStatusExportsResyncAndDisconnectCounters$' -count=1`
  failed to compile because `EventStreamStatus.TelemetryGapResyncs`,
  `PendingOverflowDisconnects`, and `PeerCredRejections` were absent (and
  `EventStream.peerCredRejections` did not exist).
- GREEN targeted regressions:
  `go test ./pkg/dataplane/userspace ./pkg/api -run
  'TestEventStreamStatusExportsResyncAndDisconnectCounters|TestEmitUserspaceEventStreamMetrics' -count=1`
  passed (both packages).
- Review-fold correction: peer-credential refusals and rate-limited resync
  suppression are separate counter families; neither is reported as a stream
  disconnect or an executed resync.
- `go test ./pkg/api -count=1` passed on the original implementation
  (127.064s) and after the semantics fold (114.397s); `go vet
  ./pkg/dataplane/userspace ./pkg/api` passed after the fold.
- Full `go test ./pkg/dataplane/userspace -count=1` did not pass. On the
  pristine base, a focused rerun independently reproduced the unrelated
  failures in `TestContractSnapshotGolden9821`,
  `TestJunosHostZoneNetdevsMatchSnapshot`,
  `TestCapFlagIsWiredEndToEnd9054`, and
  `TestSnapshotShapeIsPinnedToProtocolVersion8892`. The full run also had
  Unix-socket `bind: invalid argument` failures for long test-temp paths and
  additional fixture/source-contract failures; the targeted regressions for
  this change pass.

## Risk / residual

The counters are daemon-lifetime EventStream counters; existing helper restart
lifetime behavior is unchanged. No live event-stream peer or cluster was
exercised.
