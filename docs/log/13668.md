# #13668 — gate conntrack RMW operations through TupleGate

- **Base**: fc806aa (origin/master at implementation)
- **Branch**: fix/13668-campaign-pub

## Why
`restamp_bpf_conntrack_policy`, `restamp_bpf_conntrack_app_id`,
`refresh_bpf_conntrack_last_seen`, and `update_session_cluster_synced_origin`
did lookup→modify→`BPF_EXIST` writes without TupleGate admission. They could
clobber a live row held under a gate lease or write during a map-wide
ClearFence, unlike the gated publish/delete twins.

## What
- Added `with_conntrack_rmw` in `bpf_map/mod.rs` mirroring `with_publish`:
  decline on gate-busy/clear-fenced, holding admission across the synchronous
  RMW.
- Wrapped all four functions (refresh gated per walked tuple); `fd < 0`
  no-map early-true stays inside raw bodies so declines never fake success.
- Test seams model origin writes and count refresh writes.
- Added six regressions for lease/clear-fence declines and an unleased positive
  control in `bpf_map_tests.rs`.

## Validation
- RED on pre-fix code: two app-id regressions failed on the named lease and
  clear-fence assertions.
- GREEN focused: `cargo test --bins conntrack_rmw_` → 6 passed on current master.
- Full `cargo test --bins`: 8022 passed, 2 failed, 8 ignored. The failures were
  `afxdp::cos::queue_service::tests::sojourn::submit_local_publishes_committed_vtime_on_settle`
  and `afxdp::tests_9950::f035_parked_fragment_overlap_is_rechecked_on_retry_10659`;
  neither exercises conntrack RMW.
- RED-on-revert: bypassing gate admission made four decline tests fail; restored
  the gate implementation.

## Risk
Refresh skips a declined tuple for this slice; subsequent budgeted walks retry.
App-id and policy restamps return false so their existing retry/fail-closed
behavior remains intact. Origin transition updates silently skip; a later
transition/refresh can restamp the bit.
