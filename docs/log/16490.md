# #16490 — Journal legacy-scrub degraded flag had no /health or Prometheus consumer

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
- **Context**: The #15143 legacy-payload scrub latches
  `Journal.LegacyScrubDegraded` (polled via
  `Store.JournalLegacyScrubDegraded`) when a pre-#1896 journal segment
  cannot be rewritten or purged and may still carry cleartext api-auth
  credentials. Unlike its twin `JournalPermsDegraded` (#9898 F-113),
  nothing outside `pkg/configstore` read that flag: `/health` and
  Prometheus stayed silent, so monitoring could not learn that
  cleartext REST credentials survived in `.config.journal[.N]` and
  nobody would rotate them. The only signal was a per-retry
  `slog.Warn`.
- **Fix**: Wire `JournalLegacyScrubDegradedFn` beside
  `JournalPermsDegradedFn` through every layer: `pkg/api` `Config`
  field + `Server` field + `NewServer` copy, a non-fatal
  `journal_legacy_scrub_degraded` field in `healthHandler` (stays
  200/ok — appends continue, a confidentiality exposure on history,
  not a forwarding or durability outage), the
  `xpf_config_journal_legacy_scrub_degraded` 0/1 gauge (collector
  descriptor, descriptor-table row, collector deps, and pre-dataplane-gate
  emit so it is visible even when the dataplane is not loaded), and
  the daemon wiring `JournalLegacyScrubDegradedFn:
  d.store.JournalLegacyScrubDegraded` in `daemon_run_servers.go`.
  Nil fn omits the field and gauge, matching the twin's contract.
- **File(s)**: `pkg/api/server.go`, `pkg/api/health.go`,
  `pkg/api/metrics.go`, `pkg/api/metrics_descriptors_controlplane.go`,
  `pkg/api/metrics_descriptor_table.go`,
  `pkg/api/metrics_collector_deps.go`,
  `pkg/api/health_test.go`, `pkg/api/metrics_persist_degraded_test.go`,
  `pkg/api/README.md`, `pkg/daemon/daemon_run_servers.go`,
  `pkg/configstore/store_boundary_9898_test.go`,
  `pkg/configstore/README.md`, `docs/log/16490.md`,
  `debian/changelog`
- **Validation**: new `TestHealthHandler_ReportsJournalLegacyScrubDegraded`
  (true shows the field, false after a successful retry, both 200),
  `TestJournalLegacyScrubDegradedGauge` (1 degraded / 0 healthy with
  the dataplane unloaded), and
  `TestJournalLegacyScrubDegradedEndToEnd_16490` (fsatomic rename
  failure leaves the cleartext canary on disk and the Store getter
  true; restoring the seam retries, purges the canary, and clears
  the getter). Existing twin tests
  (`TestHealthHandler_ReportsJournalPermsDegraded`,
  `TestJournalPermsDegradedGauge`,
  `TestJournalPermsDegradedEndToEnd_9898`,
  `TestLoadScrubsLegacyJournal15143`) stay green (see PR for exact
  counts). Note: `TestCollectorDescriptorTableAgreement16202` fails
  identically on unmodified origin/master for two pre-existing
  unrelated fields (`bindingFlowCacheEvictionsArm`,
  `userspaceSessionGCExpired`); the new descriptor adds no new
  failure there.
- **Residual**: observability only — the scrub itself, its retry
  discipline, and the warn log are unchanged (#15143). A failed scrub
  remains rare (daemon runs as root; trigger is ENOSPC/EROFS/EIO and
  removal must also fail), and only pre-#1896 v1 segments are
  affected.
