# #17708 — enforce trigger-until ceiling at cooldown drain

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-28
  - **Action**: preserve the recorded cooldown crossing except when parked events have since pushed its live `trigger until` window over the ceiling.
- **File(s)**: `pkg/eventengine/engine.go`, `pkg/eventengine/evaluate.go`, `pkg/eventengine/engine_parked_window_until_16641_test.go`, `pkg/eventengine/engine_deferred_unmatched_15945_test.go`, `pkg/eventengine/README.md`, `debian/changelog`, `docs/log/17708.md`

## Why

A recorded cooldown edge was replayed solely from its park-time `withinMatches` verdict. Events on the parked direction continue filling the sliding window, but a sustained storm could push a `trigger until` window past its ceiling before drain; the unconditional recorded-verdict replay then caused an over-ceiling remediation, contrary to #16641's zero-over-ceiling acceptance. Re-evaluating all clauses at drain would regress #16848 by dropping a genuine short-window `trigger on` crossing after its window decayed.

## What

- Keep the recorded verdict and semantic-revision check, but also check only the current `trigger until` ceilings before replaying a recorded match. This suppresses a parked replay after the live window exceeds its ceiling without re-deciding or losing a decayed `trigger on` crossing.
- Keep synthetic edges without a recorded verdict on the existing full live-window re-check.
- Tighten the #16641 storm regression to count the drain replay: the 15-event storm over `trigger until 10` must leave only the initial commit; the existing sparse control still requires replay below the ceiling.
- Add a mixed short-window `trigger on`/long-window `trigger until` regression covering replay at the inclusive ceiling and suppression over it.
- Update event-engine behavior documentation and Debian changelog.

## Risk

The added check is limited to replaying recorded matches and does not change live event evaluation, trigger-on decay behavior, synthetic-edge handling, or revision/liveness validation. A parked edge is discarded if its live window exceeds any valid `trigger until` clause at drain; below the ceiling it retains the established replay behavior.
