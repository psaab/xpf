# #9637: host-inbound admission by destination address, not ingress zone

## Actions

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11T14:32Z
- **Action**: Live validation on the loss cluster, in two read-only lock
  cells. It was posted to the issue.
  - From `lan`, which admits ssh, TCP/22 to the `wan` address `172.16.80.8`
    and to the `sfmix` address `10.255.192.42` timed out, while ping answered.
  - The LAN VIP refused port 22, i.e. the SYN was admitted.
- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11T16:10Z
- **Action**: Refined the posted plan in two ways before implementing.
  - **Per view, not per zone.** The scope is per view (per zone and effective
    token set), because #3362 per-interface overrides split a zone into
    several views.
  - **Ingress rules first, destination rules kept.** The ingress-zone rules
    come first, and the destination-address rules stay unchanged as the judge
    for every netdev the scope leaves out. That makes an incomplete netdev
    enumeration cost coverage and never admission, which is the objection
    that deferred #3718 Option A.
  - **No coverage flag.** The plan's partial-coverage flag is unnecessary:
    each rule is scoped to its own view's netdevs.
