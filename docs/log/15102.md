# #15102 — WireGuard outer-fragment reassembly (A69-02)

## Defect
Worker-decapped WireGuard had no outer reassembly. A first fragment of a
WG transport record was parsed as UDP/WG, claimed by the worker, and
failed AEAD as a truncated record; non-first fragments (protocol 255)
failed the steered-port admission and went to the kernel, where the
socket path drops non-local inners as degraded transit. Any WG peer whose
IPv4 (or IPv6) outers were fragmented — DF clear with a smaller-MTU
underlay hop — lost every such record carrying transit traffic.

## Fix
- Shim (`userspace-xdp`): host-executed
  `gre_classify::wg_fragment_stays_on_helper` (UDP head or 255/UDP tail,
  fragmented, WG_RX armed). WG fragments skip the session/local/
  interface-NAT/degraded kernel-pass arms and fall through to XSK, like
  native-GRE fragments (#14645). Whole UDP and non-WG tails unchanged;
  a WG tail in degraded mode drops with its head instead of passing.
- Helper (`userspace-dp`): the bounded #14645 reassembler now keys and
  rebuilds by upper-layer protocol (GRE 47 or UDP 17): IPv6 Fragment
  next-header is patched to the reassembled protocol, the completed
  meta carries that protocol plus (for UDP) the head's ports, and
  GRE/WG datagrams sharing an outer identity can never splice.
  `stage_wg_decap` offers WG fragments to the shared per-worker pool
  only after listener admission: heads via the ordinary endpoint/zone/
  ownership gates on their UDP header, tails only against an already-
  buffered head — a stray tail creates no state and keeps its ordinary
  disposition. Completed datagrams decap exactly once through the
  ordinary `try_wg_decap` path (outer input filter included); buffered
  fragments recycle with no other adjudication. Reassembly refusals
  remain counted in `GRE_OUTER_FRAG_DROPS` /
  `xpf_userspace_gre_outer_frag_drops_total` (no new counter family).

## Tests
`tests_wg_outer_frag_15102`:
- IPv4: a real authenticated WG record split head+tail reassembles and
  decaps exactly once with a byte-identical inner (whole-decap control
  passes, head alone does not decap, stray tail first buffers nothing
  and creates no state, replayed tail does not decap again).
- Shim truth table: WG head/tail stay on the helper, whole UDP and
  GRE/TCP tails and the no-WG-configured case keep today's disposition.
- IPv6 pool level: head+tail rebuild an unfragmented UDP datagram
  (Fragment header removed, next-header patched to UDP, ports restored).
