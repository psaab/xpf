# 11004 — Directed-broadcast route/neighbor advisory

- **Issue**: #11004
- **Root cause**: `policymatch.routeDropClass` recognized multicast, limited broadcast, unspecified, and loopback destinations, but a subnet-directed broadcast is ordinary IPv4 unicast without its connected-prefix mask. The simulator therefore returned the policy result without explaining the later neighbor-resolution failure. Unlike the #4373 route-drop classes, policy is evaluated on the connected egress first.
- **Fix**: The simulator compares an IPv4 destination against the broadcast host of each statically configured interface-unit prefix, excluding `/31` and `/32`. The `directed-broadcast` advisory states that connected-egress policy is evaluated before neighbor resolution. Its initial reliance on the #10690 NOARP event gate was incomplete: #11033 established that a usable directed-broadcast neighbor can be learned and appear in a snapshot without operator static ARP. The follow-up rejects connected directed-broadcast neighbors at snapshot import, runtime lookup, and pending-neighbor retry, even when the entry is otherwise usable. A deny remains the policy result, and a permit is not forwarded; `family inet targeted-broadcast` (#4308) remains unimplemented. The address-based classifier cannot model a selected FIB table or a more-specific static route shadowing a connected prefix. Updated REST/protobuf comments and the CLI reference.
