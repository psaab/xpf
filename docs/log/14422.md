# Issue #14422 — Nested-group packed-leaf credential loses `fromGroups` provenance, survives `apply-groups-except H` (BGP/OSPF)

## Why

`#9862` nested-exclusion filtering works by provenance: `expandGroupsRecursive`
tags each group's cloned body with `fromGroups` before nested expansion, and
`mergeNodes` drops nodes whose every contributor is excluded
(`contribExcluded9862`). An untagged node falls back to checking only the OUTER
group name.

`packedBodyChildren` — the shared synthesizer for every packed-leaf body —
copies only `InheritedFrom`, never `fromGroups`. The `#7648` container-peer
path in `mergeNodes` re-tags its synthesized body (`ast_groups.go`, gated on
`haveExcept`), but the three `#9855` paths did not: the promote path
(`promoteLeafPeerForPackedGroup9855`) and both same-instance paths
(`sameInstanceContainerPeer9855`, with and without a leaf peer).

So when group `H` carries a credential as a PACKED leaf (e.g.
`neighbor 192.0.2.1 authentication-key H-SECRET;`), outer group `G` carries
`apply-groups H;` plus a braced/bare peer for the same instance, and the
destination carries `apply-groups-except H;`, the synthesized
`authentication-key H-SECRET` node arrives with `fromGroups == nil`, falls back
to `excluded[G]` (false), and is adopted — the excluded credential survives on
a strict commit reporting success. The braced spelling of the identical
credential keeps `fromGroups = [H]` and is correctly dropped. Packed and
braced spellings of one intent got opposite enforcement outcomes, silently
voiding key rotation/revocation by exclusion.

## What

- `pkg/config/ast_groups.go` (`mergeNodes`): tag the synthesized body with the
  source leaf's provenance before the recursive merge at all three `#9855`
  sites (promote: `body`; same-instance with peer: `cbody`; same-instance
  without peer: `cbody`), mirroring the `#7648` container-peer tagging and
  gated on `haveExcept` (`contrib := s.fromGroups`, falling back to
  `[]string{group}` when empty, via `addNodeContrib9862`). Tagging at the call
  sites (not inside one helper) also covers `policyTermBody12537` bodies and
  the promote unit-tail fallback (`groupPackedLeafUnmodelledTail`), which flow
  through the same two helpers.
- NOT changed: `packedBodyChildren` in `compact_tail.go`. The synthesizer is
  shared with post-expansion compiler readers, where exclusion filtering is
  already decided; the provenance tag belongs to the merge call sites, where
  the contributor (`s.fromGroups` / `group`) is known.
- New regression `pkg/config/dr15_h7_nested_except_provenance_test.go`:
  strict `CompileConfig` matrix — S (G holds braced neighbor) packed/braced ×
  no-except / except-H / except-G, P (G holds bare neighbor leaf) packed ×
  no-except / except-H, and the OSPF packed MD5 cell (`interface …
  authentication md5 7 key …` against a braced interface in G), asserting the
  compiled `AuthPassword` / `AuthKey`.

## Risk

- Behaviour delta: a nested-group packed credential excluded by a destination
  `apply-groups-except` is now actually dropped (previously silently kept).
  No-except merges, braced-credential exclusion, and the outer veto are
  unchanged (pinned by the control cells).
- Residual: the BGP cells do not fail on the current base, so their RED
  evidence at the audit SHA (`5b122dda`) could not be re-exercised here; the
  OSPF cell carries the RED/GREEN proof for the shared three-site fix.
