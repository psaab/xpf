# Issue #12164 — yield unready non-preempt incumbents to a healthier peer

## Why

The dataplane-arm monitor deliberately leaves the local RG at weight 1 so a node without a peer does not resign and lose its management/VIP path. But the non-preempt election also kept an incumbent primary at weight 1, even while its transit gate was closed and a peer at weight 255 could forward. The canonical HA example leaves `preempt` unset, so enabling preempt only in that example would not fix other non-preempt clusters.

## What

- In non-preempt mode, a primary with the reserved dataplane-arm debt now yields when a primary or secondary peer reports greater weight. Ordinary monitor debt and equal/lower-weight peers do not bypass non-preempt; the arm-debt floor remains 1, preserving standalone eligibility.
- No global preempt default or canonical config change was made. The exception is specific to the fail-closed dataplane-arm sentinel.
- Two-manager heartbeat coverage verifies the unready incumbent yields to the weight-255 peer, then stays secondary after recovery (non-preempt remains in force). Controls verify standalone primary retention and unchanged behavior for ordinary monitor debt.
- A bounded daemon fixture removes the final kernel-proven XDP link and verifies the transit knobs/barrier close, arm debt is installed, and standalone primary ownership remains.
