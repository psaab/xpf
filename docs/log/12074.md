# Issue #12074 — re-judge zone policy after FIB-only zone changes

## Why

A FIB-stale established hit already performs a fresh route lookup (#11373), but an established reverse hit previously refreshed only the reply row. Its forward companion could remain on the old route, and a fresh reverse policy stamp then let reply-only traffic continue under a now-denied forward zone pair.

## What

The forwarding-stale path compares the policy-relevant egress zone before and after route resolution. A zone change invalidates only a `LiveEgress` policy stamp, so same-zone moves retain the established verdict and NAT mapping while reverse hits check the forward companion's forwarding generation, re-resolve it, and judge only the forward zone pair. On Permit, the new route is persisted without stamping the forward policy verdict. If a FIB-stale `LiveEgress` route currently resolves to a non-forwarding disposition, the reply path Declines without revoking the session or stamping either row; the reply may still use its valid reverse route. `FabricRedirect` and non-Live fail-closed behavior is unchanged. Invalidation preserves fresh `Unvalidated`/`RecordedEgress` fences rather than laundering them into never-validated state.

## Validation

- **F1 RED-before:** The real-descriptor NoRoute/Discard reply cases before the fix observed `(revoked, tx, policy_deny, rows) = (1, 0, 0, 0)`. The archived R3 probe on parent `83b5c9553` recorded `(revoked, tx, rows) = (0, 1, 2)` and, after route restoration, a forward hit with `tx=1` and `rows=2`.
- **GREEN:** With `RUST_TEST_THREADS=1`, `fib_only_` passed 8/8 (including the pre-existing `server::tests::apply_snapshot_fib_only_advance_admitted_5169`). New NoRoute cold/warm and Discard reply cases preserve `(revoked, tx, policy_deny, rows) = (0, 1, 0, 2)` and recover after route restoration with `(hit, miss, tx, rows) = (1, 0, 1, 2)`.
- The fresh-non-Live invalidation regression passed 1/1 after its RED-before failure; the alias regression passed 1/1. The NoLocalEntry NoRoute regression passed 1/1, and forcing its `forward_route_stale` check to `false` made it fail.
- The full `tests_policy_revocation_8356` module reports 64 passed / 47 failed on head (111 tests) versus 56 passed / 47 failed on parent (103 tests); all 47 failure names match. The 22 broader targeted filters report 749 passed / 70 failed / 2 ignored on head and 739 passed / 70 failed / 2 ignored on parent; failure-name sets match.
- No live-cluster FIB or HA mutation was performed.

## Risk

FIB-stale `LiveEgress` replies whose current forward route is NoRoute, Discard, LocalDelivery, HAInactive, or otherwise non-forwarding Decline without revoking the connection or changing its policy stamps. The route remains stale, so reply-only packets may repeat the route lookup until the route recovers or a forward hit refreshes it; the established session and NAT mapping remain available for recovery. Cross-zone Permit still persists the new route without stamping policy; same-zone changes keep the existing verdict, and existing `FabricRedirect`/non-Live revocation behavior is retained. No live-cluster validation was run.
