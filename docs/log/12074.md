# Issue #12074 — re-judge zone policy after FIB-only zone changes

## Why

A FIB-stale established hit already performs a fresh route lookup (#11373), but an established reverse hit previously refreshed only the reply row. Its forward companion could remain on the old route, and a fresh reverse policy stamp then let reply-only traffic continue under a now-denied forward zone pair.

## What

The forwarding-stale path compares the policy-relevant egress zone before and after route resolution. A zone change invalidates that session's policy stamp, so the existing hit path re-derives the new zone pair; same-zone moves retain the established policy verdict and NAT mapping. Reverse hits also check the forward companion's forwarding generation, resolve its current route even for `LiveEgress` provenance, and judge only the forward zone pair. On permit, the fresh route is persisted without stamping the forward policy verdict. The policy-revalidation header and FIB alarm describe lazy next-hit route refresh and conditional cross-zone re-judgment.
