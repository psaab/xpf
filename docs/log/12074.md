# Issue #12074 — re-judge zone policy after FIB-only zone changes

## Why

A FIB-stale established hit already performs a fresh route lookup (#11373), but an established reverse hit previously refreshed only the reply row. Its forward companion could remain on the old route, and a fresh reverse policy stamp then let reply-only traffic continue under a now-denied forward zone pair.

## What

The forwarding-stale path compares the policy-relevant egress zone before and after route resolution. A zone change invalidates only a `LiveEgress` policy stamp, so same-zone moves retain the established verdict and NAT mapping while reverse hits check the forward companion's forwarding generation, re-resolve it, and judge only the forward zone pair. On Permit, the new route is persisted without stamping the forward policy verdict. If a FIB-stale `LiveEgress` route currently resolves to a non-forwarding disposition, the reply path judges the stored forward pair without persisting the route or stamping either row: Deny/Reject revokes the pair, while Permit keeps it. Decline and type-dependent ICMP still honor the reverse row's own #10507 fail-closed fence. The NoLocalEntry arm also preserves the parent's stored-pair verdict for non-Live companions; the Stale arm retains its #10507 non-Live fence. `FabricRedirect` behavior is unchanged. Invalidation preserves fresh `Unvalidated`/`RecordedEgress` fences rather than laundering them into never-validated state.

## Risk

FIB-stale `LiveEgress` replies whose current forward route is NoRoute, Discard, LocalDelivery, HAInactive, or otherwise non-forwarding judge the stored forward pair. Deny/Reject revokes on the reply; Permit retains the connection and NAT without persisting a route or stamping either row. Decline retains it only when the reverse row is not fenced; type-dependent ICMP likewise revokes when the reverse row's #10507 fence applies. Reply-only permit streams may repeat route resolution until the route recovers or a forward hit refreshes the row. NoLocalEntry non-Live companions use the parent's stored-pair verdict, while the Stale arm's #10507 non-Live fence remains fail-closed. If the forward packet arrives first and persists NoRoute before policy turns to Deny, the reply can coast until route restoration; "revokes on the reply" holds only when the reply arrives first. No live-cluster validation was run.
