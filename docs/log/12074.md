# Issue #12074 — re-judge zone policy after FIB-only zone changes

## Why

A FIB-stale established hit already performs a fresh route lookup (#11373), but an established reverse hit previously refreshed only the reply row. Its forward companion could remain on the old route, and a fresh reverse policy stamp then let reply-only traffic continue under a now-denied forward zone pair.

## What

The forwarding-stale path compares the policy-relevant egress zone before and after route resolution. A zone change invalidates only a `LiveEgress` policy stamp, so same-zone moves retain the established verdict and NAT mapping while reverse hits check the forward companion's forwarding generation, re-resolve it, and judge only the forward zone pair. On Permit, the new route is persisted without stamping the forward policy verdict. If a FIB-stale `LiveEgress` route currently resolves to a non-forwarding disposition, the reply path judges the stored forward pair without persisting the route or stamping either row: Deny/Reject revokes the pair, while Permit/Decline keep it. The NoLocalEntry arm also preserves the parent's stored-pair verdict for non-Live companions; the Stale arm retains its #10507 non-Live fence. `FabricRedirect` behavior is unchanged. Invalidation preserves fresh `Unvalidated`/`RecordedEgress` fences rather than laundering them into never-validated state.

## Validation

- **F1 RED-before:** The real-descriptor NoRoute/Discard reply cases before the R3 fix observed `(revoked, tx, policy_deny, rows) = (1, 0, 0, 0)`. The archived R5 probe on parent `83b5c9553` recorded the permitted reply cases as `(revoked, tx, rows) = (0, 1, 2)` and, after route restoration, a forward hit with `tx=1` and `rows=2`.
- **GREEN:** With `RUST_TEST_THREADS=1`, `fib_only_` passed 12/12, including the pre-existing `server::tests::apply_snapshot_fib_only_advance_admitted_5169`. NoRoute/Discard permit replies retain `(revoked, tx, policy_deny, rows) = (0, 1, 0, 2)` and recover after route restoration; Deny+NoRoute/Discard reply-first cells revoke immediately with `(1, 0, 0, 0)`, and a permit followed by Deny on route restoration still revokes.
- The fresh-non-Live invalidation and alias regressions pass. NoLocalEntry parent-parity tests pass for `None`, `Unvalidated`, and `RecordedEgress` permit pairs; the LiveEgress stored-pair Deny revokes. A RecordedEgress NoRoute descriptor reply revokes `(1, 0, 0, 0)`; the MJ widening mutant makes that assertion fail, so the cell kills the mutant. The archived MI mutant declined non-Live NoLocalEntry pairs with zero failures across 822 tests, so the NoLocalEntry arm restores the parent's stored-pair verdict while the Stale-arm non-Live fence remains.
- The full `tests_policy_revocation_8356` module reports 69 passed / 47 failed on head (116 tests) versus 56 passed / 47 failed on parent (103 tests); all 47 failure names match. The exact 22-filter set reports 754 passed / 70 failed / 2 ignored on head and 739 passed / 70 failed / 2 ignored on parent; all 70 failure names match.
- No live-cluster FIB or HA mutation was performed.

## Risk

FIB-stale `LiveEgress` replies whose current forward route is NoRoute, Discard, LocalDelivery, HAInactive, or otherwise non-forwarding judge the stored forward pair. Deny/Reject revokes on the reply; Permit/Decline retain the connection and NAT without persisting a route or stamping either row. Reply-only permit streams may repeat route resolution until the route recovers or a forward hit refreshes the row. NoLocalEntry non-Live companions use the parent's stored-pair verdict, while the Stale arm's #10507 non-Live fence remains fail-closed and is pinned by a RecordedEgress descriptor test. No live-cluster validation was run.
