# #19168: test-failover keeps the evidence of a per-stream zero FAIL

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-17 (G5)
- **Action**: Verified. The per-stream json-stream log lives on the LAN host
  at a fixed path (`/tmp/iperf3-failover.log`), and the next H1 run
  overwrites it. The orchestrator kept only a `$TMPDIR` copy named by pid,
  plus the aggregate counters. The producer counted outside-window zero
  intervals without listing them, so a FAIL on
  `stream_zero_intervals_outside_events_total` could not be attributed.
- **Action**: Producer (`scripts/iperf-json-metrics.py`): every interval it
  counts outside the windows is also listed in
  `stream_zero_intervals_outside_events`: stream id, start and end seconds,
  and `wall_start_utc`, the iperf3 client's start timestamp plus the
  interval offset (empty when the log carries no stamp). The list is
  `[]` when nothing is counted.
- **Action**: `test/incus/test-failover.sh`:
  - `failover_archive_evidence` runs right after the capture/gate block,
    PASS or FAIL, before any verdict. It copies the json-stream log, the
    metrics, and the producer and gate stderr into `FAILOVER_ARTIFACT_DIR`
    (default `$TMPDIR/failover-evidence/<UTC>-<pid>`), writes
    `outside-zero-intervals.tsv`, and prints the dir.
  - The outside-window verdict line, when non-zero, ends with
    `[stream <id> at <UTC> (+<start>-<end> s); …; evidence <dir>]`, at
    most 8 named.
  - Documented in `docs/userspace-ha-validation.md` and the script's
    settings comment.
- **Action**: New hermetic selftest `test/incus/failover-evidence-selftest.sh`
  (`make test-failover-evidence-lib`, and a `run-selftests.sh` leg). A
  fixture json-stream (2 streams, 7 intervals, a crash window [1,4]; stream
  5 zero inside it, stream 7 zero at 5-6 s outside it) runs through the
  real producer and the functions extracted from test-failover.sh. Cells:
  - the list holds only stream 7, with `2026-11-01T13:34:00.762Z`;
  - the archive holds the log, the metrics and the TSV row;
  - the detail names stream 7 and its time;
  - the archive call sits after the capture/gate block and before the
    gate-success guard, and the verdict line carries the detail.
  Mutants KILLED: no list, the archive skipping the log, the archive call
  removed.
- **Action**: `test/incus/iperf-throughput-selftest.sh` pins the no-event
  output as an exact snapshot. The new list is checked on its own: one row
  per counted interval (16), the expected keys, no wall clock in that json
  fixture. Everything else still equals the snapshot byte for byte.
