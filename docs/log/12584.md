# #12584 — go1.24.9 TempDir socket coverage

Base: #12565 left four related userspace fixtures vulnerable to go1.24's
uncapped `t.TempDir()` names. The #10702 helper and the #7468 loop skipped
on `net.Listen` errors under `TMPDIR=/tmp`, hiding both #10702 cells and
one of the three #7468 classification cells; the #9520 loop skipped its
`prefix_not_at_the_start` cell the same way under `TMPDIR=/var/tmp`; and
the #6558 fixture had a 107-byte path and discarded `EventStream.Start`
errors. Use the existing `shortSockDir9322` for these sockets, fail on bind
errors, and check stream startup.

The requested `GOTOOLCHAIN=go1.24.9 TMPDIR=/tmp go test ./... -count=1`
sweep also exposed `pkg/nfqueue`'s reconnect test binding a long-name
`TempDir` socket. Give that test a short `MkdirTemp` root.

#5483/#6130, #6132 and #6160 event-stream tests and the #12072
`servePolicyList12072` helper, with worst cases of 104-107 bytes under
`/tmp`. At `a8cbcf3cd`, `TMPDIR=/var/tmp` failed six leaf cells with
`invalid argument`; the event-stream tests discarded `Start` errors, so
they reported only the dial failure. All seven event-stream sockets and
the helper's socket now use `shortSockDir9322` (at most 41 bytes under
`/var/tmp`), and the event-stream tests fail on `Start` errors.

Files: eight userspace test files and `pkg/nfqueue/reinject_announce_9506_test.go`.

#7468 cell skipped on `bind: invalid argument`; #9520 skipped under
`TMPDIR=/var/tmp`. Reverting #10702's short-path change kills both cells

`TestStalledSyslogDoesNotParkEventReader11697` p99 < 50ms latency check,

`/var/tmp` runs on `a8cbcf3cd`. `pkg/nfqueue`'s #9506 tests pass under

full `./...` sweep found the #9506 socket failure fixed here, and also
