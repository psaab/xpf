- **Issue:** `#! [allow(dead_code)]` in `wg/mod.rs` (hiding 17 dead items in the current probe: 19 warnings across `wg/*`), `#[allow(dead_code)] mod xsk_ffi` in `main.rs` (16 items in the issue probe; 15 at current master), `event_stream/producer.rs` (named helpers whose `#[cfg(test)]` replacements were conflated with live status paths), `gre_discriminator.rs` (1), and an unused suppression in `nat/idle_lease_sync_8121.rs` (0) contained stale rationales and doc pointers naming functions not used in production (e.g., `try_wg_decap_from_frame` and `WgEngine::timer_pass`).
