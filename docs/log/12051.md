# #12051 — suppress unreachable policy TCP rejects on stamped fabric ingress

## Why

A policy-rejected TCP flow arriving from a fabric punt carries the synthetic zone-stamp MAC as its Ethernet source. The reflected TCP RST therefore targets that stamp and is queued on the fabric binding instead of reaching the client, while the policy counter and event claim an active reject was sent.

## What

The policy-reject path now suppresses only TCP RST synthesis when the ingress is a known fabric link and the Ethernet source carries the V1 zone-stamp prefix `02:bf:72:fe`. The existing enqueue outcome then leaves `policy_reject_sent` at zero and emits the truthful DENY event. Native TCP rejects, non-TCP reject legs, filter rejects, and session-miss resets retain their previous behavior.
