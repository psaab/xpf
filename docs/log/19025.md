- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-09 03:30 UTC
  - **Action**: Rebased onto the #18748 fold (f327be14d0) and implemented
    option A. `SharedCoSExactBacklog` slots carry a `nonexact_backlogged`
    flag (48 of 64 bytes), published by the owning binding on an
    empty-to-backlogged non-exact enqueue and at every activity refresh,
    stored only on a change, cleared with the slot on teardown.
    `nonexact_surplus_budget_under_exact_demand` meters each binding's
    surplus with its own bucket at `residual / n` (n = bindings with the
    flag set, burst `residual_burst / n` but at least one frame); the
    interface-wide first-come residual bucket and its #7206
    reset/refill-generation guard (and that guard's five cells) are
    removed, and both settle paths debit the binding's own bucket.
    `drain_shaped_tx` first tries `build_owed_nonexact_surplus_batch`:
    when this binding has exact demand of its own, non-exact backlog and an
    owed part covering a frame, a non-exact-only surplus batch bounded by
    that part goes before the local exact guarantee
    (`select_cos_surplus_batch_filtered`'s vestigial `allow_nonexact`
    parameter, always true in production, became `nonexact_only`). Cheap
    exits: no exact class on the interface, or fewer than two non-empty
    queues. Cells rewritten on `cos_drain_sim.rs`: R1, R2/LX, the exact-loss
    bound on a TX-bound worker (`<= residual / n`), division among
    backlogged bindings only, and the zero-residual case (owes nothing,
    strands no root). The two settle-debit cells now assert the
    per-binding debit. Docs: CoS README (new per-binding shares bullet,
    shared residual bucket sentence removed), fairness-regimes (#19025
    contract and the semantics change: guarantee-before-surplus relaxed by
    at most `residual / n` on a TX-bound worker).
