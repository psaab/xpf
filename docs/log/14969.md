# #14969 - fence abandoned host-auth closeout goroutines after post-promotion abort

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-18
- **Action**: Generation fence at the syscall boundary. `hostAuthGeneration14969` is advanced by every closeout invocation (`runHostAuthCloseoutOwners`) and every normal apply tail (`applyTailReconciles`); the runner runs each owner under its launch generation (`runWithHostAuthGeneration14969`, published per-goroutine in `hostauth_fence_14969.go`), and the host-mutation seams — `runCommandStdinTimeout` (chpasswd/useradd/usermod/id/chown/visudo/systemctl) and the seven owner entries (`applyLo0Filter`, `applyHostInboundFilterWithOverlay`, `applySystemLogin`, `reconcileSudoers`, `applySSHConfig`, `applyRootAuth`; `reconcileAbsentLoginUsers` via its deprovision exec seams) — refuse with `errHostAuthStaleOwner14969` once a newer authority has begun, so an abandoned owner abstains instead of installing stale `xpf_lo0`/`xpf_hostinbound` tables or re-granting revoked sudoers/login/root/SSH state. A stale seam that passed before the newer authority began is ordered before it and overwritten by it. No commit is ever gated (the #12169 contract — a new commit returns while an old revocation is blocked — and its applySem settlement cannot deadlock); `#12169`'s in-reconciler credential fences are unchanged. A gate/wait design was prototyped first and reverted: it deadlocked the suite-verified #12169 late-revocation interleavings, whose commits must proceed concurrently with a blocked stale owner.
- **Validation**: New `hostauth_fence_14969_test.go` — (a) a wedged owner abandoned by the real runner lands its exec-seam mutation while still current (control) and is refused with `errHostAuthStaleOwner14969` after a newer authority advances (stale leg); (b) the real `applyLo0Filter`/`applyHostInboundFilter` owner entries abstain under a superseded captured generation and are not refused under the current one; (c) uncaptured goroutines (direct reconciler tests, the normal tail) are never refused. RED on pristine master (fence symbols absent; behaviorally the stale mutation lands), GREEN post-fix. Adjacent suites: closeout (#5874/#6184), post-promotion cancel, serialize, and the #12169 first-commit fence tests — see PR for results; one pre-existing failure (`TestFirstCommitRollbackRetiresOwnedHostAuth12169`, marker survival) is base-attributed on current master in this sandbox (fails identically without the change).
- **Risk**: A superseded owner that was mid-reconcile abstains at its next seam; its remaining revocations are left to the newer authority's full tail, which reconciles the live config — the fail-closed direction. Goroutine-id parsing failure disarms the fence for that goroutine (historical behavior), never a new corruption. Sibling issue #15219 (A32-05) describes the same abandonment mechanism and is closed by this fence.
