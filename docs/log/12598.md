# #12598 — LoadMerge deactivated the wrong routing-policy term

## Why

The LoadMerge-only `FormatSetForLoadMerge` renderer emitted a bare
`deactivate ... term` for an inactive nameless policy term. Flat replay
prefix-matched that one-key path against the first sibling term, so an earlier
named term could be deactivated while the nameless term stayed active.

The literal LoadMerge preflight also treated inactive nameless terms as
malformed, unlike the inactive-pruned compilation/LoadOverride behavior. That
prevented preserving inactive configuration through merge.

## What

- In the private LoadMerge renderer, extend an inactive nameless policy term's
  deactivate path with each flattened body statement's first key. Leave public
  `FormatSet` and non-policy `term` formatting unchanged.
- Skip inactive policy terms in `ValidatePolicyNamelessTermsAST12521`, while
  retaining the #12521 rejection for active nameless terms.
- Reject, before replay, any inactive nameless term that would alias a named
  sibling in the input or existing candidate; an empty term with siblings is
  also ambiguous. The failed merge leaves the candidate unchanged.
- Add renderer replay and Store regressions for sibling order, multi-statement
  bodies, source/candidate collisions, empty-term ambiguity, and active-term
  rejection.
- Clarify the #12521 operator documentation.

## Validation

- RED before the fix: the renderer produced the ambiguous one-key deactivate;
  replay marked the preceding named `term ok` inactive and left the nameless
  term active. Collision tests also showed LoadMerge accepted source and
  candidate `term then` aliases and an empty term with a named sibling.
- `go test ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore -run '12598|12521' -count=1` — PASS
  after the collision guard.
- `go test ./pkg/configstore -count=1` — PASS.
- Full config/configstore suite run before the collision fold: configstore PASS;
  config timed out after 10 minutes with unrelated API-auth-expiry,
  inventory/schema-census, routing-instance, and slot-escape failures.
- `go vet ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore` — PASS on the final review fold.

## Risk

The private LoadMerge renderer changes only for inactive one-key policy terms.
Ambiguous merges (generated identity colliding with a named input/candidate
term, or an empty term with siblings) now fail atomically. Public display-set
formatting and non-policy terms are unchanged. No live routing daemon was
exercised.
