# #12598 — LoadMerge deactivated the wrong routing-policy term

## Why

The LoadMerge-only `FormatSetForLoadMerge` renderer emitted a bare
`deactivate ... term` for an inactive nameless policy term. Flat replay
prefix-matched that one-key path against the first sibling term, so an earlier
named term could be deactivated while the nameless term stayed active.

The literal LoadMerge preflight also treated inactive nameless terms as
malformed, unlike the inactive-pruned compilation/LoadOverride behavior. That
prevented preserving inactive configuration through merge.

## What

- For an inactive one-key policy term, merge rendering includes each immediate
  body statement's first key in its deactivate path. Flat replay turns those
  statements into sibling terms, so the path targets the right flattened term
  rather than prefix-matching a named sibling. Public `FormatSet` is unchanged.
- Skip inactive policy terms in `ValidatePolicyNamelessTermsAST12521`, while
  retaining the #12521 rejection for active nameless terms.
- Add renderer replay and Store LoadMerge regressions for inactive-before and
  inactive-after named siblings, a multi-statement body, and an active-nameless
  rejection control.
- Clarify the #12521 operator documentation.

## Validation

- RED before the fix: the renderer produced the ambiguous one-key deactivate;
  replay marked the preceding named `term ok` inactive and left the nameless
  term active.
- `go test ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore -run '12598|12521' -count=1` — PASS.
- `go test ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore -count=1` — `pkg/configstore` PASS;
  `pkg/config` timed out after 10 minutes with unrelated API-auth-expiry,
  inventory/schema-census, routing-instance, and slot-escape failures.
- `go vet ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore` — PASS.

## Risk

The behavior change is limited to the private LoadMerge renderer for inactive
one-key policy terms. Public display-set formatting and other inactive node
paths are unchanged. No live routing daemon was exercised.
