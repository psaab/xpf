# Issue #16220 — PR #16217 flow cache retains nothing

## Finding

On PR #16217 head `cac6f1fd95`, a no-failover P4 run on fw0 recorded
0 flow-cache hits / 6,963,665 misses / 6,953,766 evictions in 30 s, vs
clean master `2c8575b70e` at 35,472,287 hits / 13 misses / 1 eviction.
Session hits worked (6,953,782), so entries were inserted and then
evicted before their next packet — a cache-retention failure, not a
session failure. The rate cost was 3.6 Gbps vs 13.4 Gbps, then receiver
0.00 for the final ~10 s.

The originally suspected `neighbor_generation` stamp is falsified as
the direct mechanism: it is not part of `FlowCacheStamp` /
`FlowCacheLookup` (only `config_generation` + `fib_generation` + RG
epoch/lease). It only gates session `forwarding_resolution_is_stale`.

## What this change does

The aggregate `evictions` counter could not name the arm. This change
adds a per-arm breakdown (`FlowCacheEvictionArm`, 11 arms) counted at
every eviction site in `FlowCache::lookup_with_observed_bytes`, the
scheduler-phase flush, LRU collision insert, and the four post-hit
reclassifications in `stage_flow_cache_hit` (neighbor-MAC stale, HA /
decision invalid, deny superseded, backing session dead). The counts
fold into `BindingLiveState` at the debug tick and are exported as:

- CLI: `Flow cache evictions by arm: gen=… rg_epoch=… lease=…
  deny_expiry=… scheduler=… phase_flush=… collision=…
  neighbor_mac=… decision_invalid=… deny_superseded=… session_dead=…
  enforce_changed=… owner_active_redirect=… fabric_local_wins=…
  fabric_ingress_local_wins=… fabric_redirect_on_fabric=…
  fabric_local_same_owner=…`
- Prometheus: `xpf_userspace_binding_flow_cache_evictions_total{arm=…}`
- Wire: `BindingStatus.flow_cache_evictions_by_arm` (ordinal-indexed)

## Arm found (live, instrumented build 99.16220.2)

fw0 no-failover P4: 19,953 evictions, 0 hits, attributed as
`owner_active_redirect=18,683`, `fabric_local_wins=1,270`, all other
arms 0. Both arms fire on DIRECT (non-fabric) ingress for
`FabricRedirect` entries whose owner RG is already forwarding-active
locally: the owner-active arm required only
`FabricRedirect | fabric_ingress`, and the local-wins arm superseded
the cached redirect with a same-owner local candidate. On direct
ingress the cached redirect is the serving decision the slow path
itself recomputes, so the entry could never be served.

## Fix (commit 373bd34c8d)

`cached_flow_decision_invalid_reason` now applies the owner-active
invalidation only on fabric ingress, and returns valid for a
same-owner `FabricLocalWins` candidate on direct ingress. Fabric
ingress and different-owner supersession still invalidate. Live on the
fixed build: fw0 hits 37,334,535 / RX 38,534,300 (~97%),
`owner_active_redirect=0`. Sender rate recovered from 2.67 to
4.85 Gbps but not to master parity: 2 of 4 P4 streams carry
0.00 bits/sec and `fabric_local_wins` (different-owner) still churns
1.18M — tracked as the remaining rate gap, not a cache-retention
failure. Post-RG1-failover probe: fw1 (new owner) 56,971,539 hits /
56,972,599 RX with 433 evictions; receiver-side 0.00 in the probe is
the known iperf receiver-report artifact also present on master cells.
Cluster restored to clean master `2c8575b70e` (helper
`2f2f5cbf9b…`), RG1 primary node0.

Regression coverage: `peer_synced_import_second_packet_hits_16220`
(peer-synced import + seeded entry + second packet must HIT with no
arm moving), `neighbor_mac_stale_attributes_to_neighbor_arm_16220`
(the neighbor-MAC rejection attributes to its own arm only),
`fabric_redirect_owner_active_reason_splits_on_ingress_16220` and
`cached_flow_decision_keeps_fabric_redirect_on_non_fabric_ingress_when_local_owner_active_16220`
(the fix), and
`cached_forward_candidate_with_unowned_egress_is_invalid_16220`
(production-shaped EnforceChanged repro: egress 14 row missing/rg=0
under active rg1).

## Remaining gap

Cache retention is restored (hits ≈ RX), but sender parity is not:
~4.85 vs ~13.4 Gbps master, with 2 of 4 streams at 0.00 bits/sec and
different-owner `fabric_local_wins` churn at 1.18M. That is a
stream/fabric-locality problem downstream of cache retention, not
the #16220 retention defect; it needs its own cell (per-stream
counters during the run) before any further fix is claimed.
