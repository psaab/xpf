# #14741 — `TestGroupRouteFilterORBesideInline14416` fails 5 subtests on master

## Mechanism
Two stacked defects, both in the `mergeNodes` leaf branch of
`pkg/config/ast_groups.go`:

1. **The #14416 arm lost its body.** The #14416 fix (PR #14538,
   commit 59fa41531) added an arm adopting a distinct group
   `route-filter` OR-member beside an inline peer via
   `*dst = append(*dst, s)`. When #14417 (PR #14540, commit
   cd7e5646d) added its `prefixListDefinitionInstanceDiffers14417`
   arm as the next `else if`, the squash-merge rewrote the chain so
   the single append ended up belonging only to the new #14417
   branch. The #14416 arm kept its guard and comment but became an
   empty branch: a distinct group member matched it, did nothing,
   and hit the trailing `continue` — dropped to the inline-wins
   override exactly as before #14416. (Verified by blob inspection:
   the append is present in 59fa41531 and 2e33c7115, absent from
   cd7e5646d onward.)
2. **Restoring the append alone duplicates the member.** One group
   body is merged once per same-keyed destination container at a
   level (#9802 fan-out routes the same body into every
   policy-statement/term/from twin — e.g. the group-authored `from`
   block and the inline `from` block at the same term). Each merge
   sees only its own twin's children, so the per-merge
   `routeFilterMemberExists14416` check cannot see the sibling
   adopted into the other twin; with the append restored the member
   lands in both twins (same node pointer appended twice, verified
   by probe) and the compiler folds the twins' `from` bags into one
   term carrying the member twice. At 59fa41531 this did not happen
   because the expansion then merged the group body into a single
   destination per level; the composed master now fans out per
   twin. The #14416 test's exact-count assertion
   (`route-filters = %v, want exactly %v`, plus the duplicate-member
   check) therefore fails on the naive restoration with
   `member "192.168.0.0/16 exact" duplicated`.

## Fix
- `pkg/config/ast_groups.go` (`mergeNodes`, leaf branch): restored
  `*dst = append(*dst, s)` as the body of the
  `isRouteFilterORMember14416 && !routeFilterMemberExists14416` arm,
  ahead of the #14417 `else if`, and added a per-expansion dedup
  guard/record around it (below). The #14417, #14419, #14434, and
  #14444 arms keep their bodies and semantics.
- `pkg/config/ast_groups.go` (`groupExpandBudget`): new
  `adoptedRouteFilters14741 map[string]map[string]bool` recording
  (destination level → member identities) for every #14416 adoption
  in one expansion.
- `pkg/config/group_route_filter_or_14416.go`:
  `routeFilterLevelKey14741` (ancestor path + inline peer member
  identity — twins of one level merge against peers carrying the
  same inline member; distinct terms/inline members never collide),
  `routeFilterAlreadyAdopted14741`, and
  `recordRouteFilterAdopted14741` on the budget. A nil budget never
  suppresses (direct `mergeNodes` test callers merge once per level
  by construction).

## Regression
- Existing `TestGroupRouteFilterORBesideInline14416`
  (`pkg/config/route_filter_group_or_14416_test.go`), unchanged:
  RED on origin/master 6d677287a (5 subtests fail, group member
  missing); RED again on the naive append-only restoration (same 5
  subtests fail, member duplicated); GREEN after the full fix —
  all 8 subtests pass. Fail-on-revert: removing the append re-empties
  the arm (missing); removing only the dedup guard/record restores
  the duplication.
- New `TestGroupRouteFilterSameMemberTwoTerms14741`
  (`pkg/config/route_filter_group_or_14741_test.go`): the same group
  member beside different inline members in two different terms
  adopts in BOTH terms (2 route-filters each) — pins that the level
  key does not over-suppress across terms.
- Adjacent suites pass: `TestDR15H2` (#14417),
  `TestGroupApplicationTermLeafLeaf14434` (#14434),
  `TestGroupReplaceBlock` (#14444), `TestExpandGroups`,
  `TestApplyGroups`, `TestRouteFilter`, `TestPolicy`.
