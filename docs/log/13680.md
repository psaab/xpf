# 13680 — retain completed fragment overlap anchors

`OverlapTracker::commit_admission` previously removed an entry as soon as all
admissions settled and recorded ranges covered the terminal fragment. A repeated
fragment ident could then be admitted as a fresh datagram while the receiver's
reassembly window was still open, bypassing overlap detection.

Completed entries now remain in the tracker until normal idle-TTL or absolute-
lifetime expiry. Their ranges continue to reject replays/overlaps, and their
slots are not reclaimed by eviction: a full shard remains fail-closed. Terminal
end consistency is also checked by both the pure precheck and post-commit
recording gate, so ranges past a terminal end and conflicting terminal ends are
rejected without changing the recorded anchor.

Regression coverage exercises retained completed entries, fail-closed shard
capacity until expiry, expiry-based slot reuse, parity between pure and
recording terminal-conflict checks, plus the queue and forwarding callers that
assert retained entries after accepted completion.

RED-on-revert: restoring the old `shard.remove(pos)` in `commit_admission` made
`cargo test --bin xpf-userspace-dp fragment_overlap::tests::completed_datagram_stays_protected_through_reassembly_window_13680 -- --nocapture`
fail at the retained-entry assertion (`left: 0`, `right: 1`). With the fix
restored, that regression passed (1 passed); the complete `fragment_overlap`
test module passed (33 passed, 7984 filtered). The five queue/sojourn/forwarding
expectation tests updated for retention also passed individually.

The full dataplane binary suite ran (8,028 passed, 9 failed, 8 ignored). Five
failures were stale expectations that completed entries were removed; those
assertions were updated and each corresponding test passed individually. The
remaining failures were two GRE local-delivery tests, one session-authority
window test, and `f035_parked_fragment_overlap_is_rechecked_on_retry_10659`.
The latter also failed when the completion deletion was restored and terminal
checks were disabled, so it is not caused by this change. The broader suite was
not rerun after updating the five stale assertions.
