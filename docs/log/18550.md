# Issue #18550 — late synced imports after RG activation

## Question

Two lanes, reading the code, suspected that a peer-synced import arriving
AFTER this node activated its owner RG is never made visible to the
dataplane. The coordinator skips its USERSPACE_SESSIONS publish when the
owner RG is locally active, and the activation republish runs only once.
They proposed this as the cause of intermittent crash-takeover stream
deaths with server RSTs (loss diag7b and diag9).

## Answer: not real

Traced on master `64d3b38008`:

- The #9678 gate (`afxdp/ha/session_import.rs:1505-1516`) stops the import
  only when the SHARED map already holds a LOCAL-origin row. A local
  session wins, and its own rows are already published.
- The shared publish of the forward entry and its synthesized reverse is
  unconditional and synchronous (`session_import.rs:1654`, `:1695`). The
  packet path falls back to it on a worker-table miss
  (`shared_ops/lookup.rs:1739`).
- The coordinator steering publish is skipped on purpose for an active owner
  (`session_import.rs:1670`). The worker fan-out always runs
  (`session_import.rs:1735`). `handle_upsert_synced` then publishes the
  steering rows after its own guard (`upsert_synced.rs:259`, `:413`;
  `session/install.rs:978-984`).
- During the queue-drain window, a shim miss for transit traffic or for an
  interface-SNAT reply is XSK-redirected (`userspace-xdp/src/lib.rs:1122-1148`).
  On a redirect error it is dropped (`lib.rs:1311-1314`). It is never passed
  to the kernel.
