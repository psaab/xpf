## Defect (STEP-0 adjudicated, live at origin/master 61aa231)

#17606 introduced the #16785 bounded pre-handoff hold with ONE shared clock
(`earlyInputHandoffPendingSince`, opened by `noteEarlyInputBarrierRetained`
for EVERY retention reason) and gated all three retention arms on
`earlyInputPendingExpired()`. After 5 minutes from the FIRST retention of any
reason, all three handoff sites dropped the barrier even when the hold was a
cold-start lo0 enforcement failure or an uncovered-destination (addresses
changed) hold:

- real-install site (`daemon_nft.go`): `effectiveLo0 := lo0RetainsBarrier &&
  !pendingExpired` … `if pendingExpired && (…) { holdsBarrier = false }`
  removed the barrier and recorded handoff-done while `lo0LastFailed` stayed
  set;
- fenced-fallback site: the lo0 arm itself removed the barrier at expiry;
- no-enforcement teardown site: `if !expired { return nil }` fell through to
  removal at expiry ("handing off despite lo0 failure").

#16785 scoped the bound to a never-leasing zoned DHCP/DHCPv6 unit, whose
pending ingress keeps the #11577 per-interface backstop after handoff. An
lo0-failure or changed-address hold has NO substitute backstop: timing it out
is a fail-open handoff over a still-failed protection — a wrong-scope
availability fix.

## Fix

The bound now applies to the pending-DHCP hold ALONE:

- `noteEarlyInputBarrierRetained(detail)` (lo0 / addresses-changed) records
  the retained-hold projection state only; it never opens a bound clock and
  never expires. Those holds keep the barrier until a successful apply hands
  off (the changed hold already fails the commit to drive that retry).
- `noteEarlyInputPendingRetained(detail)` (pending DHCP intent) records the
  same projection state plus opens the bounded clock
  (`earlyInputPendingBoundSince`) on the first pending retention; repeated
  retentions cannot extend it. `earlyInputPendingExpired()` reads that clock
  only.
- All three handoff sites drop the `!pendingExpired` terms from the lo0 and
  changed arms; a mixed pending+lo0 hold past the bound stays retained (lo0
  dominates), and once lo0 recovers the already-expired pending bound hands
  off immediately (no fresh five minutes).
- The pending hold is now noted whenever it is active (the real-install site
  previously skipped the note when lo0/changed also held), so the bound
  measures pending intent itself.
- #17854 MED finding: the retained-hold state is now guarded by
  `earlyInputBarrierStateMu`; the HostInboundApplied projection and the 30s
  gap-retry loop read it without applySem.

## Risk

Fail-closed direction: a persistently failing lo0 render or a forever-churning
address set now keeps the early input barrier indefinitely (pre-#17606
behavior restored for those holds); management lifelines stay admitted by the
barrier variant, and both holds already fail their commits, driving the
existing retry paths. The #16785 availability bound for never-leasing DHCP
units is unchanged.

Out of scope (filed separately on the issue): the astra lane's second finding
— cross-ingress reachability of a late DHCP destination after the bounded
handoff — is a dataplane destination-coverage design change (nft ruleset
generation), not a daemon-hold bug; it needs its own design pass. The MED
synchronization finding is fixed here.
