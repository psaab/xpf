# #18707: helper restart waits on fixed delays before forwarding

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-01
- **Action**: Measured where an isolated helper crash spends its outage, then
  removed the three fixed delays on that path.
- **Prior evidence** (#18702's live cell, udp, 70,526 spawn-flushed rows,
  journal `short-precise`, VM clock; the 1.6 s probe could not split it):

  | event | t (s, from exit) |
  |---|---|
  | exit detected, restart scheduled `attempt=1 delay=1s` | 0.000 |
  | spawn (`event stream: listening`) | +1.001 |
  | spawn flush done (70,526 rows) | +1.072 |
  | `helper started` | +1.174 |
  | `apply_snapshot` / `set_forwarding_state armed=true` (first status tick) | +2.180 |
  | worker binds | +2.20 .. +2.99 |
  | ctrl prewarm + `starting XSK liveness probe` (ctrl=1) | +2.991 |
  | `bootstrapping NAPI queues reason=startup-prewarm` | +3.062 |
  | `XSK liveness proven` | +5.177 |
  | `RG readiness: ready` | +5.563 |

  The spawn-time 3 s NAPI timer would have fired at about +4.2 and was
  swallowed by the prewarm's 2 s debounce, so it was not on the critical
  path in this run. The 1 s backoff and the 1 s wait for the first status
  tick were.
- **Changes**:
  - First retry of a crash episode is immediate
    (`helperRestartDelay(1) == 0`); repeats keep the exponential schedule.
    Cumulative wait before the crash-loop cap is unchanged (63 s), so HA's
    `CrashLooping` response is not reached sooner; it trips at attempt 8
    instead of 7.
  - The crash restart's status loop runs its first pass immediately
    (`ensureStatusLoopFirstPassNowLocked`).
  - The spawn-time 3 s NAPI timer is replaced by a once-per-generation,
    non-debounced bootstrap fired when the status reports every binding
    bound (`ensureXSKBoundNAPIBootstrapLocked`), with the #10436 fence.
- **File(s)**: `pkg/dataplane/userspace/{process_supervisor.go,process_status.go,process.go,helper_status_apply.go,manager.go}`,
  tests (`helper_restart_latency_18707_test.go`, the ported
  `process_napi_startup_10436_test.go`, three contract updates, `statusLoop`
  call sites), `pkg/dataplane/README.md`,
  `docs/userspace-dataplane-architecture.md`, `docs/in-place-upgrade.md`.

## Live timeline, udp (2026-11-01)

- **Method**: one udp lock cell per build. Deploy, restore node0 primary,
  re-apply CoS. Each trial: a 17,000-session UDP burst from the LAN host
  (4 source aliases, 1,000/s), then a `ping -D -O -i 0.1` (10 Hz) from
  `cluster-udp-host` to `172.16.80.211`, then SIGKILL of node0's helper.
  The kill timestamp is taken on the VM clock in the same `incus exec`.
  Journal: `-o short-unix`. Ping times are shifted by the measured
  VM-minus-LAN offset (+0.001 to +0.007 s, min-RTT of 7 bracketed
  samples before and after each trial). Trials are about 72 s apart, so
  trials 2 and 3 are repeats inside one crash episode (attempts 2 and 3).
  Times are seconds after SIGKILL.

- **Reading**:
  - **An isolated crash (t1) cut transit loss from 3.48 s to 1.62 s after
    SIGKILL**, which is 1.08 s after the supervisor saw the exit.
  - Repeats still back off: after t2 and t3 are each one backoff step
    shorter than before (1 s and 2 s instead of 2 s and 4 s). The loss
    shrinks by exactly that step plus the 1 s tick.
  - The remaining ~1.6 s is work, not waiting:
    - about 0.5 s for the kernel to tear down the dead helper (SIGKILL
      to reaped exit, the same in both builds);
    - about 0.2 s for spawn, flush and ready;
    - about 0.7 s for the helper's 18 binds and fill primes.
- **XSK liveness proof is not on the transit path.** In every trial the
  first forwarded ping came before `XSK liveness proven`. Ctrl is armed
  and the shim swapped in when the probe STARTS
  (`applyXSKLivenessGateLocked` sets `Enabled=1` and then advances the
  probe). In the after builds the proof landed on the loop's second tick:
  +1.737 and +2.693, each 1.00 s after loop start (+0.733 and +1.690). It
  gates `RG readiness`
  (takeover readiness, about 1.5 s later still), not forwarding. So it was
  left on the 1 s cadence, and the transit target is met without it.
- **EBUSY / fallback**: zero `EBUSY`, `Device or resource busy` or
  fallback lines in any captured trial journal (before t1–t3, after
  t1–t2). Bind modes were identical in every captured restart: 6
  private/Copy (fabric), 6 shared-owner/ZeroCopy and 6
  shared-secondary/ZeroCopy. The data-plane summary read
  `Zerocopy bindings 12/18` and `Shared UMEM bindings 12/18` before every
  trial, the same as a fresh deploy. For before t1–t2 and after t1–t2,
  that sample also covers the previous trial's restart.
