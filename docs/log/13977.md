# #13977 — scope capture flow FIFOs by tunnel and queue identity

## Why

The NFQUEUE classifier's flow key described only the inner datagram or transport tuple. `CapturePipeline` uses that string as the owner of per-flow FIFO and retirement state, so identical packets captured through different IPsec tunnels, queues, or capture generations aliased. Retiring one flow could therefore affect another capture authority, and independent flows could block one another.

## What

Carry the admitted capture row's tunnel and VRF into `CaptureFrame`, and compose tunnel, VRF, queue number, and capture generation into the pipeline FIFO key. Whole packets and fragments use the same scope composition; the existing `FragmentKey` scope remains unchanged.
