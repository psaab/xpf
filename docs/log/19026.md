- **Why F1 rides with Part 1**: the parking itself makes the F1 ordering
  reachable on ONE worker. A parked Close(A) lets RX admit a replacement B
  on the exact key. The post-RX export then flushes Open(B) before the next
  tick retries Close(A). The binary HA Close carries no id, and the peer's
  delete in routing domain 0 is bare. Without the gate, Part 1 would
  introduce that peer delete of B.

    or its own expiry.
