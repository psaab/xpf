## Measurement that shaped the fix

Widening the sweep on the real `ConfigSnapshot` fixture finds **exactly the two
producers the old sweep found** (`Interfaces`, `Fabrics`), with no panics and no
new errors. So on the production fixture the change is indistinguishable from a
no-op, and shipping it as a #7020 fix on that evidence would have been a claim
with nothing behind it. `TestNetdevProducerSweepWidening` supplies the missing
proof on a synthetic snapshot, varying each widening SEPARATELY — a compound
cell would show "wider finds more" without establishing that both halves are
load-bearing.

The sweep was extracted into `sweepNetdevProducers` so the widest row of that
table runs PRODUCTION's sweep. A probe with its own copy would stay green after
a revert.
