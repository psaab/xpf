# #9990 / #9991 — session lookup lifetime and expiry

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-17
- **Action**: STEP-0 reproduced both defects on `origin/master` at
  `6dacb080f038f7dfecc9ae55fa5803664f0df39d`. A pre-decision local lookup
  changed a TCP forward entry's `last_seen_ns`, kept its half-open state, and
  could complete the handshake; the NAT64 quote path used the same mutating
  lookup. A UDP lookup one second beyond its deadline still returned a hit and
  re-stamped `last_seen_ns`. The throwaway regression was removed.
- **File(s)**: none (measurement)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-17
- **Action**: Added a read-only session probe for ownership/delete inspection,
  plus an expiry-aware probe for ICMP/NAT64 quote resolution. The #9048 peer
  delete guard now uses the raw probe so installed local ownership is refused
  without changing state; NAT64 quote resolution uses the expiry-aware,
  non-mutating cross-scope resolver. Normal packet lookup rejects strict idle
  deadline crossings before any state update, while the wheel remains the
  owner of physical cleanup and HA retention decisions.

- **Action**: The existing HA standby-retention cell had used a packet lookup
  as its physical-presence assertion. After the strict hit gate, that mixed
  two contracts: the held entry must remain installed, while stale traffic
  must miss until refresh. The cell now asserts both independently. The exact
  clean-master control test passed 1/1; the corrected lane test passed 1/1,
  and the scoped `session::` suite passed 310/310.

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-17
- **Action**: Hardened flow-cache admission against idle-crossed local sessions.
  `session_is_live_at` now performs a read-only deadline check before TTL,
  filters, policers, logs, accounting, or TX; `touch_if_stale` returns live
  eligibility and retains its keepalive write only after TTL handling. An
  expired cache candidate is invalidated and reclassified as a miss without
  refreshing, accounting, TX, or scratch side effects.
