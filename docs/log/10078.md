# #10078: close the modeled `security` schema world

## Change

`schemaSecurity` now arms closed-world validation at the `security` root. The
arm is deliberately compiler-shaped rather than blanket:

- modeled children that the compiler reads are declared and validated;
- compiler-owned heterogeneous grammars (`screen` family bodies,
  `static-nat`, source-NAT rule-level `persistent-nat`, and TCP MSS kind/value
  tails) end inherited closure at an explicit `closedWorldOpaque` boundary;
- packed readers and the schema walker share the same expansion for security
  log streams and destination-NAT pool runs;
- destination-NAT pool `address`, standalone `port`, and
  `routing-instance` spellings remain valid, including the packed
  `address <ip> port <n>` form; the packed suffix is arity-bounded.

The flat-set AST preserves the canonical packed destination-pool address
statement. A `port` suffix consumes exactly its declared one value; any token
left after that suffix becomes a separate schema-walk node and is rejected by
the armed pool boundary instead of being overwritten by the compiler's packed
address scanner.
