      branches and the test seam. The zero value is still ModeEBPFOnly. The
      internal readers (`manager_status.go`, `manager_ha.go`) keep reading
      the field under `m.mu`.
    - The reconcile pass releases a helper-down demotion's direct VIPs at
      the TOP of the pass, after `reconcileRG0ConfigOwnership` and before
      the per-RG readiness loop. `reconcileHelperDown` is computed there;
      its predicate reads only lock-free helper state and cluster state.
