# #16502 — pre-screen (0,0)-port fail-closed rule

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
  - **Action**: The #11332 pre-screen input-filter walk
    (`input_filter_would_deny_before_screen`) built its `TermMatchExtra` with
    only the flowless `ports_unknown` case, omitting the #9894 rule that a
    flow-backed `(0,0)` tuple is synthetic. On a tolerant-loaded
    `routing-instance` + `discard`/`reject` term with a port constraint
    literal 0 satisfies (e.g. `destination-port-except 80`), the pre-screen
    walk denied, screens were skipped, and PBR enforcement — which fails
    port-constrained terms closed on `(0,0)` — forwarded the packet with no
    screen accounting. Added one shared helper
    `filter::mark_ports_unknown_for_zero_flow` and routed every affected walk
    through it: `input_filter_would_deny_before_screen`,
    `ingress_route_table_override_with_mode` (PBR enforcement),
    `revalidate_static_pbr_route_on_session_hit`, the per-packet session-hit
    PBR arm in `evaluate_input_filter_on_session_hit`,
    `static_input_filter_deny_eval` (on top of its deliberate
    `TermMatchExtra::default()`), and `static_hit_log_for_action` (log-source
    labelling). Invariant restored: pre-screen deny implies enforcement deny.
  - **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/filter/mod.rs`,
    `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/forwarding/pbr.rs`,
    `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/poll_descriptor/filter.rs`,
    `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/tests_embedded_poll_filter.rs` (vb13 arms A–D as
    `port_zero_syn_is_screened_despite_except_discard_pbr_term_16502`, plus
    the property cell `prescreen_deny_implies_enforcement_deny_16502` over
    ports × term shapes asserting pre-screen deny ⇒ enforcement deny and
    that `(0,0)` never pre-screen-denies), `docs/log/16502.md`
  - **Validation**: targeted `cargo test --bin xpf-userspace-dp 16502`
    (see PR for counts); neighbouring 9894/11332 tests re-run.
