- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-08 (`fix/12151-campaign-pub`, base `83b5c9553`)

## Change

- The non-index-0 warning distinguishes a higher-index PCI NIC from a non-PCI management route, logs the current index-0 NIC's name and PCI address when a PCI NIC is present, and directs operators to restart xpfd or reboot after the applicable console remedy. It states that the `system management-interface` leaf is not implemented in config mode and would be silently ignored.
- Updated the bootstrap, typed-field, daemon README, xpfd README, and early input-barrier unit comments so none presents the deferred leaf as an operator remedy.
- `TestBootstrapNonIndexRemedyIsActionable12151` forces all three lifeline netlink observations to succeed, then verifies both refusal remedies and that the refusal prevents rename, reload, and `.network` creation.

## Residual / risk

`system management-interface` remains a typed internal field with deferred config-mode grammar; this change does not implement that leaf or change protected/positional rename behavior. For a higher-index PCI lifeline, operators must use the console rewire remedy; for a non-PCI route, they must move management onto a PCI NIC. The warning identifies the current index-0 NIC, and xpfd must be restarted or the host rebooted after either change.
