# #12151 — remove the unsettable bootstrap remedy

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-08 (`fix/12151-campaign-pub`, base `83b5c9553`)
- **Action**: replace the unsettable `system management-interface` remedy with distinct console guidance for higher-index PCI, non-PCI, and empty PCI enumeration; make the index-0 target explicit and place the committed chassis device-map remedy on the higher-index path.

## Change

- The non-PCI warning directs operators to move the management default route and its addressing onto the explicitly identified current index-0 NIC (`index0_interface`/`index0_pci`), without suggesting a device-map for a bond, VLAN, or bridge. The higher-index warning requires the route and addressing on the logged index-0 NIC, explains that virtio-first enumeration cannot be changed by recabling alone, and offers `set chassis device-map interface fxp0 pci <mgmt PCI>` followed by `commit confirmed` from the console.
- Empty PCI enumeration (for example, a VMBus-only host) now has a distinct “no PCI NIC enumerated” refusal and does not emit empty index-0 fields or the bond/VLAN/bridge diagnosis. All three refusal branches retain the config-mode `system management-interface` silently-ignored disclaimer; the actionable console remedies direct operators to restart xpfd or reboot after the change.
- Updated the bootstrap, typed-field, daemon README, xpfd README, and early input-barrier unit comments so none presents the deferred leaf as an operator remedy. `TestBootstrapNonIndexRemedyIsActionable12151` forces all three lifeline netlink observations to succeed and verifies all three refusals, including no rename, reload, or `.network` creation.
