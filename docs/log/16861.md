# #16861 — IPv6 ND admit for VRF-member zone interfaces

- **Base**: `b16c175608` (origin/master at branch start)
- **Branch**: `fix/16861-campaign`

## Why

Since #15931 the IPv6 ND (ICMPv6 133–136) admit for a zone interface
that is a routing-instance (VRF) member was scoped only as
`iifname <vrf master> meta sdifname <member>`, and the scoped view
suppressed the unscoped ND accept. Linux keeps the member device as
`iifname` (with no sdif) for NDISC (`vrf_ip6_rcv`), so a unicast NA/NS
addressed to the firewall on such an interface matched no scoped
rule and fell to the zone's destination catch-all `drop`: the
firewall could not resolve IPv6 neighbours on any VRF-member zone
interface whose zone did not list `protocols ndp`/`any-service`.
Multicast ND still passed via the chain policy, so neighbours could
resolve the firewall; the failure was one-way and silent apart from
the zone deny counter.

## What

- `emitHostInboundNDPAcceptsCountedNetlink`
  (`pkg/nftables/netlink_hostinbound.go`) and its text oracle
  `emitHostInboundNDPAcceptsText` (`pkg/daemon/daemon_nft.go`): for
  each VRF scope, additionally emit the ND admit keyed on the member
  as `iifname <members>` (not `meta sdifname`, not the master).
  The per-view `ndp except` withdrawal is unchanged — excluding views
  emit nothing on any scope.
- Fences: `emitHostInboundNonUnicastFenceDrops{,Netlink}` emits the
  member-`iifname` ND accept (for default-retaining views) ahead of a
  new member-`iifname` ff02/broadcast drop twin, mirroring the
  existing master+sdif pair, so a fence window neither drops the
  retaining member's multicast ND nor admits its non-ND multicast.
  NOT changed: the steady-state non-unicast guard (the scoped ND
  admits run ahead of it and chain policy accepts the fall-through —
  no fence-style drop exists there to shadow member ND), all other
  VRF-scoped service/multicast emission (non-NDISC traffic on an
  enslaved member genuinely presents master+sdif at LOCAL_IN), and
  IPv4/ARP.

## Validation

- **RED** on pristine `origin/master` (`b16c175608`, wt-16861-base)
  with only the two new tests copied in:
  `TestNDVRFMemberIIFNameAdmit16861` fails ("VRF member lost
  member-iifname ND admit"), `TestNDVRFMemberIIFNameNetlink16861`
  fails ("member-iifname ND admit missing; rules=2").
- **GREEN** post-fix: both tests pass; the #15931 suite
  (`TestNDExcept*`, `TestNDMulticast*`, `TestNDMixed*`,
  `TestNDAnyService*`) passes unchanged.
- T1 parity (`TestNftNetlinkParity`, now including retaining and
  `ndp except` VRF-member views): `host_inbound*`, `gap_fence`,
  `lo0*` pass. `cold_boot_fence`, `unleased_backstop`,
  `dhcp_vrf_backstop` fail identically on pristine master
  (pre-existing ESP/AH-first fence-ordering divergence, base-attributed
  on `b16c175608`); `mutation_weakened_verdict_zone_opened_all` is
  flaky across reruns on both trees. Full `pkg/nftables` failures
  (12 top-level, lo0/any-service unit-render class) are identical
  pre/post; the daemon host-inbound selection shows the same 13
  named failures pre/post (265 vs 264 passes, +1 new test) and full
  `pkg/daemon` aborts on the pre-existing
  `TestCommitAndApplyRespectsSemaphore` panic, identical on master.

## Risk

The member-`iifname` ND admit matches any packet genuinely arriving
on the member device, which is exactly the NDISC identity; it does
not admit the VRF master or co-members. A member listed in two zones
with different ND posture follows the pre-existing shared-ingress
semantics (any retaining view's scoped admit matches; the same
already holds for the master+sdif form). Kernel-cell verification
(the source report's `vrf-xpf` REACHABLE/deny-0 cell) was not
re-executed here; the render-level identity is pinned by the new
tests and T1 parity.
