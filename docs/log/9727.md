## Design

- **Budget.** `4096 - 256 - 14 (Ethernet) - 4 (one 802.1Q tag)` = **3822**. FCS is stripped before XDP. A frame of exactly 3822 MTU fits; 3823 does not.
- **"Bound by the userspace dataplane"** mirrors `userspaceSkipsIngressInterface` and `netdevExclusionClasses`, which pkg/config cannot import:
  - interfaces referenced by a security zone, other than `mgmt` and `control`;
  - not tunnels (interface or unit), `fxp*`, `em*`, `fab*`, `lo0`, or local fabric members.

  The effective MTU is the unit's when set, else the interface's. There is one advisory per base interface, carrying the largest over-budget MTU.
- **A warning, not a refusal.** Commit cannot tell copy mode (drops in `kernel_rx_dropped`) from zero-copy (a refused bind), and a jumbo MTU is still right for kernel-forwarded traffic.
- **Suppression.** The advisory is silenced on the tolerant boot and peer-sync paths through `opts.suppressContestedTrunkZoneAdvisory`, like the #7509 advisories.
- **Drift guard.** `TestUserspaceUMEMConstantsMatchTheHelper9727` parses both Rust constants.
