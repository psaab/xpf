# Issue #12834: Quarantine host-inbound overrides in poisoned zones

## Defect

`compileZones` marked a zone poisoned on unknown enforcement content and cleared its interface membership and screen binding after all repeated zone stanzas, but left `ZoneConfig.InterfaceHostInbound` intact. The host-inbound override resolver (`resolveInterfaceHostInbound` in `pkg/config/host_inbound_effective_view.go`) treats a bare physical override differently from a unit override: it writes the bare physical key first-writer-wins without an owner check, while the unit path checks zone ownership and contested-key quarantine. Consequently an override from a poisoned, now-unbound zone could remain active and displace the effective bare-interface host-inbound set. The stamped per-zone override lookup could also continue to expose it to diagnostics.

## Change

The `compileZones` poison post-pass now clears `InterfaceHostInbound` along with `Interfaces` and the screen binding. This occurs after every repeated zone block has been compiled, so a later sibling stanza cannot restore a partially compiled override. Removing the authored override source also makes subsequent derived host-inbound stamps and consumers see no poisoned interface override. Healthy zone host-inbound overrides remain unchanged.

Regression coverage in `pkg/config/poisoned_zone_host_inbound_12834_test.go` exercises a poisoned `alpha` zone's bare physical `system-services all` override against a healthy `zulu` unit owner, confirms the poisoned per-zone override is absent from both effective lookup methods, and retains a non-poisoned bare-override positive control. `pkg/dataplane/userspace/poisoned_zone_host_inbound_12834_test.go` separately feeds bounded bare/unit interface snapshots into the host-inbound view builder and checks the healthy unit's kernel-facing view and classifier deny result. This tests the reported quarantine behavior without claiming that the resolver map alone proves live packet forwarding.

## Risk

The change is fail-closed and limited to per-interface host-inbound overrides in zones already marked `DroppedEnforcementChild`. Unknown-content tolerant loads continue booting, but a poisoned zone no longer contributes interface-level host-inbound admission. Strict compilation remains unchanged and rejects unknown zone content.
