# #12517 — reject unknown policy then-tail tokens

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-12 (review fold 2026-10-13)
- **Base**: `master` `3a68f1cde` (cut on `c0974911c`; #12513 and #12558 are
  merged)
- **Action**: Retain unknown `then` tails for strict rejection and tolerant
  warnings; preserve authored deny for quoted/bracketed `then next-hop reject`.

## Red before

A mutation that disabled the new then-tail recorder made `CompileConfig`
accept the original 17 action/from-word rows in compact, term-line, braced,
and SetPath syntax. `CompileConfigLenient` and `Store.SyncApply` omitted the
tail warning for all 17 rows; the added community add/set/bare rows also lost
their tail warnings on the mutant. The strict-store mutation showed
`CheckText`, `LoadOverride`/`LoadMerge` + `CommitCheck`, and
`LoadOverride`/`LoadMerge` + `Commit` accepting the schema-bypass rows
`community none foo`, `load-balance per-packet foo`, `accept foo`, and
`reject foo`.

Disabling quoted/bracketed-then detection reproduced R7-F2: all three config
spellings produced `Action=accept` through lenient compile and `SyncApply`,
and FRR rendered `route-map P permit 10` instead of the authored deny.

## Change

- The inline and child action readers retain a compiler-only first-tail flag,
  action, and token when an action has excess operands, a packed (compact or
  term-line) `then` names an unknown action, or a then-side from-word is
  consumed. `validatePolicyThenTailsStrict12517` reports it after the #11780
  and #11779 policy gates; `lenientPolicyThenOperands` downgrades it to a
  warning.
- Inline from-word detection covers `protocol`, `prefix-list`,
  `route-filter`, `as-path`, and the five words the inline switch dropped
  (`family`, `neighbor`, `interface`, `instance`, `rib`). The five live in
  their own set: the #12070 map that #12513 reads is unchanged.
- A `then community` value is one token or the bracketed list at the value
  slot; a token after the list is a tail. `expandFlatRun` carries bracket
  provenance onto the statements it cuts from a chained run. A value run with
  no bracket provenance (display-set replay, pre-#6668 trees) stays whole.
- Unknown statements in a braced or set-path `then` block stay #10707
  open-world warnings.
- Quoted/bracketed `then next-hop reject` inside an AS-path-prepend run sets
  the tolerant term action to `reject`; the existing FRR action mapping emits
  deny.

## Review fold (Opus review of `d042225ed`)

- **B1**: chained `... community add|set|<bare> [ a b ] accept` was rejected
  in compact, braced, set-path, CLI-set, and LoadMerge spellings because the
  flat-run split dropped bracket provenance. Fixed by carrying the mask and
  reading the exact list extent. The same counter rejected the replay of
  `display set` output for every bracketed community list (FormatSet prints
  leaf lists without brackets); provenance-free runs now stay whole.
- **B2**: the child reader's unknown-statement rejection reversed #10707 and
  failed the configstore #10707 and #12067 cells. The rejection is gone and the
  tail gate runs after #11779, so its supported-`from` diagnostic wins.
- **B3**: a token after `]` was accepted in compact, braced, set-path, and
  LoadMerge spellings. Now rejected everywhere, including `community delete`.
- **B4**: packed `then foo`, `then foo accept`, and `then next policy foo`
  were accepted. The generic branch and both `next` branches now record them;
  the child reader counts `next`'s operand.
- **B5**: the five added from-words broke communities named like them and the
  prepend omission belt; they moved to their own set.
- Folds: diagnostics are pinned in every channel, the R7-F2 split-term row
  covers the child-path override, and the `from` clause a set-path replay
  nests under an action (#12516 shape) reports a clause, not a modifier.

## Risk

Strict commit now rejects persisted policy statements with these extra tokens;
tolerant compile/sync surfaces warnings instead of silently losing them. The
R7-F2 tolerant output changes from permit to deny, matching the authored
`next-hop reject` intent. A LoadMerge or set-path replay of a packed unknown
`then` word gives it its own statement, so it commits with the #10707 warning
rather than this rejection. A term-line community list persisted without
bracket provenance now fails strict commit on its second member, which master
silently dropped. No live FRR reload was performed.
