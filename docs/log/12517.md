## Red before

A mutation that disabled the new then-tail recorder made `CompileConfig`
accept the original 17 action/from-word rows in compact, term-line, braced,
and SetPath syntax. `CompileConfigLenient` and `Store.SyncApply` omitted the
tail warning for all 17 rows; the added community add/set/bare rows also lost
their tail warnings on the mutant. The strict-store mutation showed
`CheckText`, `LoadOverride`/`LoadMerge` + `CommitCheck`, and
`LoadOverride`/`LoadMerge` + `Commit` accepting the schema-bypass rows
`community none foo`, `load-balance per-packet foo`, `accept foo`, and
`reject foo`.

Disabling quoted/bracketed-then detection reproduced R7-F2: all three config
spellings produced `Action=accept` through lenient compile and `SyncApply`,
and FRR rendered `route-map P permit 10` instead of the authored deny.

## Change

- The inline and child action readers retain a compiler-only first-tail flag,
action, and token when an action has excess operands or a then-side from-word
is consumed. The strict policy-operand gate now reports the saved context; its
existing lenient option downgrades that rejection to a warning.
- Inline from-word boundaries cover `protocol`, `prefix-list`, `route-filter`,
`family`, `neighbor`, `interface`, `instance`, `rib`, and `as-path`. Valid
multi-action chains and the variable-length `community delete` list retain
their existing parsing.
- Quoted/bracketed `then next-hop reject` inside an AS-path-prepend run sets
the tolerant term action to `reject`; the existing FRR action mapping emits
deny. The #12558 `then` dispatch hunk and #12541 route-filter gate remain
untouched.
- Added config strict/tolerant channel coverage, action-chain controls, and
FRR deny-render regressions.
