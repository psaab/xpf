# Quarantine-consistent policy rejection reasons (#12850)

- **Issue**: #12850, research D4-03 (High, MATERIAL).
- **Cause**: `PolicyContentRejectionReasons` resolved policy references against raw zone and policy snapshots, while publication removes reserved zones and quarantines collision losers afterward. A global rule scoped to an authored `junos-global` could therefore appear healthy even though its scope zone is absent from the published snapshot; independently rejected content on a policy scrubbed for a collision loser could also leave a phantom reason.
- **Change**: Run the config-level mirror over the same post-quarantine policy and zone view used by snapshot publication. Keep global policy structural `junos-global` sentinels intact; only the missing match-scope reference is rejected. Full and scheduler snapshots stamp the reserved-zone quarantine reason from their post-quarantine view, and scrubbed collision-loser rules do not contribute stale content reasons.
- **Regression coverage** (`pkg/dataplane/userspace/policy_quarantine_reject_12850_test.go`): reserved global match-scope rejection and full/scheduler stamp parity; collision-loser policy with unrepresentable content is scrubbed without phantom rejection; scoped-global collision pruning retains only the surviving member; wildcard and unscoped global controls; unresolved typo remains rejected.
- **Validation**: The new targeted regression failed before the fix for the reserved-zone miss and collision-loser phantom. After the fix, `go test ./pkg/dataplane/userspace -run TestPolicyContentRejectionReasonsPostQuarantine12850 -count=1` passed, and the full `go test ./pkg/dataplane/userspace -count=1` passed.
- **Risk**: Rejection/reporting now follows zone quarantine for policy rules. Structural global sentinels and wildcard semantics remain unchanged.
