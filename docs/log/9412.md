the false comment, corrected as a guard rather than prose — plus two measured
corrections posted to the issue that change its fix direction and its acceptance.
The wire defect is NOT fixed, and deliberately not half-fixed.

## 2026-09-10T01:20Z — Action: CORRECTION 1 — the sweep cannot carry a mid-life change
- **File(s)**: none (measurement)
- The fix direction's option 2 ("carry close-state in the incremental sweep
  export") is not available. `pkg/cluster/sync_conn_sweep.go:187` (V4) and `:210`
  (V6) are the only session-send sites and both gate on
  `val.Created >= threshold`, `threshold = s.lastSweepTime`. `Created` is the
  install instant, stamped once (`daemon_ha_userspace_convert.go:402`/`:527`).
  `replaying` (`:182`) does not widen the gate — it withholds the `lastSweepTime`
  advance so the same window repeats after an overflow (`:231`).
- And the transition emits nothing: `session/lookup.rs:185-208` sets `closing` and
  recomputes the window, and `lookup.rs` has **zero** `push_delta` calls. Positive
  control: `push_delta` appears 7 times elsewhere, so the grep works.
- So the standby's copy is FROZEN at install-time state.

## 2026-09-10T01:35Z — Action: CORRECTION 2 — the acceptance does not test the defect
- **File(s)**: none (reasoning, from Correction 1)
- The stated acceptance installs a session that is *already* FIN-closed, which is
  the one case the cheap install-delta fix DOES cover. A fix that carries
  close-state on the install delta and nothing else passes the acceptance while
  leaving the dominant shape (install open -> close -> failover) broken. The
  acceptance needs a third cell that closes the session on the primary AFTER the
  sync.
- This is why nothing was landed: a partial fix that passes its own acceptance is
  worse than no fix, because it removes the reason anyone looks again.

## 2026-09-10T01:45Z — Action: cost the real fix, and record two traps
- **File(s)**: none (measurement)
- Option 1 needs: a close-state byte through six hops across two languages and two
  transports; a delta kind or suppression flag, because `SessionDelta` also feeds
  the RT_FLOW exporter so an extra `Open` emits a spurious `SESSION_CREATE`
  (`bulk_resync` is NOT the lever — it only gates the loss-of-sync latch,
  `afxdp/session_delta.rs:360`); and a volume argument against #8593, which
  measured a 25.26M-delta / 92%-drop overflow storm on this exact buffer.
- TRAP: do not reuse `dataplane.SessionValue.TCPState`. It is already on the
  cluster wire at a fixed offset and dormant on the userspace path (the convert
  never sets it, the sync-request builder never reads it) — but it is the BPF-ABI
  mirror of `struct session_value.tcp_state` and still flows through every
  `bpf_session_value.go` conversion, so re-encoding it is a REDEFINITION.
