# #13198 — aggregate distinct-source NAT reverse-key collisions

## Why

Each worker already counted NAT reverse-key collisions involving a different
internal source and published that count in `WorkerRuntimeStatus`. The status
refresh aggregated the total collision counter but omitted the distinct-source
subset when building `ProcessStatus`. The process field therefore remained at
its lifecycle-initialized zero, and the Prometheus metric
`xpf_userspace_session_nat_reverse_key_collisions_distinct_src_total` reported
zero even when the collision condition occurred.

## What

- `userspace-dp/src/server/helpers/status.rs` now sums
  `WorkerRuntimeStatus.nat_reverse_key_collisions_distinct_src` into the
  matching `ProcessStatus` field during status refresh.
- Added `distinct_source_nat_collisions_are_aggregated` to
  `status_wiring_tests`; it pins the aggregation assignment and fails if it is
  removed.
- `userspace-dp/src/protocol/binding.rs` now uses the existing
  `crate::protocol::u64_is_zero` predicate for the high-water field and removes
  its duplicate `is_zero_u64` helper.
- No protocol field or wire representation changed; fixture regeneration is not
  applicable.

## Risk

The status JSON and metric retain their existing names and types. Only the
previously missing aggregate becomes nonzero when workers report distinct-source
collisions. The duplicate zero-check removal preserves the predicate's behavior.
