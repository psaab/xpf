# #15219 — host-auth closeout lifetime (A32-05)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-19
- **Status**: Closed by the #14969 generation fence (PR #15346,
  `b46a2cda23`); the write/install-seam residual found by hostile
  review of that PR is tracked and fixed by #15539
  (`docs/log/15539.md`).

## Mechanism (A32-05)

`runHostAuthCloseoutOwners` (`daemon_apply_hostauth.go`) returns on
`ctx.Done` while a timed-out owner goroutine keeps running; since
#14356 that abandonment also happens on live-daemon post-promotion
aborts (`closeoutHostAuthOnPostPromotionError`), so an old-config
credential write could land after a later commit — last-writer-wins
on credential files. Severity Low in the source report (needs an
owner running past the 30s budget plus a following commit); validator
consensus MATERIAL.

## Fix

- #14969 (PR #15346): monotonic `hostAuthGeneration14969` advanced by
  every closeout and every normal apply tail; each closeout owner runs
  under its launch generation (`runWithHostAuthGeneration14969`), and
  the exec seam plus the owner entries refuse a superseded owner with
  `errHostAuthStaleOwner14969`. Also fixed the 30s/20s comment (the
  20s figure is the per-command 15s+5s ceiling, not the 30s closeout
  budget). Full record: `docs/log/14969.md`.
- #15539: hostile review found the entry-only checks left every direct
  write below them unfenced (authorized_keys, sudoers, sshd drop-in,
  root keys, nft installs, provenance markers), the exec seam had a
  check-then-exec TOCTOU, and `reconcileAbsentLoginUsers` was
  unfenced. All seams now re-check immediately before their syscall,
  the exec seam kills in-flight commands on supersession, and the
  absent-user owner is fenced. Full record: `docs/log/15539.md`.

## Validation

- `hostauth_fence_14969_test.go`: abandoned owner fenced after a newer
  authority (exec seam + nft owner entries), uncaptured goroutines
  never refused.
- `hostauth_fence_15539_test.go`: mid-owner supersession at every
  direct write/install seam with current-generation controls.
- Acceptance shape: an apply after a timed-out closeout cannot land a
  credential/nft write from the abandoned owner once a newer authority
  has begun; the newest authority always wins.
