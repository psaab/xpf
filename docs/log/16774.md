# Issue #16774: post-CommitCheck commit failures retry on every edge with no failure backoff

## Why

#14724 made a non-promoting commit refund its global-budget slot so a
broken policy cannot starve healthy ones, but nothing throttled the
failing policy itself: every RPM edge re-ran a full commit attempt under
the config lock (probe: 48 attempts/min for 16 policies × 3 edges in a
frozen minute vs the README's "four commit attempts per rolling
minute"). The README throughput paragraph also contradicted the refund
paragraph: the budget caps promoted commits, not attempts.

## What

- `pkg/eventengine/engine.go`: new per-policy failure backoff
  (`failureBackoff` = `policyCooldown`, 30 s). `applyOnce` arms it
  (`armFailureBackoff`, revision-guarded like `armCooldown`) on both
  non-promoting post-CommitCheck arms — `commitFn` returning `(nil, err)`
  and the standalone `CommitWithDescriptionAs` failure — immediately
  after the #14724 refund. The `ErrClusterReadOnly` arms refund WITHOUT
  backoff, preserving the HA deferral. `policyRuntime.failedTrigger` is
  a separate anchor folded into `lastTrigger()`, so evaluate,
  `drainDeferred`, the commit-time `staleReason` gate, and the deferred
  worker's wake computation all throttle a failed policy exactly as a
  committed one, while `armCooldown` clears the anchor on success.
  Pre-reservation failures (bad apply / CommitCheck reject) keep their
  per-event rejected re-count and are not backoff-armed.
- `pkg/eventengine/README.md`: throughput bound now states precisely
  what is bounded — promoted commits ≤ 4/rolling minute (global budget);
  non-promoting attempts ≤ 1 per policy per 30 s failure backoff plus
  its parked replay — and notes the read-only refund-without-backoff.
- Tests: new `engine_failure_backoff_16774_test.go` (probe reproduction,
  healthy-starvation cell, read-only no-backoff, recovery clears anchor);
  `TestCommitFailureReleasesLatchAndRefundsBudget14724` recovery now
  goes through the backoff; `TestCommitDebt_GenuineRejection_CountsRejected_5063`
  now asserts committed-cooldown unarmed + failure backoff armed.

## Validation

- RED (mutation): removing both `armFailureBackoff` calls reproduces the
  probe exactly — `commitFn attempts in <60s = 48, want 16` — and fails
  the starvation/recovery cells; restoring returns green.
- GREEN: `go test ./pkg/eventengine/ -run '16774|14724|5063' -count=1`
  pass; neighbours `10871|15021|16070|3750|5311|16658|16650|16641|16848|15945` pass.
- Full `go test ./pkg/eventengine/ -count=1`: only pre-existing failures
  remain — the `*6673*` multivalue-leaf cells (#14660 drift, noted in
  docs/log/14724.md) and `TestEvaluateCooldownUnlockRedefinition16645`,
  both confirmed failing identically on pristine `origin/master`
  (ca0a6bf631). `go vet ./pkg/eventengine/` clean; gofmt clean.

## Risk

A persistently failing policy now retries at most once per 30 s (plus
the parked replay) instead of per edge; a transient failure that would
have recovered on the next 3 s edge waits for the backoff/parked
replay. The refund is unchanged, so healthy policies are never
budget-starved, and the read-only standby path is byte-for-byte the
#14724 behaviour. Residual: pre-reservation CommitCheck rejects remain
per-event (pre-#14724 behaviour, explicitly out of this issue's narrowed
scope).
