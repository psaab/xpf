## Why

The authority tests initially did not reach established-session handling: the
WAN binding is tagged logical interface `reth0.80`, but the generated frame
and descriptor metadata omitted VLAN 80, so the descriptor dropped the packet
as unknown VLAN.

A second issue masked a real mutation: the hand-built ICMP session had an
invalid forwarding-generation stamp while the harness published `(7, 9)`. Its
first hit therefore re-resolved the route using the arriving packet's ingress
context and saved the new decision, generation stamp, and `owner_rg_id` before
the #9519 Owner/Foreign decision. The fixture made this write equal the seed.
Forwarding revalidation is now persisted only after the hit is classified
Owner; a foreign packet can use its packet-local resolution for policy but
cannot re-stamp the session.

## What

- Updated the test module's TCP and ICMP WAN packet fixtures to carry VLAN 80 in both the Ethernet frame and descriptor metadata, with frame offsets and packet length adjusted.
- Made the ICMP snapshot include the intended WAN/LAN redundancy-group ownership, LAN connected address, and static neighbors; seeded WAN egress now transmits through its physical parent. The policy-event matrix seeds the live generation; a separate regression keeps the invalid default stamp, changes the live next-hop MAC, proves a denied foreign hit leaves decision/owner-RG/stamp untouched, then proves an Owner hit still persists revalidation.
- Deferred `#11373` forwarding-resolution persistence until after the `#9519` Owner verdict. Stale route resolution remains available to the current packet, but a Foreign packet cannot write the owner's route, generation stamp, or owner-RG index.
