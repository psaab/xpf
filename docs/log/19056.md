# Issue #19056: a controller wall-clock step could hide LAN traffic failures

## Why

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-09 PST
- **Action**: The Codex seat (review of #19041 at 80c25a683d) reproduced
  it.
  - The soak mapped controller time to LAN-host time with an offset measured
    once at preflight, and opened and closed every move window on the
    controller's current WALL time.
  - A 3 s backward step shortened the last window. A late 1,510 ms ping gap
    and a 1,500 ms aggregate outage fell outside it, and the move PASSed.
  - #19041 had moved deadlines and durations to the monotonic clock, but not
    the traffic attribution. This predates #19041.

## What

- **File(s)**: `test/incus/rg_move_soak.py`, `test/incus/rg_move_soak_test.py`,
  `docs/ha-rg-move-correctness.md`
- `Soak._anchor`: a (monotonic, wall) pair, taken when the preflight
  measures the LAN offset.
- `lan_time(t_mono)` maps a monotonic moment through it: `t_mono - m0 + w0 +
  lan_offset`.
- Each move's `t_issue`, the final window end and #19054's
  `traffic_end_lan` now come from `Soak.now` (monotonic).
- **Cells** (`WallClockStep19056`, `LateGapCluster`): Codex's reproduction
  with a 3 s backward step FAILs on the ping gap and the aggregate outage.
  The no-step control FAILs the same way.
- A step of the LAN host's own clock is still not detected. That is noted
  in the doc.

## Validation

See the commit message (runner RED/GREEN and mutants).

## Risk

Harness only. With no clock step, windows are the same as before to within
the moment the anchor was taken.
