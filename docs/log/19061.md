# Issue #19061: at SOAK_SLEEP=2 the post-landing window could take zero samples

## Why

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-09 PST
- **Action**: In the #18780 gate (tcp, `SOAK_MOVES=40 SOAK_SLEEP=2
  SOAK_FROM=1`), move 36 was VOID: "precondition: RG2 was on node0 before a
  move to node0". Both journals show RG2 on node1 from move 35's landing
  to move 36.
  - `_owners`, which feeds the next move's precondition, is refreshed only
    by `watch_window`. Its window ends at CLI return + `SOAK_SLEEP`.
  - Move 35's landing poll (landed 2.33 s, cli 0.81 s), the UDP checkpoint
    and `helpers()` used the whole 2 s, so the window took no sample.
  - Move 36's precondition was move 34's reading, and move 35's revert and
    post-landing dual-active checks never ran.

## What

- **File(s)**: `test/incus/rg_move_soak.py`, `test/incus/rg_move_soak_test.py`,
  `docs/ha-rg-move-correctness.md`
- `watch_window` always takes at least one sample (`while now < until or
  samples == 0`). A sample taken after the window ended marks the RG's
  `window_extended`.
- `RGMoveResult.window_samples` and `window_extended` are in `result.json`.
- **Cells** (`ZeroSampleWindow19061`):
  - `SlowHelperCluster` makes each helper read 1 fake second, so at sleep 2
    every window is used up. No move is VOID, every precondition is the
    previous move's target, and each window took one extended sample.
  - A normal window samples several times and is not extended.

## Validation

See the commit message (runner RED/GREEN and mutants).

## Risk

Harness only. A move whose window was used up is followed by its next
move one sample later than before. Normal windows are unchanged.
