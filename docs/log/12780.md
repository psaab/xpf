# Issue #12780 — retain denied exact-undo rollback candidates

## Why

A one-level exact-undo rollback drops the landed target's candidate capture before the rollback publishes. Candidates permitted by the rollback target were held separately for possible re-owing, but candidates denied by that target were returned as `kept` and ignored. If the rollback publish was rejected, a later commit that still forbade those candidates could not rediscover them from a policy diff, leaving stale sessions outside the invalidation debt.

## What

`retainPolicyInvalidationCaptureLocked` now merges denied (`kept`) candidates from rollback re-authorization into the rollback capture. Permitted candidates remain separately pending and are removed from the merged capture so an unpublished rollback cannot delete them. Thus rejected rollback retains denied candidates for the next superseding target, and a successful rollback still deletes candidates the target denies.

The regression drives C0 (deny candidate) → C1 (remove the rule; candidate deletion fails) → rejected exact undo to C0 → C2 (same forbidding policy as still-applied C1). C2 has no fresh policy diff against the helper; its injected delete failure proves the candidate was re-owed in debt. The accepted exact-undo control verifies permitted candidates still discharge without leaving debt.

## Risk

Denied candidates now remain in the normal captured-debt path until a successful delete; permitted candidates retain the existing delayed-discharge behavior. Capture merging deduplicates candidate identities through the existing merge helper. The change is limited to rollback re-authorization; fresh candidates and default-policy rematch handling retain their existing rules.
