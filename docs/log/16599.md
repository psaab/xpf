# #16599: OverlapTracker sender quota counts abandoned datagrams for the full TTL

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-25
- **Action**: `OverlapTracker::check_and_record_fragment_detailed`
  (`userspace-dp/src/fragment_overlap/mod.rs`) refused a new datagram
  with `ShardFull` whenever its sender held 8 incomplete entries in the
  shard, and the only reclaims ran at the zone reserve (completed
  anchors) and the 64-entry shard cap (completed, then another zone's
  incomplete). A sender whose tails were lost upstream — or dropped by
  the firewall itself — therefore squatted its whole 8-per-shard quota
  (128 entries across 16 shards, ≈4.3 abandoned/s saturates it) with
  settled head-only entries for the full 30 s TTL, and every surviving
  fragmented datagram to every destination was ShardFull-dropped,
  because the quota key is `(routing_domain, family, src)`. A receiver
  timing out those datagrams does not refuse the sender's new ones;
  #13937 already exempted completed entries on that principle, and
  abandoned entries were the unhandled twin. At the sender bound the
  tracker now reclaims the sender's OWN oldest settled-incomplete entry
  (`overlap_entry_incomplete`: `pending == 0`, not failed, ranges not
  covering a terminal end) instead of refusing, counted by the new
  `FRAG_OVERLAP_SAME_SENDER_INCOMPLETE_RECLAIMED`. Eligibility requires
  both the quota-counting predicate and own-ness: for arrival keys the
  same `overlap_sender`, for post-translation keys the same
  `fairness_origin` — so under the #14127 poisoned broadening another
  origin's entry that merely counts against the candidate is never the
  one evicted. Still-pending and failed (poisoned) anchors are never
  reclaimed, the sender never holds more than 8 incomplete entries per
  shard (the #10658 never-completing fill bound is unchanged), and the
  reclaimed slot decrements the zone count so the insert is not charged
  twice against the 32-entry reserve. The evicted anchor loses overlap
  protection for the rest of its TTL — the same trade the completed
  (#13937/#15103) and cross-zone incomplete (#15288) reclaims accept.
  One existing cell changed meaning: `post_nat_sender_quota_is_per_tenant_14127`
  expected tenant A's ninth settled datagram to fail closed; it now
  asserts the ninth is admitted by reclaiming A's own oldest entry,
  that A's surviving and tenant B's anchors stay protected, and that
  B's tail remains admitted — the per-tenant isolation the cell guards
  is preserved, only the superseded refusal expectation moved.
- **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/fragment_overlap/mod.rs`,
  `docs/log/16599.md`.
- **Validation**: New `sender_at_quota_reclaims_own_oldest_settled_incomplete_16599`
  (8 settled-incomplete across destinations fill the quota; the 9th is
  admitted reclaiming exactly the oldest, survivors + 9th still detect
  overlap, the evicted key replants; held-token still-pending and
  failed fills fail closed evicting nothing) and
  `sender_quota_under_tail_loss_16599` (acceptance shape: 1000/s for
  60 s at 1% tail loss, simulated `now_ns`, zero ShardFull refusals,
  600 abandoned heads recycled). RED-on-revert: with the reclaim
  eligibility neutered both fail (9th drops `ShardFull`; acceptance
  train desyncs after 256 completions under pre-fix refusal). Full
  `fragment_overlap` module 48/48 green, including
  `one_domain_cannot_starve_other_domains_10658` and
  `completed_datagram_rate_does_not_exhaust_sender_quota_13937`.
  Two `afxdp::tests_nat64_overlap_8115` mint-refusal failures under a
  broader `overlap` filter are pre-existing on the base commit
  (verified by re-running one on pristine master) and untouched here.
- **Residual**: The reclaim is deliberately scoped to the sender-quota
  branch only; a sender at quota whose oldest entries are all
  still-pending or failed still fails closed, as before. Real-world
  sender mix above ≈4.3 abandoned/s per sender remains the source
  report's inferred (not lab-measured) prevalence; the module-level
  behaviour is demonstrated by the tests above.
