# #12628 — Junos-valid policy children must not black out snapshots

## Why

#12217 treated every unrecognized direct child under `security policies` as a possibly hidden rulebase, so tolerant loads installed the synthetic poison carrier and userspace refused the entire snapshot. Junos-valid non-enforcement statements such as `traceoptions`, `policy-stats`, and `pre-id-default-policy` can occur at that container, turning a diagnostic/statistics knob into default-deny for all transit.

## What

- Recognize the three reported Junos-valid non-enforcement children in the closed-world schema, while keeping their nested syntax opaque to that schema.
- Keep a compatibility warning for these currently unconsumed statements, but exclude them from unknown-enforcement validation and the synthetic snapshot-poison carrier.
- Preserve strict rejection and tolerant snapshot refusal for other unknown direct policy children, including packed unknown-keyword forms.
- Add compile regressions for the three benign children, known controls, unknown enforcement-bearing children, and a packed enforcement tail; add a tolerant `Store.Load` regression with a valid permit policy.

## Validation

- RED-before: after adding the target cells, strict `CompileConfig` rejected all three Junos-valid statements as unknown `security policies` children.
- Targeted config matrix (ordinary/no-unknown baseline, benign statements, enforcement-bearing typos, and packed unknown tail) plus `TestCompactBlockEquivalenceInventory2419` passed.
- Targeted configstore `Store.Load` pair passed: traceoptions retains the valid policy and warns without a poison locator; unknown `globel` still poisons.
- Targeted userspace snapshot regression passed: `security policies traceoptions` with a permit policy emits neither `__unsupported__` nor `PolicyContentRejected`.
- Full `go test ./pkg/config -count=1 -timeout=30m` reported failures in inventory, API-key/multi-leaf, routing-instance, and slot-escape coverage gates, then timed out in `TestSchemaSpellingDifferentialGate` after 30 minutes. These failures did not name the changed policy-child paths; base attribution was not performed. The focused changed-area and compact-block gates passed separately.
- Full `go test ./pkg/configstore -count=1` and `go test ./pkg/dataplane/userspace -count=1` passed. `go build ./...`, scoped `go vet`, and the final focused config + compact-block regressions passed.

## Risk

The three explicit allowlisted statements remain accepted but are not consumed by the policy compiler; they produce warnings and do not affect forwarding policy snapshots. Other unknown direct children retain the fail-closed poison behavior. No live-cluster validation was performed.
