- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-25
  - **Action**: Fixed #16766 — a `preemptNowCh` token posted while a VRRP instance was MASTER (sync-hold release racing a master-down promotion) was never drained, because `stepMaster` does not select on `preemptNowCh`. After the next forced resignation (`ResignRG` → `stepMaster` `resignCh` → `becomeBackup` with the stashed priority 0 applied), the first `stepBackup` consumed the stale token and re-promoted the just-resigned node at priority 0 (`shouldPreemptObservedMaster` sees no live master), producing a spurious MASTER/BACKUP event pair, GARP churn, and priority-0 adverts beyond the 3-frame resign burst until a peer advert or the 10 s posture reconcile demoted it again. Fix enforces the channel-token lifetime invariant at every edge: `triggerPreemptNow` posts nothing while MASTER (state check under `vi.mu`, serialized against `setState`); `discardPendingPreemptNow` (new, `instance_preempt.go`) drains the channel and clears `forcePreemptOnce`; `becomeMaster` drains on every MASTER entry (`instance_transition.go`); and the `stepMaster` resign edge drains again (`instance.go`) to catch kicks/force flags that landed during the tenure (e.g. `ForceRGMaster` raced past its non-MASTER filter). New regression tests in `stale_preempt_token_16766_test.go` drive real `stepMaster`/`stepBackup` with the `sendPacketFn` seam: subject (release while MASTER → resign → first BACKUP iteration) stays `[BACKUP]` with exactly the 3-frame priority-0 burst and no extra adverts/events, control (release while BACKUP) still preempts via the #2082 shortcut, token+force injected during a MASTER tenure are drained at the resign edge, `becomeMaster` drains a BACKUP-queued kick, and manager `ReleaseSyncHold` on a MASTER posts no surviving token.
