  - **Risk**: the memos assume every worker-visible forwarding snapshot
    carries a distinct nonzero `publish_epoch` and is never mutated after
    publish; `publish_runtime_view` is the only production constructor of a
    worker-visible forwarding `Arc` and a cell pins the stamping. A future
    in-place mutation of a published snapshot would leave a destination-
    owner memo stale for that flow, or a neighbor duplicate coalesced for up
    to one refresh interval. Without an undo, a caller that passes
    `rollback_possible = false` and then rolls back restores nothing; the
    hit derives the flag from the fields its drop test reads.
