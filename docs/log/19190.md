# #19190: one-line ALG `<inert> disable` dropped `disable` (ALG stayed enabled)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-18
  - **Action**: Fixed. Found by lane T in the #18847 #8939 triage, and
    confirmed through `configstore.CheckText`, comparing the compiled
    `ALGConfig` of the packed and split spellings, independent of the #8939
    instrument.
    - **First-bad commit:** 0289d5b8f5 (#16814). Every inert sub-statement
      "owned" the rest of its packed line, so a bodiless flag swallowed the
      operator's trailing `disable`.
    - **First attempt, abandoned:** I refused `<inert> ... disable` as
      ambiguous and honored any trailing `disable`. That broke #16814's own
      contract (`TestALGInertBodyDoesNotDisable16814`): `traceoptions flag
      disable` is a legitimate traceoptions body. It also missed the
      flat-set shape. A tree dump showed `disable` nested under the inert
      statement's node (a child of a flag, or `["1024","disable"]` under a
      valued statement), not in the class tail.
    - **Fix:** the inert statements carry a value arity (flag 0, valued 1,
      everything else owns the rest). `algScanTokens19190` and
      `validateALGTokens19190` apply it to the packed tail and to a
      flattened flag/valued child line (the flat-set shape). Statements of
      unrecorded arity (for example `bulk-call-mode`) keep the old
      container behaviour.
    - **Refinements, from the #8939 rows still listed after the redesign:**
      - a valued statement never consumes a literal `disable` (dns
        `doctoring disable`);
      - compile records the inert statements along a flag's or valued
        statement's whole flat-set line, so `allow-mismatch-ip-address
        disable ftps-extension` keeps the ftps-extension advisory.
    - **Residual:** tftp and pptp declare only `traceoptions`, a container,
      so `traceoptions disable` stays its body by #16814's contract.
  - **File(s)**: `pkg/config/schema_security_alg.go`,
    `pkg/config/compiler_security_alg.go`, the two cells,
    `docs/config-schema.md`, `docs/log/19190.md`
