# #16570: SessionClose/SessionCreate queue budget released on channel drain, not ACK trim

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
- **Action**: The dataplane-event queue budget (per-kind
  `ceil((8192/2)/5) = 820`, total 4096) was acquired at emit and released only
  when an ACK trimmed the frame from the replay buffer (plus replay
  eviction, enqueue failure, and shutdown). The connected drain moved frames
  from the channel to the write backlog + replay buffer with no release, and
  the Go ACK ticks every 100 ms, so any close/create burst above 820 frames
  within one ACK interval shed `QueueFull` — defeating the #14609 "below
  channel capacity a close is never rate-shed" contract for flowexport
  (NetFlow/IPFIX closes and create-gated flows, #16303) and
  `then log session-close` RT_FLOW records. The budget now releases exactly
  once when a frame leaves the bounded channel: in the steady-state drain
  (`drain_channel_into_write_buf`, before the replay push so a wrap eviction
  triggered by that push cannot take the slot), in the dormant drain-request
  channel drain (`handle_drain_request`), and in the shutdown channel drain
  (`drain_remaining`, unchanged). Replay retention holds no slot — it stays
  bounded by `REPLAY_BUFFER_CAPACITY` and counted by
  `frames_replay_evicted` on wrap (#2382, unchanged) — so
  `pop_replay_frame` / eviction / `release_replay_dataplane_event_queue_budget`
  no longer release the budget (they would double-release and trip the #1826
  underflow guard). Tests that injected telemetry frames directly into the
  replay buffer no longer seed the budget (`push_budgeted_replay_frame` keeps
  its name/shape but is now a plain push; `acquire_for_test` is retained for
  channel-exit releases). No admission cap, rate limit (including the
  SessionClose 8192/s + burst-8192 GCRA), counter, or HA session-sync
  (type-2, unbudgeted) behaviour changed; no gate weakened — the in-channel
  bound is identical, only its release point moved to channel exit.

- **Validation**: New acceptance cell
  `test_session_close_burst_5000_without_ack_16570` drives the real
  `emit_session_close_rt_flow` producer and the real
  `drain_channel_into_write_buf` interleaved with no ACK: RED without the
  drain release (write backlog short by shed frames), GREEN after
  (`queue_full == 0`, `sent == 5000`). The #14609 cell
  `test_session_close_survives_production_rate_14609` now drains through the
  real production drain instead of a test-side `rx.try_recv()` + manual
  release (the test-fidelity defect). Renamed budget cells pin the new
  invariant through the real connected loop: budget stays held while a frame
  sits in the channel, releases on drain with no ACK, ACK-trim still works
  and is not a second release, and replay retention/eviction hold and
  release nothing while #2382 eviction counting is unchanged.
- **Residual**: Production shed fraction vs ACK timing was never lab-measured
  (research noted the same); the fix is component-verified only. Bursts above
  the GCRA (8192/s sustained per zone) or above channel/write-backlog
  capacity still shed, as designed, and remain counted.
