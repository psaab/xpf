# #13972: export control-plane session removals

## Change

Locally initiated session clears and policy deletions now produce one close
delta from the removed forward session, carrying its traffic counters, timestamps,
identity, and a clear/policy-delete reason. Peer-originated deletes and peer
replicas remain silent, preventing duplicate close records. Go renders the two
new close reasons as `clear` and `policy delete`.
