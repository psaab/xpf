- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-02
- **Action**: Strict `CompileConfig` accepted functional-zone management configurations whose brace-elided `host-inbound-traffic` compiled weaker than the braced twin. The compact-header form (`functional-zone management { ... host-inbound-traffic system-services ssh; }`) produced an empty `SystemServices`; the two-level form (`functional-zone management host-inbound-traffic system-services ssh;`) produced nil `HostInboundTraffic`. - **Cause**: `managementFunctionalZoneInstances15668` passed the compact-header node to the shared zone-body compiler without constructing the omitted management/HIB body. The ordinary security-zone reader reaches equivalent input through the compact normalizer; functional-zone management is a separate AST path.
- **Fix**: The functional-zone management reader now schema-expands packed host-inbound-traffic children into braced-equivalent management bodies only. It preserves quote/bracket provenance, leaves unrelated functional-zone packed properties untouched, and feeds the same normalized body to the compiler and strict zone validators. Census probes use accepted host-inbound values so #18771 can measure the fixed sites rather than classify their generic samples as `UNCLASSIFIED`; the two fixed zone-level rows are removed while per-interface rows remain unchanged.
