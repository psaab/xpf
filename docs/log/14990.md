# #14990 — configstore: stop AuthorizeCommitAs minting unexpiring internal authority for unheld named sessions (D38-02)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-18
  - **Action**: RED first. New cells in
    `pkg/configstore/commit_authority_14990_test.go` fail on
    `origin/master` (5b3b02a61f):
    - `AuthorizeCommit(named, not in config mode) = nil (IsInternal=true)`,
      want refusal.
    - `AuthorizeCommit(evicted after ForceExit) = <nil>`, want
      `ErrConfigLockedByOther`.
  - **Action**: Fix, scoped to the cited mechanism.
    - `AuthorizeCommitAs` (`pkg/configstore/commit_authority.go`): only
      `sessionID == ""` (explicit internal/system caller) receives
      `authorityInternal`. A non-empty session must be the recorded holder
      (`configDir` and `effectiveHolderLocked() == sessionID`) and receives
      the holder-bound, epoch-bound authority; otherwise refused with
      `ErrConfigLockedByOther`. Previously the `!configDir` /
      empty-holder bypasses minted `InternalCommitterAs`, which
      `verifyCommitAuthorityLocked` accepts unconditionally and without
      expiry, so a non-holder/evicted named session could promote another
      operator's candidate at any later time via
      `CommitWithDescriptionGenAs`/`CommitConfirmedGenAs` (daemon
      `commitWithGenBinding` path).
    - `ForceExitConfigure` (`pkg/configstore/store_lock.go`): records the
      evicted holder via `markReclaimedHolderLocked(effectiveHolderLocked())`
      before clearing holder fields, mirroring `reclaimStaleLockLocked`
      (#9632). Its log line now reports the effective holder (exclusive
      holders were previously logged as empty). Re-entering configure
      (shared or exclusive) clears the mark, as before.
    - `pkg/configstore/store.go`: `reclaimedHolders` comment now cites
      both the lease reclaim and the force-exit eviction.
  - **Action**: Preserved neighbouring behaviour (controls in the new
    cells and existing suites):
    - Explicit internal (`""` / `InternalCommitter()`) still commits an
      unowned internal candidate and promotes it.
    - A never-evicted named session may still *edit* an unowned
      local-CLI candidate (#5059 `*As` mutators); it may not commit it.
    - The recorded holder still receives a bound (non-internal)
      authority and commits; re-entering after force-eviction restores
      `AuthorizeCommit`.
    - #9632 reclaimed-holder refusal on the internal candidate is
      unchanged and now also covers force-evicted holders.
  - **Action**: One neighbouring fixture updated:
    `pkg/api/config_commit_test.go` `newAPIEBPFRejectStore` now holds the
    candidate with `testRESTConfigSessionID` (via `EnterConfigureSession` +
    `LoadSetAs`), because a named REST session may no longer commit an
    unowned internal candidate; the eBPF-retirement assertions are
    unchanged and pass. gRPC/CLI eBPF fixtures use the internal (`""`)
    session and are unaffected.
  - **Action**: Pre-existing failure noted, not caused here:
    `pkg/grpcapi` `TestSessionFilterKeepsEveryZoneInterface6960` (and
    siblings) fail identically on unmodified `origin/master` (3a10a520d5)
    with a #14574 vlan-tagging commit-check error.
  - **Action**: Docs: `pkg/configstore/README.md` documents the #14990
    rule next to the #9632 paragraph in the authority-bound commit
    section.
