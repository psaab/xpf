# #18815 — ARP-learn sender fixtures satisfy the on-link contract

## Why

PR #15624 / #15247 (`50e1a767fd`) intentionally rejects off-link ARP/NDP advertisements from XSK neighbor learning. Three older ARP tests used senders outside the ingress logical interface's connected prefixes, so they no longer reached the learn behavior their key assertions are meant to pin.

## What

Changed the known-VID trunk test sender to `10.0.50.50` (on `reth0.50`'s `10.0.50.0/24`) and the unitless-port untagged sender to `10.0.60.10` (on `reth2`'s `10.0.60.0/24`, not its own `.1` address). The two-VLAN distinct-key test retains one identical sender IP and all three `(12, ip)`, `(13, ip)`, and not-`(11, ip)` assertions; its fixture now gives both logical units a shared connected `172.16.99.0/24` and uses sender `172.16.99.99`. No product code or learn-key assertion changed.

## Validation

Before the fixture repairs, all three exact tests failed at their learn assertions. Afterward, each named cell passed individually: `unknown_vid_and_vid_zero_on_tagged_trunk_are_recycled_before_arp_learning_11297`, `unknown_vid_on_unitless_port_is_recycled_before_arp_learning_10656`, and `arp_two_vlans_same_ip_distinct_logical_keys_2370`. The adjacent controls `off_link_arp_na_not_learned_and_xsk_learn_ages_15247` and `ndp_learns_vlan_neighbor_under_logical_ifindex_2370` each passed. The full `poll_stages::tests` module passed (78 tests). The full prerouting scope module ran 19 tests: 17 passed and two unrelated VID-0 cells for #18814 failed at their stale child assertions (`explicit_untagged_unit0_keeps_vid_zero_fallback_11297` and `vid_zero_is_unknown_on_unanimous_and_contested_tagged_trunks_11297`).

Mutant proof: replacing the ARP on-link conjunct with `true` made `off_link_arp_na_not_learned_and_xsk_learn_ages_15247` fail at `off-link ARP sender must not be learned`; after restoring the on-link conjunct, the control passed.

## Risk

The extra shared prefix exists only in the two-VLAN test snapshot and preserves the identical-IP collision premise while letting both ingress logicals exercise the production on-link gate. The two unrelated #18814 assertions were left untouched.
