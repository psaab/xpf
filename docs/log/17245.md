# #17245 — remove dated verifier figures from `lib.rs`

## Why

`userspace-xdp/src/lib.rs` carried dated #15854 instruction-count and headroom
figures beside its own rule that verifier measurements belong in a dated record.
The durable explanation is the relationship: the outlined LAND and readiness
paths avoid repeating map walks and trace verification across calling states.
The historical measurements already live in `docs/log/15854.md`.

The #8241 census did not catch every source format: its count matcher required a
comma, and a percentage was considered only when `headroom` appeared on the same
line. A nearby floor could also make an otherwise bare figure look grounded.

## What

- Replaced the #15854 figures in the LAND and readiness comments and the
  `land_gate` explanation with the durable structural rule and a pointer to
  `docs/log/15854.md`; the historical measurements are not restated in `lib.rs`.
- Expanded the census to recognize comma-optional counts and `~NNNk` shorthand,
  and to find `headroom` across adjacent lines for percentage figures. Added
  hermetic fixtures for an uncommaed count, approximate count, and wrapped
  percentage. The expanded matcher also exposed historical deltas and kernel
  charge variation; their allowlist entries now document why they are not
  current object headroom measurements and have dedicated control fixtures.
- Regenerated the verifier verdict and source manifest after comment-only Rust
  edits.

## Risk

confirmed the object bytes are unchanged. Future verifier figures in normative
texts still require a measurement commit or a named floor unless they are an
explicitly documented historical differential or other accepted exception.
