# #17251 — correct the `parse_l4` cost-model comment

## Why

The `parse_l4` rationale at base `5e65228f29e113394a4e728357830b611286d61a`
made false claims about the current call graph and inline cost. It said:

- "`parse_l4` is reached from `parse_ipv4`, `parse_ipv6` and both GRE inner
  classifiers";
- "`#[inline(always)]` on a fn reached from FOUR call sites multiplies its code
  by four" and "Grow `parse_l4` and you pay 4x";
- "`classify_native_gre_inner*` two hundred lines up" and "`parse_l4` is small
  and reached from four, so outlining bought nothing and paid four walks";
- "Callers with no declared bound (IPv6, GRE-inner) pass `data_end`".

The actual source call expressions are two in `parse_ipv4`, one in
`parse_ipv6`, and one in `first_fragment_l4`; neither GRE inner classifier
calls `parse_l4`. The two IP parsers are each `#[inline(always)]` at two
callers, and `first_fragment_l4` is itself `#[inline(always)]` into
`parse_ipv4`, so the four source sites produce eight inlined copies, not four.
The same contrast described both GRE classifiers as deliberately
`#[inline(never)]`, but only the IPv4 classifier carries that attribute; the
IPv6 classifier has no forced inline attribute.

The call to `write_userspace_meta` also carried two consecutive copies of the
`pkt_len` rationale. The removed first copy said "`pkt_len` is stored here
rather than passed" before the call. That location was stale: the actual store
is after the call, and the retained second copy correctly says "Stored AFTER
the call" and explains why the callee's whole-struct store requires that order.

## What

- Rewrote the caller list to match the four real `parse_l4(` call expressions
  and removed the stale GRE-caller claim from the `declared_end` rationale.
- Replaced the four-copy claim with the actual expansion: two direct IPv4
  sites, one first-fragment site inlined into IPv4, and one IPv6 site, each
  duplicated across two inlined IP-parser callers: `(2 + 1 + 1) × 2 = 8`.
- Named `classify_native_gre_inner_ipv4` and
  `classify_native_gre_inner_ipv6` explicitly, corrected their distance to
  roughly 1,400–1,500 lines above `parse_l4`, and distinguished IPv4's
  `#[inline(never)]` from IPv6's lack of a forced inline attribute.
- Removed only the stale pre-call `pkt_len` rationale copy. No Rust code,
  attribute, generated object, manifest, or gate-verdict token was edited.
