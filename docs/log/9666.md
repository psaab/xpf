# #9666 — frr: every `protocols isis export` renders `redistribute <proto>` under `router isis`, which isisd's grammar rejects

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-10
  - **Action**: Rendered IS-IS redistribution in isisd's grammar,
    `redistribute <ipv4|ipv6> <proto> <level-1|level-2> [route-map X]`
    (FRR stable/10.6 `isis_cli.c` `isis_redistribute_cmd`). The family and the
    level are mandatory there, so every IS-IS export used to fail the managed
    reload.
    - `resolveRedistribute` (pkg/frr/redistribute.go) is split into a
      structured core, `redistributeEntries`, which returns (source,
      route-map) pairs. All the existing decisions and warnings stay in it:
      direct→connected, the self skip, the #9510 family filter, the #4481
      fail-closed redistribute alias, and the skip warnings. The core feeds
      two formatters, and the sanitizeFRRValue / frrName belts sit where each
      formatter writes its line. `formatRedistEntries` keeps the OSPF, OSPF6,
      BGP and RIP lines byte-identical.
    - `resolveISISRedistribute` / `isisRedistributeLines` render the isisd
      form. The families come from `frrRedistSourceAFI` (ospf/rip IPv4 only,
      ospf6/ripng IPv6 only, the rest both). xpf enables both
      `ip router isis` and `ipv6 router isis` on its circuits (#8450).
  - **Action**: Level decision, which the issue left open. The level follows
    the router's is-type, canonicalised by the same `CanonicalISISLevel` as
    the is-type line (#8446):
    - level-1 renders level-1;
    - level-2 renders level-2;
    - **level-1-2 renders one line per level**, because a router in both
      levels redistributes into both and each FRR line names exactly one;
    - an unset or unrecognized value renders the narrow level-2 default,
      never both.
  - **Action**: A prior cell that pinned the defect was corrected.
    TestGenerateProtocols_ISISExport asserted `redistribute connected\n`
    under `router isis`, exactly the form isisd rejects. It now asserts the
    ipv4 and ipv6 level-2 lines, and the absence of the plain form.
  - **Action**: The #9510 census was extended.
    TestRedistNodeAFICoversEveryCallSite_9510 went blind to the IS-IS site
    once IS-IS moved to `resolveISISRedistribute`. It now counts those calls
    as "isis" only after proving the function passes the literal "isis" into
    `redistributeEntries`. It also requires that core to have exactly the two
    wrappers as callers.
  - **Action**: New cells in pkg/frr/isis_redistribute_grammar_9666_test.go:
    - every is-type with a dual-family source, including level-1-2 → four
      lines, and the unset and garbage defaults;
    - IPv4-only (ospf) and IPv6-only (ospf6, ripng) sources;
    - direct normalised to connected;
    - self-redistribution still dropped;
    - a policy-statement export → one route-map line per source, family and
      level;
    - no plain-form line under `router isis` for any source or is-type (a
      line-grammar regex);
    - the OSPF line unchanged.

    pkg/frr/README.md documents the form. A lab `vtysh` parse check was not
