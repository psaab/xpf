# #12905 — flowless translated cold-route liveness (D10-03)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-14; branch `fix/12905-campaign-pub`, worktree `wt-12905`, base `origin/master` `39a53bda3`.
- **Action**: Stop dropping flowless packets whose address-only static-NAT / DNAT / inbound-NPTv6 destination rewrite was selected but whose post-translation resolution lands on `NoRoute` / `MissingNeighbor`. Route them through the normal cold arms — zone policy on the POST-translation tuple, then the missing-neighbor probe + `pending_neigh` park (translated replay) or the #7480 NoRoute adjudication + translated kernel reinject — keeping every untranslated fence and every deny doctrine fail-closed.

## Defect (research D10-03, validated 3-way)

`flowless_base_resolution` (`poll_descriptor/flowless_verdict.rs`) returned `None` whenever `nat.rewrite_dst.is_some()` and the resolution disposition was `NoRoute | MissingNeighbor`. The only caller (`poll_descriptor/mod.rs`, flowless session-miss arm) treats `None` as an immediate fail-closed drop (`nat_flowless_untranslated_dropped`, or `nat_frag_untranslated_dropped` for a real non-first fragment) and recycles the descriptor — before zone policy evaluation, before the neighbor probe, before `pending_neigh` buffering, and before the slow-path reinject. Net effect: a flowless static-DNAT/DNAT/NPTv6 flow whose next-hop neighbor was unresolved (every cold start, every ARP/NDP cache flush) or whose translated destination had no userspace-FIB route was blackholed with a NAT-fence attribution, while the untranslated population kept its probe/defer/retry liveness. The #11435 rationale ("those paths cannot carry the rewrite") was false: `pending_neigh` replay applies the parked `decision.nat` via `rewrite_forwarded_frame_in_place`, and the reinject builder applies it via `extract_l3_packet_with_nat`.

## Change

- `flowless_verdict.rs` — `flowless_base_resolution` no longer drops a translated cold-route miss; it returns the resolution plus the recorded NAT so the disposition arms own the handling. The `Untranslatable` fail-closed fence (#11435 checksum/port-map whitelist) is untouched.
- `poll_descriptor/mod.rs`, MissingNeighbor arm — the flowless policy gate adjudicates the POST-translation destination (mirrors the ForwardCandidate flowless gate and the #2345 cold-path doctrine: the verdict must not depend on whether the neighbor happened to be resolved), and the #6122/#10679 NAT fence skips its destination half when the decision already carries the rewrite, probing only the source side (`flowless_source_nat_requires_translation`, the same split the ForwardCandidate translated fence uses).
- `poll_descriptor/mod.rs`, NoRoute arm — the flowless adjudication tuple is likewise post-translation, and `flowless_no_route_requires_nat_translation` now receives the recorded decision.
- `poll_descriptor/frag_assoc.rs` — `flowless_no_route_requires_nat_translation` branches on the decision: translated ⇒ source-side-only probe per candidate egress (the reinject applies the destination rewrite); default ⇒ the unchanged both-sides probe. The no-egress-identity fail-closed fallback tail is unchanged for both populations.
- `tests_txn_flow_cache.rs` — replaced the #11435 cold-drop assertions (which pinned the defect) with six #12905 regressions: static-DNAT GRE parks translated on MissingNeighbor; dynamic-DNAT non-first fragment parks translated; inbound-NPTv6 GRE parks translated; static-DNAT NoRoute reinjects with the translation visible in the captured TUN bytes; a NoRoute default-deny that never reaches the kernel; and a fail-closed matrix (default-deny and pre-NAT-VIP-only policies deny BEFORE any probe/park/reinject — counted as policy denies, never NAT-fence drops — while a post-NAT permit parks).
- `README.md` (#11435 section) — documents the new cold-path carry behavior.

## Residual / risk

- The DNAT rule-use counter is bumped at committed TX/install sites; a parked cold representative that never transmits (neighbor never resolves) intentionally does not bump it, matching the no-NAT park behavior — `PendingNeighPacket` carries no counter, and adding one is counter-parity polish, not correctness.
- The flowless cold arms now park/reinject translated traffic, so a permitted flow's first packet can take one retry-sweep dwell (~ms) before TX; duplicates per `(egress_ifindex, next_hop)` keep the #1771 one-frame bound.
- The flow-BACKED NoRoute adjudication still evaluates the wire (pre-NAT) destination tuple; only the flowless half was aligned here because that is the D10-03 population. A flow-backed parity pass is a separate ticket.
