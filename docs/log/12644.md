# #12644 — defer synced-session writes until authority

- **Issue**: #12644 — synced-session promotion writes decision/owner-RG before verdict.
- **Branch**: `fix/12644-campaign-pub`
- **Worktree**: `/var/worktrees/campaign-pub/wt-12644`

## Why

`resolve_flow_session_decision_with_conntrack` runs before the poll path's
`session_hit_authority` decision. A promotable `SyncImport` or
`SharedMaterialize` hit that re-resolves to `ForwardCandidate` could therefore
be promoted before the packet was classified Owner or Foreign. Promotion
updates the local session decision and metadata (`owner_rg_id` and
`fabric_ingress`), changes origin, republishes shared/BPF state, and replicates
an upsert. A foreign packet could thus rewrite a session it does not own.

Shared-hit materialization and translated transient-hit purge were on the same
pre-verdict path, so those writes are deferred alongside promotion.

## What

- `ResolvedFlowSessionDecision` carries deferred promotion, shared
  materialization, and transient purge effects. Resolver calculations remain
  packet-local; they do not persist those effects before authority is known.
- `poll_descriptor` consumes deferred purge/materialize/promote work only for
  `HitAuthority::Owner`; the mutation boundaries independently reject
  `Foreign`/`Unresolved` verdicts before consuming deferred state.
  Stale-route revalidation is likewise applied only at the Owner site.
- The direct `resolve_flow_session_decision` wrapper now requires an explicit
  verdict and routes all promotion, purge, materialization, and revalidation
  through those gates. Non-poll test callers specify `Owner`; it cannot
  silently apply effects without a verdict.
- `resolve_flow_session_decision_promotes_stale_fabric_shared_hit_to_local_owner_path`
  exercises the deferred boundaries: Foreign leaves the worker table, shared
  origin, owner-RG metadata, and fabric-ingress metadata unchanged; Owner then
  materializes and promotes.
- `tests_probe_12644_kill.rs` drives the real descriptor/poll path: foreign
  shared hits (deny and permit postures) do not materialize or promote, the
  Owner control still does, and a translated transient purge waits for Owner.

## Risk

Foreign hits still use the resolver's packet-local forwarding decision for that
packet, but do not materialize, purge, promote, publish, or replicate session
state. Owner hits retain the prior transitions after the verdict. No wire
format or protocol behavior changes.
