# #9395: policy for an oversized DF-clear IPv4 datagram

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11
  - **Action**: Measured the wire fate on the loss userspace cluster: build 5f28e89ac, one `with-cluster.sh` lock cell, fw0 primary. The sender was cluster-userspace-host (LAN); the receiver was xpf-mouse-target, 172.16.80.201 on VLAN 80. Delivery was read at the receiver (UDP receipts by size, `/proc/net/snmp`), not from ping replies. The sender's PMTU cache was flushed before every cell.
    - **Baseline**, every MTU 1500: DF-clear 1450- and 1350-byte UDP and ICMP were delivered. The 1422-byte payload arrived 11 times for 10 sent (one duplicate, not investigated).
    - **`set interfaces reth0 unit 80 family inet mtu 1400`**: kernel `ge-0-0-2.80` was 1400 and physical `ge-0-0-2` stayed 1500.
      - DF-clear 1450-byte UDP 10/10 and ICMP 5/5 were delivered WHOLE: receiver `Ip.ReasmReqds` stayed 0, and mlx5 `tx_xsk_err` / `tx_xdp_err` stayed 0.
      - `egress_mtu_exceeded_forwarded_no_df` was recorded for UDP and ICMP (len 1464), read via `show chassis cluster data-plane interfaces`.
      - Positive control: DF-set 1450 drew `Frag needed and DF set (mtu = 1400)`, so the decision really saw 1400.
    - **`set interfaces reth0 mtu 1400`** did not change the RETH member link, which stayed 1500. The planned "datagram larger than the physical link" cell therefore could not be reached.
  - **Decision**: keep forwarding, with the exception recorded, decided from the plain-forward measurement. A drop would break the measured working path, and a downstream router that cannot carry the datagram may fragment it (RFC 791).
    - The decision covers what this arm does, not every path's outcome. What the measurement does not cover is stated once, on `ForwardOversizeNoDf`.
    - The exception is sampled (see `record_exception`), so it shows that such decisions happen but is neither a count nor a loss counter.
    - `oversized_v4_without_df_forwards` and `oversized_no_df_is_forwarded_and_counted_9328` now state keep-forwarding as the decided policy rather than a placeholder.
  - **#2301 re-read**: its Impact names "oversized UDP, ICMP, ESP, GRE, non-first fragments" with no DF qualifier. For DF-clear IPv4 on a plain forward it does not hold when the link carries the datagram: one above only a configured MTU was delivered in the measured topology. The paths where it can still hold are the ones `ForwardOversizeNoDf` lists.
  - **Filed** (neither queued):
    - #9757 (MEDIUM): deleting a unit's family mtu leaves the VLAN sub-interface kernel MTU, and the userspace egress MTU, at the old value;
    - #9758 (LOW, enhancement): no IPv4 egress fragmenter for the physical-link case;
    - #9761 (LOW): an interface-level `mtu` on a `vlan-tagging` interface whose zone references are all tagged units never reaches the parent link, because `planPhysDesired` skips tagged references. This is why the physical-MTU cell was unreachable.
  - **Cluster**: because of #9757, the cell's restore left `reth0.80` at kernel MTU 1400 on both nodes from 02:30:37 to 02:34:42. It was repaired in a second lock cell (commit an explicit mtu 1500, then delete it), verified on both nodes, and team-lead was told about the window.
