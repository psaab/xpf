# #17088 — Single-source the NAT session-key transform (`translated_session_key` parity)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-27
  - **Action**: STEP-0 confirmed the defect live on current master
    (f681f07dd7): `userspace-dp/src/session/key.rs` carried three
    verbatim NAT64 AF/protocol remaps (`forward_wire_key`,
    `reverse_wire_key`, `reverse_session_key`) while
    `translated_session_key` had none, and its ICMP arm read only
    `rewrite_src_port` — so under a REVERSE decision
    (`NatDecision::reverse` moved the forward `rewrite_src_port` into
    `rewrite_dst_port`, nat/mod.rs:481-492) the reverse ICMP-SNAT
    alias kept the translated id, and NAT64 aliases were mixed-family
    keys (AF_INET with a V6 `src_ip`) no packet carries. The alias is
    inserted/removed/validated at session/mod.rs
    `index_forward_nat_key_parts` / `remove_forward_nat_index_parts`
    and session/lookup.rs:496,2160 — none gated to TCP/UDP DNAT.
    Replaced the four hand-kept builders with one
    `#[inline] fn nat_applied_key(key, nat, Dir::{Same, Reversed},
    Disc::{Keep, Reverse}, Domain::{Keep, Zero})` plus one
    `nat64_family_protocol` helper in key.rs; `forward_wire_key`,
    `translated_session_key`, `reverse_wire_key` and
    `reverse_session_key` are now one-line wrappers. The shared ICMP
    rule is the direction-agnostic
    `rewrite_src_port.or(rewrite_dst_port)` (#4074: at most one is set
    per ICMP flow). `reverse_canonical_key` is untouched (no NAT
    applied). Behaviour change is confined to
    `translated_session_key`: ICMP alias under a reverse SNAT decision
    now names the original id, and NAT64 aliases are single-family
    (pre-translation tuple in the forward family). All other builders
    are byte-identical by construction (pinned by the twin controls).
  - **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/session/key.rs`,
