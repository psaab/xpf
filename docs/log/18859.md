# Issue #18859 — include the generation-guarded DNS swap in the census

## Why

`TestActiveSnapshotPublishCoversEverySwap9905` pinned a count of nine compiled-pointer swaps. `PublishRefreshedDNSForGeneration` in `store_gen.go` adds a tenth legitimate swap: it assigns `s.compiled = refreshed` and immediately publishes the active snapshot. That path was introduced by e3f5ebba9c (#17744), so the inventory count had drifted while the per-swap publication check remained correct.

## What

- Updated the structural census to expect 10 swaps and named `PublishRefreshedDNSForGeneration` as the 10th, citing its origin in #17744 (e3f5ebba9c).
- Left the per-swap publish-follow check unchanged.

## Validation

- `go test ./pkg/configstore/ -run 9905 -count=1` passed, including the active-snapshot test file and the census guard.
- Mutant check: removing `s.publishActiveLocked()` after `s.compiled = refreshed` in `store_gen.go` made `TestActiveSnapshotPublishCoversEverySwap9905` fail at `store_gen.go:145`; restored the publication and reran the 9905 selection successfully.

## Risk

Test-only census correction; no production behavior changed.
