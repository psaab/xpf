## Finding and fix

`packedBody` expands the compact `ids-option` tail into synthesized family siblings for `compileScreen`, but the #5180 duplicate-family walk inspected only the authored `ids-option.Children`. A repeated packed family therefore bypassed the duplicate gate while `compileScreen`'s `FindChild` reader silently ignored later siblings. The family walk now inspects the same `packedBody` expansion before checking duplicate names, preserving the existing strict-error and tolerant-warning behavior.

The regression covers all five recognized families, triples, nested hierarchical duplicates, a packed distinct-family control, lenient warnings, and flat `set` merging.

## Risk

The change is limited to duplicate detection for `security screen ids-option` family siblings. It reuses `packedBody` and the existing diagnostic path, so hierarchical behavior and strict/tolerant policy remain unchanged; packed repeats now receive the same explicit treatment as hierarchical repeats.
