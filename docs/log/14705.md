- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-17
  - **Action**: Gated the heartbeat duplicate-node-id warning behind HMAC
    verification on keyed nodes (#14705). `heartbeatReceiver.readLoop`
    previously called `NoteDuplicateNodeIDHeartbeat` before `admitFrame`
    ran HMAC, so an on-link sender spoofing the configured peer IP (the
    #6888 pin is source-IP only, not an authentication boundary) could
    forge the EventRG history entry and keep the shared 30s
    `duplicateNodeIDWarningDueLocked` limiter fresh, suppressing the
    authenticated `NoteDuplicateNodeIDBeacon` diagnostic. Added
    `duplicateNodeIDAuthenticated` (HMAC-only check against every accepted
    key; no liveness/election/replay-state side effects; unkeyed nodes
    keep the legacy dual-accept warning) and gated the readLoop call.
    Regression `TestRecvLoop_DuplicateNodeIDHeartbeatRequiresHMAC_14705`
    drives the real readLoop over UDP from the peer IP: unsigned, wrong-key
    and tampered frames produce no history and do not consume the limiter
    (a genuine beacon immediately after still warns); signed primary and
    rotation-alt-key duplicates still warn, are discarded, and do not
    consume replay state; unkeyed duplicates still warn. RED on revert,
    GREEN with the fix; documented in `pkg/cluster/README.md`.
