# #18168 — correct the IPv4 RETH first-hop expectation in connectivity MTR checks

## Why

The IPv4 cluster MTR checks expected the LAN RETH VIP (`10.0.61.1`) to be
hop 1. The current loss-cluster diagnosis instead captured `169.254.2.1` as
hop 1 for both the WAN-gateway and public IPv4 destination probes, while
`ip route get` still showed the VIP as the LAN host's gateway. The RETH's
link-local address is the TTL-expired source reported by MTR; the routed VIP
is not that source. This stale expectation predates `ca05433c7b` and is
confirmed in [#18168 comment 6464012802](https://github.com/psaab/xpf/issues/18168#issuecomment-6464012802).

## What

- Changed only the two cluster IPv4 `mtr_test` calls (WAN gateway and
  `1.1.1.1`) to require an IPv4 link-local (`169.254.0.0/16`) first hop rather
  than the RETH VIP.
- Kept the path check non-vacuous: the requested target must appear as the
  final hop row at `0.0%` loss, and it must be beyond hop 1. The link-local
  first-hop response shows the RETH answered the TTL-expired probe; target
  reachability shows the path continued beyond it. This also avoids treating
  an answering intermediate hop as destination success.
- Left the internet-IPv6 MTR and ping cells, Group 11 cells, fabric pings, and
  standalone MTR expectation unchanged. The IPv6 and fabric leads remain open
  on #18168.

## Validation

- Added `test/incus/connectivity-mtr-selftest.sh` (run via
  `make test-connectivity-mtr-selftest`) with the captured WAN and
  IPv4-internet MTR reports plus negative fixtures for VIP/WAN-gateway hop 1,
  unresolved hop 1, missing or lossy destination, an unresolved tail after the
  destination, and a destination at hop 1.
- `bash -n test/incus/test-connectivity.sh test/incus/connectivity-mtr-selftest.sh` — PASS.
- `shellcheck -e SC1091 test/incus/test-connectivity.sh test/incus/connectivity-mtr-selftest.sh` — PASS. `SC1091` is excluded for the existing sourced-library informational diagnostics.
- `make test-connectivity-mtr-selftest` — PASS, all 9 fixture cases (2 captured positive reports and 7 negative cases).

## Risk

This is a harness-only IPv4 MTR expectation correction. It does not change
routing, firewall policy, or packet processing. It intentionally does not
resolve the separate fabric-ping or internet-IPv6 failures tracked by #18168.
