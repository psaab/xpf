# Issue #15189: inline-metered input policer charged twice when filter carries a routing-instance term (D01-01)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-19
- **Action**: `evaluate_non_pbr_input_filter`
  (`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/poll_descriptor/filter.rs`) ran its
  routing-bearing remainder whenever the whole filter had
  `affects_route_lookup` and the family had any input three-color
  policer, and the remainder
  (`evaluate_filter_ref_tx_selection_runtime_routing_bearing`) re-walked
  from term 0. An inline-metered (host-bound / Stage-11) packet whose
  filter carried ANY `routing-instance` term — including one it never
  matched — was therefore charged twice per packet (router-bound traffic
  policed at about half the configured CIR/burst). The non-routing walks
  (`evaluate_filter_ref_non_routing_counted_v4/v6` in
  `filter/engine/eval.rs`) now record the term index they deferred at in
  the new `FilterResult::deferred_at_routing_term` field, and the caller
  runs the remainder only when the walk actually deferred, via the new
  `routing_bearing_remainder_policer_drop_from(.., from_term)` in
  `filter/engine/tx_selection.rs`, which threads `from_term` through the
  runtime TX-selection walks (`&filter.terms[from_term..]`, zero
  allocation, `from_term = 0` on every pre-existing full walk). A policer
  on the deferring routing term (or a later term that walk reaches
  before terminating) still meters exactly once; a packet that never
  defers pays no remainder. NOT changed: the tunnel outer gate keeps the
  full from-0 `evaluate_filter_ref_tx_selection_runtime_routing_bearing`
  (its input evaluation passes `DeferToTxSelection`, so nothing there
  was charged twice), transit deferral, the #14659 fall-through
  preservation, counter ownership, and every non-inline path.
  `userspace-dp/src/filter/README.md` #14120 section documents the fix.

## RED -> GREEN proof

## Risk

Bounded to inline-metered input-filter evaluation on host-bound /
Stage-11 paths. Transit packets still defer to the TX-selection leg
exactly once; a policer on or after a matched routing-instance term
still meters exactly once via the sliced remainder. No wire, counter,
or `show` behaviour change outside the cited double-charge mechanism.
