## Why

`policyHasStaticSource11447` compared the stored source token to the exact lowercase string `static`, while strict validation accepts case- and whitespace-normalized source-protocol tokens. A matching static policy could therefore miss the QNH policy map in the managed-section redistribution attachment.

## What

- Routed the QNH static-source predicate through `config.CanonicalSourceProtocol`, sharing the routing-protocol domain's alias, case, and whitespace normalization.
- Added a compile-to-managed-section regression for canonical, uppercase, mixed-case, and quoted trailing-/surrounding-space tokens. Each spelling must attach the synthesized QNH policy map, whose body carries the metric, and produce a full managed section byte-identical to the canonical `static` render. A connected-only control checks that it does not attach a QNH policy map.

## Validation

### RED before the fix

The original whitespace cells appended unquoted tokens. `ParseSetCommand` therefore stripped their padding: `static ` became `static`, while ` Static ` became `Static`. The latter's pre-fold base failure was caused by case, not preserved whitespace; the former passed on base. The revised cells quote both tokens so their padding reaches the predicate. At base, those padded spellings fail to attach the QNH map and use the ordinary `POLICY-static-xpf-redist` map instead.

## Risk

The change extends QNH static-source recognition to protocol spellings already accepted by strict validation. Non-static behavior is not broadly covered by the connected-only control. The newly attached QNH metric can affect IGP path selection; no live cluster mutation was performed.
