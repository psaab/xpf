# #15200 — tolerant-path poison-predicate gap for `*-address-excluded` operands (A07-02)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-19
- **Action**: treat any operand under `match source-address-excluded` /
  `destination-address-excluded` as dropped content: strict compilation
  rejects it, tolerant compilation warns, and `compilePolicy` poisons the
  policy via `LenientContentDropped`.
- **Files**: `pkg/config/compiler_policy_match.go`,
  `pkg/config/compiler_policy_match_excluded_operand_15200_test.go`,
  `pkg/config/README.md`, `docs/log/15200.md`.

## Red before

New tests in `compiler_policy_match_excluded_operand_15200_test.go` failed on
`origin/master` (1feb5d66b8): all seven strict cases were accepted with no
error, and all three tolerant cases compiled with
`LenientContentDropped=false` and no `#15200` warning. The bare-modifier
control passed before and after. Probe on base confirmed the mechanism:
hierarchical `source-address-excluded bad-value` lands on `Keys[1:]`,
flat-set/block spellings land as a child node, and in every shape
`compilePolicy` set the excluded flag while ignoring the operand, with no
warning on strict or lenient paths.

## Change

- `policyUnsupportedMatchLeafFindings` (the shared #3113 walk consumed by
  both `validatePolicyMatchLeavesStrict` and `compilePolicy`'s #5575
  poison) now emits a finding for every operand returned by
  `firewallMatchValues` under a `*-address-excluded` leaf, covering
  `Keys[1:]` and child spellings for zone-pair and global policies.
- `validatePolicyMatchLeavesStrict` reports that finding under `#15200`,
  naming scope, policy, leaf, and dropped operand: hard error on strict,
  warning on tolerant ingress.
- No new lenient flag was needed: the walk already runs under
  `lenientPolicyMatchLeaves` on tolerant paths, and any finding already
  poisons in `compilePolicy`. A bare `*-address-excluded` with no operand
  produces no finding and stays valid Junos.

## Validation

- `go test ./pkg/config -run 'TestExcludedOperand' -count=1` — RED pre-fix
  (10 subtests failed as above), GREEN post-fix.
- Adjacent controls GREEN: `TestPolicyMatchUnsupportedLeaf*`,
  `TestPolicyMatchSupportedLeavesStillCommit`,
  `TestPolicyValuelessMatchDoesNotOverReject`,
  `TestPolicyMatchAddressExcluded*`, `TestClosedWorldSecurity9878*`.
- Checks main agent should run: full `go test ./pkg/config -count=1`,
  `go vet ./pkg/config`, `gofmt -l` on touched files.

## Risk

Bounded to the cited mechanism. Configs persisted before #9878 that carry
an operand under `*-address-excluded` now warn and have that one policy
poisoned (snapshot refused, previous-good retained) instead of silently
enforcing the bare exclusion flag; the compiled flag behaviour is
otherwise unchanged. Bare modifiers are unaffected.
