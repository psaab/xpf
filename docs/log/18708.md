- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-08
- **Base**: `dc97628b7c`
- **Action**: Prevent a later unrelated stream-group pause in a wide crash window from replacing a fast-takeover anchor, and align resume-zero excusal with the crash cell's judged stream set.

  - `test/incus/iperf3_sum_parse.py`: leave the stream candidate search unbounded, but require aggregate interval k+1 to be contiguous and below half the mean aggregate rate in the preceding five seconds.   - `test/incus/iperf-throughput-selftest.sh`: cover a pre-carrier straggler, a stream stalled before the judged set, cross-event eligibility leakage, a run-6-shaped zero after resume, and an inverse overlapping-event case. Mutants removing carrier-set or first-resume gating must flip their fixtures.

## Final review fold

- Removed the fixed two-second stream-search cap. A stream stop candidate is
  accepted only when its contiguous aggregate interval k+1 is below half of
  the preceding five-second mean. The fast-takeover fixture keeps the delayed
  genuine stop at lo+4.25 s, while a later three-stream pause at lo+1.9 s
  cannot anchor and cannot replace the fallback's conservative VOID with a
  false COST.
- Resume excusal now ends at the stream's first full post-event data interval.
  The run-6-shaped fixture charges its later zero as outside, and the inverse
  overlap fixture proves event A cannot excuse a zero after its own resume
  merely because that zero also lies in A/B's overlapping windows.
- Stream `start` and `end` use explicit `None` checks, preserving real `0.0`
  bounds.
- The parser-to-metrics handoff remains raw: `ha_crash_resume_at` keeps only
  `anchor.seconds`, so `--resume-event-sec` evaluates its window at raw T;
  the stream cell uses `anchor.latest` (= seconds plus uncertainty) for
  post-event evidence. Near-boundary counter classification can therefore
  differ from the cell's conservative resume cutoff; this is documented in
  both the producer and shell handoff comments.
