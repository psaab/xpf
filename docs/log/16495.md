# #16495 — D11 IPsec-inner admission refused admissible frames on lock contention at three sites

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-24
- **Context**: The #15151 fix (6a8f8df820) made per-queue contention BUSY instead of FULL, but left three `try_lock` refusal sites on the D11 admission path: `IpsecInnerRouter::admit` refused `WORKER_QUEUE_FULL` (47, counting E23) when `inflight_by_flow` was held by a worker's per-verdict `release()`; `WorkerSetAuthority::try_is_authoritative`/`try_route` refused `EVALUATOR_UNAVAILABLE` (52) when `live` was held by `is_live`; and `IpsecInnerTombstones::try_insert` returned false → `LEASE_EPOCH` (50) when `entries` was held by `complete()`/`claim_stale()`. In `slowpath.rs` these map to ADMIT_FULL / ADMIT_SHUTDOWN / ADMIT_BAD_LEASE, Go drops the frame (`VerdictDrop`), and ADMIT_BAD_LEASE sets the attestation ledger TerminalState to FAIL. A realistic harness measured 1.9–5.9% of admissible frames refused with an empty queue.
- **Fix**: make admission non-refusing on contention at all three sites by taking blocking `lock()` where hold time is bounded (the issue's accepted alternative to lock-free redesign). `WorkerSetAuthority` gains `authoritative_route` (blocking; `None` only when unpublished/empty — genuine E28) and the two `try_*` methods are deleted. `Router::admit` uses it and takes a blocking in-flight lock (O(1) entry update competing with O(1) `release`); its only remaining FULL increment is the genuine per-flow bound. `Tombstones::try_insert` becomes blocking `insert` (competing holds are bounded O(cap≤512) scans); LEASE_EPOCH now means only duplicate/zero/at-cap. Per-queue `try_enqueue` BUSY behaviour (#15151, accepted risk) is unchanged. Module invariant updated: E23 moves only at genuine capacity (`IPSEC_INNER_QUEUE_DEPTH` or `IPSEC_INNER_MAX_INFLIGHT_PER_FLOW`).
- **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/ipsec_inner_queue.rs`, `debian/changelog`, `docs/log/16495.md`
- **Validation**: three new deterministic `*_16495` cells hold each competing lock from another thread across a spawned admit and require `Ok(0)` (inflight cell also asserts E23 delta 0; tombstone cell also pins genuine duplicate → LEASE_EPOCH). Each admits a positive control first. Existing genuine-full / per-flow-bound / BUSY cells remain as controls (see PR for exact counts).
- **Residual**: producer now briefly blocks on bounded holds instead of dropping; holders are O(1)/short-CONTROL/O(cap) with no lock held across adjudication, so the wait is bounded by one holder. Production loss rate remains unmeasured in a lab, matching the finding's evidence level. Per-queue BUSY drops (#15151 accepted risk) are out of scope and unchanged.
