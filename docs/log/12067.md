Bare term-line `route-filter` markers are preserved regardless of position, except when the marker is the immediate value of `then community add|delete|set`. The separate #12513 action-dispatch behavior is unchanged.
- Added CheckText and Store regressions for bare route-filter markers without `from` (N1/N3/N6/N7), the `then community add route-filter` value control, invalid tolerant term-line `upto` operands, missing `upto` length, then-first/body/group/packed variants, valid `/24`, V1/V2/V3 value-reference controls, braced/quoted controls, quoted-head/provenance behavior, and #8437 Q5/X5/C4 diagnostics.

An absent term-line operand and legacy braced/compact zero-length `upto` remain warning-only with the historical `le maxLen` fallback; see the risk section.

## Residual / risk

The #11779 tolerant quarantine changes these term-line cases: bare `from
route-filter` and `from prefix-list route-filter` previously rendered
permit-all, while `from route-filter ... exact route-filter` rendered a
permissive matched permit. They now compile to `Action=reject` on `Store.Load`
and `SyncApply` (deny at boot or sync). This is fail-closed, but a rolling HA
upgrade can temporarily diverge between peers.

Term-line `upto` operands that are present but invalid or out of range remain
unconsumed, are recorded as unknown `from` tokens, and compile to `Action=reject`
on `Store.Load` and `SyncApply`. By contrast, an absent term-line operand and
legacy braced/compact zero-length `upto` cases remain warning-only and retain
the historical `le maxLen` renderer fallback. No live-FRR run was performed.

The separate then-first LoadMerge/LoadSet/direct-set `from`-clause loss is filed as follow-up #12516.
