### Findings while writing the cells (for the design review)

- **Debts armed without a bulk attempt.** `armColdPrimeLocked` has three
  callers that attempt no bulk:
  - the peer-reboot edge (`sync_conn.go` `applyPeerIncarnationSwitchLocked`,
    reached from an inbound BulkStart);
  - the two capable-discovery re-arms (`sync_failover_recv.go`).

  Each leaves the debt to the sweep. A fix that schedules a retry only where a
  bulk FAILS (the design note's option A) does not reach them. The peer-reboot
  edge's classifier is a changed peer OS boot id
  (`/proc/sys/kernel/random/boot_id`) on a priming BulkStart: a VM or host
  REBOOT of the peer, whether its old connection lingers or left before the
  BulkStart. A clean xpfd restart keeps the boot id and never takes it.
  Corrected twice: abf635b4d6's message called it the rolling-upgrade shape,
  and 05df554815 then said it "needs a no-FIN corpse"; both are wrong (Codex
  P3 on the design).
- **Idle backoff after a failed re-drive.** A failed re-drive syncs no rows, so
  `interval` doubles toward the idle 60 s. In production the waits go 15 s,
  30 s, 60 s, unless the liveness cap applies.
- **A prompt retry can stack a second full bulk.** While a connect-time cold
  prime runs, nothing marks it in flight: the pending ack is published just
  BEFORE BulkEnd is written (corrected: an earlier line said after; Codex P3),
  but the snapshot walk precedes both, and `bulkRedriveInFlight` covers
  re-drives only. The guard cell above pins this.
- **The daemon's prime-retry loop cannot help inside the window.** Each refused
  pre-hook attempt bumps `syncPrimeRetryGen`
  (`daemon_ha_userspace_readiness.go:200`), which kills that loop, and its
  defer restarts the loop with its 10 s first wait. The loop starts only while
  `!syncPeerBulkPrimed`, i.e. on a cold start.

### What the implementation does

| rule | where |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility.** A re-drive may start only when debt is owed, no ack for it is young, the bulk is not fenced, and its retry deadline has passed. The branch checks this itself (Codex P1-1) | `owedColdPrimeRetryEligible`, the sweep's owed branch |
| **Deadline.** Advanced only by a non-fence failure while debt is owed: 1 s, 2 s, 4 s. Reset on an arm, a discharge, a successful send, or the runtime being wired. The #18752 owed age and last failure are untouched | `noteColdPrimeRetryResult` (from `noteBulkResult`), `armColdPrimeLocked`, `dischargeColdPrime`, `SetRuntimeDomains` |
| **Scheduling.** The timer is lowered to the debt's due time (later of the deadline and a young ack's 35 s expiry); a due time already passed gets 1 s, so a just-armed debt is re-driven about 1 s later and a debt whose re-drive cannot run does not spin. A wake only moves the loop's deadline EARLIER | `nextSweepDelay`, `StartSyncSweep` |
| **Wakes.** Sent on an arm, every bulk completion, the fence lift (no new generation), and the runtime being wired (Codex P1-2, P1-3) | `armColdPrimeLocked`, `noteBulkResult`, `handlePeerCapabilities`, `SetRuntimeDomains` |
| **Fence.** A fenced debt is not eligible. A fenced snapshot bulk is refused before its table-truth read; the window's revalidate-on-consume stays the authority (Codex P1-3) | `bulkFenceNow`, `doBulkSyncWithBulkSend` |
| **Admission.** The re-drive takes `bulkSendMu` and rechecks eligibility under it; there is no new in-flight mark (Codex P1-4) | `redriveOwedColdPrime` |
| **Operator text.** The debt detail and docs name the new cadence | `ColdPrimeDebtDetail`, `docs/ha-failover-status.md` |

## Timer arms exactly at a future due time (coordinator's v3 points)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-15
- **Action**: the coordinator asked for two points to be settled for the
  re-review: the 1 s floor's effect on first-retry latency, and whether
  "reset on a successful send" keeps #9626's ack suppression.
  - **Point 1.** `max(due - now, 1 s)` could make a retry up to 1 s LATE: a
    timer that fired just before `notBefore` re-armed a full second. The
    timer now arms EXACTLY at a due time in the future, and uses
    `coldPrimeRetryBase` (1 s) only when the due time has already passed (a
    just-armed debt, or a re-drive that could not run). Worst-case first
    attempt:
    - an arm with no attempt in flight: about 1 s;
    - after a failure: its backoff exactly;
    - after a fence lift: about 1 s;
    - a young ack: its 35 s expiry.
  - **Point 2.** The reset clears only `notBefore` and `failures`.
    Eligibility still needs `!coldPrimeAckAwaited()`, and due is
    `max(notBefore, ackExpiry)`, so a sent bulk whose ack never comes is
    retried at its 35 s expiry. That is intended.

### What changed from v3

- **One backoff for every retry (finding 1).** `bulkRetryNotBefore`
  (formerly `coldPrimeRetryNotBefore`) is advanced by ANY authoritative bulk
  that ends in a non-fence error, and gates:
  - the forced resync re-armed after a failure;
  - the owed re-drive;
  - the aged-ack re-drive.

  The sweep is a switch:
  - forced, if the backoff is open;
  - else, while a debt is owed, the owed re-drive or nothing (no
    fall-through);
  - else, aged-ack, if the backoff is open.

  Verified at 949d187975: the forced branch ignored the deadline, and an
  ineligible owed retry fell through to the aged branch.
- **Outcome under the lock (finding 12, new P1).** `doBulkSyncAdmitted`
  holds `bulkSendMu` across the bulk and its result hook; `doBulkSync` uses
  it. Verified: the window released the lock in its own defer before the
  outer `noteBulkResult` ran.
- **Admission by each caller's own obligation (finding 4).** Rechecks under
  the lock:
  - connect-time: `forcedConsumed || !coldPrimeCovered()`;
  - aged-ack (a real recheck now; v3's claim was false);
  - daemon prime: `DoPrimeBulkSync`;
  - barrier-fence re-prime.

  `bulkRedriveInFlight` is released by `defer` on every path.
- **IPsec fence conjunction (finding 3).** The code already had
  `peerCapabilitiesLearned() && IpsecTunnelDiscriminatorCapable()` at
  949d187975; only the design text omitted it. A pinning cell now holds it.
- **Scheduling (finding 13).**
  - The timer is armed exactly at a future due time.
  - A new debt is eligible at once, and its first re-drive is scheduled
    100 ms after the arm (`coldPrimeFreshUntil`).
  - A due debt that could not run is polled every 1 s.

  A first cut made a new debt INELIGIBLE for 100 ms. That broke three #9626
  cells, two #10387 cells and one #9618 cell, all of which require the next
  sweep tick after an arm to send it. Eligibility and scheduling are now
  separate.
- **Reset on a successful send (finding 14)** means `err == nil`, i.e.
  BulkEnd was written, under the lock.
- **The requirement (findings 7, 16).** Inside the window the debt reaches a
  qualifying pending bulk; admission is NOT guaranteed after a stream move,
  since the barrier fence refuses until that bulk's ack arrives. The daemon
  oracle now observes the qualifying pending state directly.
- **Transport (finding 8).** Exports and abandonment go over the control
  socket (`requestDetailedAtSocket`, `controlReqMu`); installs use the
  session socket. Acceptance limits are defined in the design.

### What changed

- **The backoff holds back ONLY the owed cold-prime re-drive.** The v4 gates
  on the forced-resync and aged-ack sweep branches are removed: a forced
  resync re-armed after a failure retries on the next sweep tick, as on
  master (`sync_conn_sweep.go:537` at bd1a23d3fe). The aged-ack re-drive's
  under-lock recheck no longer checks the backoff either. The barrier-fence
  re-prime and `DoPrimeBulkSync` never had a backoff gate; only the docs that
  claimed one changed. The backoff is still ADVANCED by every failed
  authoritative bulk, so the owed re-drive does not retry right behind
  another caller's failure.
- **Kept, as Codex closed them:**
  - the sweep switch with no owed-to-aged fall-through; while a debt is
    owed, the owed re-drive is the sweep's retry or nothing;
  - the per-caller own-obligation rechecks under `bulkSendMu`;
  - the deferred `bulkRedriveInFlight` release;
  - the result hook under the lock;
  - eligibility separate from scheduling;
  - the IPsec conjunction.
- **No fall-through.** The aged-ack recheck still refuses while a debt is
  owed. That is the second of the two defences; see the mutants below.
- **Finding 18 cannot recur for the owed branch.** Its due time is what
  `nextSweepDelay` schedules: the backoff deadline, or a young ack's expiry.
  The branches that lost their gate run on the tick again, so they have no
  deadline to schedule.
- **Follow-up filed:** #19135, "forced/barrier-fence bulk retries have no
  backoff (pre-existing, master sync_conn_sweep.go:537)". It cites Codex
  findings 1, 17 and 18.
- **The timing claim (finding 7).** "Attempts at most 4 s apart" is
  replaced by the conditional bound, in the code comment, the README and
  `docs/ha-failover-status.md`. Measured from an observed COMPLETED failure,
  the next attempt reaches a qualifying pending bulk inside the 5 s window
  when these add up to less than 5 s:
  - the remaining backoff (at most 4 s);
  - the scheduling and lock delay;
  - that attempt's successful time-to-pending.

  operator-facing debt line now reads "1 s after a failure, then 2 s, then
  4 s after each later failure" (it said "then every 4 s").
