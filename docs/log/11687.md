# #11687 — cap tunnel TCP MSS by the overlay route MTU

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-02
- **Action**: Constrained GRE and WireGuard MSS advertisements by the overlay
  `route_mtu` in addition to the tunnel's physical/transport-derived inner
  budget. The explicit `tcp_mss_gre_out` ceiling is also bounded by that
  route-derived maximum.
- **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/forwarding/mss.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/tunnel_tests.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/wg/mss.rs`.

## MTU budgets

Tunnel resolutions carry separate outer `transport_route_mtu` and inner
`route_mtu` constraints. GRE MSS now uses the zero-preserving
`min_nonzero_mtu(inner, route_mtu)` before subtracting inner IP/TCP headers,
and a configured gre-out value cannot exceed that derived ceiling. WireGuard
keeps its existing outer-derived formula for `route_mtu == 0`; otherwise the
formula caps its pad-aware inner MTU before deriving the inner TCP MSS. A zero
inner budget remains zero instead of being replaced by an overlay budget.
