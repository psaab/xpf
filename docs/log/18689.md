  - **Action**: Track the last answered status poll independently of any-request
    liveness. Successful and answered-but-undecodable polls atomically refresh
    the generation-local clock at reply return, before acquiring `m.mu` or
    passing the publication fence; silence and other failures do not. Before
    its first answer, a published cached status uses generation start as its
    conservative age anchor and becomes stale after 30 s.
  - **Action**: Expose status age and the 30-second stale bound on `/metrics`

    there is no cached status, or when the helper generation is not live;
    retained status values are explicitly marked stale. Unexpected-exit
    cleanup retires the generation's status clock so restart backoff has no
    stale age from a departed process.
