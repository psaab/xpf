    when `zoneTailHoldsBody9656` finds a complete statement that can hold the
    body. Otherwise every token is a zone, which is #8794's reading. The new

    a body. The configstore gate adds a policy naming zone `tcp-rst`.

    `configstore.Store` at base and on the branch.
