# Issue #15133 — persistent-NAT inbound lookups for non-zone rule-sets collapse to UNSCOPED

- **Issue:** psaab/xpf#15133 (A91-07, #14322 residual). Persistent-NAT inbound
  lookups for interface-, routing-instance- or global-scoped source-NAT
  rule-sets pass an empty `rule.from_zone`, which the allocator treats as
  UNSCOPED, re-opening the cross-egress permission merge #14322 closed for
  zone-scoped rules: a `target-host-port` persistent mapping is widened to
  `any-remote-host` inbound when a sibling non-zone rule-set shares the pool
  with a permissive mode.
- **Lane:** `fix/15133-campaign-pub` in `/var/worktrees/campaign-pub/wt-15133`;
  base RED worktree `/var/worktrees/campaign-pub/wt-15133-base` at `85433e0906`.

## Defect

Two rules naming one pool share one `PortAllocator` and one
endpoint-independent lease (#9389) — lease identity holds. #14322 bucketed
the lease's permission grants by the admitting rule's `(from_zone, to_zone)`
fold. That fold keys on the PACKET's resolved zones at mint
(`nat/source/match_rules.rs`), so two siblings that differ only in interface
or routing-instance scope (both `from_zone == ""`) mint into ONE bucket.
The inbound lookup (`afxdp/poll_descriptor/mod.rs`, mirrored in
`policy_revalidation.rs`) then recomputed the scope from
`rule.from_zone` — empty for those rule-sets — and
`persistent_inbound_lookup` mapped empty to `PERSISTENT_NAT_EGRESS_UNSCOPED`,
where `permits()` admits ANY bucket. The strict sibling's
`target-host-port` binding therefore admitted arbitrary remotes whenever a
permissive non-zone sibling had minted any grant on the shared lease.

A second, latent conflation surfaced while fixing the mirror: a scoped
lookup returned `Denied` for every live lease on the tuple, so in the
multi-rule scan the first sibling in snapshot order vetoed inbound even
when another sibling's bucket permitted it.

## Fix

Key every mint and every lookup by the ADMITTING RULE's normalized
effective scope — one key, computed in one place
(`SourceNatRule::persistent_rule_scope`):

- `nat/allocator.rs`: `persistent_egress_scope_v2` folds the rule's six
  authored scope fields followed by the packet `(from, to)` pair. Authored
  empty zones are replaced by the packet's resolved zone BEFORE folding, so
  an interface-scoped rule inside `trust` agrees with a zone-scoped `trust`
  rule's bucket, while siblings differing on any authored field (interface,
  routing-instance, zone) never share a bucket. A `1`-tag domain-separates
  v2 from the v1 `(from, to)` fold so a persisted v1 hash cannot alias a v2
  bucket; `0` remains reserved for NAT64 BIBs, legacy/imported records and
  direct allocator calls. `persistent_inbound_lookup_for_rule` is the
  production entry point; the legacy zone-pair spelling is retained for
  direct/NAT64 callers. A lease with no bucket for the lookup's scope is
  now a Miss, not a Deny (`PersistentPermissions::has_scope`), so a sibling
  that never minted cannot veto one that did.
- `nat/source/match_rules.rs` + `nat/source/synced.rs`: both mint sites
  (local match, HA synced reserve) record grants under
  `rule.persistent_rule_scope(...)` instead of the raw packet/rule zone
  pair, so standby and active land in the same bucket.
- `afxdp/poll_descriptor/mod.rs` (admission) and
  `policy_revalidation.rs` (mirror): pass the rule plus the packet pair
  (internal zone, arrival zone) — resolved from `rule.from_zone` for
  zone-authored rules, the arrival zone otherwise; revalidation resolves
  the internal zone from the session metadata's egress zone id. Neither
  site passes `&rule.from_zone` alone any more.
- `nat/tests_persistent_zone_14322.rs`: the #14322 cells now exercise the
  same rule-keyed lookup production uses.

NOT changed: lease identity / translated-tuple sharing (#9389), the
1024-remote cap accounting, the idle-lease wire `egress_scope` encoding,
and NAT64/legacy unscoped behaviour. Pre-#15133 in-memory grant buckets
folded under v1 are not migrated — they expire with their leases (bounded
by the persistent-NAT inactivity timeout); `persistent_egress_scope_reencode_for_peer`
marks the on-wire v1↔v2 translation point for the lease-control follow-up.

## Risk

- Behaviour delta is fail-closed only for the cited merge: inbound on a
  shared persistent lease now admits solely under the admitting rule's own
  grant bucket. Zone-scoped cells (#14322/#14802/#14315) are unchanged in
  outcome.
- HA: synced reserves and idle-lease export/import carry the scope hash
  opaquely; a mixed-version pair folds differently (v1 vs v2) until both
  sides run this build, so a standby may miss (fail closed, session
  re-mints outbound) rather than merge grants during the upgrade window.
