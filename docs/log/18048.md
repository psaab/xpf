## Why

`TestHostInboundTeardownClearsEnforcedThenFailedReinstallFences_5790` failed on `origin/master` (verified at `478a19d841`, reproduced before edit):

```
--- FAIL: TestHostInboundTeardownClearsEnforcedThenFailedReinstallFences_5790 (0.39s)
    host_inbound_teardown_enforced_5790_test.go:52: step 2: unexpected delete target "xpf_hostinbound_preactivation"
```

The test's step-2 `del` allow-list (`pkg/daemon/host_inbound_teardown_enforced_5790_test.go:49-52`) accepted only `xnft.HostInboundTableName` and `xnft.HostInboundGapTableName`. Production teardown in `applyHostInboundFilter`'s no-enforcement path (`pkg/daemon/daemon_nft.go:902,919,925`) deletes all three tables — main, gap (#5789), and preactivation (`xnft.HostInboundPreActivationTableName`, nil-ing `hostInboundPreActivationFence`) — intended since the pre-activation fence isolation (#14012/#14033) and retained through #16823. A lingering preactivation fence would keep dropping addresses after the surface is torn down, so the third delete is required behavior, not a regression.

## What

- Widen the step-2 `del` allow-list to also accept `xnft.HostInboundPreActivationTableName`.
- Strengthen (not just widen): seed `d.hostInboundPreActivationFence = &xnft.GapFenceSpec{PreActivation: true, UncoveredV4: []string{"203.0.113.10"}}` after step 1, then assert in step 2 that the preactivation table IS deleted (`deleted[xnft.HostInboundPreActivationTableName]`) and the in-memory fence is cleared (`d.hostInboundPreActivationFence == nil`). The previous cell could not distinguish "teardown forgot the preactivation table" (fail-open retention, mirror of #16823's gap arm) from the expected third delete; now it can.
- Keep and extend the "no install op may run" property: step 2 still fails if `InstallHostInbound` runs, and now also fails if `InstallGapFence` or `InstallColdBootFence` runs during teardown.

No production files changed; no other test files changed.
