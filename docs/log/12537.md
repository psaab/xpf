# #12537 — preserve packed route-filter tails in apply-groups merges

- **Action**: when apply-groups merges a packed routing-policy term whose tail the generic packed-body expander cannot model, rebuild the tail with the compiler's own readers, so the group's matches and actions are merged and validated as the direct spelling is instead of being dropped (match-all accept).

## Red before

A group containing `term T from route-filter 10.0.0.0/8 orlonger reject;` applied alongside an inline `term T then accept;` passed `CheckText` and compiled with zero route filters and `Action=accept`. The direct spelling and group-only spelling were rejected, isolating the defect to the packed-term merge. The generic packed-body expander (`packedBodyChildren`) is all-or-nothing and declined the group term's unmodelled tail; apply-groups promotion then kept the inline term and dropped the whole group term. #12687's shapes (`upto /bogus`, `prefix-length-range /24-/abc`, `through bogus`) are the same drop.

## Change

- `policyTermBody12537` (`pkg/config/policy_term_packed_12537.go`) rebuilds a declined policy-options term tail as the braced body the compiler reads:
  - every `from` clause becomes one `from` container holding `splitPolicyTermFromRun11779`'s split of its run (the compiler's packed `from` reader), so known matches merge one statement at a time and an unknown word stays an opaque leaf for the #11779 gate;
  - every `then` clause becomes one `then` container with one statement per action (declared operands, multi-value values, and trailing tokens stay on their action for the #12517 gate), the shape the expander produces for runs it can model.
- Group side: `packedLeafMergeParts9855` uses the rebuild when `groupPackedLeafBody` declines a policy-options term (leaf-peer promotion and same-instance container paths).
- Inline side: `promoteLeafPeerForPackedGroup9855` rebuilds a declined inline policy-options term instead of bailing, so its group's route-filter is no longer dropped; an inline `then` that opens with an unknown action word still bails, so the direct reader rejects it.
- NOT changed: firewall filter terms (generic path), the generic expander, the compiler, the validators, the schema.

## Review fold (#12696)

- **B1**: the original change replaced every promoted `term`'s `from` (firewall included) with a routing-policy-schema re-read, splitting `destination-port 80` into a valueless leaf plus a stray value. The unconditional replacement is removed; the rebuild runs only for policy-options terms and only where the generic expansion declined.
- **B2**: two `from` clauses merge as one `from` (no stray `from` leaf, no duplicated route-filter); a rebuilt two-`from` tail is rejected for its real tail.
- **F3**: the hand-written `from` reader is gone; `splitPolicyTermFromRun11779` gives the direct spelling's result for `protocol static bgp`, `prefix-list PA PB`, and a quoted `then`.
- **F4**: the group's `then` is rebuilt rather than discarded, so `then reject bogus` beside an inline `then accept` is rejected with the direct spelling's `then reject: unknown modifier "bogus"`; the tolerant path warns and keeps the group's `reject`.
- **F5/F6**: Risk below corrected; #12687's shapes are pinned.
- **F7**: assertions name the direct-spelling diagnostics; firewall, two-`from`, parity, malformed-`then`, inline-declined, and #12687 cells added.

## Risk

- Operator-visible: group policy-options term content that the expander declined is now merged instead of silently dropped. A stored config whose group term carries an invalid tail (or whose inline term carries an unbracketed list or `then community add …`) now fails strict commit or gains the group's matches/actions; the tolerant load warns as the direct spelling does.
- A group `then` beside an inline braced `then` line still loses to that line (existing hasMatchingLeaf rule), valid or malformed.
- A group `then` that opens with an unknown action word merges as an unknown `then` statement and warns (#10707), as the braced spelling does; the packed direct spelling rejects it.
- The firewall-filter analogue (a firewall group term the expander declines, e.g. `from source-address 10.0.0.0/8 except`, is dropped beside an inline peer) is unchanged.
- No live dataplane validation was performed.
