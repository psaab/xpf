- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-15
  - **Action**: `EventStream.readLoop` now checks ordinary session-frame sequence continuity before payload decoding. For oversized frames, the reader waits until a within-ceiling frame drains successfully before trusting its header and treating a gap as a hard session-sync break. Over-ceiling or short-drain headers remain untrusted and use the existing rate-limited refusal path.
