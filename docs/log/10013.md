# #10013: preserve holdDropped across feed reconfiguration

- **Timestamp:** 2026-09-17
- **Branch:** `fix/10013-feeds-hold`
- **File(s):**
  - feeds: `pkg/feeds/feeds.go`, `pkg/feeds/feeds_hold_carry_10013_test.go`, `pkg/feeds/README.md`

## Decision

A hold-interval drop is persisted enforcement state, not transient fetch failure
state. `carryForwardSnapshot` must carry `feedState.holdDropped` even when the
predecessor has no snapshot: the drop-to-empty path clears `prefixes` and
`hasSnapshot`, so the old snapshot guard returned before copying the marker.
Without the marker, a persisting feed became indistinguishable from a
never-fetched feed after every reconfiguration and #9689 `fail-mode drop`
semantics were silently lost.

Transient `lastError` and `staleSince` remain intentionally non-carried. The
replacement endpoint gets a fresh stale window; only the already-enforced
`HOLD-DROPPED` state crosses the reconfiguration boundary.

## Change

- Copy `holdDropped` before the snapshot-presence guard in
  `carryForwardSnapshot`.
- Document the distinction between transient failure markers and the persisted
  hold-drop marker in the carry-forward comment and feeds README.
- Add real Apply/reconfigure regressions covering immediate preservation,
  continued failure, recovery without another hold interval, and the
  never-fetched fail-closed control.
