# 13924 — Rejudge ICMP session hits against the live egress zone

## Boundary

A type-constrained ICMP policy term arms a packet-scoped session-hit check. That check was judging the established session's recorded egress zone, so re-zoning the live egress into a denying zone did not revoke the old permit. A type-dependent ICMP policy declines the generation-based revalidation walk, leaving this packet-scoped verdict as the required gate.

## Change

The owner-hit ICMP verdict now resolves the policy destination from the current forwarding resolution's egress ifindex. The foreign-hit ICMP verdict uses the same live-zone resolution. `FabricRedirect` retains its recorded policy destination because its stored egress is the fabric transport rather than the flow egress. The descriptor regression covers no move, a move into a default-denying zone, a move into a zone explicitly permitted for the flow, and the foreign-hit guarded twin.
