## Decision

The issue recommends a Go-side liveness signal, and the code supports it. `statusLoop` polls status every second, and `superviseHelper` / `handleUnexpectedHelperExitLocked` already fail the node closed and restart the helper with a bounded backoff on an unexpected exit (#5838). What was missing is an exit: a helper wedged by a descriptor leak keeps running. So the daemon counts unanswered polls and kills the helper, and the existing supervisor path does the recovery.

The helper-side options in the issue were not taken. A reserved descriptor keeps accept draining without recovering the leak. An fd-count signal needs a threshold that separates a leak from load, which is the question an accept errno could not answer either.

## Change

- `noteStatusPollResultLocked(err, now)`:
  - An answered poll (`err == nil`) or an in-band refusal (`errors.Is(err, errHelperRejected)`) resets the count.
  - A transport failure increments it.
  - It asks for a kill only when `failures >= 30` AND `now - firstFailure >= 30s`, AND the last `update_ha_state` is at least 10s old.
- `killWedgedHelperLocked`: logs the count and span at Error, resets the count, increments `liveness.kills`, and SIGKILLs `m.proc`. The supervisor reaps it as an unexpected exit.
- `statusLoop`: the answered branch resets, and the failed branch feeds the count and kills when asked. `m.mu` is held for the whole tick, as for every other field it touches.
- `liveness helperLiveness` is on `Manager`, guarded by `mu`.
- `statusLoop` still skips the whole tick during a RETH link cycle, so no poll is counted then.

## Cells (`helper_liveness_9651_test.go`)

- **`TestWedgedHelperIsKilledAndRestarted9651`:** 31 transport failures 1s apart. The kill is requested at the 31st, the first poll where both count and span hold. It goes through the real supervisor (`spawnSupervisedChild`): the crash is recorded for the killed pid, a restart is scheduled, and `TakeoverReady` is false.
- **`TestRecoveringPressureIsNeverKilled9651`:** five rounds of 29 failures, each followed by an answer. Never wedged.
- **`TestInBandRefusalIsAnAnswer9651`:** 90 refusals are never wedged, and they do not leak into a later transport count.
- **`TestFastFailuresNeedTheMinimumSpan9651`:** 60 failures inside 6s are not wedged; one at +30s is.
- **`TestSlowFailuresNeedTheFullCount9651`:** failures 3s apart reach the span at the 11th poll, but are wedged exactly at the 30th. At the real 1s cadence the span always binds first, so without this cell an off-by-one in the count would be invisible.
- **`TestNoKillInsideTheRGActivationGrace9651`:** wedged conditions 2s after an RG activation wait until the 10s grace has elapsed.
- **`TestStatusLoopWiresTheLivenessCount9651`:** a source cell requiring both of `statusLoop`'s branches to call into the count, and the failed branch to kill.
