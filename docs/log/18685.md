- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-01
  - **Action**: Fix.
    - `helper_liveness_18685.go`: a per-generation tracker with a leaf
      mutex.
      - Every control round trip of every verb is recorded at
        `requestDetailedAtSocket`: an answer clears the silence, an
        undecodable reply is neither, and a deadline or EOF (even after a
        partial prefix) is silence. The silence start is the START of the
        first silent round trip, taken after `controlReqMu`.
      - Answered statuses feed per-worker heartbeats and `ForwardingArmed`.
      - Suspected (demote, reversible): a worker heartbeat frozen for 10 s
        (judged against the latest status time, not the wall clock), or two
        silent round trips spanning 5 s. It clears after 5 s and 3 answers
        of healthy behaviour.
      - Kill: 30 s of silence, or a worker frozen for 2 min, outside the
        10 s RG grace. A per-generation watchdog goroutine SIGKILLs the
        generation's own process without `m.mu`, and the status loop asks
        the same tracker as a second path. A generation is killed at most
        once.
      - A generation started after an unexpected exit inherits a not-ready
        verdict until it reports `ForwardingArmed` and then looks healthy
        (#18697).
    - `helper_liveness_9651.go` keeps the #18293 decode accounting and
      delegates the decision to the tracker. The 30-poll count is gone.
    - `pkg/daemon`: the transit gate reads `HelperElectionNotReady()`
      lock-free. While it is true, arm debt is installed (data RGs yield to
      a healthy peer, #12164; RG0 stays). F2's pass-through now applies
      only to an intentional stop or respawn, so a config commit does not
      move the RGs. The "do not wipe unrelated arm debt" half is kept.
    - `/metrics`: `xpf_userspace_helper_answer_age_seconds`,
      `xpf_userspace_helper_election_not_ready`, and
      `xpf_userspace_helper_wedge_kills_total`.
    - `pkg/dataplane/README.md`: the HA-response bullet (#18697) and the

    - **Finding.** The frozen-worker verdict counted a worker frozen only
      while `hb.Equal(last)` held across polls. The helper re-derives every
      heartbeat on every status call from two fresh clock reads, at
      nanosecond resolution:
      - K measured 1207 distinct values in 2000 conversions of one fixed
        heartbeat;
      - L measured 152 in 1000.

      So the 10 s demote and the 2 min frozen-worker kill were inert. My
      cells fed identical `time.Time` values, and the SIGSTOP A/B
      exercised only the silence path.
    - **Fix.**
      - A worker is frozen when its heartbeat is at least 10 s OLD at an
        answered status (status time minus heartbeat). That is the quantity
        `fwdstatus.heartbeatsHealthy` reads, and the quantity the sampler
        showed discriminates: 16-22 s in the convoy, 0.0-0.2 s healthy.
      - The verdict needs 2 consecutive statuses to agree, so the first
        poll after a link-cycle gap cannot raise it.
      - It is judged only while forwarding is armed, since a disarmed
        helper parks its workers.
      - A heartbeat ahead of the status time is not evidence of a freeze.
    - **K's LOWs.**
      - (a) `stopLocked` retires the tracker generation.
      - (b) A cell pins that a stale-generation answer does not clear
        silence; K's fence-removal mutant had survived.
