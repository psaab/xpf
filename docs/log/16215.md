# Issue #16215 — third shape: peer-synced FabricRedirect rows revoked on fabric-ifindex egress miss

## Finding (FZDiag10, PR head 2549682f12 + DISC16215)

Aged 30 s P4 iperf3 to 172.16.80.200:5211, RG1 failover node0->node1
at 30 s: all 4 streams zero from 31 s (125 zero lines). On fw0,
`snat_revoked` 0->4 at stream age ~28 s — ~2 s BEFORE the manual
failover command, in the pre-failover demote/republish window.

Per-row DISC evidence (all 4, fw0): origin `sync_import`
(peer-synced, incl. DemoteOwnerRGS-retagged locally-authored rows),
forward hit, stored = packet = judged resolution `FabricRedirect`
with egress 4 (ge-0-0-0, the fabric parent `fab0@ge-0-0-0`),
dynamic SNAT `rewrite_src=172.16.80.8`, `stored_static=Some(false)`,
`dest_valid=true`. Failing check: the FIRST check in
`source_nat_translation_still_valid` — `forwarding.egress.get(&4)`
misses, because a fabric-redirect pseudo-egress has no
`EgressInterface` row, zone pair, or SNAT geometry. Any
FabricRedirect row reaching SNAT revalidation therefore revoked
unconditionally, regardless of rule validity.

Why the existing PR legs missed it:

1. Terminal decline (`policy_revalidation.rs`): keyed on
   `DiscardRoute | ClusterTerminalHAInactive` with egress <= 0.
   FabricRedirect egress 4 is neither.
2. Substitution: the packet decision is also FabricRedirect, outside
   the substitutable `{ForwardCandidate, MissingNeighbor}` set.

## Fix

In `source_nat_revocation_on_session_hit_with_ingress`, after the
terminal decline: a peer-synced row (`is_peer_synced()`, which
includes rows demoted/retagged to `SyncImport` by DemoteOwnerRGS)
whose STORED resolution is `FabricRedirect` DECLINES judgement —
exactly like the terminal decline. The fabric leg's NAT validity is
judged on the egress owner when the packet arrives there; this node
has no local egress verdict to judge against. Decline covers
regardless of failover timing. A genuinely withdrawn SNAT rule still
revokes once the row gains a real egress resolution (live or
re-resolved) and runs the full geometry check. Locally-authored rows
are judged exactly as before (the decline is peer-synced-scoped).
