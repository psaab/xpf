# #14130 — Post-teardown bring-up failures wedge worker recovery / mixed snapshots

## Why

Three findings from the D3-09 research gate, confirmed at `origin/master` `c6f058672`:

1. `plan_workers` (`reconcile/bringup.rs`) inserted per-slot `workers.live` + `identities`
   entries while planning — before the IPsec-SA readiness gate and before any thread
   spawned. The `IpsecSaNotReady` arm returned without unwinding them (no
   `stop_inner`), so `live_count`/`planned_counts` reported a full fleet with zero
   running threads.
2. The `SpawnFailed` arm (#4952's deliberate differential) returned without
   `stop_inner`, leaving already-launched workers forwarding the candidate snapshot
   while `server/handlers/snapshot.rs` rolled the in-memory baseline back to the
   prior snapshot — a mixed-snapshot window.
3. `same_plan_apply_needs_binding_reconcile` (`server/helpers/planning.rs`) required
   an eligible binding with an EMPTY `last_error` in addition to a live/planned
   shortfall. A failed bring-up that recorded a cause (the #8558 bind-failure map,
   or a live slot's bind error) therefore suppressed the identical-plan retry
   forever: the retry took the same-plan refresh leg, which refreshes forwarding
   state but cannot spawn workers.

## What

- `plan_workers` no longer registers anything on the coordinator. `spawn_workers`
  captures each worker's slot rows before the `BindingPlan`s move into the worker
  body and calls `register_planned_slots` only in the post-spawn-success arm,
  alongside the #6242 record insert. `publish_runtime` builds the mirror map from
  the plan (`planned_slot_maps`) instead of from the coordinator maps.
- Every post-plan failure arm in the `bring_up_workers` shell —
  `IpsecSaNotReady`, `SpawnFailed`, `BindIncomplete` — now runs
  `stop_inner(false)` and re-records its stage afterwards, so a failed bring-up
  leaves the coordinator exactly as a completed teardown does: no live/identity
  slots, no records, zeroed planned counters, defaulted runtime view. A rejected
  snapshot is forwarded by nobody.
- `same_plan_apply_needs_binding_reconcile` drops the `last_error.is_empty()`
  conjunct: a live/planned shortfall with any unbound/unregistered runnable slot
  is the recovery signal. The reconcile itself re-validates and fails closed
  again if the fault persists.
- New `#[cfg(test)]` seam `force_ipsec_sa_not_ready` (per-instance) so the
  `IpsecSaNotReady` unwinding is unit-verifiable without a privileged
  NETLINK_XFRM bind; production keeps the mandatory #10516 gate.

Not changed:

- The readiness barrier, the #8558 cause-recording order (recorded after
  `stop_inner`), and the handlers' post-teardown refusal handling
  (`ok=false`, no persist, baseline rollback) are untouched — they were
  already fail-closed; what was missing was the coordinator-side unwinding
  and the retry.

## Risk

Behavior delta is confined to failed bring-ups and same-plan retries of a
short fleet. Successful reconciles spawn and register identically (the
registration point moved from plan time to spawn-success time; the
`Planned` stage still reports the planned slot count). The spawn arm's
teardown costs one `stop_inner` (join + map cleanup) that the previous
code deferred to the next reconcile — the correct trade for never
forwarding a rejected snapshot. Same-plan applies on a healthy fleet are
unaffected: the predicate's shortfall term still gates the reconcile.
