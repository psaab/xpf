## Why

A two-node partition can produce an unconfirmed peer-loss decision without a quorum or peer-fence confirmation. Readiness may leave the local RG secondary, but the peer may still be forwarding. Existing degraded status did not raise an explicit dual-primary-risk alarm. A never-seen peer is not proof of absence: both nodes can miss initial heartbeats during simultaneous startup.

## What

- Keep the existing fail-open election behavior, but render every unconfirmed peer-loss decision as `Peer-loss decision: UNCONFIRMED` in cluster status and information, `CRITICAL` in local and gRPC `show system alarms` while the peer is not alive, and a `slog.Warn`.
- The alarm denotes unconfirmed peer-loss/fencing state, not proof that local ownership changed. A cold-start readiness gate can keep the RG secondary while the alarm remains active; a live peer aborting an unsuccessful fence wait stays silent in the active alarm list.
- Clear the active alarm when a peer heartbeat returns.
- Add a decisive default-policy cluster cell with peer-primary/local-secondary preconditions, fresh connected sync, an initial suppressed timeout, and explicit expiry of the 5s suppression cap; it asserts actual promotion and alarm. Add confirmed-fence, abort, configured cold-start ready/not-ready, CLI, and gRPC cells; update operator docs.
