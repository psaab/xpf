# #18554 — Ignore retired SessionSync bulk-complete callbacks

- **Why:** A late `OnBulkSyncReceived` can release startup holds/prime readiness for a successor; a late `OnBulkSyncAckReceived` can stop that successor's daemon-wide cold-prime retry loop.
- **What:** Guard both callbacks and revalidate their `SessionSync` identity under `clusterCommsMu`, retaining the publication lock through their daemon-wide effects. Add deterministic supersession-race cells for bulk completion and ACK, plus locked-effects cells that prove publication waits for each current callback to finish its effects.
