# #16820 — demotion IPsec sweep raced failback and terminated the re-initiated SA

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-25
  - **Action**: `terminateDemotedRGIPsecSAs` was a fire-and-forget
    goroutine (launched from `clearRethServicesForRG` and the RG0
    demotion arm of `applyRG0OwnershipTransition`) that listed live
    SAs once and terminated each by name with no ownership re-check.
    If the same RG failed back while the sweep was still running
    (each `swanctl` list/terminate shells out, so the window grows
    with SA count), the sweep force-terminated the SA
    `reinitiateIPsecSAsForRG` (or the remote peer via the VIP) had
    just re-established — recreating the #15194 failback black hole
    on the owning node. Fixed by adding a per-RG ownership epoch on
    `Daemon` (`ipsecRGOwnershipEpochs`, bumped on every promotion
    edge in `applyRethServicesForRG`, every demotion edge in
    `clearRethServicesForRG`, and both arms of
    `applyRG0OwnershipTransition`); the demotion caller captures the
    epoch and `terminateDemotedRGIPsecSAs(rg, epoch)` re-checks
    `epoch unchanged && !IsLocalPrimary(rg)` immediately before each
    terminate, aborting the remainder on mismatch. Added
    `ipsec_demote_sweep_race_16820_test.go`: SUBJECT (failback during
    the blocked live-SA read) must end with `initiate:vpn-rg1` and
    exactly one (belt) terminate, CONTROL (sweep completes first)
    still terminates `[vpn-rg1]`, an RG0-arm SUBJECT, and a per-RG
    isolation cell (RG2 failback does not abort an RG1 sweep). RED
    evidence: with the per-terminate guard disabled the SUBJECT
    cells fail with
    `events=[terminate:vpn-rg1 initiate:vpn-rg1 terminate:vpn-rg1]`.
  - **File(s)**: `pkg/daemon/daemon.go`, `pkg/daemon/daemon_ha.go`,
    `pkg/daemon/ipsec_demote_sweep_race_16820_test.go`,
    `docs/log/16820.md`
