# #10034 — retry-debt HA inventory replay

## Mechanism

A timeout-but-landed `apply_snapshot` is retained as retry debt by #9642 and
converges through `syncSnapshotLocked`. That path previously repeated the
snapshot bookkeeping but omitted the successful Compile HA tail. A helper
respawned during the debt episode could therefore hold an empty HA inventory;
a cluster-to-standalone transition could likewise leave stale helper HA groups.

## Change

During retry-debt convergence, `syncSnapshotLocked` now:

- arms a persistent clustered HA-replay obligation immediately after
  `apply_snapshot` succeeds, before helper-status reconciliation;
- invalidates the pre-retry helper inventory and keeps ctrl fail-closed while
  the obligation is outstanding;
- refreshes clustered HA state from the `rg_active`/`ha_watchdog` maps and
  publishes the full inventory with `syncHAStateLocked`; and
- runs the idempotent standalone empty-inventory clear, preserving the existing
  `pendingHAStateClear` retry debt if that clear fails.

The same debt-qualified arm is used by the deferred-worker, policy-scheduler,
and route-overlay full-snapshot republish paths. An election's direct
`update_ha_state` publish also settles the obligation at its ACK boundary.
The clustered obligation is independent of `publishedSnapshot`, so a failed
`update_ha_state` or helper-status reconcile remains a status-tick retry until
the helper acknowledges a non-empty inventory update. If map refresh produces
no valid groups, `syncHAStateLocked` intentionally leaves the obligation
pending and keeps ctrl fail-closed rather than claiming an empty inventory was
published. A non-empty obligation is cleared immediately after its RPC
acknowledgment, before any later status or forwarding reconcile can fail, so a
settled inventory is never published twice. A settled snapshot call still exits
at the generation gate and does not replay HA state again.

## Validation

- RED before the implementation: the initial three convergence cells failed.
  The clustered cell observed 0 map refreshes, the standalone cell observed 0
  `update_ha_state` clears, and the idempotency cell observed 0 refreshes and
  0 HA requests.
- RED boundary cell: with the replay obligation armed only after helper-status
  reconciliation, `TestRetryDebtConvergenceRetainsHAReplayAfterStatusFailure10034`
  failed because the obligation was lost.
- RED no-duplicate cell: clearing the obligation only after the entire helper
  status reconcile made `TestRetryDebtConvergenceDoesNotRepeatAckedHAAfterStatusFailure10034`
  fail after an acknowledged inventory was followed by a status error.
- RED forwarding-tail cell: a forwarding-state failure after the HA ACK made
  the replay debt remain pending until the ACK-boundary clear was moved earlier.
- RED same-tick cell: without the tick-local replay marker,
  `TestRetryDebtConvergenceStatusTickPublishesHAOnce10034` observed 2 map
  refreshes and 2 HA updates in one status cycle.
- RED failed-replay cell: without separating “attempted” from “still pending,”
  `TestRetryDebtConvergenceStatusTickFailedHAReplayOnce10034` observed 2 HA
  updates before the next tick after the first RPC failed.

  `TestRetryDebtConvergence.*10034` cells passed, including HA RPC fault
