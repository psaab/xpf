# #13956 — IPsec decrypted-ingress local selector and policy gate

## Why

The decrypted-ingress permit path checked both inner source and destination against the remote, route-derived selector projection. A destination in the local traffic selector, routed through a non-tunnel main-table leg, was therefore rejected. The same path also returned `WouldPermit` without evaluating the VPN-ingress to egress-zone policy.

## What

- Added a distinct destination-prefix projection from local traffic selectors intersected with non-tunnel unicast main-table routes. The remote projection remains authoritative for inner-source containment.
- Added the projection to the Go/Rust snapshot protocol, validate it against the Rust route index before making the tunnel row valid, and use it for destination containment.
- Evaluate zone-pair policy after selector and route checks; non-permit decisions deny before `WouldPermit`.
- Added IPv4/IPv6 Go projection coverage and Rust regressions for remote-to-local-LAN admission, default-deny policy, explicit VPN-to-LAN permit, and remote-selector-only destination refusal.

## Risk

The new snapshot field requires Go/Rust lockstep. A missing or inconsistent local projection makes the row invalid or denies the destination; it cannot broaden tunnel source authority. Zone policy may further deny a packet that previously reached `WouldPermit`, intentionally restoring policy enforcement.
