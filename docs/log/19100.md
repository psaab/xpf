## G6 `coloc` cell: PASS criterion, pre-registered before the run (2026-11-15)

**Cell.** The arm-a shape on node0, CoS ON, RSS pinned, 30 s, 3 reps:
- v6 class 10: port 5210, -P2, cport 42100. Class 10 is `iperf-24g`, exact
  24 Gb/s.
- v4 class 11: port 5211, -P8, cport 42000. Class 11 is `iperf-uncapped`,
  non-exact.
- `reth0.80` is shaped at 25 Gb/s, so R = 1 Gb/s while class 10 demands.

A v4 class-11 stream is **colocated** when it shares its node0 worker with a
v6 class-10 stream. A worker's class-11 **instance rate** is the sum of the
median interval rates of the v4 streams on that worker. Intervals are 0.5 s,
and each stream's first 1.0 s is excluded.

**Premises (otherwise the rep is VOID, not PASS):**
- **P1 (colocation).** At least one v4 class-11 stream is colocated.
- **P2 (capacity-bound exact).** The +13 s CoS snapshot shows class 10
  backlogged on that colocated worker. Without that, the exact class is
  rate-bound there and the starvation shape is absent.
- **P3 (the control discriminates).** In master's control arm, at least one
  colocated class-11 stream has zero-rate intervals in at least half of its
  intervals. This is the #19100 defect: the drain serves exact first and the
  colocated instance is never visited. If master does not show it, the cell
  cannot discriminate. Report it; it is not a PASS.

**PASS (PR arm), in every rep:**
- **C1, no starvation.** No colocated v4 class-11 stream has a zero-rate
  interval. The contract gives a minimum turn every 1 ms epoch, and a funded
  turn about every 31 exact packets on a capacity-bound worker.
- **C2, the share.**
  - Each colocated instance rate is at least 0.5 × the median of the
    non-colocated instance rates. The contract gives the colocated instance
    about C_w/32 (about R/3 in the lab) against its peers' about R/2 from the
    pool; that ratio is about 0.69, and 0.5 allows for TCP and RSS spread.
    Where every worker carries the same number of v4 streams, this equals a
    per-stream comparison against the non-colocated streams' median.
  - Each colocated instance rate is also at least 0.5 × (that worker's v6
    class-10 rate) / 31: the turn-credit rate phi_max/(1-phi_max) times the
    worker's exact rate, halved for tolerance.
- **C3, the exact cost.** The median over reps of the PR arm's v6 class-10
  aggregate is at least (1 − 1/32) × the median of master's control. The
  noise allowance is master's own rep spread, (max − min)/median of its
  per-rep aggregates; a PR median below 0.969 × control − that spread FAILS.
  phi_max = 1/32 caps funded displacement at 3.1% of a colocated worker.

**What master's control is expected to show:**
- C1 and C2 FAIL: the colocated class-11 streams are starved, with zero-rate
  intervals in most or all of the window (0.000 in the simulator; 0 Gb/s for
  the whole window in the #19100 lab report).
- The non-colocated class-11 streams are served from the pool.
- The class-10 aggregate is the baseline for C3.

**Confound, written down before the run.** A low-rate TCP stream can show a
zero-rate 0.5 s interval through retransmission-timeout backoff even while
its queue is served. A zero-rate interval in the PR arm still counts as a C1
FAIL. G6 reports that stream's retransmit count beside it, and the
disposition goes to the user (retransmit FAILs are dispositioned per case).
