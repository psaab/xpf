- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11 ~12:15 UTC
  - **Action**: Fixed by marking the section and re-sampling it. Nothing is held, and the lost update's content is not carried forward. The reasoning is stated once, in `partial_update_outcome_9684.go`.
    - **Marking.** `Manager.partialOutcomeUnknown` is a two-bit set: neighbors, fabrics. A verb failure other than an in-band refusal marks its section. An in-band refusal marks nothing, and neither does a #6034 fence the ACK check recognises.
    - **Re-sampling.** Every publish that copies `*m.lastSnapshot` re-samples the marked sections into the copy it sends (`resampleUnresolvedSectionsLocked`), using the builders the verbs use. It unmarks them once the publish lands.
    - **`syncSnapshotLocked`** re-samples before its same-plan and plan-change checks. It takes its plan key and dedup hash from the re-sampled copy, then writes the sections into `m.lastSnapshot`.
    - **Other unmarks.** A landed verb unmarks its section. A landed Compile unmarks both, because `buildSnapshot` samples both fresh.
    - **Neighbor re-send.** While neighbors are marked, both neighbor senders re-send even an unchanged kernel set.
    - **Test seam.** Added `neighborSnapshotBuilder`, mirroring `fabricSnapshotBuilder`. `SyncFabricState` now samples through `sampleFabricsLocked`.
    - **Docs.**
