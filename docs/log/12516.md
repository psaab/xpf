# Issue #12516: then-first policy terms lose `from` on set replay

## Why

`term T then accept from ...` is accepted by the hierarchical parser as one packed term node, and LoadOverride's `parsePolicyTermInlineKeys` reads the `from` tail as the term's match clause. `FormatSetForLoadMerge` preserves that spelling in a flat command, but `SetPath` follows the schema chain beneath `then`: the `from` clause lands under whichever action precedes it, where the policy-term compiler never reads it. Any action can precede it (`then metric 10 accept from ...`, `then next-hop self accept from ...`, `then community add C1 accept from ...`), and a `community` or `as-path-prepend` value list absorbs it outright. A route-filter or protocol predicate can consequently disappear while the term commits as an unconstrained accept.

## What

`ConfigTree.SetPathQuotedGrouped` replays a then-first policy term line as two ordinary set paths: the `from` clause on the term, then the actions (`pkg/config/policy_then_from_12516.go`). The term-level `then` is found by following SetPath's own container walk to the policy-term `then` schema node, so the root and `groups <name>` mounts (which share schema nodes), wildcard and bracket-widened names resolve alike, with no fixed token offsets. The scan after `then` steps over each action's declared operands (`community add|delete|set` take the operation plus one value) and decides each `from`:

- At a clause position, quoted or not, it starts the clause, the reading LoadOverride gives the packed spelling.
- In a `then community` value slot it is a community name, as the schema walk and the brace spelling read it.
- Unquoted in another operand slot it starts the clause; commit refuses the action left without its operand.
- Quoted in such a slot, or bracketed anywhere, it could be a value or the clause, so SetPath refuses the line before creating any node.

Each half keeps the quote and bracket provenance and the closed bit of its tokens, so an unclosed `from` list is still refused (#11779).

`delete`, `deactivate`, and `activate` of a then-first line with a `from` clause are refused before any mutation, with an error that names the term or its `from` and `then` statements as the alternative. After replay the line is two statements, and applying the verb to both would not mean the line either: a chained action such as `then metric 10 accept` reaches only its last link, which would leave a match-all `set metric`.

Store regressions run each shape through hierarchical and flat `LoadMerge`, `LoadSet`, and direct `set`: route-filter exact, `upto /24`, protocol, and combined matches; metric, local-preference, next-hop, community add, as-path-prepend, next policy, accept-then-local-preference, and community value-list action prefixes; a quoted `from`; the reject direction at the root and in an applied group; bracket-list provenance (the unclosed list through the three flat ingresses, since hierarchical text re-renders it closed); and the quoted and bracketed refusals and community names spelled `from`. The verb refusals run through `LoadSet`, interactive `delete`, a hierarchical merge of an inactive packed term, and the display-set reload of one loaded by override. The earlier malformed-route-filter and from-less cells are unchanged. Config-package cells pin the split index for 29 spellings, provenance on the replayed tree, and that refused sets and verbs leave the tree untouched.

## Risk

- Scope: `set` replay and the `delete`/`deactivate`/`activate` verbs of policy-statement term paths whose term-level `then` is followed by `from`. Every other path is unchanged.
- Spellings that used to commit now fail at load: an inactive then-first term with a `from` clause (`inactive: term T then accept from ...;`) cannot be merged, and its `show | display set` output cannot be reloaded, because the `deactivate` line is refused; rewrite it as `inactive: term T { from ...; then ...; }` or deactivate the whole term. Master loaded it and left the term active as an accept-all. A bracketed `from` and a quoted `from` in an operand slot are refused too; LoadOverride still reads a packed bracketed `from` as the match clause.
- Configs saved before this fix keep the old tree: a `from` clause nested under an action (`then { accept { from ... } }`) still compiles as an accept-all on commit, boot, and HA SyncApply, and nothing migrates or quarantines it. `display set` renders such a term as `then accept from ...`, which now replays to the constrained term, so reloading that output narrows what is enforced. Deleting or deactivating the nested clause by that spelling is refused like any then-first line; `delete ... term T then accept` or the whole term still works. #12517 (PR #12669) rejects the saved shape at commit; boot and SyncApply only warn.
- Hierarchical `LoadMerge` re-renders an unclosed `from` list closed (`FormatSetForLoadMerge`), so it commits the listed members where LoadOverride refuses; the from-first spelling behaves the same on master.
- Out of scope and unchanged: a policy-level `then accept from ...` drops the `from` on every path, LoadOverride included, and a bare `from` matches all routes on every path.
