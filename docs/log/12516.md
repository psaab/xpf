# Issue #12516: then-first policy terms lose `from` on set replay

## Why

`term T then accept from ...` is accepted by the hierarchical parser as one packed term node. `FormatSetForLoadMerge` preserves that spelling in a flat command, but `SetPath` follows the schema chain beneath `then/accept`; the `from` match is then invisible to the policy-term compiler. A route-filter or protocol predicate can consequently disappear while the term commits as an unconstrained accept.

## What

`ConfigTree.SetPathQuotedGrouped` now recognizes the supported policy-term action followed by a `from` clause and applies it as two ordinary schema paths: the `from` clause on the term and the action on the term. This keeps direct set-path replay, `LoadSet`, flat `LoadMerge`, and hierarchical `LoadMerge` on the same tree shape. The fix carries quote and bracket provenance with each resulting path and does not reinterpret quoted/bracketed `from` tokens or action operands.

Store regressions cover route-filter exact, route-filter `upto /24`, protocol, and combined match conditions through hierarchical/flat `LoadMerge`, `LoadSet`, and direct set input, plus applied-group `LoadMerge` and `LoadSet` paths. They assert compiled match values and action, preserve the no-`from` accept case, and require malformed then-first route-filter forms to fail rather than commit a match-all permit.

## Validation

- `go test ./pkg/configstore -run 'TestThenFirst' -count=1` — passed.
- `go vet ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore` — passed.
- `go test ./pkg/config ./pkg/configstore -count=1` — failed on unrelated existing config/configstore tests (inventory census drift, API-auth expiry fixtures, routing-instance fixtures); the config suite also timed out after 10 minutes. The command did not reach its chained vet step; vet was run separately and passed.

## Risk

The path rewrite is restricted to root or `groups <name>` policy-options policy-statement terms with an unquoted, unbracketed `from` immediately after `then accept` or `then reject`. Other set paths are unchanged.
