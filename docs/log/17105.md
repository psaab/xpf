# #17105 — delete the dead `reset_binding_counters` zero-pass

- **Timestamp:** 2026-10-27
- **Branch:** `fix/17105-campaign`
- **File(s):** `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/coordinator/reconcile/reset.rs` (deleted), `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/coordinator/reconcile/mod.rs`, `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/coordinator/tests.rs`, `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/coordinator/refresh_bindings_9956_tests.rs`, `userspace-dp/debug-leg.excluded`

## Why

`reset_binding_counters` hand-zeroed 97 `BindingStatus` fields immediately after teardown, but both reachable reconcile exits then call `refresh_bindings`, which rewrites every slot through `copy_live_snapshot` or `zero_unbound_slot` — both supersets of the reset list. The intervening `bring_up_workers`/`plan_workers` reads only identity and shared-UMEM fields the reset never touched. The reset was a dead store that every new counter PR had to update in lockstep (15/15 recent `reset.rs` commits also touched `refresh_bindings.rs`), and its four per-field cells pinned a pass no operator could observe.

## What

- Delete `reconcile/reset.rs` (the fn and its four `reset_9956_tests` cells: flowless 9956, NAT-fence 11066, transit 15941, pre-L3 10498), its `pub(super) mod reset;` declaration, and its sole call at `reconcile/mod.rs:360`. `refresh_bindings` (no-snapshot arm and post-`bring_up_workers`) is now the sole owner of post-teardown binding state; the next counter PR touches `refresh_bindings.rs` only.
- Update the stale rationale comments: the `no_snapshot` arm in `reconcile/mod.rs`, the #2515 doc comment and survivor-field comment in `tests.rs`, the refresh-phase comment in `reconcile_with_none_snapshot_reaches_no_snapshot_early_exit`, and the header/inline comments in `refresh_bindings_9956_tests.rs` that cited the deleted companion cell.
- Remove the deleted test's `userspace-dp/debug-leg.excluded` registry entry so the debug-leg census stays exact.
- The deleted cells' field families remain covered end-to-end by the `refresh_bindings_9956_tests.rs` round-trip cells (which drive `zero_unbound_slot`) and by the #5190 structural census.

## Risk

Behavior-preserving: the deleted writes were overwritten by `refresh_bindings` before any status left `reconcile` on every reachable exit (the `apply_snapshot` None arm returns an error after `refresh_bindings` has not run, but it also returns no bindings to any reader — it is defensively unreachable post-#2484 and its error path does not publish binding state). No wire, counter, or status field changes; the only removed surface is the dead pass and its four cells, whose assertions are subsumed by the `zero_unbound_slot` round-trip cells and the #5190 census.
