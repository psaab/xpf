# #9766: a FullResync export and the reconciliation drain queue deltas without a shared lock

Shipped together with #9767 (`docs/log/9767.md`).

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11 08:30 PDT
- **Action**: Verified the race at master `ad2ba883a`.
  - `handleEventStreamFullResync` exports and queues the batch with no lock held.
  - `eventStreamFallbackLoop` drains under `userspaceDeltaSyncMu` on its own goroutine,
    in both its connected arm and its disconnected arm.
  - The event stream's own delta frames cannot interleave, because the export runs
    inline on the reader goroutine (#9630).
- **File(s)**: none (read-only)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-09-11 08:50 PDT
- **Action**: Implemented the fix.
  - `handleEventStreamFullResync` holds `userspaceDeltaSyncMu` across
    `exportUserspaceOwnerRGSessionsWithConfig`, which covers the snapshot and the
    queueing.
  - Both fallback-loop arms drain through the new `drainUserspaceSessionDeltasLocked`,
    so neither arm can drop the lock without a pin failing.
  - With #9767's pacing, the drain can now be held off for as long as the export takes
    to queue. Its cadence is 5 s while the stream is up and 100 ms while it is down, and
    a delayed drain loses nothing.
  - Cells:
    - The drain fires inside the export, after its snapshot, carrying the close of the
      exported session. The queue must then read install, then delete.
    - An AST pin checks that the loop has two locked drains and no unlocked one.
