## Why

Peer-heartbeat-timeout suppression was split across the pkg/cluster boundary: the daemon held the policy (`shouldSuppressPeerHeartbeatTimeout`), the 5s cap and the `hbSuppressStart` stamp, while heartbeat admission (the reset event) ran inside `cluster.Manager.handlePeerHeartbeat` under `Manager.mu`, joined by three hooks (`SetPeerTimeoutGuard`, `SetPeerHeartbeatRecoveredFunc`, `SetPeerNeverSeenSyncFreshFunc`). That split caused #12165 (stamp never re-armed on recovery, second gap inherited an expired cap) and forced an unlock/guard/relock recheck in `handlePeerTimeout` to repair late guard stamps.

## What

- Move the policy into `pkg/cluster/heartbeat_suppress.go` as `(*Manager).syncSuppressesPeerTimeoutLocked(nowMono)`, `hbSuppressCapExceeded`, `maxPeerSyncSilence` (2s) and `maxSyncSuppressDuration` (5s, renamed from the daemon-local `maxSuppressDuration`), with `hbSuppressStart` a plain field under `m.mu`.
- Heartbeat admission clears the stamp in the same critical section (`notePeerHeartbeatRecoveredLocked` in `handlePeerHeartbeat`); publishing a sync source also resets it (fresh-connection reset preserved).
- `handlePeerTimeout` decides under `m.mu` with no unlock/relock: transfer-commit check, #2080 freshness re-check, sync suppression, transfer-commit re-check — identical ordering and timing.
- The daemon replaces the three hooks with one `d.cluster.SetPeerSyncSource(ss)` in `wireClusterPeerFailoverHooks`, and `SetPeerSyncSource(nil)` at the two stop sites (`stopClusterComms` sessionSync=nil and `unpublishSessionSyncIfCurrent`). The sync-connect reset becomes the Manager publish reset. Never-seen freshness (`peerSyncFreshLocked`, #11682 silence window) reads the same source.
- Remove the three hook setters/fields, their `InstalledHooks` keys/constants, and the daemon `hbSuppressStart` atomic. Add `SyncSourceInstalled()` so the #7280 wiring test binds the publish (with false-before-wiring).
- Migrate tests: daemon #12165 cell now drives the Manager through the real wiring and observes the stamp via `HBSuppressStartForTesting`; the guard-recheck/interleaving cells drive the Manager directly with a fake sync source (`SetFakePeerSyncSourceForTesting`); cap-boundary and recency/reset cells live in `heartbeat_guard_recheck_test.go` (`TestSyncSuppressesPeerTimeoutLocked17056`, `TestHBSuppressCapExceeded17056`); never-seen cells publish a fake/real source instead of a hook.

## Risk

Timing is unchanged (2s recency, 5s cap, CLOCK_MONOTONIC). One behavioural tightening: suppression state no longer survives a sync-source republish (publish resets the stamp, matching the old fresh-connection reset), and a timeout concurrent with heartbeat admission now serialises on `m.mu` instead of racing an unlocked guard — the #12165 late-stamp interleaving is unrepresentable rather than repaired. `peerHeartbeatFreshLocked` is consulted before suppression (previously only after the guard); a fresh heartbeat aborts either way, so no outcome changes.
