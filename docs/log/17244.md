## What

The shape walk records `ConfigSnapshot.Config` but stops descending into its
configuration subtree. Rust currently stores that field as opaque
`serde_json::Value`; `config_snapshot_config_stays_opaque_to_helper17244`
asserts the exact Rust type, so a future typed field invalidates the exemption.
The Rust protocol version and Go shape pin both remain at v77.

The v77 baseline before exemption measured 1,018 lines in the config subtree
out of 1,725 total digest lines. At this head the re-pinned digest is
`9f1e98f40a45a87f20988483f6106cffdb45704d479f3f6b98830a159d5db341` with 707
lines; GLM independently recomputed the digest byte-exactly.
