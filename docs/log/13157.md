# #13157 — close DHCP host-inbound VRF and shared-netdev gaps

- **Source**: deep-review finding D3-11, validated by three-way adversarial research.
- **Files**: `pkg/dataplane/userspace/zones_host_inbound.go`,
  `pkg/dataplane/userspace/dhcp_backstop_d311_13157_test.go`.

## Why

DHCP host-inbound backstops omitted unzoned DHCP units enslaved to a VRF, since
main-table builders discarded them and the zoned builder intentionally only
visits enforcing zone members. A DHCPv6 admit for an unzoned unit could also
share a kernel netdev with a zoned DHCPv6 unit whose effective policy denies the
service. Disabled interfaces were included in the DHCP host-inbound rules.

## What

Build `sdifname` backstop/admit entries for unzoned and quarantined VRF DHCP
units while retaining the existing no-address condition. Suppress DHCPv6 admits
on every netdev where a zoned DHCPv6 unit denies the service, including a
same-netdev sibling case. Ignore administratively disabled interfaces in both
unzoned and zoned DHCP builders. Added regressions for VRF DHCPv4/v6, unzoned
DHCPv6 admits, shared-netdev policy denial, and disabled interfaces.

## Risk

admit is removed from a shared netdev whenever a zoned DHCPv6 member on it denies
the service. No user-facing wire format changes.
