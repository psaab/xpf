# Issue #13726: Preserve published helper counter snapshots on clear

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-15
- **Action**: changed the no-helper branches of `clearHelperPolicyCountersLocked` and `clearHelperNATCountersLocked` to copy their cached counter slices before zeroing packet/byte values. The manager cache and re-published status now observe the clear while callers holding prior shallow status snapshots retain their original counters. Zone and flood clear helpers already replace their slices and remain unchanged.
- **Regression**: added `TestClearHelperCountersDoNotMutateHandedOutStatus13726`, which captures a shallow `ProcessStatus` snapshot, clears both policy and NAT counters, and checks that the captured values remain unchanged while the current cached values are zero and keep their identities.

## RED -> GREEN proof

- **RED**: before the fix, `go test -count=1 -run '^TestClearHelperCountersDoNotMutateHandedOutStatus13726$' ./pkg/dataplane/userspace` failed because the handed-out policy snapshot changed to zero packets/bytes.
- **GREEN**: after the fix, the same targeted command passed. `go test -race -count=1 -run '^(TestClearHelperCountersDoNotMutateHandedOutStatus13726|TestClearPolicyCountersZerosCachedHelperCountersWithoutHelper|TestClearNATRuleCountersZerosCachedHelperCountersWithoutHelper)$' ./pkg/dataplane/userspace` passed. `go vet ./pkg/dataplane/userspace`, `gofmt -d` on all touched Go files, and `git diff --check` passed.
- **Broader suite**: `go test -count=1 ./pkg/dataplane/userspace` was attempted twice and failed on tests outside the changed clear-helper paths, including contract/snapshot golden mismatches and test helpers whose temporary Unix socket paths fail with `bind: invalid argument`. These failures were not attributed to this change. The three relevant clear tests passed under `-race`.
