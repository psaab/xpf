# Issue #13654 — distinguish requeued session deltas from event-stream loss

## Why

When `flush_session_deltas` could not acquire a close's tuple-gate permit, it
requeued the remaining deltas into the worker's `SessionTable` and still marked
the event stream out of sync. That converted a retryable local contention into
a full owner-RG `LossResync` walk. Purge-retirement closes also fenced their
reverse tuple even though retirement owns only the forward tuple.

## What

Use the same close-key selection in the flow-cancel and delta-flush paths:
purge-retirement closes acquire only the forward tuple permit; ordinary closes
retain the forward/reverse pair. A tuple-gate failure requests resync only when
there is no session-table retry slot to preserve the delta suffix. Added tests
for successful requeue without wedge, missing-slot resync, prior-wedge
monotonicity, FIFO suffix preservation, and avoiding reverse-key contention for
purge retirement.

## Risk

No change to ordinary-close tuple fencing or the no-retry-slot loss recovery.
