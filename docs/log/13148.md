# #13148 — exclude quarantined zones from screen enforcement

- **Source**: deep-review finding D3-02, validated by three-way adversarial research.
- **Files**: `pkg/dataplane/userspace/screens.go`,
  `pkg/dataplane/userspace/zones_quarantine.go`,
  `pkg/dataplane/userspace/screens_quarantine_13148_test.go`.

## Why

Screen snapshot and diagnostic builders iterated configured zones without
applying the userspace zone-quarantine exclusions. A reserved zone or
StableZoneID collision loser could therefore publish screen state and diagnostic
rows despite being absent from `snap.Zones`; a quarantined zone's SYN-flood
screen could also activate SYN-cookie protection globally.

## What

Apply `ZoneQuarantineExclusionsForConfig` consistently in screen snapshots,
missing/inert profile diagnostics, and the SYN-cookie activation predicate.
During snapshot quarantine, remove screen and diagnostic rows for every excluded
zone (including peer-union exclusions), and clear SYN-cookie key material when
no surviving screen row enables SYN-cookie protection. The regression explicitly
attaches a SYN-flood profile to quarantined z214 and checks both exclusion and
positive active-zone preservation.

## Validation

- Regression on pristine pre-fix `662ee842d`:
  `GOCACHE=/var/worktrees/.cache/go-build GOTMPDIR=/var/worktrees/t go test
  ./pkg/dataplane/userspace -run
  '^TestQuarantinedAndReservedZonesExcludedFromScreens13148$' -count=1`
  — RED: quarantined z214 incorrectly activated global SYN-cookie and produced
  key material, and its screen row was published.
- The focused regression passes with the fix.
- `go build ./...`: GREEN.
- `go vet ./pkg/dataplane/userspace`: GREEN.
- Full `go test ./pkg/dataplane/userspace -count=1`: FAIL. Base-tree runs
  reproduced the `TestSnapshotShapeIsPinnedToProtocolVersion8892`,
  `TestGroupOnlyZoneNameDoesNotQuarantineLiveZone12247`,
  `TestContractSnapshotGolden9821`, `TestPoisonedZoneOverrideAbsentFromKernelHostInboundView12834`,
  `TestBareZoneBindOnlyTunnelHostInbound13100`,
  `TestBareZoneBindOnlyTunnelInheritsPhysicalOverride13100`, and
  `TestCapFlagIsWiredEndToEnd9054` failures. Some socket-listener tests also
  fail with `bind: invalid argument` under the available temp-path constraints.

## Risk

Quarantined and reserved zone screens no longer publish or affect the global
SYN-cookie enablement decision; surviving zone behavior is unchanged.
