## Why tailing is safe here (enumeration is not load-bearing)

`RouteSnapshot` (`userspace-dp/src/protocol/snapshot.rs:311-368`) derives
`Default`; every field the literal enumerated beyond the four kept ones is
exactly the type default: `reject: false`, `cluster_terminal: false`,
`next_hop_weights: vec![]`, `discard: false`, `next_table: String::new()`,
`preference: 0`, `rule_priority: 0`, `mtu: 0`, `next_table_route: false`
(field order in the literal differed; values did not). The constructed
`RouteSnapshot` is therefore field-for-field identical before and after.

The owning test `wg_tun_origin_outer_recursion` is not a census: it pushes
this one route into a `ConfigSnapshot`, builds forwarding state, and asserts
a route *shape* — FIB egress ifindex 500 for `192.0.2.1`, a
non-`LocalDelivery` disposition, `resolve_tunnel_outer_to(...)` returning
`None`, and the builder mapping that to
`Err("wg_tun_origin_outer_recursion")`. It never iterates, counts, or
serializes `RouteSnapshot` fields, and no other cell consumes this literal
(it is a local `snap.routes.push`). Unlike the #17142 `SessionDeltaInfo`
specimen (#18298), nothing needs exhaustiveness: a future additive field
entering at its `Default` is exactly the #7689 design intent, so the
preferred disposition (tail) applies and the STOP-and-report census branch
does not.
