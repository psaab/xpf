- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-31
  - **Action**: Measured where the workers block.
    - wchan: 7–9 of the 10 workers sit in `futex_do_wait`, and the
      running worker rotates (a convoy).
    - Off-CPU dwarf stacks: the workers wait on the global
      `shared_sessions` / `forward_wire` mutexes. During the burst they
      wait in publish and lookup. After the burst, 11,933 of 12,021
      resolved sleeps are at `resolve.rs:602`, identified by disassembly
      at `resolve_flow_session_decision_with_conntrack_inner+0x1bf5`.
    - uprobe counts: the resolve site and publish are near zero after the
      burst, while `lock_contended` stays high. So those sites are
      waiters.
  - **File(s)**: none

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-31
  - **Action**: Found the holder, `run_ct_slice` (a936a6f802 / #16217):
    - full-table walks run every slice;
    - `shared_row_stage_and_counts` is called per emit key and scans the
      whole shared session and wire maps while holding both global
      mutexes;
    - worker replication makes each worker's emit set equal to the
      node's whole session count.

    The cost is therefore O(sessions²) under the global mutex. Timing
    from uprobe entry and return:
    - 0.3 ms per slice at ~1.2k rows;
    - 10–15 ms per slice at ~4k forward rows, uncontended;
    - 100 ms to 1.15 s per slice under contention;
    - `shared_row_stage_and_counts` at 9–14k calls per 0.5 s, continuously
      after the burst.

## Fix (branch `fix/18655-ct-slice-keyed-lookup`)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-31
  - **Action**: Equivalence census before replacing the per-key scan. It
    is compile-based: a scratch worktree (since removed) replaced
    `FastMap<SessionKey, SyncedSessionEntry>` with a newtype that has
    `Deref` and no `DerefMut`, so `cargo check --bins` turned every
    mutable use of a shared map into E0596. The type checker listed 26
    production sites (`/var/tmp/fz/r-18655/census-pass3.log`). By map:
    - `shared_sessions`:
      - one insert, `publish_shared_session` (store.rs:785), keyed by
        `entry.key`;
      - removals: `remove(key)` (store.rs:1114) and `clear`
        (session_index.rs:654);
      - get_mut sites (lookup.rs:658/675/704/739/821, store.rs:335/954)
        change only stage, counts, origin or policy. They never change
        `key` or `decision.nat`.

      **Map key == `entry.key` holds.**
    - `shared_forward_wire_sessions`: three inserts.
      - publish (store.rs:909) keys the row by
        `forward_wire_key(entry.key, entry.nat)`, the translated tuple,
        never by `entry.key`.
      - The ct-slice alias insert (lookup.rs:896) keys a canonical clone
        by the CALLER's NAT.
      - Removal's survivor reinsert (store.rs:1251) puts the survivor
        under the removed wire key.

      Removals are the owner-checked `remove` (store.rs:1015) and `clear`.
      A republish of the same canonical key under a changed NAT leaves the
      old alias under the old wire key. Nothing removes it: removal derives
      the alias from the current NAT.

      **Map key != `entry.key`, and not always `fwk(entry.key, nat)`.**
      Keyed `get(F|R|W)` therefore misses rows the scan matched.
  - **File(s)**: none (scratch worktree removed)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-31
  - **Action**: Fix.
    - `shared_row_stage_and_counts` (per key, both whole maps scanned
      under both mutexes) is replaced by `shared_rows_stage_and_counts`.
      It makes ONE pass over both maps per batch, matching rows by
      `entry.key` against a wanted-key index built outside the locks. It
      is exact by construction (same vouching rules, max-only folds), and
      a batch visits `rows(shared) + rows(wire)` whatever its size.
    - The ct-slice's two `Vec::contains` dedups become `CtSlicePairSet`
      (O(1), same order and content).
    - The per-pair `restamp_shared_packet_counts` calls become
      `restamp_shared_packet_counts_batch` (each mutex once per batch).
    - `restamp_shared_forward_wire_established`'s `iter().find` under the
      wire mutex becomes a first-wins map.
    - Comments: the ct-slice comment's "per-worker tables are small"
      premise is corrected.
    - Docs: `worker/README.md` (ct_slice row) and
      `docs/userspace-newflow-ceiling.md` (new table-size-driven
      contention class).
