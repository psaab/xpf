# Issue #12210 — Stamp successful retry and rollback applies

## Why

A successful config-debt retry or commit-confirmed rollback could leave the active config's applied digest unset. `ActiveApplied()` then reported false after convergence, so a newly constructed session-sync object skipped active zone-ownership seeding and withheld the BulkAck for its peer's bulk.

## What

The retry owner and confirmed-rollback success path now capture `ActiveDigest` while holding `applySem` and stamp that captured digest only after successful apply completion. Failed applies and incomplete policy-session invalidation remain unstamped. The retry lifecycle regression reconstructs and publishes a fresh session-sync object, verifies its trust-zone ownership snapshot, and observes a matching BulkAck over a live loopback session-sync pair.

## Validation

 - **RED before:** On base `a714c90d472a3c3d98a3799831cfef3ec311a61c`, `go test ./pkg/daemon -run '^TestBootRetryReconstructsZoneSnapshotAndReceivesBulkAck12210$' -count=1` failed on the missing marker, zone snapshot, and matching BulkAck. The rollback tests failed on marker assertions after converging retry and successful rollback; failed attempts remained unmarked.
 - **GREEN after:** The targeted retry, rollback, zone-seed, #6296 marker, and BulkAck tests passed; `go test ./pkg/daemon -count=1` passed; `go build ./...` passed; `gofmt -l` on all changed Go files returned no files.
 - The reconstruction test uses loopback sockets only; no live cluster was changed.

## Risk

The marker write is serialized with apply and keyed to the captured digest; later promotions cannot make it certify a config that the retry or rollback did not apply.
