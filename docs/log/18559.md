- **Timestamp**: 2026-11-08 17:30-18:40 UTC
- **Action**: Fix (Option A, chosen by the coordinator on the #18559 fork).
  - **Decision.** On the owner of a data RG with a live peer, the untargeted
    form runs the node-targeted transfer to the peer (pre-activation, #5640
    fence, commit), through one shared decision
    `clusterfailover.UntargetedPeerTransfer`, in the gRPC handler (which the
    remote `cli -c` reaches) and the in-process CLI.
  - **Unchanged: local resign.** RG0, a non-owner, a dead peer and a
    peer-forwarded request keep `ManualFailover`. RG0 owns no forwarding,
    so there is nothing to make before the break; its local resign and the
    peer's heartbeat claim are as before.
  - **Fallback.** Only a request that provably never reached the peer falls
    back to the local resign, logged at Info with the reason. That is the
    #18585 clock refusal, or `dialPeer` finding no fabric address (the
    proxies now report `sent`).
  - **Reported, never resigned on top of.** A peer that is not
    takeover-ready (its readiness reasons, no demotion: #18731 class), an
    unknown outcome (`codes.Unknown` passed through), and any failure after
    the RPC was issued.
  - **Operator-visible changes, documented.**
    - The command blocks about 1-1.8 s and prints `... (transfer committed)`.
    - The old owner ends `secondary` / `Manual: no`, not in the Manual hold.
    - The CLI help text is updated.
  - **Harnesses.** No harness required the Manual hold: every non-owner check
    (`deploy-lib.sh`, `stress-failover-lib.sh`, `ha-assurance-lib.sh`,
    `rg_move_soak.py`) accepts `secondary` or `secondary-hold`. The
    `deploy-lib.sh` comment that described the hold is updated.
