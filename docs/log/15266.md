# #15266 — make NPTv6 commit gate zone-aware so it agrees with the helper (B09-01)

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-19
- **Action**: gate the overlap leg of `validateNPTv6Strict` on
  `zonesConflictNPTv6`, so same-prefix NPTv6 rules in distinct `from zone`
  rule-sets commit, while same-zone and wildcard-vs-zone overlaps still
  reject.

## Why

`validateNPTv6Strict` (`pkg/config/compiler_validate_strict_nat.go`)
tracked already-seen prefixes in `seenPrefix{net, ones, ruleSetName,
ruleName}` — no zone — and checked overlap across every rule-set
regardless of `rs.FromZone`. The Rust helper partitions by zone scope
(#5176): `find_overlap` in `userspace-dp/src/nptv6.rs` gates every
candidate on `zones_conflict`, and the Go pre-pass predicate
(`nptv6FindOverlap`/`zonesConflictNPTv6` in
`pkg/config/nptv6_overlap.go`) mirrors it. Two distinct non-empty
`from zone` scopes are disjoint because a packet carries exactly one
relevant zone, so a same-prefix split-horizon pair is legitimate and the
helper installs both — but strict commit rejected it, and the lenient
warning falsely claimed the helper rejects the whole snapshot. Severity
Low: over-rejection of a documented-legitimate configuration; no
enforcement loss.

## What

- `pkg/config/compiler_validate_strict_nat.go` (`validateNPTv6Strict`):
  `seenPrefix` now carries `fromZone` (populated from `rs.FromZone` for
  both internal and external seen lists), and both overlap comparisons
  require `zonesConflictNPTv6(rs.FromZone, prev.fromZone)` in addition to
  the existing prefix-overlap test. This is the same zone-aware predicate
  `NPTv6OverlapConflict` uses, so commit and helper share one answer.
  The #4339 `sameRule` self-skip, the prefix parsing/length/family/
  host-bits checks, and the message wording are unchanged.
- `pkg/config/nptv6_overlap.go`, `pkg/dataplane/compiler_nat.go`:
  comment-only updates recording that the historical "validateNPTv6Strict
  carries no zone" rationale is superseded by this fix.
- `pkg/config/compiler_nptv6_zone_overlap_15266_test.go` (new): five
  cells through `CompileConfig`/`CompileConfigLenient` — split-horizon
  distinct zones commits (strict), split-horizon produces no false
  lenient overlap warning, and three controls that stay rejected
  (same-zone identical prefixes, wildcard-vs-zone, nested /64 under a
  /48 in the same zone).

NOT changed: the Rust helper (already zone-aware), `NPTv6OverlapConflict`
behaviour, the dataplane pre-pass, any other strict NAT gate, and
neighbours outside the cited overlap mechanism.

  `TestNPTv6SplitHorizonLenientNoFalseWarning15266` failed (strict
  rejected / lenient warned on the split-horizon pair); the three

- GREEN after the fix: `go test ./pkg/config/ -run
  'TestNPTv6.*15266' -count=1` passes all five cells.
- Adjacent suites stay green: `go test ./pkg/config/ -run '4339|5818'
  -count=1`, `go test ./pkg/dataplane/ -run '7078' -count=1`, and
  `go vet ./pkg/config/ ./pkg/dataplane/` clean.
- `gofmt -l` clean on all touched Go files.

## Risk

- Behaviour delta is strictly narrower rejection: configurations the
  helper already installed (distinct-zone same-prefix NPTv6) now also
  pass commit; every overlap the commit gate rejected within a
  conflicting zone scope (same zone, either side wildcard) still
  rejects with the same message. No wire or dataplane change.
- Interface/routing-instance-scoped NPTv6 remains rejected by the
  separate #5818 scope gate, unchanged; its `FromZone` is empty, so it
  still participates in overlap as a wildcard, as before.
