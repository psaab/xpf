## Why

The IKE established-exchange exemption (`IkeExchangeTable`, #6471) was
keyed on Initiator SPI + peer/local IPs only
(`forwarding/ipsec.rs`), and the Stage-11 local-destination claim
(`owns_ipsec_local_destination`, `types/forwarding.rs`) was
address-global. A Responder-SPI-nonzero IKE packet arriving on a
second, IKE-denied zone (multihomed peer or spoof-capable AF_XDP
secondary ingress) with the same SPI/source/destination as an exchange
admitted on an IKE-permitting zone skipped its own zone's coarse
host-inbound check and was delegated to strongSwan. Likewise an
address configured only in another VRF was treated as locally
terminated absent a matching transit DNAT override. This is a
forwarding/policy ownership error at the coarse-admission layer;
subsequent fine policy and strongSwan authentication were never the
claim.

## What

Scope both mechanisms to the ingress packet's own zone + routing
domain (VRF):

- `IkeExchangeKey` gains `ingress_zone_id: u16` and
  `routing_domain: u32`. Seed sites pass the admitting scope:
  `poll_descriptor/mod.rs` (Stage-11 NEW-IKE seed, resolved via
  `ike_host_inbound_gate_context` + the flow's stamped domain) and
  `tunnel.rs` local-origin GRE seed (tunnel egress zone + flow
  domain, via `maybe_seed_local_origin_ike`'s new parameters).
  Stage-11 match in `poll_stages.rs` builds the key from the current
  packet's from-zone and `flow.forward_key.routing_domain`.
- `ForwardingState::owns_ipsec_local_destination_in_table` (new,
  `types/forwarding.rs`) requires the destination to also be locally
  owned in the ingress interface's canonical table
  (`local_tables_*` / `interface_nat_tables_*`, or the wildcard
  `local_nat_any_table_*`), mirroring fib.rs's table-scoped
  local-delivery decision. Stage 11 claims only through this
  predicate; the unscoped predicate is unchanged for its other
  callers. Cross-VRF claims now return `NotClaimed` and take ordinary
  forwarding/policy instead of XFRM reinjection (fail-closed).
- NOT changed: ESP/AH/ESP-in-UDP exemption, fine (lo0/junos-host)
  per-packet re-gating, table cap/LRU/idle semantics, HA behaviour.

Regressions (all RED on the pre-fix key/predicate by construction —
the cross-zone/cross-VRF lookups hit the seed / the unscoped claim
returned true):

- `forwarding::ipsec::tests::exchange_match_is_zone_and_vrf_scoped_14124`
- `poll_stages::tests::stage_ipsec_established_seed_does_not_cross_zone_or_vrf_14124`
  (secondary-path integration: seed on permit zone, replay same
  SPI/src/dst on deny zone → `Denied`; same-zone control →
  `Passthrough`; tenant-domain flow → `Denied`)
- `poll_stages::tests::owns_ipsec_local_destination_in_table_is_vrf_scoped_14124`
  (overlapping destination in another VRF, no transit DNAT)

Existing seed-key call sites updated to the scoped key (zone/domain
of their fixtures).

## Risk

A legitimate exchange whose follow-ups ingress a different zone or
VRF than its initiation (asymmetric multihoming) now re-faces the
ingress zone's host-inbound gate instead of riding the seed — the
intended fail-closed posture; zones listing `ike` are unaffected.
Single-VRF/single-zone deployments are bit-identical (zone/domain
resolve consistently on seed and match). GRE local-origin replies
remain seeded under the tunnel zone/domain the builder stamps.
