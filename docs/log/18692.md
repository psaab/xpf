Reading:

- All 24 re-judged runs keep their verdict, and every FAIL keeps the same
  stream.
- The resume instants are bimodal: 0.96–2.91 s, then six stragglers at
  3.75–4.59 s.
- On four runs (dbg2, dbgm1, master2, mb-055714) the integer anchor was
  not floor(c).
- In dbg2 the recorded PASS came from a wrong anchor either way. At 56,
  the window counted the interval that holds the crash (pre-crash bytes).
  At 58, the window was one second late. The in-phase 57 FAILs it.
- In master2 the recorded FAIL is right. At 55 it would have PASSed.
