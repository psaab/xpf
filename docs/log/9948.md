# 9948 — Enforce zone policy on queued embedded ICMP errors

## The defect

The flowless ICMP-error arms in `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/poll_descriptor/mod.rs`
reverse or translate an embedded error into a prebuilt frame. Both `Queued`
branches previously continued immediately, so the queued wire packet skipped the
flowless zone-policy evaluation in the `#3291` path below them. An error arriving
from an untrusted zone could therefore be delivered to the internal client even
when the `wan -> lan` policy denied it. The input-filter and PBR checks remain
hoisted before both arms (`#7359` and `#9528`); this issue was the missing final
zone-policy decision on the queued result.

STEP-0 on origin/master (`6d3775e11`) ran the two existing positive-path
controls: both passed (`2 passed; 6277 filtered`). The source trace showed each
`Queued` arm continuing before the `#3291` policy block, and there was no policy
check on the prebuilt result.

## The fix

`enforce_queued_embedded_icmp_policy` in
`userspace-dp/src/afxdp/poll_descriptor/embedded_icmp.rs` now authorizes the
actual prebuilt wire identity before either queued request owns its descriptor:

- parses the queued Ethernet/VLAN and IPv4/IPv6 outer packet, including the
  cross-family NAT64 output;
- preserves the real ingress interface and VLAN for zone attribution and keeps
  the existing fabric-owner override gating;
- evaluates the flowless L3-aware policy with ICMP packet semantics and zero
  ports for `ForwardCandidate` resolutions;
- preserves `FabricRedirect` as the explicit peer-owned exception: #3291
  adjudicates that disposition on the owning peer and TX #1946 transmits its
  prebuilt frame, including the actual ingress-zone MAC stamp;
- fails closed for `HAInactive`, `PolicyDenied`, and every other terminal or
  non-sendable resolution because queued prebuilts skip all later disposition
  arms and TX dispatch transmits them unconditionally;
- emits the normal policy-deny event, removes the queued prebuilt request, and
  recycles the descriptor on deny.

Both NAT64 (`#6472`) and same-family (`#5690`) `Queued` arms call the helper.
Successful fixtures state an explicit reverse-direction permit, while policy
denials and inactive-HA windows are pinned with event/counter assertions.

## Coverage

- `poll_descriptor_embedded_icmp_reversal_zone_policy_denies_9948`: a forged
  WAN-to-LAN embedded error is denied, does not remain queued, recycles its
  descriptor, emits `PolicyDeny`, and increments `policy_deny`.
- `poll_descriptor_embedded_icmp_reversal_ha_inactive_denies_9948`: an
  explicitly permitted same-family error is still denied during an inactive
  owner-RG window.
- `poll_descriptor_nat64_icmp_error_zone_policy_denies_9948`: the NAT64
  queued prebuilt has the same deny/event/counter contract; its event carries
  the rewritten IPv6 outer identity.
- `poll_descriptor_embedded_icmp_reversal_fabric_redirect_passthrough_9948`:
  a split-RG failover keeps the peer-owned prebuilt queued on fabric parent
  ifindex 21, carries peer destination MAC plus the actual WAN zone stamp, and
  is accepted by an inverse peer parser without a local policy-deny event.
- `poll_descriptor_nat64_icmp_error_fabric_redirect_passthrough_9948`: the
  translated prebuilt uses the same peer destination and WAN zone stamp, and
  the inverse peer accepts it.
- `poll_descriptor_nat64_icmp_error_ha_inactive_denies_9948`: NAT64 remains
  fail-closed during an inactive owner-RG window even with a policy permit.
- `poll_descriptor_embedded_icmp_reversal_reachable_on_flowless_path_5690` and
  the `#7169` NAT64 v4-to-v6 Packet-Too-Big positive control keep explicitly
  permitted same-family/PTB delivery working.
- Existing NAT64 routing-domain (`#9162`), GRE decapsulation (`#8271`), and
  input-filter/PBR (`#7359`/`#9528`) controls remain covered with successful
  fixtures using active HA state and explicit reverse-direction permits where
  required.

Both queued call-site gates have fail-on-revert proof: temporarily replacing
each `policy_allowed` computation with `true` made its corresponding denial
cell observe a queued forward and fail; restoring the gate made both GREEN.
The FabricRedirect carve-out is independently load-bearing: replacing its
peer-owned return with `false` made the same-family passthrough cell fail with
zero queued forwards before restoration.
