# #12179 — count transient seeds toward per-source session quota

- **Issue**: #12179 — MissingNeighborSeed and FabricPuntSeed entries occupy worker-table slots but bypass per-source session accounting.
- **Branch**: `fix/12179-fix`
- **Worktree**: `/var/worktrees/12179-fix`
- **Base**: `1c384b9a47ff7d6e567fa50632faa75c3995ae87` (`origin/master`)

## Why

The new-flow source quota reads the SessionTable's counted-entry totals. Both transient seed origins were excluded from that shared accounting predicate, so each permitted unresolved-neighbor/fabric seed occupied a table slot without consuming the source's allowance. Repeated distinct flows could therefore exceed the configured quota while the neighbor remained unresolved.

## What

- Count forward MissingNeighborSeed and FabricPuntSeed entries through the shared session-limit accounting path, while continuing to exclude WorkerLocalImport replicas and reverse entries.
- Keep quota accounting separate from HA provenance: transient seeds remain node-local and do not emit Open deltas.
- Add a bounded limit-10 regression covering ten distinct tuples to one unresolved next hop, rejection of the eleventh, and per-install accounting for both seed origins. Extend seed-install delta coverage to both origins and update the counted-class invariant documentation/tests.
