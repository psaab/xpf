# #12858 — preserve LocalDelivery sessions on first-policy rename

First-policy rematching now recognizes the representation difference between a
transit session and a LocalDelivery session. A LocalDelivery flow explicitly
matched by `from-zone lan to-zone junos-host` records the local interface's
zone in `metadata.egress_zone`; the worker therefore validates explicit
`junos-host` ancestry and evaluates the replacement policy using the host-only
tier ordering (without counting) in the reserved JUNOS_HOST zone. This avoids
letting transit `to-zone any` rules affect host-bound policy. The retained
session keeps its local egress-zone stamp. Unproven ancestry or a replacement
deny still purges the session.

LocalDelivery LocalMiss entries can be a single forward entry with no reverse
companion. Policy rebind and BPF restamping now permit that single-entry shape
only for LocalDelivery; transit pairs still require their reverse companion.
