# Issue #17867: shared-token community matches in mixed terms

## Timestamp

2026-10-26T15:34:13Z

## Action

- Lower mixed terms with one distinct chain community through the existing
  per-member gate, then their ordinary sibling-dimension variants. A matched
  non-terminating variant jumps past the rest of that term's variants so a
  route matching overlapping OR alternatives receives modifications once.
- Preserve orphan community OR semantics by directing a failed alternative's
  guard to the next alternative, with only the last failure leaving the term.
- Count the emitted post-lowering shape in `config.RouteMapSequenceCount`:
  chain-lowered community references contribute one body dimension and the
  member/guard slots are counted once per distinct chain.
- Add interpreter regressions for shared-token siblings, repeat-reference
  count/emission parity, exact sequence ceiling, action multiplicity, and
  orphan alternative traversal.
