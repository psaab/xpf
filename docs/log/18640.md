# #18640: per-source session cap charged every HA copy on every worker

## Investigation (2026-10-31)

- **Action**: located the cap.
  - It is not the screen `limit-session` (tcp configures no screen). It is
    the dataplane default `DEFAULT_MAX_SESSIONS_PER_SOURCE = 4096` per
    (ingress zone, source IP) per worker
    (`userspace-dp/src/session/mod.rs`).
  - It is checked at the new-flow decision (`new_flow_session_limit_drop`,
    `afxdp/poll_descriptor/session_admission.rs`), which emits
    `SCREEN_DROP "session limit (source)"`.
  - The counter is `SessionTable::session_limit_src_counts`.
- **Action**: census of the primitives in `SessionTable`.
  - Two production slab inserts (`insert_record` from the fresh install and
    from `upsert_synced_with_origin`); one slab remove (`remove_entry`).
  - Three in-place counted-class transitions: `finish_refresh_in_place`,
    `demote_owner_rg`, and the two `rebind_zones*` moves.
  - No other site writes `origin`, `is_reverse` or `ingress_zone` on a
    forward row.
  - Within one table the count is balanced: reverse rows and
    `WorkerLocalImport` aliases never count, and `SyncImport` copies do.
- **Action**: probe cells (N = 4 worker tables). For 8 logical sessions from
  one source the node-wide count was:
  - 8 on the primary (owner plus replicas);
  - 32 after `demote_owner_rg`;
  - 32 on a standby fed by the `UpsertSynced` fan-out;
  - 0 after expiry, so nothing leaks.
- **Finding**: HA sync installs every peer session on EVERY worker, and each
  worker charged its copy. A standby's per-worker count was therefore the
  source's whole total, so a source the primary held under its per-worker
  cap was over the cap on every worker after failover. Posted as
  issuecomment 6548613655.

## Fix

- **Action**: one keyed predicate, `SessionTable::session_limit_counted(key,
  origin, is_reverse)`, now drives every site:
  - fresh install;
  - synced import;
  - the destination back-count;
  - the refresh tail;
  - demotion, which gains the counted-to-uncounted decrement;
  - zone moves;
  - `remove_entry`.

  A fanned-out copy (`SyncImport` / `SyncLiveness`) counts only on the worker
  that a process-keyed hash of the forward key, modulo the fan-out width,
  designates. **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/session/mod.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/session/install.rs`.
- **Action**: wiring. Bring-up passes each worker its rank and the set width
  through `WorkerLaunchPlan::with_session_limit_shard`, and the worker calls
  `SessionTable::set_session_limit_shard`, which rebuilds the counts.
  **File(s)**: `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/worker/launch.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/coordinator/reconcile/bringup.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/worker/loop_body/mod.rs`.
- **Action**: cells in `userspace-dp/src/session/session_limit_fanout_18640_tests.rs`:
  - fan-out charges the node once and spreads across workers;
  - re-import, delete and expiry keep it balanced;
  - demotion charges once;
  - a promoted copy moves the charge to the serving worker;
  - a shard change rebuilds the counts.
- **Action**: docs. `userspace-dp/src/session/README.md`, in the session-limit
  lifecycle section and the per-worker scoping section.
