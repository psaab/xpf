# 12219 — Retain unknown host-inbound children

## Boundary

`parseHostInboundNode` only consumed `system-services` and `protocols`, silently
losing other inner keywords. A compact unknown child such as
`host-inbound-traffic system-service ssh;` stays on the stanza's `Keys` tail
after normalization and was also dropped. Strict Store commit and tolerant
load/sync therefore accepted that zone typo with an empty `HostInboundTraffic`
and no warning. Per-interface deny-all explanations also ignored
physical-plus-unit override unions and case-insensitive runtime service matching.

## Fix

- Retain unknown inner child keywords and unknown compact `Keys` heads on
  `HostInboundTraffic`; valid packed `system-services` / `protocols` tails
  remain normalized and compile as before. Preserve unknown-child provenance
  through repeated-block merges and per-interface clones.
- Reject unknown zone-level children on strict compilation. Tolerant compilation
  and Store load/sync warn with the zone/interface and discarded keyword;
  `LoadOverride` followed by `Commit` and `SyncApply` cover the compact spelling.
- Explain per-interface deny-all only when the effective interface override has
  no recognized service or protocol. Use physical-plus-unit override resolution
  and case-folded known-token lookup, matching runtime admission.

## RED then GREEN evidence

- At parent `83b5c955`, the R1 Store probe showed the compact `system-service`
  typo passed `LoadOverride` + `Commit` with an empty host-inbound set and zero
  related warnings; `SyncApply` also returned zero warnings. A pre-edit direct
  lenient probe reproduced the empty stanza and zero warnings.
- After the change, the targeted host-inbound config and configstore suites below
  pass. Regressions assert strict rejection and tolerant warnings for compact
  unknown heads, valid packed tails and bracket lists still bind, repeated-block
  merging retains a second-block unknown, and the two false deny-all cases no
  longer claim deny-all.

## Risk

A tolerant unknown-only per-interface override still denies all host-inbound
traffic on that interface, as before. Its warning now describes the effective
physical-plus-unit override accurately and does not call recognized uppercase
services deny-all. The existing #6735 structural gate can additionally warn
about malformed packed per-interface tails; strict rejection remains owned by
that earlier gate.
