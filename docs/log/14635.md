# #14635 — SYN-flood attack/alarm threshold is one per-zone aggregate, not per destination

## Defect

`ZoneScreenState` held one `syn_counter: RateCounter` and one
`syn_off_attack_bucket: TokenBucket` per zone (`screen/zone.rs`).
`syn_flood_gate` incremented them with no dst/port key, minted a cookie
challenge (or `DropAggregate`) from the zone sum, and latched
`syn_cookie_active_until_secs` zone-wide. Junos counts `syn-flood`
attack/alarm SYNs to the same destination address (+port/ingress port)
per second, never per zone, and xpf presents the knob as a Junos clone.
Two services at 0.75x threshold therefore summed and false-dropped
(cookie-OFF) or false-challenged — every caught client's first connect
reset (`frame/tcp.rs` answers a validated cookie ACK with RST) — while
a single hot service was under-protected until the zone sum crossed.

Probe (re-review, cookie-OFF, threshold 200): one dst@150/s drops=0;
two dsts@150/s drops=802; one dst@300/s drops=801.

## Fix

Primary per-`(dst_ip, dst_port)` keying in `screen/zone.rs` /
`screen/syn_rate.rs`:

- `syn_attack_sketch: SynRateSketch<RateCounter>` +
  `increment_ip_port_and_classify` (CMS AND/min, fail-closed collisions)
  classifies attack/alarm per key.
- `syn_off_attack_sketch: SynRateSketch<TokenBucket>` +
  `increment_ip_port_token` is the cookie-OFF drop gate per key.
- `syn_cookie_active_per_dst` / `syn_alarm_last_emit_per_dst`
  (bounded 4096, perishable) hold per-key cookie activation and alarm
  cadence; local ACK active checks the per-key map (zone latch retained
  as secondary/legacy state).
- Old zone counters retained only as SECONDARY 8x ceiling counting
  per-dst-admitted packets (mirrors #4112): cookie-OFF
  `syn_zone_off_bucket`, cookie-ON zone latch.
- #3315 dst/src sub-thresholds and their ordering are unchanged.

NOT changed: Go wire/config (thresholds already crossed; only the Rust
keying was wrong), per-source logic except its cookie-active gate now
keys on the packet's destination.

Consequential update: `syn_cookie_whitelist_does_not_widen_past_the_validated_client_9419`
pinned the zone-latch premise — a *different* service was challenged on
its first SYN because the 443 flood had latched the whole zone. Under
per-(dst,port) keying that first SYN passes; the test now pins that it
does NOT inherit the bypass (Pass, not `SynCookieBypass`) and is
challenged only after its own key crosses the threshold.
