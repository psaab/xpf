# #14672 — HA standby idle-lease import never refreshed expiry on re-import

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-17
- **Action**: Refresh a live idle same-binding persistent-NAT lease in
  `PortAllocator::import_idle_lease_inner` from the incoming record, and
  reclaim an expired idle incumbent before the existing-binding lookup.
  Residual of #13692 (do not reopen); references #13692.
- **Files**: `userspace-dp/src/nat/idle_lease_sync_8121.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/nat/allocator.rs`,
  `userspace-dp/src/nat/tests_idle_lease_sync_8121.rs`,
  `docs/log/14672.md`

## Why

`import_idle_lease_inner` (`idle_lease_sync_8121.rs`) returned
`SkippedExisting` for a same-binding incumbent after merely merging
permitted remotes. It never refreshed `expires_at_ns`/`timeout_ns` and
never re-keyed `lease_expirations` / `lease_expirations_by_addr`, so a
standby copy died at its FIRST import's expiry even though the
originator still held the lease and periodically re-exported it with a
fresh `remaining_ns`. The expired-incumbent leg (no
`reclaim_expired_idle_lease_locked` before the lookup) was already
fixed on master by #13692's reclaim-then-lookup (kept, regression cell
`expired_idle_lease_does_not_block_import_13692` stays green); the
refresh leg remained live (finding D5-03, deep-review `0c5f646c`).

## What

- On a same-binding incumbent that is idle (`active_flows == 0`) and
  not revoked: compute `refreshed_expiry = now_ns + remaining_ns`
  (already clamped to the local timeout), update `expires_at_ns` and
  `timeout_ns`, and re-key both expiration indexes
  (`remove_lease_expiration_locked` on the old expiry, mutate,
  `insert_lease_expiration_locked` on the new one). Still returns
  `SkippedExisting`; permission merging is unchanged.
- `remove_lease_expiration_locked` widened `fn` -> `pub(super)` so the
  sibling module can re-key (mirrors `insert_lease_expiration_locked`).
- NOT changed: active leases (`active_flows > 0`) — expiry is owned by
  live flows and re-armed on last release; revoked draining shells —
  a peer re-export must not revive them; mismatched bindings; the
  clear-fence check; capacity/identity/port-busy admission; permission
  merge semantics. The expired-idle reclaim before the lookup is
  pre-existing #13692 behavior, untouched.

## Risk

- A re-import with a *shorter* remaining lifetime now shortens the
  standby expiry to the sender's current truth — that is the intended
  semantics (the record is authoritative for remaining lifetime); the
  originator re-exports periodically, so a transient short record is
  corrected by the next cycle.
- No wire-format, config, or operator-visible change; standby leases
  for still-held bindings simply survive periodic re-export as
  intended. Lab HA-failover verification remains the campaign smoke's
  job, not this unit change's.
