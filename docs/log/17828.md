# 17828 — evict retired system syslog cache entries before reuse

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-28
- **Action**: A successful system-syslog client could remain cached after
  retirement. A failed same-endpoint configuration change closed the old
  client but left its prior fingerprint cached; reverting then reused the
  closed pointer and incorrectly cleared retry debt. A foreign handler swap
  or shutdown drain could likewise close a cached client while making the
  identity gate fail open into per-host reuse. Cache eligibility and the
  early fingerprint gate now reject terminally closed clients using
  `SyslogClient.Closed`; the cache entry is evicted and rebuilt instead.
  Added regressions for failed-change/revert, external retirement, same-identity
  `SetClients(Clients())`, and reordered `SetClients([b,a])`, with live-send
  assertions on the two new close/reinstall cases.
- **File(s)**: `pkg/logging/syslog.go`, `pkg/daemon/daemon_system.go`,
  `pkg/daemon/syslog_system_idempotent_16767_test.go`, `docs/log/17828.md`
- **Validation**: The two added regressions, existing #17828 cells, and
  `TestSetClientsPreservingKeepsSharedClient16767` require focused execution;
  results are recorded in the PR body.
- **Risk**: Closed cache entries are no longer eligible for either the
  fingerprint fast path or per-host reuse. Healthy clients still installed
  and open retain per-host reuse.
