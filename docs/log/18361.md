# #18361 — preserve RIP group permits beside empty host-boundary filters

- **Timestamp:** 2026-10-28
- **Action:** Filed follow-up from the #18350 review and fixed the RIP per-group renderer on `fix/18361-campaign`.
- **File(s):** `pkg/frr/protocols_render_rip_group_16625.go`, `pkg/frr/rip_group_export_16625_test.go`, `docs/log/18361.md`

## Why

A `/32 longer` route-filter is a valid empty set. The RIP per-group renderer treated it as unrenderable, which failed the terminal term and emitted a deny-all list, discarding otherwise valid sibling permits. The generic renderer preserves such an empty max-length boundary with a bare exact-host deny when a broader permit could catch the host route.

## What

- Keep max-length `longer` filters as empty boundaries instead of propagating them as unrenderable.
- Treat a lone boundary term as no-match so later terms can run; when an accepting term has a broader sibling permit, emit a bare exact-host deny before it. Other longest-match stops and unrenderable filters retain their fail-closed behavior.
- Add regressions for an empty boundary beside a broader sibling, a lone empty term followed by a sibling permit, and fail-closed behavior when a later accept cannot be represented.
