# #18361 — preserve RIP group permits beside empty host-boundary filters

- **Timestamp:** 2026-10-28
- **Action:** Filed follow-up from the #18350 review and fixed the RIP per-group renderer on `fix/18361-campaign`.
- **File(s):** `pkg/frr/protocols_render_rip_group_16625.go`, `pkg/frr/rip_group_export_16625_test.go`, `docs/log/18361.md`

## Why

A `/32 longer` route-filter is a valid empty set. The RIP per-group renderer treated it as unrenderable, which failed the terminal term and emitted a deny-all list, discarding otherwise valid sibling permits. The generic renderer preserves such an empty max-length boundary with a bare exact-host deny when a broader permit could catch the host route.

The later-accept lookahead also crossed an explicit reject default. That policy terminates the chain, so any accepts in following policies are unreachable; treating them as live made a representable host-boundary deny and broader permit fall back to deny-all. It also treated disjoint later accepts as relevant even though they cannot change the boundary host's outcome, incorrectly discarding valid permits.

## What

- Keep max-length `longer` filters as empty boundaries instead of propagating them as unrenderable.
- Treat a lone boundary term as no-match so later terms can run; when an accepting term has a broader sibling permit, emit a bare exact-host deny before it. Other longest-match stops and unrenderable filters retain their fail-closed behavior.
- Add regressions for an empty boundary beside a broader sibling, a lone empty term followed by a sibling permit, and fail-closed behavior when a later accept cannot be represented.
- Stop the later-accept lookahead at an explicit reject default, and cover a later exact-host accept that is unreachable after the reject.

- Restrict the lookahead to later accepts whose destination match can include the boundary host; disjoint accepts no longer force deny-all, while reachable same-host and covering-prefix accepts remain fail-closed.

## Risk

- A reachable later accept that can match the empty-boundary host (before a terminating reject) can change its outcome; because a flat per-group prefix-list cannot express that fallthrough, this case remains fail-closed. Disjoint later accepts do not affect the host and are ignored; accepts after an explicit reject default are unreachable and ignored. Other invalid or inexpressible filter shapes retain existing fail-closed behavior; the generic renderer is unchanged.
