# Issue #10431: host-inbound ingress residuals

## Disposition

Fixed the host-inbound residual paths that could fall through to
destination-only policy. VRF-enslaved interfaces now resolve to the effective
VRF master visible at `LOCAL_IN`, including tagged children of bare routing-instance
members. Ambiguous shared effective netdevs receive a destination-scoped,
fail-closed drop instead of being silently omitted. Lifeline-only identities are
excluded, including when a lifeline member shares a VRF master with a data
member. Original-direction established traffic is re-evaluated by ingress rules
before the residual established accept; reply-direction traffic retains its
early accept.

When a tolerant snapshot contains conflicting VRF claims for one collapsed
Linux netdev, this builder cannot infer the runtime's actual VRF master from
those claims alone. It keeps the effective target empty and intentionally
retains destination-only judgment rather than guessing or over-denying both
candidate VRFs.
