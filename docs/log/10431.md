# Issue #10431: host-inbound ingress residuals

## Disposition

Fixed the host-inbound residual paths that could fall through to
destination-only policy. VRF-enslaved interfaces now resolve to the effective
VRF master visible at `LOCAL_IN`, including tagged children of bare routing-instance
Ambiguous shared effective netdevs remain excluded from every individual zone's
ingress scope. The #11331 follow-up now applies destination-owner service rights
on those netdevs before a counted fail-closed catch-all, preserving admitted
services while denying and counting unmatched traffic under the reserved
`junos-host` counter. The daemon warns once when ambiguous VRF ownership appears
and logs recovery once it clears. Lifeline-only identities remain excluded,
including when a lifeline member shares a VRF master with a data member.
Original-direction established traffic is re-evaluated by ingress rules before
the residual established accept; reply-direction traffic retains its early
accept.

When a tolerant snapshot contains conflicting VRF claims for one collapsed
Linux netdev, this builder cannot infer the runtime's actual VRF master from
those claims alone. It keeps the effective target empty and intentionally
retains destination-only judgment rather than guessing or over-denying both
candidate VRFs.

## #11331 follow-up

The ambiguity marker remains on one view so ordinary ingress scoping is
unchanged. For a shared effective netdev, the renderer now applies each
destination owner's configured host-inbound service and protocol rights,
including address-scoped any-service and WireGuard listen ports, before a
counted catch-all deny. The guard shares the reserved `junos-host` counter with
addressed-but-unzoned drops and precedes destination-only fallback. This avoids
broad fail-closed denial while retaining a counted fail-closed path for
unmatched traffic. On apply, the daemon logs ambiguity entry and recovery
transitions rather than warning on every commit.

The stale-reply guard uses the same destination-owner rights on ambiguous
ingress, so admitted reply tuples remain reachable while denied catalog tuples
are dropped before the broad reply-direction accept. v4/v6 builder and renderer
regressions pin SSH/ping service admits, configured link-local handling,
text/netlink parity, and stale-reply owner rights;
`TestHostInboundSharedParentPayloadParses11331` syntax-checks the payload when
`nft` is installed.
