# #15110: carry persistent siblings as lease joins on pool reseed (A76-03)

`reseed_retained_from` (`userspace-dp/src/nat/allocator.rs`) snapshots the
previous allocator's `live_by_flow` — one entry per flow — and re-reserved
each port-bearing entry through the non-persistent `reserve_flow`. Two
flows from one source under persistent NAT share one lease and one
translated tuple but are distinct flow keys, so the carry held one entry
per sibling: the first sibling claimed the occupancy bit as a plain PAT
record (`persistent_key: None`), and every later sibling was refused by
the occupied-bit check in `reserve_flow_maybe_persistent` (an occupied
bit is admissible only for the incumbent or a persistent-lease owner).
The refused sibling was counted `refused` and not carried at all, while
its session kept forwarding on the tuple; the surviving sibling's record
named no lease, so its first release freed the bit — reopening the #6765
reissue / reply-misdelivery hazard for persistent pools through an
ordinary add-an-address pool edit. Single-flow persistent records were
carried lease-less the same way.

## Fix

The snapshot pass now also computes `carried_leases`: the
non-deterministic persistent leases that have at least one live flow
itself carried in range (retained address, port inside the new range;
address-only records qualify on the address check alone, preserving the
existing #8597 K11 behavior). The port-bearing carry arm resolves each
record's `persistent_key` against that map and, when present, calls
`reserve_flow_maybe_persistent(..., Some((key, lease.timeout_ns)))`
instead of `reserve_flow`, on both the untracked and per-worker holder
arms. The first sibling mints the lease in the new allocator, later
siblings join it (`active_flows` derived from joins that succeeded), and
the occupancy bit is freed only by lease teardown after the last flow
releases plus the carried idle timeout. `dropped_persistent_*` counting
now skips carried leases — a rejoined lease is not dropped — while idle
leases (no live flow to carry) and leases on removed addresses are still
counted and reported exactly as #7560 required.

NOT changed, deliberately:

- Address-only carry: unchanged (`reserve_address_only`); address-only
  persistent records keep dropping their lease (#14622/#8597 scope).
- Deterministic (CGNAT/NAPT64 BIB) leases: excluded from
  `carried_leases` — their `deterministic_identity` cannot be rebuilt
  from a flow record, and minting a plain lease under the key would
  corrupt the BIB linkage. They keep the plain carry and the counted
  #7560 drop.
- Clear-replay fences: still carried first, so a lease an operator
  cleared is fenced out of the join and the flow falls back to a plain
  reservation — the pin is not resurrected.
- No caller changes: all three `reseed_retained_from` sites inherit the
  behavior; the #7560 note now simply does not fire for carried leases.

- `reseed_carries_persistent_siblings_as_one_lease_15110` — THE binder.

  control: an idle lease is still counted dropped, never resurrected.
- `reseed_persistent_sibling_outside_new_range_still_skipped_15110` —

## Risk

The carried lease is minted with `imported: true` by the existing
synced-join constructor, so a config-apply carry makes the lease
invisible to idle-lease export until a local flow completes on it —
conservative (no echo to the peer) and self-healing on completion.
