# Issue #16182 — Split pkg/feeds feeds.go into parse.go and fetch.go mechanically

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-21
  - **Action**: Mechanical, same-package split of `pkg/feeds/feeds.go`
    (2,855 lines at base `ba55ef764a`). `parse.go` now holds the feed-body
    parse leg: `fetchResult`, `countingReader`, `parseFeed`,
    `isDefaultRoute9248`, `boundInvalidSample`, `canonicalize`,
    `coversWholeAddressSpace`, `prefixLastAddress`, `hashPrefixes`, and
    the parse caps (`maxLineBytes`, `maxInvalidSample*`,
    `maxFeedPrefixes`). `fetch.go` now holds the fetch/read leg:
    `fetchFeed`/`readFeed`, the body/timeout caps (`maxFeedBodyBytes`,
    `httpClientTimeout`, `feedDialAttemptTimeout`), the redirect policy
    (`maxFeedRedirects`, `sameOriginFeedURL`, `feedOriginPort`,
    `checkFeedRedirect`), SSRF destination validation
    (`blockedFeedDestinationPrefixes`, `feedDestinationAllowed/Blocked`,
    `resolveFeedIPs`, `resolveAndValidateFeedDestination`,
    `pinnedFeedClient`) and feed-URL construction (`escapedPathJoin`,
    `joinFeedURL`, `resolveBaseURL`, `isPlaintextFeedURL`).
    `feeds.go` retains the Manager, `feedState`, shrink-guard epoch
    machinery (intentionally NOT extracted — `installSnapshot` owns the
    ack/epoch gates, #14395), publication/hold/failure handling and
    `SetPrivateFeedAllowlist`/`SetLabFeedTLSRoots`. Imports were pruned
    per file. No statement, signature, symbol visibility or behaviour
    changed, and no test file was modified; the pure-guard redesign and
    `evaluate(candidate)` extraction named as non-goals in the issue
    were not done.
  - **File(s)**: pkg/feeds/feeds.go, pkg/feeds/parse.go (new),
    pkg/feeds/fetch.go (new), pkg/feeds/README.md, docs/log/16182.md,
    debian/changelog
