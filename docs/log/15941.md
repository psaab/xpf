# #15941 — transit drops after forward-candidate are silent

## Defect
Split-RG transit frames admitted at the owner were forward-counted and
created sessions, then vanished pre-egress with zero operator-visible
evidence (fw0-sink-glm.md / split-live-diag2.md on master 61c7d45603):
(A0) transit output-filter/policer `cos.drop` → build-None
(`forward_request.rs:475`) wrote only `dbg.build_fail` (slow path) or
nothing (flow-cache fallback H2b); (B1/B2) #10683/#11074 dispatch
rechecks wrote only `dbg.policy_deny`; the pre-accounting transit gates
(`poll_descriptor/mod.rs:8947-8976`) wrote only `counters.touched`;
`build_fail`/`no_egress_binding` dbg counters were never exported.

## Fix
Six named per-binding counters, batched in `BatchCounters` (no
packet-path allocation — increments on already-taken drop branches
only), flushed to `BindingLiveState`, snapshotted, copied to Rust
`BindingStatus` (serde `default`), Go `BindingStatus`, summed in
`show chassis cluster data-plane statistics`, and exported as
unconditional Prometheus series:

- `transit_output_filter_drops` (`xpf_userspace_transit_output_filter_drops_total`):
  counted in `apply_cos_drop_side_effects` (covers slow-path build and
  deferred neighbor retry), at the cached-descriptor drop and the
  flow-cache fallback build-None in `flow_cache_hit.rs`. Slow-path
  build-None also records exception `transit_output_filter_drop`;
  deferred retry records the same reason.
- `transit_source_martian_drops` (`..._transit_source_martian_dropped_total`):
  #11074 source-class refusals at all poll gates, cached/session hits,
  and the final-TX recheck (which also records `transit_source_martian`).
  Split from destination `martian_dropped` (whose final-TX backstop now
  also records `transit_destination_martian`).
- `transit_ipsec_fence_drops` (`..._transit_ipsec_fence_drops_total`):
  #10683 fence refusals at poll gates, cached hits, and final TX
  (ring: `transit_ipsec_selector_fence`).
- `transit_l2_group_drops` (`..._transit_l2_group_drops_total`):
  #10691 L2-group refusals at poll gates and cached hits.
- `forward_build_failures` (`..._forward_build_failures_total`):
  exported mirror of `dbg.build_fail` — direct live atomic in
  `handle_forward_build_failure` plus the slow-path build-None batch
  record.
- `no_egress_binding_drops` (`..._no_egress_binding_drops_total`):
  exported mirror of `dbg.no_egress_binding` at the dispatch
  no-binding arm (ring already carried `missing_egress_binding`).

BindingLiveState grows by six unconditional u64s before both sentinels:
offsets +48, size unchanged (2688).

## Validation
Unit cells proving each exit increments its counter: builder reject
(`transit_output_filter_drops==1`), dispatch fence/source/destination
rechecks (counter + ring reason), dispatch no-egress, build-failure
handler (live atomic), cached source-martian hit, poll source gate
(#10689 harness), poll L2 gate (#10691 harness), and the
batch→live→snapshot→status→wire→reset round trip. Go:
`TestEmitDropClassCounters_4768` (14 series) and the status golden.

## Risk
Counters only; no forwarding verdict changes. The one attribution
judgment: the flow-cache fallback builder's sole `None` return is the
`cos.drop` verdict, so its `None` is counted as an output-filter drop.
