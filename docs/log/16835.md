# Issue #16835: ordered permutation entries cannot match adjacent literals or one community satisfying two regex members (R3-F12-02)

## Why

#15779 renders a multi-member expanded community definition as one
FRR expanded community-list of ordered permutation patterns over the
joined, sorted community string. #16788 rebuilt those patterns with
structural token boundaries, which fixed the adjacent-literal row
(two literal members consecutive in the sorted string now share the
one separator instead of each consuming it). The shared-token row
cannot be fixed at that layer: Junos evaluates each member against
each community independently, so `[65000:.* .*:100]` must match a
route carrying only `65000:100`, but every ordered sequence pattern
consumes at least one whole token per factor. Even a zero-width
factor occurrence has nonempty consumed extent in a consuming
match, so no finite set of whole-string patterns can express the
sharing (the stdlib letter-run proof: factor languages drawn from
`sigma+` are regular, but the conjunction requires one token to be
counted twice, which a consuming left-to-right match cannot do).
A `then reject` term over such a list therefore let targeted routes
fall through to later terms — a silent filter bypass — and accept /
modify terms skipped them.

## What

- `pkg/frr/community_chain_16835.go` (new): the #16835 chain
  evaluation. `communityChainListName16835` derives one generated
  single-factor list name per (public name, distinct member);
  `communityLiteralFactorPattern16835` gives a literal member its
  pipe-free whole-token pattern; `communityChainMembers16835` /
  `communityChainWidth16835` resolve a definition to its chain
  members (multi-member, expanded, renderable, within the #15779
  bound — the same predicates `renderCommunityListDefinitions`
  emits under, so definition and reference cannot drift);
  `communityExtraTermSeqWeight16835` reserves max(0, members-1)
  continuation sequences per chain alternative and
  `communityTermSeqCount16835` computes the next term's start past
  the widest reserved prefix (route-map numbering gaps are legal,
  so over-reservation is inert and under-reservation would collide
  with a chain slot); `communityOrphanTerm16835` classifies the
  chain-eligible term shape lexically (a chain reference with no
  route-filter / prefix-list / as-path / protocol dimension);
  `communityMemberChainSequences16835` renders the per-member
  `match community` permit chain with `on-match next`.
- `pkg/frr/policy_render.go`: the #16835 interaction comment now
  records that the shared-token row is evaluated by the chain, not
  claimed by the sequencer; `communityListNameDigest` factors the
  generated-name digest; `renderCommunityListDefinitions` emits one
  single-factor chain list per distinct member of every rendered
  multi-member expanded definition (referenced or not, so definition
  and reference cannot drift), with regex members in their #16788
  per-community translation and literals whole-token anchored;
  `communityListCollision` refuses the apply when a chain list would
  fuse with an operator list, a delete backing, or another
  definition's chain list.
- Tests: `pkg/frr/community_shared_token_16835_test.go` pins the
  full acceptance matrix through the production renderer —
  shared-token match, distinct-token control, absent-member
  controls, substring controls (`65000:2000`, `165000:200`), the
  adjacent-literal row with its separated/absent controls — with
  every pattern checked for POSIX compilation and pipe-freedom,
  plus the render-shape, public-list RED witness, policy-render,
  orphan-shape, and live-bgpd daemon cells.
- Docs: this file; the `pkg/frr/README.md` multi-member bullet now
  states the chain evaluation and the preserved caveat.

NOT changed: the public list keeps its #15779 permutation render
byte-for-byte (the chain is additive definitions, so a referencing
term's list match is unchanged); sibling-dimension terms (route-
filters, prefix-list, as-path, protocol) and mixed chain/non-chain
alternative terms keep the historical render because a goto cannot
re-enter the Cartesian variant loop mid-term — their public lists
still under-match the shared row (documented residual). the delete backing and
`communityDeleteTarget` are untouched (chain lists are never a
delete target); the sequence-count SSOT in `pkg/config` is not
narrowed — chain reservation is a route-map-internal invariant
computed in `pkg/frr`, and the count's ceiling only makes rejection
more conservative, never unsound.

## Risk

The chain lists are additive: the public list render is unchanged,
so no existing match widens or narrows. Preserved caveat (documented
in `community_chain_16835.go`): a token match consumes a whole token
even on failure in FRR's walk, so a route whose only 65000-prefixed
community is its last token is matched by the `65000:.*` member via
that first positional hit while a later chain member is not re-tried
against the same token — the Junos-correct fail-closed direction
(a reject term over-matches nothing), and the same positional
consumption exists in the #15779 sequence patterns. A post-merge wiring pass (this head) connected the chain into
`renderPolicyTermSequencesWithDefinitions` after the merged commit
shipped the chain definitions without callers; the live admission
e2e (two-netns eBGP, production render) then proves the daemon's
route-map evaluation end-to-end: 10.99.0.0/24 (community 65000:100,
satisfying both members with one token) is rejected, 10.98.0.0/24
(65000:7) and 10.97.0.0/24 (1:100) are accepted, and swapping the
term to the public SH list leaks 10.99.0.0/24. Residuals: the strict commit gate ordering for
chain reservations at the FRR sequence ceiling is the conservative
direction (a policy near the ceiling may be quarantined rather than
rendered past it).
