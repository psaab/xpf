## F-044 heap budget (measured, not assumed)

- Measured (1M representative keys, `runtime.MemStats` deltas): gen/close-class
  maps 55.9 B/entry; tombstone list node 64 B/entry (+~58 B elems-map entry).
- Ten guard-map families share per-side effective caps: six sender maps
  (generation, close-class, and install-table, v4/v6) and four receiver maps
  (generation and install-table, v4/v6). The effective ceiling is the wired
  helper aggregate `MaxSessions` (logical sessions, workers×131072), clamped
  to [200k default, 5M absolute backstop]; unwired keeps the default.
- At the 5M absolute ceiling, a single family can hold 5M entries, so the
  measured aggregate upper bound is approximately 10 × 5M × 56 B ≈ 2.8 GB,
  plus tombstone-order nodes. The 200k unwired default is approximately
  10 × 200k × 56 B ≈ 112 MB, plus tombstones. This is a per-map safety
  ceiling, not a promise that every family is full simultaneously.
- Kernel/legacy is wired once at construction to `conntrack.MaxSessions/2`
  (BPF-addressed). The code comment at `sync_conn_gen.go` is the canonical
  aggregate-budget statement; this section records the measured inputs and
  the resulting ten-family bound.
