# Issue #16223 — split-RG flows never leave the 20 s TCP opening window

## Finding

On PR #16217 head `2458ac562d`, split posture (RG1 fw0, RG2 fw1) P4
60 s: all 4 streams die at 21–22 s with `fabric_local_wins=0` and
~1.2 M served flow-cache hits per node. SESS16222 expiry lines prove
the sessions underneath the retained cache never leave the TCP
opening window: fw0 forward rows expire `established=false,
handshake_pending=false` (wire Opening, `origin=worker_local_import/
forward_flow, fabric_ingress=true`), fw1 reverse rows expire
`handshake_pending=true`, and `last_seen_ns ≈ created_ns` on the
replica rows despite continuous service. The sole failover-arm
survivor (45866) is the only row that flipped to `Timeout: 1800`.

## Failing conjuncts (predicate-level, unit-proven)

Two distinct sites, both reproduced in
`session/split_handshake_16223_tests.rs` (fail pre-fix, pass post-fix):

1. **Completion predicate** (`probe_hit_inner` /
   `touch_if_stale_with_payload`, session/mod.rs): requires
   `handshake_pending && ACK && !SYN && !closing &&
   (syn_ack_first == metadata.is_reverse)`. For a plain
   HandshakePending pair hit on its **reverse** half, the orientation
   conjunct is `false == true` — never fires; the completing forward
   ACK is cache-served on the fabric peer. For pure Opening rows
   (`handshake_pending=false`) the predicate never even arms.
2. **GC companion retention** (`companion_keeps_alive`,
   session/expire.rs): refuses `!companion.established ||
   companion.handshake_pending`, so a crossed pending half reaps even
   with a freshly-served companion. Combined with the keepalive
   throttle (`expires_after/4`; throttled hits re-stamp nothing) and
   replica rows never re-stamped on the serving worker, both halves
   cross the 20 s deadline together under continuous cache service.

## Fix

- `cache_hit_handshake_progress` (session/mod.rs): companion-aware
  completion consulted by `probe_hit_inner`,
  `touch_if_stale_with_payload`, and the slow-path `lookup` when a
  hit carries payload progress on a classic-pending
  (`handshake_pending`, non-SYN-ACK-first, non-closing) row. Evidence
  must prove served reverse traffic: promoted companion, a half
  refreshed past creation, or nonzero reverse counters (folded only
  on admitted hits — a dropped spoofed SYN-ACK charges none).
  Completion promotes both halves to `tcp_established` (Timeout
  1800) with the usual companion propagation + HA Update. An
  idle-crossed hit half is resurrected only by the same proof;
  otherwise the probe still refuses. A both-halves re-stamp arm
  keeps a progressed-but-not-yet-completable pair alive.
- `opening_companion_keeps_alive` (session/expire.rs): GC retention
  for classic-pending halves, gated on the same served-evidence
  proof plus the companion being within its own window and nonzero
  admitted reverse counters. Pure Opening pairs keep the
  #6752/#13679 refusal (a retransmitted SYN-ACK alone holds
  nothing); SYN-ACK-first pairs still require their reverse ACK
  (#4109); closing pairs keep their fixed fuse (#10885/#14630).

## Round 2 (node-level evidence, live shapes)

FZDiag7 proved the round-1 gates refuse before evidence is consulted:
the fw1 dying row is a lone `syn_ack_first=true` reverse replica with
no local companion, and the fw0 replicas are pure Opening — while the
promoted serving pair (`fabric_punt_seed`/`reverse_flow`, Timeout
1800, reverse counters in the thousands) lives in other tables on the
same node. Round 2 therefore consults node-level served evidence
BEFORE the local `syn_ack_first`/`handshake_pending` refusals:

- `NodeServedEvidence` (session/ctx.rs): shared synced-map handshake
  wire (Established) plus the serving pair's admitted reverse-packet
  count. `promoted_and_served()` requires both, so a promoted-but-
  never-answered pair cannot launder a half-open flow.
- `cache_hit_handshake_progress` and `opening_companion_keeps_alive`
  check it first; the serving worker stamps it on admitted hits and
  completions (`note_node_served_handshake`), the flow-cache path
  restamps the shared entries to Established on completion, and the
  expiry context merges the shared-map wire with the local stamps.
- Replica fence (B4): a `WorkerLocalImport` expiry whose shared map
  still holds the promoted serving entry under the same `session_id`
  skips all BPF-conntrack/flow-cache/NAT teardown in
  `reap_expired_sessions` — the replica adopts the serving
  incarnation's id, so the incarnation-gated mirror delete would
  otherwise pass and delete the serving pair's row.
- Tests model the LIVE shapes in separate tables:
  `syn_ack_first` companion-absent replica, Opening `pend=false`
  replica, zero-served-reverse guard, and the B4 cache fence with
  control.
