# 13909 — Bound local persistent-NAT clear fences

## Boundary

Issue #13909 reports that a local persistent-NAT clear fences all currently
known remote origins at their current generation. Those peers keep advertising
at the same durable generation unless they independently clear, so same-
generation leases created after the local clear are silently rejected forever.
The scope is limited to making that local-clear replay fence temporary and
operator-visible while retaining durable protection during the replay window.

## Change

Local-clear equal-generation fences now expire after two minutes. The expiry is
stored alongside the per-origin generation floor, survives daemon restarts, and
is pruned and durably persisted when an import arrives after expiration. A
peer's newer generation removes its fence immediately. Existing durable state
written before expiry metadata existed is upgraded on load with one bounded
window. Repeated drops are rate-limited per origin and emit a warning including
the floor, expiry, and issue number. Older generations remain rejected by the
separate durable peer-generation watermark.
