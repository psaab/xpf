## Why

The persistent-lease 0->1 join (activation snapshot, expiry de-index,
timeout/promotion, refcount) and the idle-retire (de-index, remove,
free-by-mode) were hand-copied per allocation mode: 6 join sites in
4 shapes including two ~70-line near-twins inside
`allocate_deterministic_v6_at`, and retire copies bypassing
`reclaim_expired_idle_lease_locked`. Missed copies shipped #9158,
#9536, e63ca0cab5, dc6cd537e7 and #14670. Two live divergences
remained: (1) the v6 claimed-port cap-fail returned
`AllocatorExhausted` without bumping `exhaustion_total` while the
full-block and address-only branches bumped it; (2) a revoked-live
lease mapped to `LeaseReuse::LiveModeMismatch` in the PAT copy but
both BIB copies filtered `!revoked` into a bare exhaustion.

## What

- New `nat/allocator/lease_table.rs`: `JoinPolicy { timeout_ns,
  promote_imported, refresh_expiry }`, `JoinOutcome`,
  `join_persistent_lease_locked` (the one snapshot assignment),
  `RetiredLease`, `retire_idle_lease_locked` (unconditional idle
  retire through the single `remove_persistent_lease` seam) and
  `reclaim_expired_idle_lease_locked` (the expired-or-revoked-idle
  predicate). The helpers mutate only live-state; bitmap frees stay
  at call sites because their timing differs (clocked/stamped,
  deferred-past-guard, unstamped) — the retire returns the
  `(addr_index, port)` to free, `None` for address-only (#6041).
- All 6 join sites migrated: local PAT (`reuse_existing_lease_locked`,
  policy local/promote/refresh), both BIB branches via one new
  `PortAllocator::join_bib_locked` (full-block refresh=true,
  claimed-port refresh=false per #15948), synced PAT and synced
  address-only (promote=false, refresh=false — peer origin and
  expiry preserved), local address-only (local/promote/refresh).
- Retire sites migrated to the shared helpers (with each site's exact
  free preserved): PAT revoked-idle + expired/mode-mismatch, BIB
  expired (both branches), local address-only classification,
  `clear_persistent_leases` (deferred stamped frees),
  `rollback_idle_lease_import` (unstamped), GC `reclaim_expired_lease_locked`
  (deferred into `freed`), and the `reclaim_expired_idle_lease_locked`
  wrapper now delegates. `rollback_flow`'s fresh-activation removal
  remains the one caller-direct `remove_persistent_lease` (its expiry
  was never indexed — a different transition, now the documented
  exception).
- **Decided policies (documented in lease_table.rs header):**
  revoked-live is never joined/retired — mode-gated paths keep their
  leave-standing answer, the BIB (no unpinned fallback) refuses;
  every `AllocatorExhausted` in `allocate_deterministic_v6_at` bumps
  `exhaustion_total`, so the claimed-port cap-fail now bumps it (and
  still frees only the candidate port it claimed).
- Preserved allocator behaviour otherwise: admission/cap checks,
  holder bits, `record_pat_owner`, counters (`reuses_total`,
  `allocations_total`), expiry-refresh deltas, and all free timings
  are unchanged at every site.

## Risk

- One deliberate behaviour change: claimed-port BIB cap failures now
  increment `exhaustion_total` (operator-visible counter only; the
  refusal itself is unchanged).
- The `PersistentLeaseTable` struct from the issue's sketch was not
  introduced: live-state fields stay on `PortAllocatorLiveState`
  (dozens of readers), and the transition functions own the
  bookkeeping — the acceptance greps and parity cell, not the
  container shape, were the binding criteria.
- Synced address-only join no longer defers its expiry de-index to
  after the flow insert; the de-index now happens in the shared join
  under the same guard (an active lease is never indexed either
  way; pinned by the parity cell's index assertions).
