## Change and scope

- **Changed:** the three status-apply exits return `nil` deltas, and the passthrough ignores deltas on error.
- **Not changed:** `DrainSessionDeltas` in `manager_status.go` has the identical shape and is deliberately left alone. It is the incremental drain: its deltas are consumed from the helper and must reach the caller even when the status apply fails, so it has no all-or-error contract. That is why the edit is anchored inside `ExportOwnerRGSessions` rather than on the shared text.
